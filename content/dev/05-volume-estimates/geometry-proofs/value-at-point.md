This theorem shows that the $p$-adic absolute value of a line function is the same at all integral points of a model. Its statement form is [`eigen_line_p_adic_frame`](/entry/eigen-line-p-adic-frame/), and its user is [`finConstAll`](/entry/fin-const-all/).

## Statement

Let $E$ be a CM field, $X$ a moduli space with signature $(p_0, q_0)$ and coefficient field $F$, and $Y$ a model of $X$ over a discrete valuation ring $O$ with uniformizer $\varpi$ and $\beta \colon O \to F$. Let $\kappa \colon F \to \mathbb{C}_p$ be an embedding with $|\kappa(x)| \le 1$ exactly when $x \in \beta(O)$. Let $Z$ be a component of $Y$ (`CptOver`) with a Lie module $V$ and an action $\rho$ of $\mathcal{O}_E$ on $V$.

A *line function* $\varphi$ (`IsLineFn`) gives a scalar $\varphi(y, v, w) \in K$ for each $K$-point $y$ and tangent vectors $v_1, \dots, v_{p_0}$, $w_1, \dots, w_{q_0}$. It is nonzero on framed points, changes by $\det A / \det B$ under a change of frames by $A$, $B$, and is compatible with field extensions.

**Theorem.** Assume:

- $v$ and $w$ are bases of the $\tau$ and $\bar\tau$ eigenspaces of $\rho$ in $V$;
- $\mathcal{H}$ is a Hodge lattice of rank $p_0 + q_0$ in $V \oplus V^*$ (`HodgeLat`), and $u$ is adapted to $v, w$ (`IsAdapted`);
- at each point of $Z$, the determinant lattice of the $\tau$ eigenquotient of $\mathcal{H}$ along $u$ is generated by $e$ with $\alpha/\kappa' = c\,e$ and $c^M / \varpi^a$ a unit, with $M > 0$ (`OrderAt`);
- $\varphi$ is a line function, and $(\alpha \bigwedge v) / (\kappa' \bigwedge w)$ represents $\varphi$ on $Z$ (`Represents`).

Then there is $t \in \mathbb{R}$ (`HasFinNorm`) such that $\log |j(\varphi(y, v', w'))| = t$ for each discrete valuation ring $R$ with fraction field $K$, each $j \colon K \to \mathbb{C}_p$ whose unit ball is $R$, each $\beta_K$ with $j \circ \beta_K = \kappa$, and each framed $K$-point $y$ whose frames $v', w'$ come from frames over $R$ (`IsIntFrame`).

The proof gives $t = \tfrac{a}{M} \log |\kappa(\beta\varpi)| + \log |\gamma|$, with $\gamma$ the constant below.

## Proof

1. `cm_type_valuation_embedding`, with `cp_embedding_factors_through_complex`, gives a constant $\gamma \neq 0$: in each Hodge lattice with these eigenspace ranks, the determinant of a basis of the eigenquotient against an adapted $u$ is a unit times $\gamma$.
2. Fix $R, K, j, y$. The point $y$ extends to an $R$-point $z$ of $Y$ (`dvr_point_eigen_frame_reduction`). This gives a map $\psi$ from the local ring of $Z$ at $y$ to $K$ (`dvr_point_component_eigen_reduction`).
3. `hodge_lattice_eigen_decomposition_local` gives local bases $b_1, b_2$ of the eigen sublattices and a new adapted $u'$. Reduction by $\psi$ (`reduce_lattice_coords_to_residue` and others) turns them into integral frames at $y$.
4. The change rule compares the given frame with the reduced one. `Represents` gives $\varphi$ at the reduced frame as $\psi$ of a ratio $d$ of determinants. `order_valuation_compare` gives $\psi(d)^M = \beta_K(\varpi)^a \cdot (\text{unit})$.
5. `residue_hodge_lattice_adapted_basis` and step 1 give the remaining determinant as a unit times $\gamma$. Units have norm 1. Take logarithms.
