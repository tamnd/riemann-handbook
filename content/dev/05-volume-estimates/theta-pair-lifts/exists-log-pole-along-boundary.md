Near each point $y$ of the flag cover $Y_0$, this theorem builds one reduced equation $\delta$ of the boundary and a unit $u$ with $\delta\,\omega_0 = u$ on the interior. The equation $\delta$ is a determinant of logarithmic vector fields on the base, pulled back to $Y_0$. Its one user is [`log_pole_along_boundary_is_simple`](/entry/log-pole-along-boundary-is-simple/), which passes from $\delta$ to any reduced equation.

## Statement

The setting is that of [the parent entry](/entry/log-pole-along-boundary-is-simple/): $Y_0$, its interior $U_0$, the bundle of top forms $K$, the line bundle $\mathcal{L}_\kappa$ and $\omega_0 \in \Gamma(U_0, K \otimes \mathcal{L}_\kappa^{-1})$. This theorem takes `Hypotheses''`, `FnAlgClosed`, `KSIso` and `IsVariety₀`, but not `GrothendieckDeformation`.

**Theorem.** For each $y \in Y_0$ and each open $V \ni y$, there are an open $W \ni y$ in $V$, a chart $i$ of $K$ and a chart $k$ of the flag space with $W$ in both, and $\delta, u \in \mathcal{O}(W)$ such that:

1. $u$ is a unit;
2. for $z \in W$, the germ $\delta_z$ is a unit if and only if $z \in U_0$;
3. $\delta_z$ divides $f_z$ for each $f$ on an open $W' \subseteq W$ that vanishes at all boundary points of $W'$, and each $z \in W'$;
4. for each field $L$ and each $L$-point $z$ of $W$ in $U_0$, $\delta(z)\,\omega_0(z) = u(z)$, where $\omega_0(z)$ is the value of the coefficient of $\omega_0$ in the charts $i$ and $k$.

So $\delta$ is a reduced equation of the boundary on $W$, and $\delta\,\omega_0$ extends to a unit.

## Proof

1. **Coordinates.** `exists_lieEigenBasis_near_boundaryFlagSpace_point` and `exists_bigCell_chartFrame_eigenbasis` give an affine neighbourhood $U$ of the image of $y$ in the base, an eigenbasis of the Lie algebra, and big cell coordinates $N$ on $W$. `exists_logKS_symPair_basis_alt` gives logarithmic vector fields $b$ on $U$. `exists_coordinates_logVectorFields_determinant` gives coordinates $x$ on an affine $U' \subseteq U$ with $dx$ a frame of top forms, and with the boundary ideal on $U'$ generated by $\det b(x)$ (the Lean `logDet`). Then $\delta$ is the pullback of $\det b(x)$ to $W$.
2. **The equation $\delta$.** `etale_bigCellCoordinates_flagSpace` says that $Y_0$ is étale over the base times affine space through $N$. So `reducedEquation_pullback_unitLocus_divides_vanishing` carries the reduced equation of the base to $Y_0$. This gives clauses 2 and 3. `isTopFormFrame_base_coordinates_add_variables_etale` says that the base coordinates with the cell coordinates give a frame $g$ of top forms, so its Jacobian is a unit. The unit is $u = a \cdot d \cdot \operatorname{Jac}(g)$, with $a \neq 0$ rational and $d$ a unit of the big cell chart (`diagUnit`).
3. **The identity at points.** `kodaira_spencer_comparison_along_boundary` gives a rational $s \neq 0$ with `KSCompare`, and `logKodairaSpencer_det_eq_const_mul_horizontal` turns it into the constant $a$. At an $L$-point $z$, `exists_bigCell_framedPoints_triangular_frameChange` gives framed points, and `KSIso` gives a tangent frame. The normalization `IsKS` of $\omega_0$ expresses $\omega_0(z)$ through the determinant of the tangent frame on $g$. That matrix is block triangular (`det_block`). The vertical block has determinant 1 (`vertical_tangentFrame_jacobian_det_one`). The lemma of $a$ evaluates the horizontal block through $\det b(x)$. The rest is bookkeeping.

We have not unfolded `KSCompare` or the coordinate lemmas.
