This chapter collects the local results on Bessel models that the unitary part of the proof uses. At a real place it proves that a bounded Bessel map on a discrete series of $\mathrm{U}(p, q)$ is unique up to a scalar when the gaps of the Harish-Chandra parameter are large, and it bounds the convolution integrals and amplitudes of matrix coefficients uniformly in the parameter. Over a finite field it proves that the restriction of a cuspidal representation of $\mathrm{GL}_{n+1}$ to the mirabolic subgroup is induced from a generic character. It then gives the ingredients of Zuckerman tensoring with the standard representation, the Pieri rule for the values of Bessel functionals on the summands and the resulting moment bounds, and the domination of the normal form of the Bessel function near the boundary of the big cell. These are put together in the weighted bound on rotated points, a product of local bounds for orbital integrals of discrete series coefficients. The last section proves that unitary groups over a CM field have finite covolume. The chapter needs from Volume 1 the [discrete series](/learn/real-groups/#discrete-series-and-formal-degrees) and their [branching law](/learn/periods/#interlacing-and-the-branching-law-for-discrete-series), the [Bessel periods](/learn/periods/#bessel-and-fourier-jacobi-periods) and [local multiplicity one](/learn/periods/#local-multiplicity-one), [highest weights and branching](/learn/representations/#highest-weights-and-branching) for the Pieri rule, the [asymptotics of matrix coefficients](/learn/real-groups/#asymptotics-of-matrix-coefficients), [cuspidal representations](/learn/representations/#cuspidal-representations-and-segments), and [quotients of finite volume](/learn/groups/#quotients-of-finite-volume).

The [weighted bound on rotated points](/entry/weighted-bound-rotated-uniform/), the [uniqueness of Bessel maps](/entry/bessel-map-uniq-gap/), the [uniform amplitudes](/entry/amp-unif-holds/), the [convolution bound](/entry/real-obj-in-infinity-x-jsz/), the [Zuckerman ingredients](/entry/zuckerman-ingredients/) and the [moments of Bessel functionals](/entry/moment-first-length/) are used in the step [HypsAlt is nonempty](/step/nonempty-hyps/) of [the unitary hypotheses](/proof/unitary-hypotheses/), where they give the local inputs of the closed bound. The mirabolic restriction is the statement of [Theorem 1.6.4 of Volume 8](/entry/cuspidal-mirabolic-holds/), a published input of the proof, and the finite covolume gives [the finite volume of unitary groups](/entry/fin-vol-unitary-group/) used in the counting of Hermitian lattices.
