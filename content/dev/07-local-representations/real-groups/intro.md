This chapter collects the facts about real and $p$-adic reductive groups that the proof takes from representation theory, each proved in the explicit form that the later estimates need. For the compact group $U(p) \times U(q)$ it proves the Weyl dimension formula, and for $\mathrm{GL}_N$ the Gelfand and Tsetlin branching rule with integral branching maps and the polarization words that describe products in the Fock model. For the discrete series of $U(p,q)$ it proves that the lowest $K$-type has the predicted infinitesimal character, by the theorem of Casselman and Osborne, and computes the norm of the explicit lowest $K$-type coefficient through the formal degree. On the $p$-adic side it proves a uniform admissibility theorem for Hecke algebras through a polynomial identity, Flath's tensor product theorem, and Zelevinsky's segment representations of $\mathrm{GL}_n$. It ends with Harish-Chandra's finiteness theorem for automorphic forms, an unfolding identity over a finite field, and a slope bound of Bost type for the glued lattices of indefinite forms. The chapter needs from Volume 1 the [highest weights and branching](/learn/representations/#highest-weights-and-branching), the [representations of real groups](/learn/representations/#representations-of-real-groups) and their [discrete series](/learn/representations/#discrete-series), the [smooth representations of $p$-adic groups](/learn/representations/#smooth-representations-of-p-adic-groups), the [finiteness of automorphic forms](/learn/trace-formula/#finiteness-of-automorphic-forms) and the [slopes of hermitian lattices](/learn/arakelov/#hermitian-lattices-and-slopes).

The results on $U(p,q)$ and on segments are used in the steps [EveryCharacter from the literature and VWin](/step/every-character-of-literature/) and [EveryCharacter](/step/every-character/), where the discrete series with a given parameter are built and their formal degrees fixed.
