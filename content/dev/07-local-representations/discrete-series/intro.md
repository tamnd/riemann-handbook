This chapter proves the local facts on discrete series of the unitary groups $U(p,q)$ and on $p$-adic induced representations that the comparison of theta lifts needs. Sections 1 to 3 compute the volumes of unitary groups, write the Haar measure in polar coordinates and fix the compact dual normalization. Sections 4 to 7 study a discrete series through its matrix coefficients: they are square integrable eigenfunctions of the Gelfand invariants, averages over $K$ have a trace given by the lowest $K$-type through a Dirac type operator, the coefficients decay like a fixed power of $\|g\|$ when the Harish-Chandra parameter has a large gap, and a shift of the parameter by $\pm e_s$ is a summand of the tensor product with the standard representation, with an explicit ratio of formal degrees. Sections 8 and 9 move Harish-Chandra modules along steps and prove that every irreducible square integrable unitary representation is a discrete series with a Harish-Chandra parameter. Section 10 builds a majorant on $U(r+k, r+1)$ that is integrable along unipotent subgroups. Section 11 proves interpolation and pointwise bounds for square integrable theta sections on tori with complex multiplication. Section 12 proves Bernstein's irreducibility theorem for $\mathrm{GL}_{a+b}$ induced from two unitary characters, and deduces local results for theta lifts at split and ramified places.
