This chapter bounds the matrix coefficients of a discrete series representation of a real unitary group $\mathrm{U}(p, q)$ pointwise on a big cell, by the $L^2$ norm times an explicit power of the distance to the boundary. The proof puts the operators of a place in normal form, bounds derivatives along words of these operators on the big cell, and removes a cutoff near the boundary by dominated convergence. A bound at three linked places then gives the bound for eigenfunctions, and for coefficients of discrete series. A section at a finite place bounds the least value of theta generators through characters of formal groups. The last section shows that a function that is a locally uniform limit of finite sums of matrix coefficients is a convergent series of them, by the $K$-Fourier series and a rewriting with a common growth exponent. The chapter needs from Volume 1 the [discrete series and formal degrees](/learn/real-groups/#discrete-series-and-formal-degrees), the [asymptotics of matrix coefficients](/learn/real-groups/#asymptotics-of-matrix-coefficients), [highest weights and branching](/learn/representations/#highest-weights-and-branching), [Sobolev norms](/learn/analysis/#sobolev-norms), [Lie groups and Lie algebras](/learn/analysis/#lie-groups-and-lie-algebras), and [formal group laws](/learn/formal-groups/#formal-group-laws).
