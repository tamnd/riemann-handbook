This chapter collects the Whittaker and Kirillov theory of $\mathrm{GL}_n$ that the proof uses, together with a few global results that rest on it. Sections 1 and 2 prove the theorem of Gelfand and Kazhdan: an irreducible admissible representation of $\mathrm{GL}_n$ over a [local field](/learn/representations/#smooth-representations-of-p-adic-groups) has at most one [Whittaker functional](/learn/representations/#generic-representations-and-local-factors) up to a scalar, by way of distributions invariant under an anti-involution ([local multiplicity one](/learn/periods/#local-multiplicity-one)). This is used in the step [EveryCharacter from the Galois row](/step/every-character/). Section 3 shows that a representation that contains a principal series type is a subquotient of a principal series, through the Jacquet module; it is used in [Theorem](/entry/toral-of-lower/). Section 4 is the analogue over finite fields: ranks of Whittaker idempotents of $\mathrm{GL}_N(\mathbb{F}_g)$, used in [Theorem](/entry/bad-share-win/). Section 5 proves the multiplicity one theorem for the Bessel integral of a [discrete series](/learn/real-groups/#discrete-series-and-formal-degrees), used in the step [HypsAlt is nonempty from the closed bound](/step/nonempty-hyps/).

The archimedean part starts in Section 6 with a Dixmier-Malliavin lemma on the line, which writes the value of a smooth function as a series of integrals of its derivatives. Section 7 proves the Kirillov theorem at the complex places: one Whittaker function with a nonzero value at $1$ gives, by one integral operator of rapid decay, every smooth Whittaker function on the mirabolic group that is compactly supported modulo $N$. Section 8 carries this to a finite set of places and to [cusp forms](/learn/automorphic-forms/#fourier-coefficients-and-whittaker-functions), for use in [Theorem 8.7.6 of Volume 8](/entry/vanish/). Section 9 proves the tensor product theorem of Flath for $\mathrm{GL}_n$ ([automorphic representations](/learn/automorphic-forms/#automorphic-representations)), used in [Theorem](/entry/loc-iff/). Section 10 proves the global functional equation of the Godement-Jacquet zeta integral of a matrix coefficient of two cusp forms, used in [Theorem 7.30 of Volume 8](/entry/gen-fe/). The last two sections are of a different kind: Section 11 proves that an abelian variety over a strictly henselian field with many rational torsion points has good reduction ([abelian schemes](/learn/abelian-varieties/#abelian-schemes)), used in [Theorem 3.4.7 of Volume 5](/entry/has-good-reduction-of-infinite-torsion-unramified/), and Section 12 weakens a global inclusion of lattices to local inclusions in an index bound, used in [Theorem](/entry/on-of-has-at-places/).
