This chapter proves the facts about representations of $\mathrm{GL}_n$ and of unitary groups, over $\mathbb{R}$, $\mathbb{C}$ and $p$-adic fields, that the base change argument of Volume 8 and the unitary dual of $\mathrm{GL}_n$ in this volume take as inputs. Sections 1 to 4 work with $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{C})$ as a real group. They prove a form of the theorem of Casselman and Osborne on Lie algebra homology, the injectivity of the top compact class, the existence of a concrete model built from an induced function, and the case $\mu = \rho$ of the theorem of Parthasarathy, Ranga Rao and Varadarajan; these feed [Theorem 7.1.10 of Volume 8](/entry/injective-decomposition-action-holds/), [Theorem 7.1.12 of Volume 8](/entry/mult-g-of-mult-for/) and [Theorem 7.23.6 of Volume 8](/entry/core-g/). Sections 5 to 9 develop the theory of Bernstein and Zelevinsky for a $p$-adic field: the functors $\Phi$ and $\Psi$ on mirabolic products, the exact sequence for $\Phi(\tau \times \sigma)$ and its rank zero case, the vanishing of fibre integrals behind [Lemma 7.5.3](/entry/ker-le-tw-ker/), and the Leibniz rule for derivatives with ordered layers, which gives [Theorem 7.5.2](/entry/leibniz-ord/). Section 10 proves the inverse and swap reductions for unramified principal series of quasi-split unitary groups, used in [Theorem 7.3.4 of Volume 8](/entry/extreme-u/).

From Volume 1 the chapter needs [smooth representations of p-adic groups](/learn/representations/#smooth-representations-of-p-adic-groups), [parabolic induction and the Jacquet module](/learn/representations/#parabolic-induction-and-the-jacquet-module), [representations of real groups](/learn/representations/#representations-of-real-groups) and [compact groups](/learn/representations/#compact-groups). The results on $\mathrm{GL}_n$ serve the step [EveryCharacter](/step/every-character/): the real ones through the archimedean base change of Volume 8, the $p$-adic ones through the classification of the unitary dual of $\mathrm{GL}_n$.

Sections 11 to 23 complete the chapter: the swap step, spherical vectors and exponents for unramified representations, twisted centralizers, pairings on derivatives, the geometric lemma, finite $K$-types and smooth cusp forms, Bernstein irreducibility at split places, self-duality of abelian schemes, the Grothendieck ring of $\mathrm{GL}_n$, the geometry of $U(p, q)$ at infinity and the Iwahori factorization.
