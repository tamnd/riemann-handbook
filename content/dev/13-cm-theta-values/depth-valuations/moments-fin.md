This theorem says that the jets of the torsion translates of the theta family, sent into $\mathbb{C}_p$, are moments of $p$-adic measures of norm at most 1. It is the analytic content of [the integral jet moments](/entry/jet-moments/), which gets it through [its second name](/entry/unit-period-translates-moments-finite/).

## Statement

Fix a datum $D$: a CM field $E$ over an imaginary quadratic field $K$, a finite set $T$ of embeddings $\tau \colon E \to \mathbb{C}$, an embedding $\varphi_0$ and a prime $d \equiv 7 \pmod{16}$ such that $\varphi_0(\mathcal{O}_K) = \mathbb{Z}[(1+\sqrt{-d})/2]$. Let $c \ne 0$ be an ideal of $\mathcal{O}_K$ with $N(c)$ prime to $2d$, and $\delta \in \mathbb{C}$ with $\delta^{12} = \Delta(\varphi_0(c))$. Let $\Lambda = \delta\varphi_0(c)$. Put $V = (\mathbb{C}^{N' \times N})^T$ with $H(z,w) = \sum z\bar w / \mathrm{area}(\Lambda)$. For lattices $L, L'$ let $\Lambda_{L,L'} \subset V$ be generated by $w\,\mathrm{emb}(A)$, $w \in \Lambda$, $A$ in a module $Y(L,L')$ of matrices. Let $U_t G(z) = G(z+t)\,e^{-\pi H(z,t) - \pi H(t,t)/2}$ and $W_y = \epsilon(y) U_y$ with a sign $\epsilon(y)$. Let $e_\chi(N,N',L,L')$ be the theta family `B.eNat χ` of a datum $B$ and a character $\chi$ on ideals of $E$ (`IsChi`). We did not unfold its theta function.

**Theorem.** Assume (1) the three equivariance laws `EqP` for every such $(D, c, \delta, B, \chi)$, and (2) that all Taylor coefficients of $U_t e_\chi$ at 0 are algebraic, for $N, N'$ even, $L, L'$ self-dual and $t$ with $a t \in \Lambda_{L,L'}$ for some $a > 0$. Let $p \ne 2$ be a prime with $\left(\frac{-d}{p}\right) = 1$ and $p \nmid N(c)$, and $\lambda \colon \overline{\mathbb{Q}} \to \mathbb{C}_p$. Then there is $\Omega \in \mathbb{C}_p$ with $|\Omega| = 1$ and `MomentsAt`:

For $N, N', L, L'$ as above and $t$ with $n t \in \Lambda_{L,L'}$ for some $n > 0$ prime to $p$, there is $N_0$ prime to $p$ such that for each $p$-local basis $b_1, \dots, b_r$ of $Y(L,L')$ (`NNBasis`) there is a functional $\mu$ on $C(\mathbb{Z}_p^r, \mathbb{C}_p)$ with $\|\mu\| \le 1$. For each $n$ and each $y$ in the group generated by $\varphi_0(\kappa) N_0 p^{-n} x$ with $x \in \Lambda_{L,L'}$, $\kappa \in \mathcal{O}_K$ and $|\lambda(\varphi_0(\bar\kappa))| \le p^{-2n}$:

1. the Taylor coefficients of $G = W_y U_t e_\chi$ are algebraic;
2. for directions $y'_1, \dots, y'_m$ and each continuous $g$ with
$$g(a) = \lambda\bigl(e^{-2\pi i\,\mathrm{Im} H(\delta\,\mathrm{emb}(\textstyle\sum a_i b_i),\, y)}\bigr) \prod_j \Omega^{-1} \sum_i a_i\, \lambda\varphi_0\bigl(h(y'_j, b_i)\bigr)$$
for $a \in \mathbb{N}^r$, where $h(x,y) = \mathrm{Tr}_{E/K} \sum x_{ij}\overline{y_{ij}}$, we have $\lambda\bigl(\partial_{y'_1} \cdots \partial_{y'_m} G(0)\bigr) = \mu(g)$.

## Proof

1. `cmBaseField_integers_and_curveIdeal_facts` gives $d$ prime and $d \equiv 7 \pmod{16}$. `exists_ringEquiv_extending` extends $\lambda$ to a field isomorphism $\iota \colon \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}_p$. `exists_per` gives $\delta_0$ with $\delta_0^{12} = \Delta(\varphi_0(\mathcal{O}_K))$, an even theta function $\theta$ and a unit period $\Omega_0$ with the theta laws. `discOneScale_ratio_ideal_ring_algebraic_unit` shows that $\delta/\delta_0$ is algebraic and a $p$-adic unit. The theorem takes $\Omega = \Omega_0\, N(c)\, \lambda(\delta/\delta_0)$.
2. `exists_analyticSetup_periodLatticePair` gives a linear isomorphism $V \cong \mathbb{C}^g$ that carries $\Lambda_{L,L'}$ and $H$ to a lattice and a Hermitian form of a good setup. By `normalizedThetaVector_isometry_isTheta` the family is a theta function there. Hypothesis (2) at $t = 0$ and `algebraicJets_of_algebraic_taylor` make its jets algebraic.
3. The main step is `GlobalTheory.MomOne.Translates.moments_of_translates`. For each prime-to-$p$ torsion $t$ it gives a measure $\mu$ with the moment property for $U_t e_\chi$, but with no bound on $\|\mu\|$.
4. The bound $\|\mu\| \le 1$: `momentMeasure_norm_le_one_iff_latticeIntegral` reduces it to lattice integrality of the jets of $U_t e_\chi$. That is `normalizedVector_translates_prime_order_integral`, the only use of hypothesis (1), carried to $\mathbb{C}^g$ by `latticeJetsAreBoundedMoments_pullback`.
5. For each $y$: `goodLift_eq_idempotent_lift` replaces $W_y$ by the operator of a standard lift with an idempotent of $\mathcal{O}_K$. `coefIn_weilOperator_translate_algebraic` and hypothesis (2) give part 1. `eq_on_padicInt_of_eq_on_naturals_moment` extends the formula for $g$ from $\mathbb{N}^r$ to $\mathbb{Z}_p^r$, and `pairing_scaledDirections_eq_period_pairing` matches the pairings. The moment property then gives part 2. The rest is bookkeeping.

## In the Lean code

The theorem is in [UnitPeriodTranslatesMomentsFinite.lean](lean:Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/GlobalInterpolation/UnitPeriodTranslatesMomentsFinite.lean#L77). Lines 32 to 75 are six transport lemmas for $V \cong \mathbb{C}^g$, such as `U_transport`. `MomentsAt` is in [TranslateMomentsAll.lean](lean:Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/GlobalInterpolation/TranslateMomentsAll.lean#L23), and `moments_of_translates` in [CmLatticeTranslationOperatorEqPhaseMulNegComp.lean](lean:Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/JetHarmonics/CmLatticeTranslationOperatorEqPhaseMulNegComp.lean#L288). We read the statements of the lemmas of the proof, not their proofs.

The conclusion is weaker than `TranslateMoments` (same file as `MomentsAt`): it excludes $p = 2$ and gives only $|\Omega| = 1$, not the condition `IsOmP` on the formal logarithm. The sign $\epsilon(y)$ (`wPh`) is a `Classical.epsilon` choice for $p \ne 2$; the proof fixes it through `wPh_of_int` when $\sqrt{d}\,H(y,y)/2 \in \mathbb{Z}$. The word "finite" in the name does not describe the conclusion, which is a norm bound.
