This chapter proves the local bounds on depths and valuations that the indefinite tree of the unitary proof needs. Sections 1 and 2 show that a formal group of [height one](/learn/formal-groups/#height) over $\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{C}_p}$ is isomorphic to the multiplicative group, and deduce that a curve with [complex multiplication](/learn/elliptic-curves/#complex-multiplication) has a multiplicative chart at each split prime. Section 3 computes the moments of translates of a CM theta function, as in [algebraicity and integrality of CM values](/learn/theta-functions-cm/#algebraicity-and-integrality-of-cm-values). Section 4 shows that products of short rules and theta rules stay in the integral span of short rules, and gets lattices at the leaves. Sections 5 and 6 build packages of row data at integral points over good primes from the character statement for [formal group laws](/learn/formal-groups/#formal-group-laws), and turn them into depth bounds at the good places of a leaf, over the [local fields](/learn/number-fields/#p-adic-numbers-and-local-fields) of the coefficient field. Section 7 proves sharp valuations of columns at level primes from five kernel laws and the [integral moments of jets](/entry/jet-moments/). The later sections prove the [existence of periods](/entry/exists-per/), the [finiteness of moments](/entry/moments-fin/) and the integral moments of jets.

The results feed [the depth bounds of a record](/entry/leaf-depth-all-record/), [the lattices at the leaves](/entry/leaves-sup-span-data/) and [the six rows at a member](/entry/six-rows-at-member-alt/) in Volume 15. Through them they reach Branch 2 of [the unitary hypotheses](/proof/unitary-hypotheses/).
