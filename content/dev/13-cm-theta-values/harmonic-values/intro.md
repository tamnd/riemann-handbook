This chapter studies harmonic values: harmonic polynomials in the letters of a datum, evaluated on the jets of sections of a line bundle on the universal abelian scheme. Section 1 identifies the harmonic polynomials of a fixed row degree with a tensor product of dual Weyl modules. Sections 2 and 3 build the analytic chart at a complex point and show that there a harmonic value is an ordinary derivative of a product of theta functions. Section 4 gives the archimedean estimates: small integral spanning sets, upper bounds on translated vacua and lower bounds for sums of squares of theta rules. Sections 5 to 7 work at a finite place. They compute translation by a torsion point in a formal chart, sum harmonic values over the kernel of a similitude, and prove that chart values are integral up to an explicit power of the discriminant. Section 8 bounds the jets of a theta series of Dwork type at a prime $l$, and Section 9 gives short bases for hermitian Gram forms and the exponential charts. The chapter needs from Volume 1 the [Fock model](/learn/theta/#the-fock-model), [highest weights and branching](/learn/representations/#highest-weights-and-branching), [formal group laws](/learn/formal-groups/#formal-group-laws) with their [logarithm](/learn/formal-groups/#the-invariant-derivation-and-the-logarithm), [abelian schemes](/learn/abelian-varieties/#abelian-schemes), [semi-abelian degenerations](/learn/abelian-varieties/#semi-abelian-degenerations-and-mumfords-construction), [$p$-adic numbers and local fields](/learn/number-fields/#p-adic-numbers-and-local-fields) and [lattices in hermitian spaces](/learn/hermitian-lattices/#lattices-in-quadratic-and-hermitian-spaces).
