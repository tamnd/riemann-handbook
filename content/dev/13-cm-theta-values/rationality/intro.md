This chapter proves that the theta series of the development have rational coefficients, and that after a fixed scalar their coefficients are integral, with a denominator bound that does not depend on the level. Section 1 gets rationality and integrality from Artin reciprocity. Sections 2 and 3 move the series by central flips and half level translates and bound the denominators uniformly in the level. Section 4 bounds the coefficients and computes the complex conjugate of a series, and Section 5 shows that the series of a rational row span the whole space of forms and that a dictionary of definite unitary forms keeps the Satake parameters at inert places. Section 6 builds Wach lattices in all layers from the prismatic theory of Bhatt and Scholze. Sections 7 to 9 assemble the local data of the main line: Hermitian column data in standard form, the oscillator representation in the Fock model with its harmonic records and theta vectors, level charts at the middle steps, and lifts of multiplicities through compact dual pairs. Section 10 holds three geometric results: a Chern-Weil theorem for good metrics, a statement on squares of degeneration coordinates, and fine and smooth invariant fans of polarizations.
