This chapter proves that theta functions of CM lattices have integral jets at the prime $2$. It starts from a formal power series form of the Frobenius-Stickelberger formula for three points, and pulls it back along the logarithm of the formal group of a Weierstrass curve. This gives integrality of the even sigma function at a point of order $2$, first as a statement on formal series and then for the analytic theta function of a lattice. With complex multiplication by an order in $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-d})$, the translates of the theta function of a half period have integral transport at $2$, and Section 6 turns this into the integral jets at $2$. Sections 7 to 9 hold results that the proof uses next to these: a kernel bound for the formal group law of height two at an odd prime, hermitian frames and local standard lattices at an inert prime, and four assembly results on comparison data and twisted columns. The chapter needs from Volume 1 the [lattices and the Weierstrass function](/learn/elliptic-curves/#lattices-and-the-weierstrass-function), [complex multiplication](/learn/elliptic-curves/#complex-multiplication), [reduction and minimal models](/learn/elliptic-curves/#reduction-and-minimal-models), [formal group laws](/learn/formal-groups/#formal-group-laws) with their [height](/learn/formal-groups/#height) and [logarithm](/learn/formal-groups/#the-invariant-derivation-and-the-logarithm), and [$p$-adic numbers and local fields](/learn/number-fields/#p-adic-numbers-and-local-fields).

The integral jets at $2$ are one of the three parts of the [local jets at all primes](/entry/loc-jets-all/), and the [inputs of the algebraic step](/entry/inputs-of-alg-t/) copy these parts. The step [nonempty hypotheses](/step/nonempty-hyps/) uses the chapter through these two entries.
