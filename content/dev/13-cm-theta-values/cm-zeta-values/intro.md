This chapter collects the results on values of lattice sums and theta functions at CM points that the published inputs of the proof need. Sections 1 to 5 study the [Eisenstein sums of lattices](/learn/elliptic-curves/#eisenstein-sums-of-lattices) of a CM curve. They give the lattice series of a Hilbert Eisenstein series of weight two, the class invariance of the derivatives of CM lattice sums, the integrality of their divided power jets, the Galois conjugation of lattice values, and the toric denominators that follow from these facts. Section 6 constructs [Gross's curves](/learn/elliptic-curves/#grosss-curves): curves with [complex multiplication](/learn/elliptic-curves/#complex-multiplication) by $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-p})$ that have good reduction outside $p$ and minimal discriminant $-p^3$. Sections 7 and 8 prove the key rules at the archimedean place and the $p$-adic integrality of theta jets and of their torsion translates, as in [algebraicity and integrality of CM values](/learn/theta-functions-cm/#algebraicity-and-integrality-of-cm-values). Section 9 computes the Weil operators on theta vacua at inert primes and collects the inputs of the norm bound. Section 10 proves a class number formula for column groups, builds harmonic modules in the Fock model, and bounds the slopes of test lattices.

The results feed [the nonempty hypotheses](/step/nonempty-hyps/), [the key rules for every character](/step/every-character-of-literature/), [the record of published inputs](/step/published-results-eigen/), [the literature join](/entry/join-out-of-literature/), [the family of curves](/entry/exists-family-of-curve/) and [the leaf bound for test lattices](/entry/leaf-mm-of-test/).
