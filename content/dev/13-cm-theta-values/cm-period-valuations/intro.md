This chapter proves the service row of the unitary hypotheses from global Artin reciprocity. [Global Artin reciprocity](/learn/class-field-theory/#global-class-field-theory) gives good models at every prime $l \ge 5$ for the elliptic curves with [complex multiplication](/learn/elliptic-curves/#complex-multiplication) by the ring of integers of $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-d})$. From these [good models](/learn/elliptic-curves/#reduction-and-minimal-models) the chapter derives six hypotheses: algebraic torsion, genus one models over lattices, the curve dictionary, inert integrality, inert admissibility and curve existence. Sections 1 to 6 build the first part of the service row. They prove that the joints hold at every tuple over the true columns, build the joint sites from [adelic](/learn/number-fields/#adeles-and-ideles) chain data, prove the three laws and the kernel difference law at a joint site, and deduce that the system at each place is admitted and has local models. The later sections give the second part of the service row, the inert theta admissibility theorem, the formal isomorphisms at primes of good reduction, which use [formal group laws](/learn/formal-groups/#formal-group-laws), and the bounds on the valuations of CM periods. The chapter needs from Volume 1 also the [lattices in Hermitian spaces](/learn/hermitian-lattices/#lattices-in-quadratic-and-hermitian-spaces) and the [CM values of theta functions](/learn/theta-functions-cm/#algebraicity-and-integrality-of-cm-values).

The main result is [the service row from Artin reciprocity](/entry/serv-h-row-of-artin/). It is used in [the service row of the unitary hypotheses](/entry/serv-h-row-link-r-alt/) of Volume 15, and through it in the step [the unitary hypotheses](/step/hyps-alt-def/) of [the written proof](/proof/unitary-hypotheses/).
