This chapter proves that the torsion values of elliptic functions with complex multiplication are integral, and it proves the local facts on formal groups that this needs. Sections 1 to 4 build the formal group of a Weierstrass curve over a $p$-adic field, bound its addition and its multiplication by $n$ in the kernel of reduction, show that the coordinates of torsion points are integral and unramified, and deduce that the values of the Weierstrass function at the torsion points of a CM lattice are algebraic integers. Section 5 makes a model with good reduction from an integral, unramified $j$-invariant. Sections 6 to 8 give the local theory at a prime: Honda theory for the formal group at an inert prime, bounds for theta jets at a split prime, and the admissibility of the pending tuples of a formal group system. Sections 9 and 10 collect results on lattices and representations that other volumes use: the class map of a symmetric system, the genus of unimodular Hermitian lattices, lengths of quotients, the density of the lowest $K$-type of a discrete series, and the index condition for indefinite forms.
