## Statement

Let $\ell \ge 5$ be prime, $\iota \colon \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}_\ell$ a field isomorphism, and $K$ the closure in $\mathbb{C}_\ell$ of the field generated by the roots of unity of order prime to $\ell$. Let $\wp$ be the [[Weierstrass function|361NAK68QK]] of a lattice $L$ with complex multiplication by $(1 + \sqrt{-d})/2$, where $d$ is squarefree, $d \equiv 3 \pmod 4$ and $\ell \nmid d$.

**Theorem.** Let $\lambda \ne 0$. Suppose $\lambda^4\iota g_2$ and $\lambda^6\iota g_3$ lie in $K$, have norm at most $1$, and give a discriminant $(\lambda^4\iota g_2)^3 - 27(\lambda^6\iota g_3)^2$ of norm $1$. If $z \notin L$, $Nz \in L$, $N > 0$ and $\ell \nmid N$, then $\lambda^2\iota\wp(z)$ and $\lambda^3\iota\wp'(z)$ lie in $K$ and have norm at most $1$.

This is the classical fact that on a curve with good reduction, torsion points of order prime to $\ell$ are integral and unramified (Silverman, *The Arithmetic of Elliptic Curves*, Prop. VII.3.1 and Thm. VII.7.1, the Néron-Ogg-Shafarevich criterion).

## Proof

`cm4` is `cmTorsion_of_point torPt`. Here `torPt` (`scaled_torsion_point_of_add PtOfAdd_of`) says that $(\lambda^2\iota\wp(z), \lambda^3\iota\wp'(z)/2)$ is a point of order dividing $N$ on a Weierstrass curve.
