## Admissible pending tuples

This section proves that the pending tuple of a formal group system is admissible: a differential operator of a given level, applied after a change of frame, sends power series with coefficients of valuation at least $g$ into the lattice of level $j + 1$ attached to a lattice tuple, up to an explicit power of $p$. The proof reduces this to a general statement over any field $K_c$ of characteristic $0$ with a valuation ring, and the general statement reduces it to eight identities on frames, each proved with no hypothesis. The result is the input of [the admissibility theorem for kernel laws](/entry/admissible-general-of-kernel-law/) in the chapter on inert primes.

### Formal group systems

We describe the objects only as far as the statements need them. A *formal group system* $X$ at a prime $p$ consists of:
- a group datum, with a discrete valuation ring $\mathcal{O}$, its fraction field $K_f$, a finite set of places $v$, at each place a $p$-adic field $Q_v$ and a rank, and a bound $r$ for the types of edges;
- for each level $j$ below a top level $m$, a lattice setup $P_j$, whose tuples assign a lattice to each vertex; a *lattice tuple* is a tuple whose entries are lattices;
- at each level, a ring of polynomials in the frame variables $Z_j$, the weight spaces $W(L)$ of a lattice tuple $L$, which are spaces of polynomials over $K_f$, and the column choices $I$ with the differential operators $D_{j, I, i_0}$ they define.

A *frame* from a tuple $L_a$ at level $j$ to a pair $(c', L'')$ at level $j+1$ is a pair of invertible matrices at each place, compatible with the two tuples. A frame $\varphi$ gives a linear substitution $f \mapsto f \circ \varphi$ of the frame variables, and a kernel map $\delta_j(G, \cdot)$ for $G \in W(L_a)$.

For a field $K_c$ of characteristic $0$ that contains $K_f$ and the base field $K_0$ attached to $p$, and a subring $\mathcal{O}_c$ of $K_c$ with $x \in \mathcal{O}_c$ or $x^{-1} \in \mathcal{O}_c$ for every $x \ne 0$, we say that $a \in K_c$ has *valuation at least* $g$ if $a$ lies in the set defined by $p$, $\mathcal{O}_c$ and $g$.

The *edge condition* on $(L', L'', i_0)$, for tuples $L', L''$ at level $j+1$ and a function $i_0$ from places to $\{0, \ldots, r\}$, says: at each vertex $v'$ over the place $v$, either $i_0(v) = 0$ and $L''_{v'} = L'_{v'}$, or $i_0(v) \ge 1$ and $(L''_{v'}, L'_{v'})$ is an edge of type $i_0(v)$.

### The general statement

**Lemma 7.8.1 (Admissibility over a valued field).** Let $X$ be a formal group system at $p$, and let $K_c$ and $\mathcal{O}_c$ be as above, with $\mathcal{O}$ mapping into $\mathcal{O}_c$. Assume the eight frame identities of the system: the shape identity, the branching identities at the source and at the target, the injectivity of the frame change, the normal span, the middle identity, the unit identity and the edge identity. Let $j$ be a level, $L_a$ a lattice tuple at level $j$, $L'$ a lattice tuple at level $j+1$, $L''$ a tuple at level $j+1$, $c'$ a component, and $i_0$ with $i_0 \le r$ such that $(L', L'', i_0)$ satisfies the edge condition. Then there is a frame $\varphi$ from $L_a$ to $(c', L'')$ with the following property. Let $f$ be a power series in the variables $Z_j$ over $K_c$ and $g \in \mathbb{R}$, such that for every column choice $I$ every coefficient of $D_{j, I, i_0} f$ has valuation at least $g$. Then for every $G \in W(L_a)$,
$$p^{\sum_v N'_v}\, \frac{\prod_v p^{\,m_v \ell_v(L_a)}}{\prod_v p^{\,m_v \ell_v(L'')}}\; \delta_j\bigl(G, f \circ \varphi\bigr)$$
lies in the additive group generated by the elements $a z$, with $a$ of valuation at least $g$ and $z \in W(L')$. Here $N'_v$ is the exponent attached to the weight at $v$, the level $j + 1$ and $i_0(v)$, $m_v$ is the multiplicity at $v$ at level $j+1$, and $\ell_v$ is the length of a tuple at $v$.

Lean: `Formalism.L2.PairingIndexBounds.Ent.admissible_general_lattice_tuple`

*Proof.* The edge identity gives invertible matrices $g'_v$ and $h'_v$ whose column spans are the lattices of $L''$ and $L'$ at $v$, with $h'$ equal to $g'$ times the diagonal matrix of type $i_0$. Choose any frame $\varphi_0$ from $L_a$ to $(c', L')$ and replace its target part by $g'$; this is $\varphi$.

Let $S_1$ be the scalar in the statement and $s'$ the product over places of the determinants of the frame matrices of $h'$, raised to the exponents of the system. The shape, source branching, injectivity and normal span identities show that $S_1 s'^{-1} \delta_j(G, f \circ \varphi)$ lies in the span of products of bideterminants. The middle and unit identities, together with the bound on the coefficients of $f$, show that its pairings with the bideterminants of each column choice have valuation at least $g$. A lemma on bideterminants then puts it in the additive group generated by $a$ times products of polynomials $F_b$ in the bideterminant lattices of $h'$, with $a$ of valuation at least $g$.

Finally the branching identity at the target shows that $s'$ times such a product lies in $W(L')$. Multiplying by $s'$ gives the claim. $\square$

### The pending tuple

A formal group system has a *pending tuple*, built from a complex embedding $E$, the finiteness of the classes of each level, the finiteness of stabilizers, a Lubin-Tate isomorphism, an enumeration of the positions and a family $F$ of power series over $\mathbb{C}_p$. It takes $K_c = \mathbb{C}_p$ and $\mathcal{O}_c$ the valuation ring of $\mathbb{C}_p$. The tuple is *admissible* if for each $j < m$ and each $(L_a, c', L', L'', i_0)$ as in the lemma there is a frame $\varphi$ such that, for every $f$ and $g$ as in the lemma and every $G \in W(L_a)$, the kernel of $f \circ \varphi$ at $G$, multiplied by $p^{\sum_v N'_v}$, lies in the lattice of level $j+1$ and valuation $g$ at $L'$.

**Proposition 7.8.2 (Admissibility from the frame identities).** Let $X$ be a formal group system at $p$ with the data of a pending tuple. If the eight frame identities hold, then the pending tuple is admissible.

*Proof.* Take $K_c = \mathbb{C}_p$, with the algebra structures given by the Witt vectors and by the complex embedding, and $\mathcal{O}_c$ the valuation ring of $\mathbb{C}_p$. Every nonzero $x \in \mathbb{C}_p$ has $x$ or $x^{-1}$ in $\mathcal{O}_c$, and $\mathcal{O}$ maps into $\mathcal{O}_c$ by the construction of the pending tuple. The kernel and the lattice of the pending tuple are those of the lemma, so the lemma gives the frame. $\square$

**Lemma 7.8.3 (The frame identities).** For every formal group system, the eight frame identities hold. In particular the middle identity, the normal span identity and the unit identity hold.

Lean: `Formalism.L2.PairingIndexBounds.Ent.lattice_shape_middle_holds` (also `Formalism.L2.PairingIndexBounds.Ent.lattice_span_normal_holds`, `Formalism.L2.PairingIndexBounds.Ent.lattice_shape_unit_holds`)

*Proof.* Each identity is a statement about all systems, proved from the definitions of frames and bideterminants. The normal span and unit identities follow from the shape identity. The middle identity follows from two shape identities, the identity for the frame change of the pairing, the blockwise image of the transposed frame, and the compatibility and group action of frame changes. $\square$

**Theorem 7.8.4 (The pending tuple is admissible).** For every formal group system $X$ at $p$ and all data of a pending tuple, the pending tuple is admissible.

Lean: `Formalism.L2.PairingIndexBounds.Ent.pending_tuple_admissible`

*Proof.* By the lemma the eight frame identities hold, so the proposition applies. $\square$
