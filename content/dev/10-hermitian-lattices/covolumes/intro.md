This chapter measures the terms of the double coset sum for a [unitary group](/learn/groups/#unitary-groups) of a [Hermitian space](/learn/algebra/#hermitian-and-skew-hermitian-forms) over a CM field. Sections 1 to 5 bound the weight of a term, a ratio of covolumes of arithmetic stabilizers, by the weight of a split term near the reference lattice, and sum these weights over the orbits and the frame classes of one datum and over the pairs of terms with one row lattice. The sums are compared with genus means, and the genus means with the [mass of the genus](/learn/hermitian-lattices/#genus-class-and-spinor-genus). Sections 6 to 9 compute the fibre volumes of normal-form stabilizers, bound the degenerate amplitude at a real place, give two-sided bounds for the covolume of the stabilizer of the reference lattice, and combine these into a bound for the amplitude of each relevant term. Section 10 joins the amplitude bound and a count of pairs from the [mass formula for unitary groups](/learn/hermitian-lattices/#the-mass-formula-for-unitary-groups) into [the type bound](/entry/rows-pos-type-p/). Sections 11 and 12 use [strong approximation](/learn/groups/#strong-approximation) to match the terms of one row and one rational double coset with orbits of global terms, and prove that the squared group sum of a family class is the row weight times the square of a product of local amplitudes. The chapter needs from Volume 1 also the [lattices in Hermitian spaces](/learn/hermitian-lattices/#lattices-in-quadratic-and-hermitian-spaces) and the [discrete series](/learn/representations/#discrete-series) at the infinite places, whose Bessel vectors give the local amplitudes.

The type bound and [the factorization of the family group sums](/entry/family-group-sum-factors-row-weight-amplitude/) are used in the step [HypsAlt is nonempty from the closed bound](/step/nonempty-hyps/).
