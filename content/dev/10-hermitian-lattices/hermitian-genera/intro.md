This chapter proves an identity between two sums attached to a unitary datum: a CM field $F$, a central simple $F$-algebra $C$ of dimension $n^2$ with an involution of the second kind, and Hermitian forms at the infinite places. On one side is a weighted sum, over classes of definite pairs $(\beta, \gamma, c)$, of terms that count fixed points in the way of Kottwitz. On the other side is a sum, over classes of definite Hermitian forms $\beta$, of traces of Hecke operators on spaces of automorphic forms of the definite unitary similitude group of $\beta$. The first section shows that classes of marked pairs and classes of definite pairs correspond, with the same terms. The second and third sections prove that only finitely many classes of definite pairs with irreducible polynomial have a nonzero term. The ratio of two forms lies in the centralizer field of $\gamma$, it is a local norm up to scalars at almost all primes, and a global count of such elements is finite. The fourth section regroups the sum over pairs by forms and conjugacy classes and compares it with the traces term by term. The fifth to tenth sections treat other results on Hermitian lattices over a CM field $K$: local densities and the induction step for the mass of unimodular lattices, the level splitting of a flag parabolic, Haar measures and an energy bound at the archimedean places, and the parametrization of row lattices by adelic cosets with the ties between Gram matrices.
