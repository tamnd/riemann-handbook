## Local densities and the index identity

The last six sections of the chapter prove a mass formula for Hermitian lattices by induction on the rank, and they prove two results of a different kind on unitary groups over the adeles. This section starts the mass formula. It defines the local density of a Hermitian lattice at a prime and proves that, for two lattices in the same space, the ratio of their densities is a ratio of indices of their automorphism groups. It then splits off one vector of the space and proves that a certain normalized count of vectors near the orbit of that vector does not depend on the lattice. The next section uses both results to compute the local factor of the mass of the orthogonal complement. In these sections the field names change. We write $K$ for a CM field and $K^+$ for its totally real subfield, as in [Hermitian forms](/learn/algebra/#hermitian-and-skew-hermitian-forms) and [Hermitian lattices](/learn/groups/#hermitian-lattices-and-the-mass-formula). The fields $F$ and $E$ of the first four sections do not appear.

### The setting

**Definition 6.5.1 (Unramified CM field).** Let $K$ be a number field with an involution $a \mapsto \bar a$. We say that $K$ is an *unramified CM field* if three conditions hold. First, $K$ has no real embedding. Second, $\varphi(\bar a) = \overline{\varphi(a)}$ for each embedding $\varphi \colon K \to \mathbb{C}$ and each $a \in K$. Third, for each maximal ideal $P$ of $\mathcal{O}_K$ there is $\theta \in \mathcal{O}_K$ with $\theta - \bar\theta \notin P$. Let $K^+ \subseteq K$ be a subfield with $\bar a = a$ if and only if $a \in K^+$.

The third condition says that the involution acts nontrivially on each residue field of $\mathcal{O}_K$. So no finite prime of $K^+$ ramifies in $K$. For a maximal ideal $\mathfrak{p}$ of $\mathcal{O}_{K^+}$, let $q = N\mathfrak{p}$ and let $\eta(\mathfrak{p}) = -1$ if $\mathfrak{p}\mathcal{O}_K$ is prime (the inert case) and $\eta(\mathfrak{p}) = 1$ otherwise.

Let $V$ be a $K$-vector space of dimension $m$ and $h \colon V \times V \to K$ a form that is conjugate linear in the first variable, linear in the second and has $h(y, x) = \overline{h(x, y)}$. We say $h$ is *totally positive definite* if $\operatorname{Re} \varphi(h(x, x)) > 0$ for each $x \neq 0$ and each embedding $\varphi$. A *lattice* is a finitely generated $\mathcal{O}_K$-submodule $L \subseteq V$ that spans $V$. It is *integral* if $h(x, y) \in \mathcal{O}_K$ for $x, y \in L$. An *automorphism* of $L$ is a $K$-linear isometry $g$ of $(V, h)$ with $gL = L$. They form a group $\mathrm{Aut}(L)$.

Fix $\mathfrak{p}$. The *localization* $L_{\mathfrak{p}}$ is the $\mathcal{O}_K$-module of the $t^{-1} x$ with $x \in L$ and $t \in \mathcal{O}_{K^+} \setminus \mathfrak{p}$. For $a, b \in K$ and $k \geq 0$ we write $a \equiv b \bmod \mathfrak{p}^k$ if $t(a - b) \in (\mathfrak{p}\mathcal{O}_K)^k$ for some $t \in \mathcal{O}_{K^+} \setminus \mathfrak{p}$. We write $\mathfrak{p}^k L$ for $(\mathfrak{p}\mathcal{O}_K)^k L$.

**Definition 6.5.2 (Local densities).** Let $L$ be a lattice, $n \in \mathcal{O}_{K^+}$ and $k \geq 0$. Let $N_k(L, n)$ be the number of classes in $L / \mathfrak{p}^k L$ that contain some $y$ with $h(y, y) \equiv n \bmod \mathfrak{p}^k$. Let $A_k(L)$ be the number of $\mathcal{O}_K$-linear maps $X$ of $L / \mathfrak{p}^k L$ to itself with $h(x', y') \equiv h(x, y) \bmod \mathfrak{p}^k$ whenever $x'$ lies in the class $X(\bar x)$ and $y'$ in the class $X(\bar y)$. The *representation density* of $n$ and the *local density* of $L$ are
$$\alpha_{\mathfrak{p}}(L, n) = \lim_{k \to \infty} \frac{N_k(L, n)}{q^{k(2m - 1)}}, \qquad \alpha_{\mathfrak{p}}(L) = \lim_{k \to \infty} \frac{A_k(L)}{q^{k m^2}}.$$

The denominators are the expected sizes. The module $L/\mathfrak{p}^k L$ has $q^{2km}$ elements, and $h(y, y)$ takes values in $K^+$, which has $q^k$ classes modulo $\mathfrak{p}^k$. The unitary group of $V$ has dimension $m^2$ over $K^+$.

**Definition 6.5.3 (Discriminant).** The *discriminant ideal* $\mathfrak{d}(L) \subseteq \mathcal{O}_K$ is the ideal generated by the determinants $\det(h(x_i, x_j))$ for $x_1, \dots, x_m \in L$. Let $v_{\mathfrak{p}}(L)$ be the largest $e$ with $\mathfrak{d}(L) \subseteq (\mathfrak{p}\mathcal{O}_K)^e$, and let
$$\nu_{\mathfrak{p}}(L) = \frac{\alpha_{\mathfrak{p}}(L)}{q^{m\, v_{\mathfrak{p}}(L)}}.$$

For lattices $A, B$ let $o(A, B)$ be the number of lattices $gB$ with $g \in \mathrm{Aut}(A)$. By the orbit-stabilizer theorem, $o(A, B) = [\mathrm{Aut}(A) : \mathrm{Aut}(A) \cap \mathrm{Aut}(B)]$.

### The density ratio

**Theorem 6.5.4 (Ratio of local densities).** Let $K$ be an unramified CM field with real subfield $K^+$. Let $h$ be a totally positive definite Hermitian form on $V$, $\dim V = m$, and let $A, B$ be integral lattices. Let $\mathfrak{p}$ be a maximal ideal of $\mathcal{O}_{K^+}$. Then
$$\alpha_{\mathfrak{p}}(A_{\mathfrak{p}})\, q^{m v_{\mathfrak{p}}(B_{\mathfrak{p}})}\, o(B_{\mathfrak{p}}, A_{\mathfrak{p}}) = \alpha_{\mathfrak{p}}(B_{\mathfrak{p}})\, q^{m v_{\mathfrak{p}}(A_{\mathfrak{p}})}\, o(A_{\mathfrak{p}}, B_{\mathfrak{p}}).$$

Lean: `Formalism.HermitianLattices.Ext.L3.local_density_ratio_holds`.

*Proof.* Write $A$ and $B$ for $A_{\mathfrak{p}}$ and $B_{\mathfrak{p}}$. We compare three families of subgroups of $\mathrm{GL}(V)$. Let $G_A = \{X : XA = A\}$. Let $S_A(k)$ be the set of $X \in G_A$ with $h(Xx, Xy) - h(x, y) \in \mathfrak{p}^k \mathcal{O}_{K, \mathfrak{p}}$ for $x, y \in A$, and $N_A(k)$ the set of $X \in G_A$ with $(X - 1)A \subseteq \mathfrak{p}^k A$. Then $N_A(k) \subseteq S_A(k)$, and the same holds for $B$. Four facts hold for $k$ large. First, the sequence $A_k(A)/q^{km^2}$ is constant, so $\alpha_{\mathfrak{p}}(A) = A_k(A)/q^{km^2}$. Second, $A_k(A) = [S_A(k) : N_A(k)]$: each map counted by $A_k(A)$ lifts to an element of $S_A(k)$, and two lifts differ by $N_A(k)$. Third, $[S_A(k) : S_A(k) \cap G_B] = o(A, B)$: the orbit of $B$ under $S_A(k)$ is its orbit under $\mathrm{Aut}(A)$. Fourth, there is $c \geq 0$ with $N_B(k + c) \subseteq N_A(k)$, of index $q^{2m^2 c}$, and with $S_B(k + c) \cap G_A \subseteq S_A(k) \cap G_B$, of index $q^{m^2 c + m v(A) - m v(B)}$. The exponent $2m^2 c$ counts $c$ steps of the filtration of $\mathrm{End}(A)$ by powers of $\mathfrak{p}$, since each step has $q^{2m^2}$ elements. Now compute $[S_A(k) : N_B(k + c)]$ in two ways. Through $N_A(k)$ it is $A_k(A)\, q^{2m^2 c}$. Through the chain
$$N_B(k + c) \subseteq S_B(k + c) \cap G_A \subseteq S_A(k) \cap G_B \subseteq S_A(k)$$
it is $o(A, B)\, q^{m^2 c + m v(A) - m v(B)}\, A_{k + c}(B) / o(B, A)$, where the first fact, applied to $B$ at level $k + c$, gives the last factor as $[S_B(k + c) : N_B(k + c)] / [S_B(k + c) : S_B(k + c) \cap G_A]$. Divide both sides by $q^{(k + 2c) m^2}$ and use the first fact for $A$ at level $k$ and for $B$ at level $k + c$. This gives the identity. $\square$

**Corollary 6.5.5 (Index form of the ratio).** Under the hypotheses of the theorem,
$$\nu_{\mathfrak{p}}(A_{\mathfrak{p}})\, [\mathrm{Aut}(B_{\mathfrak{p}}) : \mathrm{Aut}(A_{\mathfrak{p}}) \cap \mathrm{Aut}(B_{\mathfrak{p}})] = \nu_{\mathfrak{p}}(B_{\mathfrak{p}})\, [\mathrm{Aut}(A_{\mathfrak{p}}) : \mathrm{Aut}(A_{\mathfrak{p}}) \cap \mathrm{Aut}(B_{\mathfrak{p}})].$$
Also $\nu_{\mathfrak{p}}(A_{\mathfrak{p}}) > 0$.

Lean: `Formalism.HermitianLattices.Ext.L3.LIOrb.nu_mul_relIndex` (also `Formalism.HermitianLattices.Ext.L3.LIOrb.nu_pos`, the positivity).

*Proof.* Write each orbit count as an index, by the orbit-stabilizer theorem, and divide the identity of the [theorem on the ratio of local densities](/entry/local-density-ratio-holds/) by $q^{m(v(A) + v(B))}$. Positivity holds since $\alpha_{\mathfrak{p}}(A_{\mathfrak{p}}) = \alpha_{\mathfrak{p}}(A) > 0$. $\square$

### A split vector

Mass formulas are proved by induction on $m$: one splits off a vector and passes to its orthogonal complement. We fix the following data.

**Definition 6.5.6 (Split vector).** Let $(V, h)$ and $(V', h')$ be Hermitian spaces over $K$. A *split vector* consists of $x_0 \in V$ with $h(x_0, x_0) \neq 0$ and an injective $K$-linear map $\iota \colon V' \to V$ with $h(\iota a, \iota b) = h'(a, b)$, $h(x_0, \iota a) = 0$ for all $a, b$, and $V = K x_0 + \iota V'$. For a lattice $M \subseteq V$, its *cut* is the lattice $M' = \iota^{-1}(M)$ of $V'$. For $g' \in \mathrm{GL}(V')$, let $\hat g'$ be the map of $V$ with $\hat g'(c x_0 + \iota a) = c x_0 + \iota(g' a)$.

The map $g' \mapsto \hat g'$ is an injective group homomorphism. Let $\mathrm{Aut}^{x_0}(M)$ be the group of $g' \in \mathrm{GL}(V')$ with $\hat g' \in \mathrm{Aut}(M)$. Then $\hat{}$ maps $\mathrm{Aut}^{x_0}(M)$ onto the group of $g \in \mathrm{Aut}(M)$ with $g x_0 = x_0$, since such a $g$ preserves $x_0^\perp = \iota V'$. Let
$$o(M) = [\mathrm{Aut}(M') : \mathrm{Aut}(M') \cap \mathrm{Aut}^{x_0}(M)].$$

**Definition 6.5.7 (Tube count).** Let $M$ be a lattice, $n \in \mathcal{O}_{K^+}$ and $J, k \geq 0$. The *tube* of level $J$ around the orbit of $x_0$ is
$$\Omega_J(M) = \{ y \in V : y - g x_0 \in \mathfrak{p}^J M \text{ for some } g \in \mathrm{Aut}(M) \}.$$
Let $c_k(M, n, \Omega)$ be the number of classes in $M / \mathfrak{p}^k M$ that contain some $y \in \Omega$ with $h(y, y) \equiv n \bmod \mathfrak{p}^k$. Let
$$\kappa_{J, k}(M) = \frac{c_k(M, n, \Omega_J(M))}{q^{v_{\mathfrak{p}}(M)}\, \nu_{\mathfrak{p}}(M)} \cdot \frac{\nu_{\mathfrak{p}}(M')}{o(M)}.$$

The tube collects the vectors that look like $x_0$ to order $J$. The factor $\kappa$ is the part of $N_k(M, n)$ that comes from this tube, after the densities of $M$ and of its cut are divided out.

**Theorem 6.5.8 (Independence of the tube factor).** Let $K$, $K^+$, $h$ and $\mathfrak{p}$ be as in the theorem on the ratio of local densities, and fix a split vector $(x_0, \iota)$. Let $M_1, M_2$ be integral lattices and $n \in \mathcal{O}_{K^+}$, $n \neq 0$, with $h(x_0, x_0) = n$, $x_0 \in M_{1, \mathfrak{p}}$ and $x_0 \in M_{2, \mathfrak{p}}$. Then for all large $J$, and then for all large $k$,
$$\kappa_{J, k}(M_{1, \mathfrak{p}}) = \kappa_{J, k}(M_{2, \mathfrak{p}}).$$

Lean: `Formalism.HermitianLattices.Ext.L3.LIOrb.kap_eq`.

*Proof.* Write $M_i$ for $M_{i, \mathfrak{p}}$, $c_i$ for $c_k(M_i, n, \Omega_J(M_i))$, and $\Gamma_i = \mathrm{Aut}(M_i)$, $\Gamma_i^{x_0} = \Gamma_i \cap \{g : g x_0 = x_0\}$. The input is a comparison of tube counts. For large $J$ and then large $k$,
$$c_1\, q^{v(M_2)}\, [\Gamma_2 : \Gamma_1 \cap \Gamma_2]\, [\Gamma_1^{x_0} : \Gamma_1^{x_0} \cap \Gamma_2^{x_0}] = c_2\, q^{v(M_1)}\, [\Gamma_1 : \Gamma_1 \cap \Gamma_2]\, [\Gamma_2^{x_0} : \Gamma_1^{x_0} \cap \Gamma_2^{x_0}].$$
The tube $\Omega_J(M_i)$ is a union of translates of $\mathfrak{p}^J M_i$ indexed by the orbit of $x_0$, so this is an orbit count of the same kind as in the theorem on the ratio of local densities. Since $\hat{}$ is injective and maps $\mathrm{Aut}^{x_0}(M_i)$ onto $\Gamma_i^{x_0}$, the indices between the $\Gamma_i^{x_0}$ equal the indices between the $\mathrm{Aut}^{x_0}(M_i)$. Apply the [index form of the ratio](/entry/nu-mul-rel-index/) twice: to $M_1, M_2$ in $V$, and to the cuts $M_1', M_2'$ in $V'$, which are integral lattices for $h'$. The first gives $\nu(M_1)/\nu(M_2)$ as a ratio of indices between $\Gamma_1$ and $\Gamma_2$. The second gives $\nu(M_1')/\nu(M_2')$ as a ratio of indices between $\mathrm{Aut}(M_1')$ and $\mathrm{Aut}(M_2')$. Let $P = \mathrm{Aut}^{x_0}(M_1)$, $W = \mathrm{Aut}^{x_0}(M_2)$, $Q = \mathrm{Aut}(M_1')$ and $R = \mathrm{Aut}(M_2')$, so that $P \subseteq Q$ and $W \subseteq R$. Relative indices are multiplicative along chains of subgroups, so $[Q : P]$ and $[R : W]$, which are $o(M_1)$ and $o(M_2)$, are tied to the indices between $Q$ and $R$ and between $P$ and $W$. All these indices are finite and nonzero. Substitute these relations into the quotient $\kappa_{J,k}(M_1)/\kappa_{J,k}(M_2)$; the indices cancel and the quotient is $1$. $\square$

**Remark 6.5.9 (Formalization).** The theorem on the ratio of local densities is proved in [LocalDensityRatioHolds.lean](lean:Formalism/GL3MeanSquares/GenusMeanAsymptotics/UnimodularClassRepresentatives/LocalDensityRatioHolds.lean#L42) and proves the proposition `LocalDensityRatio` of [LocalDensityRatio.lean](lean:Formalism/GL3MeanSquares/GenusMeanAsymptotics/UnimodularClassRepresentatives/LocalDensityRatio.lean#L134). The four facts of its proof are lemmas in `MemLocFOfSmulMem.lean` (lines 211 and 223), `CoeMemOfMemSmulTop.lean` (line 101) and `RelIndexEqOfMap.lean` (line 206); we read their statements, not all of their proofs. The densities are defined with `limUnder` ([LocalDensityRatio.lean](lean:Formalism/GL3MeanSquares/GenusMeanAsymptotics/UnimodularClassRepresentatives/LocalDensityRatio.lean#L51)), which returns an arbitrary value when no limit exists; the first fact of the proof shows that the limit exists. The orbit count is a `Nat.card` (line 102), which is $0$ for an infinite set. The groups $\mathrm{Aut}(M_{\mathfrak{p}})$ are groups of global isometries of $V$ that preserve the localized lattice, not groups over the completion. The comparison of tube counts in the last proof is the lemma `pair` of [MemSmulTopIff.lean](lean:Formalism/GL3MeanSquares/GenusMeanAsymptotics/UnimodularClassRepresentatives/MemSmulTopIff.lean#L255); we read its statement only. The name `kap` of the tube factor ([ExtHomInjective.lean](lean:Formalism/GL3MeanSquares/GenusMeanAsymptotics/UnimodularClassRepresentatives/ExtHomInjective.lean#L51)) says nothing about its content. The integer $v_{\mathfrak{p}}(L)$ is a supremum in $\mathbb{N}$, which would be $0$ if $\mathfrak{d}(L)$ were zero; it is nonzero here since $h$ is nondegenerate.
