This chapter counts Hermitian lattices over a CM field. Over a local field, the lattices that are compatible with two flags are counted by the isomorphisms between the cokernels of two Gram matrices, with a marked element and a linear form; the count depends only on the classes of the Gram matrices under $\mathrm{GL}_{r+1}$ of the local integers. Over a number field, unimodular lattices that contain a given sublattice are glued from local pieces along discriminant modules, and their weighted number is a mass constant times explicit local densities. The chapter also proves the local-to-global bijection for partners of a term, the covolumes of the integral unipotent group at infinity, the splitting of the Haar measure along the stabilizer of a frame, and the value of the normalizing constant of the theta lift.

The chapter uses Hermitian forms and matrices ([Hermitian forms](/learn/algebra/#hermitian-and-skew-hermitian-forms), [matrices](/learn/algebra/#matrices), [modules](/learn/algebra/#rings-ideals-and-modules)), the arithmetic of number fields ([ideals and the class group](/learn/number-fields/#ideals-and-the-class-group), [places and completions](/learn/number-fields/#places-and-completions), [adeles and ideles](/learn/number-fields/#adeles-and-ideles)), unitary groups and their adelic points ([unitary groups](/learn/groups/#unitary-groups), [compact open subgroups](/learn/groups/#adelic-points-and-compact-open-subgroups), [Hermitian lattices and the mass formula](/learn/groups/#hermitian-lattices-and-the-mass-formula)) and [Haar measure](/learn/analysis/#haar-measure). Its results are used in the step [HypsAlt is nonempty](/step/nonempty-hyps/), which turns the closed bound into explicit constants, and in the step [theta local matching](/step/theta-local-matching/).
