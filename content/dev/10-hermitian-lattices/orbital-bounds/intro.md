This chapter closes the local and global bounds that the orbital terms of the theta lifts need. Most of its statements are the conclusions of theorems from other chapters, and here their hypotheses are discharged. The first sections are on Lie theory and analysis. Phase functions on formal groups have integral jets. The Weyl dimension formula holds for $U(p, q)$, and the Gelfand and Tsetlin branching rule gives interlacing patterns. Transfer operators are Hermitian and invariant, and derivatives along slices cost a fixed power of the norm. The middle sections are on lattices. They give local densities by corank, square pairs, a standard lattice of CM type, volumes of flag subgroups and orbits, and indices, classes and point counts in adelic unitary groups. The last sections bound orbital terms and coefficients, the local index of a lattice of cusp forms modulo its theta part, and the intersection lattices of Theorem A, and they reduce the mass of the rows to the mass of one lattice by gluing.
