## Rank two definite unimodular classes

This section bounds the number of classes of positive definite unimodular Hermitian lattices of rank $2$ over a CM field $E$. The bound is polynomial in the root discriminant: it is $4^f (2v_E)^{5f}$ times a power of a logarithm. The proof splits each plane along a short vector into two lines, and counts the lines and the ways to glue them. The gauge volume of a definite unitary group of rank $2$ is at most a constant times this class number. With the volume ratio of the fourth section, the bound gives the finiteness of the volume of the adelic quotient used in the flag measure bound, and the absolute volume bound used in the counting theorem.

### Classes

Let $E$ be a CM field with maximal totally real subfield $F$, $f = [F : \mathbb{Q}]$, and $v_E$ as in the second section. Suppose that $E/F$ is unramified in the sense of the global setting, so that each embedding of $E$ carries the involution of $E$ to complex conjugation. Let $h_E$ be the [class number](/learn/number-fields/#ideals-and-the-class-group) of $E$.

**Definition 3.6.1 (Definite unimodular classes).** Let $r \ge 1$. A *definite unimodular pair of rank $r$* is a pair $(X, L)$ of a [[Hermitian form|8NJ7XY6F1S]] $X \in M_r(E)$ and an $\mathcal{O}_E$-lattice $L \subseteq E^r$ with these properties:

1. $\varphi(y^* X y)$ has positive real part for every embedding $\varphi \colon E \to \mathbb{C}$ and every $y \ne 0$;
2. $x \in L$ exactly when $y^* X x \in \mathcal{O}_E$ for all $y \in L$.

Two pairs $(X, L)$ and $(Y, L')$ are *equivalent* if there is $\gamma \in GL_r(E)$ with $\gamma^* Y \gamma = X$ and $\gamma y \in L'$ exactly when $y \in L$. Let $h^+(E, r)$ be the number of classes, a cardinal in $\{0, 1, 2, \dots, \infty\}$.

So $h^+(E, r)$ is the class number of the genus of positive definite unimodular Hermitian lattices of rank $r$, counted over all such genera at once (see [class numbers](/learn/hermitian-lattices/#class-numbers)).

### Glue data

Put $Y = (2 v_E)^f = \sqrt{|d_E|}$.

**Definition 3.6.2 (Real ideals).** Let $\mathcal{R}(Y)$ be the set of nonzero ideals $\mathfrak{n}$ of $\mathcal{O}_E$ that are generated by elements fixed by the involution, with norm $\mathrm{N}\mathfrak{n} \le Y^2$.

**Definition 3.6.3 (Glue data).** For $\mathfrak{n} \in \mathcal{R}(Y)$ consider rank one pairs $(\beta, N)$ with $\beta \in E$ and $N \subseteq E$ a fractional ideal, modulo the relation $(\beta, N) \sim (\beta', N')$ when $\beta' = \bar c \beta c$ and $c N = N'$ for some $c \ne 0$; we keep the classes that belong to $\mathfrak{n}$. A *glue datum* is a triple of an ideal $\mathfrak{n} \in \mathcal{R}(Y)$, two such line classes $(\beta_1, N_1)$ and $(\beta_2, N_2)$, and a lattice $L \subseteq E^2$ with these properties:

1. $L$ is unimodular for the diagonal form $\operatorname{diag}(\beta_1, \beta_2)$ in the sense of the definition above;
2. $t \in N_1$ exactly when $(t, 0) \in L$, and $t \in N_2$ exactly when $(0, t) \in L$.

Let $\mathcal{G}(Y)$ be the set of glue data.

### The injection into glue data

**Theorem 3.6.4 (Classes and glue data).** If $E$ is a CM field, unramified as above, then
$$h^+(E, 2) \le \# \mathcal{G}\big((2v_E)^f\big).$$

Lean: `GlobalTheory.RankTwoCount.defClassNum_le_card`.

*Proof.* We relate each class to a glue datum, and show that the relation is injective on classes. Let $(X, L)$ be a definite unimodular pair of rank $2$. The proof has three steps.

1. There is $u \in L$, $u \ne 0$, with $\varphi(u^* X u) \le 2 v_E$ for every embedding $\varphi$. This is a short vector bound for definite unimodular planes.
2. Split the plane along $u$: there are $\gamma \in GL_2(E)$ and $\beta_1, \beta_2 \in E$ with $\gamma^* X \gamma = \operatorname{diag}(\beta_1, \beta_2)$, and two lines $N_1$, $N_2$ such that $\gamma^{-1} L$ meets the two axes in $N_1$ and $N_2$. The norm ideals of the two lines are the same ideal $\mathfrak{n}$, and $\mathfrak{n}$ is nonzero and generated by elements fixed by the involution. The bound of step 1 gives $\mathrm{N}\mathfrak{n} \le (2v_E)^{2f} = Y^2$. So $\mathfrak{n} \in \mathcal{R}(Y)$.
3. Send the class of $(X, L)$ to the glue datum formed by $\mathfrak{n}$, the classes of $(\beta_1, N_1)$ and $(\beta_2, N_2)$, and the lattice $\gamma^{-1} L$ moved to the chosen representatives. The class of $(X, L)$ is equivalent to the pair $(\operatorname{diag}(\beta_1, \beta_2), \gamma^{-1} L)$, so two classes with the same glue datum are equal. $\square$

### The bound

**Theorem 3.6.5 (Rank two classes).** If $E$ is a CM field, unramified as above, then
$$h^+(E, 2) \le 4^f (2 v_E)^{5f} \big(1 + f \log(2 v_E)\big)^{5f}.$$

Lean: `GlobalTheory.RankTwoCount.rankTwoClasses` (also `GlobalTheory.T.rank_two_definite_unimodular_hermitian_class_bound`, a second name).

*Proof.* Put $\rho = 2 v_E$ and $Y = \rho^f$. The proof has five steps.

1. Since $E$ is totally complex of degree $2f$, $\rho \ge 3/2$ and $\rho^f = \sqrt{|d_E|}$. So $Y \ge 1$ and $\log \rho \ge 0$.
2. Count glue data by their parts. There are at most $h_E\, 2^f$ line classes for each ideal, and for fixed line classes there are at most $\mathrm{N}\mathfrak{n} \le \lfloor Y^2 \rfloor$ gluings. With the theorem on classes and glue data,
$$h^+(E,2) \le \#\mathcal{R}(Y) \cdot (h_E 2^f)^2 \lfloor Y^2 \rfloor.$$
3. The ideals of $\mathcal{R}(Y)$ come from ideals of $F$ of norm at most $Y$, and $\#\mathcal{R}(Y) \le Y (1 + \log Y)^f$.
4. Minkowski's bound gives $h_E \le \sqrt{|d_E|}\,(1 + \log \sqrt{|d_E|})^{2f} = Y (1 + \log Y)^{2f}$ (see [the class group](/learn/number-fields/#ideals-and-the-class-group)).
5. Multiply: the bound is at most $Y (1 + \log Y)^f \cdot 4^f Y^2 (1 + \log Y)^{4f} \cdot Y^2 = 4^f Y^5 (1 + \log Y)^{5f}$. Since $\log Y = f \log \rho$, this is the bound of the theorem. $\square$

The exponent $5f$ is not optimal. What matters later is that the bound is finite and polynomial in $\sqrt{|d_E|}$ up to logarithms.
