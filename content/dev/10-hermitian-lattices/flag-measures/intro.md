This chapter proves the flag measure bound for unitary groups over a CM field and the counting theorem that it feeds. For a unitary group $G$ of signature $(p,q)$ at every infinite place, the probability that a random point of the adelic quotient has a short isotropic flag of type $j$ with sizes in a set $B$ is at most an explicit constant $\kappa(E,p,q,j)$ times the integral over $B$ of a flag density. The proof goes from the local to the global. At each real place the Iwasawa decomposition $G = NMK$ along an isotropic flag gives an integration formula with an explicit constant. Globally the covolume of the integral points of the unipotent radical is bounded by heights of the pieces of the flag, and the integral over the Levi factor reduces to slab volumes of general linear groups. One flag class at a time, these bounds combine; summed over all classes and divided by the volume of the quotient, they give the flag measure bound. The volume ratio between ranks comes from the volume step, which follows from the Siegel-Weil formula in its convergent range. The flag measure bound then controls the near terms of the trace formula in the counting theorem: the dimension of a space of automorphic forms with discrete series type at the infinite places is its main term up to a factor $1 + O(e^{-nf\log n/16})$. The last sections prove the row estimates along families with large gaps and the off-diagonal bound for spectral ties.
