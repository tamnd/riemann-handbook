This chapter computes the local volumes that enter the comparison of orbit integrals for a unitary group $G$ of a Hermitian space over a CM field $E$ with totally real subfield $F$. At an archimedean place the group is $\mathrm{U}(p, q)$, and we compare its Haar measure, normalized by its density at the identity in exponential or Cayley coordinates, with the measure on an orbit of frames. The result is the orbit constant $a'$ and the Jacobian constant of the big cell, which together give the real bracket of the [theta local matching](/step/theta-local-matching/). At a finite place we compute the self-dual volume of the orbit of a frame under the integral points, as a power of the local different times a product of factors $1 - \eta_v^j N(v)^{-j}$. We then turn to the global side: the count of row lattices through the mass formula for unitary groups, a bound for Gelfand-Graev averages over finite fields, strong approximation for the unipotent radical, a lattice point count, and the closed statements that feed the [nonemptiness of the hypotheses](/step/nonempty-hyps/).
