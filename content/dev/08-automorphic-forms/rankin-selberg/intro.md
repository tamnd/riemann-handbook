This chapter proves strong multiplicity one for cuspidal representations of $\mathrm{GL}_n$, in the form of the rigidity theorem of Jacquet and Shalika: if two finite families of cuspidal representations have [Satake parameters](/learn/representations/#unramified-representations) with the same sum at almost all places, the families agree up to order. This is the uniqueness of [isobaric sums](/learn/automorphic-forms/#isobaric-sums). The tool is the partial Rankin-Selberg $L$-function $L^T(s, \pi \times \pi')$; see [Rankin-Selberg integrals](/learn/trace-formula/#rankin-selberg-integrals). Sections 1 to 5 build it: the pole at $s = 1$ and the cell integrals, the unfolding of a theta integral into an Euler product of [Schur polynomials](/learn/combinatorics/#schur-polynomials), matched [Whittaker vectors](/learn/automorphic-forms/#fourier-coefficients-and-whittaker-functions), the integral for $\mathrm{GL}_n \times \mathrm{GL}_m$, and the meromorphic continuation in the same degree. Section 6 proves that $L^T(s, \pi \times \pi')$ does not vanish at $s = 1$, and Section 7 deduces the rigidity theorem. The rigidity theorem is used in [Integral Satake parameters at all places](/entry/slope-all-of/).

The last two sections have no relation to Rankin-Selberg theory. Section 8 derives the [local Langlands correspondence](/learn/galois-representations/#the-local-langlands-correspondence) for $\mathrm{GL}_n$ over a [$p$-adic field](/learn/number-fields/#p-adic-numbers-and-local-fields) from thirteen inputs, among them its existence, and shows that it is compatible with isomorphisms of fields; it is used in [the theorem on ordinary cocycles](/entry/ord-van2/). Section 9 proves the local inputs of [Base change keeps Satake parameters at ramified places](/entry/ram-sph/): the base change of a principal series at a place that does not split is the principal series of the characters composed with the norm. It also records the Speh form of base change from unitary groups, from [`baseChangeNB`](/entry/base-change-nb/). These results are used in the step [EveryCharacter](/step/every-character/).
