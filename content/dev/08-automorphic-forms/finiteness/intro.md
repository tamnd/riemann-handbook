This chapter proves the finiteness theorem of Harish-Chandra for [automorphic forms on adelic groups](/learn/automorphic-forms/#automorphic-forms-on-adelic-groups): the space of automorphic forms with a fixed level, fixed $K$-types and a fixed eigencharacter of the center of the enveloping algebra is finite-dimensional. Sections 1 and 2 give the analytic inputs on real groups, from [representations of real groups](/learn/representations/#representations-of-real-groups): lowest $K$-types and globalisation for $U(p,q)$, derivative bounds for functions killed by a polynomial in the Casimir, and smoothing by convolution. Section 3 proves the theorem for [cusp forms](/learn/automorphic-forms/#constant-terms-and-cusp-forms) on $\mathrm{GL}_n$ over a totally complex field, Section 4 passes to all automorphic forms on $\mathrm{GL}_n$ by induction on constant terms, and Section 5 proves the theorem for unitary groups of a CM field.

The last three sections collect inputs of other kinds. Section 6 proves that cuspidal representations of $\mathrm{GL}_n$ over a $p$-adic field have [Whittaker functionals](/learn/automorphic-forms/#fourier-coefficients-and-whittaker-functions), the local converse theorem of Henniart, and the restriction of cuspidal representations over a finite field to the mirabolic subgroup. Section 7 proves that completed Hecke L-functions of a CM field with nontrivial infinity type are entire and bounded in vertical strips. Section 8 records the local conditions of Bloch and Kato, Siegel sets and rapid decay of cusp forms for unitary groups, elliptic regularity, and a sup-norm bound for holomorphic sections. These results are used in the step [EveryCharacter](/step/every-character/), in [EveryCharacter of a row](/step/every-character-of-row/), and in [EveryCharacter from the literature](/step/every-character-of-literature/).
