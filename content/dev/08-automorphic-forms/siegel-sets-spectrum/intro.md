This chapter collects the reduction theory and the spectral facts about the discrete spectrum that the comparison of trace formulas for unitary groups and $GL_N$ needs, over a CM field. The first four sections prove the Iwasawa decomposition at the finite places, the compactness of the minimal parabolic modulo its rational points, the existence of [Siegel domains](/learn/trace-formula/#arithmetic-subgroups-and-siegel-sets) for unitary groups, and the finiteness of the regular semisimple classes that meet the support of a compactly supported function. Sections 5 to 8 prove the equidistribution of lattice points, the rank one theta means, and the integrability of the archimedean Siegel-Weil integrals and of the logarithmic singularities in archimedean intersection numbers. Sections 9 and 10 compute twisted traces on representations of complex groups by [highest weights](/learn/representations/#highest-weights-and-branching) and build kernels with the regularity that the trace formula needs. Section 11 shows that square integrable forms of a fixed type keep half of their mass on one compact set and glues limits of such families, and Section 12 proves a functional equation for a degenerate [Eisenstein series](/learn/automorphic-forms/#eisenstein-series) by a gallery of walls. The last section proves that a [discrete automorphic representation](/learn/automorphic-forms/#the-discrete-spectrum) of $GL_N$ with regular integral infinitesimal character embeds into an induced representation of Speh type, with Speh representations as local components, after Mœglin and Waldspurger.

The chapter needs from Volume 1 [Haar measure](/learn/analysis/#haar-measure), [Hermitian forms](/learn/algebra/#hermitian-and-skew-hermitian-forms), [lattices in Hermitian spaces](/learn/hermitian-lattices/#lattices-in-quadratic-and-hermitian-spaces), the [Weil representation](/learn/theta/#the-weil-representation), [representations of real groups](/learn/representations/#representations-of-real-groups), [cuspidal representations and segments](/learn/representations/#cuspidal-representations-and-segments), and [constant terms](/learn/automorphic-forms/#constant-terms-and-cusp-forms). Most results are used in the step [EveryCharacter](/step/every-character/), among them the [residual spectrum theorem](/entry/asc-seed-holds2/), the [equidistribution](/entry/equidistribution/) and the [Iwasawa decomposition](/entry/unitary-adelic-iwasawa-compact/). The [integrable fibre sums](/entry/pts-abs-integrable/) and the [archimedean Siegel-Weil integrals](/entry/integrable-star-loc-inf/) are used in the step [EveryCharacter from the literature](/step/every-character-of-literature/).
