This chapter proves the Jacquet-Rallis fundamental lemma for unit functions in every rank, and deduces from it the unit lemma, an identity between counts of self-dual lattices in algebras with an involution. The fundamental lemma compares an orbital integral on the Lie algebra of $\mathrm{GL}_{n+1}$, twisted by the quadratic character, with an orbital integral on the Lie algebra of a unitary group; see [the relative trace formula and the Jacquet-Rallis fundamental lemma](/learn/trace-formula/#the-relative-trace-formula-and-the-jacquet-rallis-fundamental-lemma). The proof is an induction on the rank. Sections 1 to 4 set up the orbital integrals, for [Haar measures](/learn/analysis/#haar-measure) on the general linear and unitary groups, and the matching of test functions, build [the Weil representation](/learn/theta/#the-weil-representation) of $\mathrm{SL}_2$ for [Hermitian forms](/learn/algebra/#hermitian-and-skew-hermitian-forms) over étale algebras, prove the transfer in rank one, and show that matching commutes with the partial [Fourier transform](/learn/analysis/#the-fourier-transform). Section 5 runs the induction: the elements of $\mathrm{SL}_2$ that preserve the matching of unit functions form a monoid, and it is the whole group.

Sections 6 and 7 turn orbital integrals into counts of [lattices in Hermitian spaces](/learn/hermitian-lattices/#lattices-in-quadratic-and-hermitian-spaces) and prove the unit lemma, first for the Lie algebra and then for the unitary group. Section 8 records Zelevinsky's theorem that the Grothendieck ring of $\mathrm{GL}_n$ over a $p$-adic field is a polynomial ring on the segments, which has no relation to the rest of the chapter. All these results are used in the step [EveryCharacter](/step/every-character/).
