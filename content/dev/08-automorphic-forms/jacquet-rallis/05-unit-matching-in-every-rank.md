## Unit matching in every rank

This section proves the fundamental lemma in every rank, by induction on $r$. The classical statement is the following.

**Theorem 4.5.1 (Jacquet-Rallis fundamental lemma).** Let $E/F$ be an unramified quadratic extension of $p$-adic fields. Give $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathcal{O}_F)$ and the unitary group of the standard Hermitian lattice volume $1$. Then the unit function of $\mathfrak{gl}_{n+1}(\mathcal{O}_F)$ matches the pair whose component on the split Hermitian space is the unit function of its integral Hermitian matrices, and whose other component is $0$.

This is the conjecture of Jacquet and Rallis, proved by Yun for large residue characteristic and by Beuzart-Plessis in general; see the [relative trace formula](/learn/trace-formula/#the-relative-trace-formula-and-the-jacquet-rallis-fundamental-lemma) and the references at the end of the chapter. Here we prove it in the form of [the first section](/entry/unit-match-at-of-one-le-norm/), with no condition on the residue characteristic. The induction step uses two Weil representations of $\mathrm{SL}_2(F)$, one on each side. The set of $g \in \mathrm{SL}_2(F)$ that preserve the matching of the unit functions is a monoid. It contains $w$ by [Fourier matching](/entry/fourier-match-of-weil-field/), and it contains enough of the $n(t)$ by [the step for large moments](/entry/unit-match-at-of-one-le-norm/). So it is all of $\mathrm{SL}_2(F)$. The result is used, through [the unit lemma](/entry/unit-lemma-lie-and-group/), in the step [EveryCharacter](/step/every-character/), and its Lie algebra part also enters [the matching of principal congruence functions](/entry/match-principal-congruence-inert/).

Throughout, $(F, E)$ is an unramified quadratic pair. For $X = (S, v, d) \in \mathfrak{h}_r$ put $q(X) = \frac{1}{2}\mathrm{Tr}_{E/F}(\bar v^t v)$. If $X$ has invariants $a$, then $\bar v^t v = m_0$, so $q(X) = m_0$, the first moment of $a$.

**Lemma 4.5.2 (Two Weil representations).** Fix measures in rank $r$ and a standard $\psi$.

1. There is a representation $W_1$ of $\mathrm{SL}_2(F)$ on test functions on $\mathfrak{g}_r(F)$ with $W_1(n(t))f'(Y) = \psi(t\, m_0(Y)) f'(Y)$ and $W_1(w)f' = \mathcal{F}f'$ for test functions $f'$.
2. Assume the conclusions of the [Theorem on Weil representations for etale pairs](/entry/weil-et-of-weil-field/) and of the Theorem on the Weil constant of the standard form. Then there is a representation $W_2$ of $\mathrm{SL}_2(F)$ on test functions on $\mathfrak{h}_r$ with $W_2(n(t))f(X) = \psi(t\, q(X)) f(X)$ and $W_2(w)f = \mathcal{F}f$ for test functions $f$.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.T.exists_weilRepSL2_glBorderedBlock`, `GlobalTheory.T.exists_weilRepSL2_hermitianBlock`.

On the linear side, $(b, c) \mapsto cb$ is a split quadratic form on $F^r \times F^r$, and its Weil representation has constant $1$. On the Hermitian side, $v \mapsto \bar v^t v$ is the norm form of the standard Hermitian space, and the Weil constant is $1$ by the theorem cited.

**Lemma 4.5.3 (Operations on matching).** Fix measures in rank $r + 1$, let $a \in \mathrm{Inv}_{r+1}(F)$ with first moment $m_0$, and let $h : F \to \mathbb{C}$. Write $h f'$ for $Y \mapsto h(m_0(Y)) f'(Y)$ and $h f$ for $X \mapsto h(q(X)) f(X)$.

1. If $f', f$ match at $a$, then $h f', h f$ match at $a$.
2. If $h(m_0) = 0$, then $h f', h f$ match at $a$ for all $f', f$.
3. If $h(m_0) \neq 0$ and $h f', h f$ match at $a$, then $f', f$ match at $a$.
4. If two pairs of test functions match, their sum matches.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.JR.UnitStep.matchAt_mul`, `GlobalTheory.JR.UnitStep.matchAt_mul_zero`, `GlobalTheory.JR.UnitStep.matchAt_of_mul`, `GlobalTheory.T.jacquetRallis_matching_add`.

*Proof.* The first moment is constant on orbits, so $O(h f', \mathrm{sec}(a)) = h(m_0)\, O(f', \mathrm{sec}(a))$, and $O(h f, X) = h(m_0)\, O(f, X)$ when $X$ has invariants $a$. Items 1 to 3 follow. Item 4 holds because orbital integrals of test functions are integrals of integrable functions, so they are additive. $\square$

**Lemma 4.5.4 (Generation).** Let $T$ be a submonoid of $\mathrm{SL}_2(F)$ that contains $w$ and contains $n(t)$ for every $t \in F$ with $|ty| \le 1$ for all $|y| < 1$. Then $T = \mathrm{SL}_2(F)$.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.JR.UnitStep.submonoid_eq_top`, `GlobalTheory.T.closure_weyl_and_integral_unipotents_eq_univ`.

*Proof.* The subgroup generated by $w$ and these $n(t)$ is $\mathrm{SL}_2(F)$; we take this as given. The monoid $T$ contains the inverses of the generators: $w^{-1} = w^3$, and $n(t)^{-1} = n(-t)$ with $-t$ again allowed. So $T$ contains the subgroup they generate. $\square$

The condition on $t$ says $|t| \le q$, where $q$ is the size of the residue field.

**Theorem 4.5.5 (The induction step).** Assume the conclusions of the [Theorem on Weil representations for etale pairs](/entry/weil-et-of-weil-field/) and of the Theorem on the Weil constant of the standard form. Let $r \ge 0$, assume Fourier matching in rank $r + 1$ and the fundamental lemma in rank $r$. Then the fundamental lemma holds in rank $r + 1$.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.JR.UnitStep.unitMatch_succ` (also `GlobalTheory.T.jacquetRallisUnitMatch_succ`, a second name).

*Proof.* Fix an unramified quadratic pair and measures in rank $r + 1$. A standard character $\psi$ exists. Let $W_1$ and $W_2$ be the representations of the Lemma, in rank $r + 1$. Write $u' = 1_{\mathfrak{g}_{r+1}}$ and $u = 1_{\mathfrak{h}_{r+1}}$; these are test functions fixed by $\mathcal{F}$.

Step 1: $\mathrm{SL}_2(F)$ preserves matching. Let $T_0$ be the set of $g$ such that $W_1(g)f'$ and $W_2(g)f$ match for all matching pairs of test functions. It is a monoid, since $W_1$ and $W_2$ are representations that preserve test functions. It contains $w$ by Fourier matching in rank $r + 1$. It contains every $n(t)$ by item 1 of the Lemma on operations, with $h(z) = \psi(tz)$. By the Lemma on generation, $T_0 = \mathrm{SL}_2(F)$.

Step 2: the defect monoid. Let $T$ be the set of $g$ such that $W_1(g)u' - u'$ and $W_2(g)u - u$ match. It contains $1$, since $0$ matches $0$. For $g, h \in \mathrm{SL}_2(F)$,
$$W(gh)u - u = W(g)\big(W(h)u - u\big) + \big(W(g)u - u\big)$$
on both sides. If $h, g \in T$, the first term matches its partner by Step 1, and the second by assumption. By item 4 of the Lemma on operations, $gh \in T$. So $T$ is a monoid.

Step 3: $T$ contains $w$. Indeed $W_1(w)u' - u' = \mathcal{F}u' - u' = 0$, and the same holds on the Hermitian side.

Step 4: $T$ contains $n(t)$ for $|t| \le q$. Here $W_1(n(t))u' - u' = h u'$ and $W_2(n(t))u - u = h u$ with $h(z) = \psi(tz) - 1$. Let $\Delta(a) \neq 0$. If $|m_0| \ge 1$, then $u'$ and $u$ match at $a$ by [the step for large moments](/entry/unit-match-at-of-one-le-norm/), which uses the fundamental lemma in rank $r$, and item 1 of the Lemma on operations applies. If $|m_0| < 1$, then $|t m_0| \le 1$, so $\psi(t m_0) = 1$ and $h(m_0) = 0$; item 2 applies.

Step 5: conclusion. By the Lemma on generation, $T = \mathrm{SL}_2(F)$. Let $a$ have $\Delta(a) \neq 0$ and $m_0 \neq 0$. Since $\psi$ is standard, there is $x$ with $\psi(x) \neq 1$. Then $n(x/m_0) \in T$, so $h u'$ and $h u$ match at $a$ with $h(z) = \psi(xz/m_0) - 1$, and $h(m_0) = \psi(x) - 1 \neq 0$. By item 3, $u'$ and $u$ match at $a$. Since $r + 1 > 0$, part 2 of the Lemma on regular invariants, in the section on Fourier matching, shows that $u'$ and $u$ match. $\square$

**Lemma 4.5.6 (Rank zero).** The fundamental lemma holds in rank $0$.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.T.jacquetRallisUnitMatch_sizeOne` (also `GlobalTheory.JR.SizeOne.unitMatch_zero`, a second name).

*Proof.* In rank $0$ the groups are trivial and have volume $1$. An element of $\mathfrak{g}_0$ or $\mathfrak{h}_0$ is a scalar $d$, and $\omega = \eta(1) = 1$. Both orbital integrals equal $1$ if $|d| \le 1$ and $0$ otherwise. Every invariant comes from an element of $\mathfrak{h}_0$, so the second clause is empty. $\square$

**Theorem 4.5.7 (Fundamental lemma from the field case).** Assume the conclusion of the [Theorem on Weil representations for fields](/entry/weil-field-main/). Then the fundamental lemma holds in every rank $r$.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.JR.unitMatch_of_weilField` (also `GlobalTheory.T.jacquetRallisUnitMatch_of_weilRep_quadratic`, a second name).

*Proof.* Induction on $r$. Rank $0$ is the Lemma above. For the step, the hypothesis gives Weil representations for etale pairs by the [Theorem on etale pairs](/entry/weil-et-of-weil-field/), and Fourier matching in rank $r + 1$ by the [Theorem on Fourier matching](/entry/fourier-match-of-weil-field/). The Theorem on the Weil constant holds. Apply the induction step. $\square$

**Theorem 4.5.8 (Fundamental lemma).** The fundamental lemma holds in every rank: for every $r \ge 0$, every unramified quadratic pair $(F, E)$ and every choice of measures in rank $r$, the functions $1_{\mathfrak{g}_r}$ and $1_{\mathfrak{h}_r}$ match.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.JR.unitMatch_all` (also `GlobalTheory.T.jacquetRallisUnitMatch_all_ranks`, a second name).

*Proof.* This is the previous Theorem applied to the [Theorem on Weil representations for fields](/entry/weil-field-main/). $\square$

**Remark 4.5.9 (Formalization).** The Hermitian side uses $q(X) = \frac{1}{2}\mathrm{Tr}_{E/F}(\bar v^t v)$, which divides by $2$ in $F$ ([JacquetRallisUnitMatchSucc.lean](lean:Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/InertiaEigenvalues/JacquetRallisUnitMatchSucc.lean#L21)); this is harmless since $F$ has characteristic $0$. The rank $r$ of the statement is the size of the matrix block, so rank $r$ here is the classical case $n = r$, with $\mathfrak{gl}_{r+1}$. Only the standard Hermitian form appears on the unitary side; the other Hermitian space enters only through the second clause of matching. The lemmas on Weil representations of $\mathrm{SL}_2(F)$, on generation and on additivity are proved in files that this chapter does not cover.
