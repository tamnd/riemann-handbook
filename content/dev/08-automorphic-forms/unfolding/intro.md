This chapter proves that the local first unfolding of a twisted Eisenstein series at a split prime of degree one does not vanish on a whole double coset as soon as it does not vanish at one element. From this it builds the twisted character index of the column construction and proves that it is good. It also collects four results on hermitian lattices and periods that the same part of the proof uses: the commutation of Hecke operators at split primes, the uniqueness of the unimodular genus, the existence of reference frames modulo an integer, and a bound for the length of the module of poles of a lifted family.

The chapter uses [Eisenstein series](/learn/automorphic-forms/#eisenstein-series), [Hecke characters](/learn/class-field-theory/#hecke-characters) and [additive characters](/learn/class-field-theory/#additive-characters), [parabolic subgroups](/learn/groups/#parabolic-subgroups), [the splitting of primes](/learn/number-fields/#the-splitting-of-primes), [discrete valuation rings](/learn/commutative-algebra/#regular-local-rings-and-discrete-valuation-rings), and the [genus](/learn/hermitian-lattices/#genus-class-and-spinor-genus) and [local classification](/learn/hermitian-lattices/#local-classification) of hermitian lattices. Its results feed the descent of the column construction in Volumes 13 to 15 and the period estimates of Volume 10, and through them the step [nonempty hypotheses](/step/nonempty-hyps/).
