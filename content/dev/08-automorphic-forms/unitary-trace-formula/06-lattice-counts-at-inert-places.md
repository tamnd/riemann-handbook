## Lattice counts at inert places

This section computes the local orbital integrals of the simple twisted trace formula at a place that is inert in a CM field. On the twisted side the test functions are the unit function of $\mathrm{GL}_m(\mathcal{O}_w)$ and a level function attached to a principal congruence subgroup. On the unitary side they are the unit function of the maximal compact subgroup of $\mathrm{U}(n)$ and the indicator of its principal congruence subgroup. In every case the orbital integral is a sum of counts of self-dual lattices in an algebra with involution: the sum $S(B)$ on the twisted side and the signed sum $\kappa(B_1, B_2)$ on the unitary side. These are the sums of [the unit lemma](/entry/unit-lemma-lie-and-group/). The section is used in [the matching of principal congruence functions](/entry/match-principal-congruence-inert/), where the unit identity between the two sums becomes a matching of test functions. For the background on Hermitian lattices, see [lattices in Hermitian spaces](/learn/hermitian-lattices/#lattices-in-quadratic-and-hermitian-spaces) and [local classification](/learn/hermitian-lattices/#local-classification).

### The local setting

Let $E$ be a CM field with maximal totally real subfield $E^+$, and suppose that every finite place of $E$ is unramified over $E^+$. Let $w$ be a finite place of $E$ that is not split over $E^+$. Let $E_w$ be the completion, $\mathcal{O}_w$ its ring of integers, $\mathrm{ord}_w$ the normalized valuation, and $q_w$ the size of the residue field. Let $c$ be the continuous automorphism of $E_w$ that extends complex conjugation of $E$.

We use the language of [lattice counts](/entry/count-identity-of-unit-match/): an unramified quadratic datum $K = (\mathcal{O}, c_K, q)$ with fraction field $E_K$, its valuation $\mathrm{val}$ and the sign $\eta(x) = (-1)^{\mathrm{val}(x)}$; an algebra with involution $(B, \sigma, g)$ over $K$, with characteristic polynomial $P$ of $g$; the forms $h_x(y, z) = \mathrm{Tr}_{B/E_K}(x\, y\, \sigma(z))$; and the counts $N(x)$ of self-dual lattices stable under $g$, weighted by relative indices. From [the unit lemma](/entry/unit-lemma-lie-and-group/) we use the set $\mathrm{Cl}(B)$ of classes of $\sigma$-fixed units modulo norms $u\sigma(u)$, the sum $S(B) = \sum_{k \in \mathrm{Cl}(B)} N(y_k)$ and the signed sum $\kappa(B_1, B_2)$ on $\mathrm{Cl}(B_1 \times B_2)$.

**Definition 2.6.1 (Model of an inert place).** A *model* of $E_w$ is an unramified quadratic datum $K$ with a ring isomorphism $e : E_K \to E_w$ such that:

1. $e \circ c_K = c \circ e$;
2. $x \in \mathcal{O}$ if and only if $|e(x)|_w \le 1$;
3. $\mathrm{val}(x) = \mathrm{ord}_w(e(x))$ for all $x \in E_K$;
4. $q_w = q^2$.

For a polynomial $Q$ over $E_w$ we write $Q^e$ for its image under $e^{-1}$.

A model exists at every inert place: $E_w / E_w^{c}$ is an unramified quadratic extension. We use models only to state the results.

**Definition 2.6.2 (Twisted and unitary orbital integrals).** Let $m \ge 1$ and $G = \mathrm{GL}_m(E_w)$ with $K_G = \mathrm{GL}_m(\mathcal{O}_w)$.

1. The *twist* is $\theta(g) = \Phi_m\, {}^t c(g)^{-1}\, \Phi_m^{-1}$, for a fixed matrix $\Phi_m$ with $\Phi_m\, {}^t\Phi_m = 1$. The *norm* of $y \in G$ is $\mathcal{N}(y) = y\, \theta(y)$. Two elements $y, y'$ are *twisted conjugate* if $y' = x^{-1} y\, \theta(x)$ for some $x \in G$.
2. For $y \in G$ let $G^\theta_y$ be its twisted centralizer and $C_y \subseteq G^\theta_y$ the subgroup generated by the elements of $G^\theta_y$ that generate a relatively compact subgroup. For a function $\varphi$ on $G$, the *twisted orbital integral* is
$$TO_y(\varphi) = \sum_{x \in G^\theta_y \backslash G / K_G} [\,C_y : C_y \cap G^\theta_y \cap x K_G x^{-1}\,]\ \mathrm{av}_{k \in K_G}\, \varphi\big((xk)^{-1} y\, \theta(xk)\big),$$
where $\mathrm{av}$ is the average over $K_G$ modulo the stabilizer of the function.
3. For a monic $Q$ of degree $m$, the *stable twisted orbital integral* is $SO^\theta_Q(\varphi) = \sum_y TO_y(\varphi)$, the sum over the twisted conjugacy classes of $y$ with $\det(X - \mathcal{N}(y)) = Q$.
4. $\mathrm{U}(n) = \{g \in \mathrm{GL}_n(E_w) : {}^t c(g)\, g = 1\}$ and $K_U = \mathrm{U}(n) \cap \mathrm{GL}_n(\mathcal{O}_w)$. The orbital integral $O_\gamma(f)$ on $\mathrm{U}(n)$ is defined as in item 2 with $\theta$ trivial and $K_U$ in place of $K_G$.
5. For $\gamma \in \mathrm{U}(n)$ and a polynomial $Q_1$, let $v_1, \dots, v_d$ be a basis of $\ker Q_1(\gamma) \subseteq E_w^n$, and put $\epsilon_{Q_1}(\gamma) = (-1)^{\mathrm{ord}_w \det(\sum_k c(v_{i,k}) v_{j,k})_{i,j}}$.
6. For monic $Q_1, Q_2$ with $\deg Q_1 + \deg Q_2 = n$, the *$\kappa$-orbital integral* is $O^\kappa_{Q_1, Q_2}(f) = \sum_\gamma \epsilon_{Q_1}(\gamma)\, O_\gamma(f)$, the sum over the conjugacy classes of $\gamma \in \mathrm{U}(n)$ with characteristic polynomial $Q_1 Q_2$.

The weight in item 2 is the ratio of volumes for Haar measures that give $K_G$ and $C_y$ volume $1$. The sign in item 5 is the class of the discriminant of the Hermitian form on $\ker Q_1(\gamma)$; it is the transfer factor $\kappa$ at the inert place. A polynomial $Q$ over $E_w$ is *conjugate self-reciprocal* if $c(Q(0))\, Q$ is the reverse of the polynomial $Q^c$ obtained by applying $c$ to the coefficients. The characteristic polynomial of an element of $\mathrm{U}(n)$, and of the norm $\mathcal{N}(y)$, has this property.

For $k \ge 0$ let $K_m(k) \subseteq \mathrm{GL}_m(\mathcal{O}_w)$ be the principal congruence subgroup: the $g$ with $\mathrm{ord}_w(g_{ij} - \delta_{ij}) \ge k$ for all $i, j$.

### Unit functions

**Theorem 2.6.3 (Twisted orbital integrals of the unit function).** Let $(K, e)$ be a model of $E_w$. Let $Q$ be a monic separable conjugate self-reciprocal polynomial of degree $a$ over $E_w$. Then there is an algebra with involution $B$ over $K$ with $g\sigma(g) = 1$ and $P = Q^e$, such that
$$SO^\theta_Q(\mathbf{1}_{\mathrm{GL}_a(\mathcal{O}_w)}) = S(B).$$

Lean: `GlobalTheory.InertTwUnit.twisted_unit_eq_stSum` (also `GlobalTheory.T.inertPlace_stableTwistedOrbital_unit_eq_latticeSum`, a second name).

*Proof.* Since $e$ commutes with conjugation, $Q^e$ is conjugate self-reciprocal relative to $c_K$. So there is an algebra with involution $B$ with $P = Q^e$ and $g\sigma(g) = 1$: take $B = E_K[X]/(Q^e)$, $g$ the class of $X$, and $\sigma$ the semilinear involution with $\sigma(g) = g^{-1}$. As $Q$ is separable, so is $P$. There is a unit $\beta_0$ of $B$ with $\sigma(\beta_0) = (-1)^{a-1}\beta_0 g$ such that, for every $\sigma$-fixed unit $x$, a lattice is unimodular for the twisted form attached to $\beta_0 x$ exactly when it is self-dual for $h_x$. Since $w$ is not split, the twisted conjugacy classes of $y$ with $\det(X - \mathcal{N}(y)) = Q$ are in bijection with $\mathrm{Cl}(B)$, the units modulo norms. For a class $k$ with image $y$, the double cosets $G^\theta_y \backslash G / K_G$ are the orbits of the torus $\{t : t\sigma(t) = 1\}$ on the lattices $x\mathcal{O}_w^a$, the condition $x^{-1} y\,\theta(x) \in K_G$ says that the lattice is self-dual and stable under $g$, and the weights agree. So $TO_y(\mathbf{1}_{K_G}) = N(y_k)$. Summing over $\mathrm{Cl}(B)$ gives $S(B)$. $\square$

**Theorem 2.6.4 (κ-orbital integrals of the unit function).** Let $(K, e)$ be a model of $E_w$. Let $Q_1, Q_2$ be monic and conjugate self-reciprocal over $E_w$, with $\deg Q_1 + \deg Q_2 = n$ and $Q_1 Q_2$ separable. Let $B_1, B_2$ be algebras with involution over $K$ with $g_i \sigma(g_i) = 1$ and $P_i = Q_i^e$. Then
$$O^\kappa_{Q_1, Q_2}(\mathbf{1}_{K_U}) = \kappa(B_1, B_2).$$

Lean: `GlobalTheory.InertUnitOrb.kapOrb_unit_eq_kapSum` (also `GlobalTheory.T.inertPlace_kappaOrbital_unit_eq_signedLatticeSum`, a second name).

*Proof.* Let $B = B_1 \times B_2$. Then $P = Q_1^e Q_2^e$ is separable, $g\sigma(g) = 1$, and $\dim B = n$. Transport $\mathrm{U}(n)$ to matrices over $E_K$ by $e^{-1}$. A *Hermitian model* of $\gamma \in \mathrm{U}(n)$ is a $\sigma$-fixed unit $x \in B$ with an $E_K$-linear isomorphism $\psi : B \to E_K^n$ such that $\psi(g y) = \gamma\, \psi(y)$ and the standard Hermitian form pulls back to $h_x$. We use four facts.

1. Every $\gamma$ with characteristic polynomial $Q_1 Q_2$ has a Hermitian model.
2. If $x$ and $x'$ are models of $\gamma$ and $\gamma'$, then $\gamma$ and $\gamma'$ are conjugate in $\mathrm{U}(n)$ if and only if $x$ and $x'$ have the same class in $\mathrm{Cl}(B)$.
3. If $N(x) \ne 0$, then $x$ is a model of some $\gamma \in \mathrm{U}(n)$: a self-dual lattice exists, so $(B, h_x)$ is the standard Hermitian space.
4. If $x$ is a model of $\gamma$, then $O_\gamma(\mathbf{1}_{K_U}) = N(x)$ and $\epsilon_{Q_1}(\gamma) = \eta(\delta_1(x_1))$.

The value $\eta(\delta_1(x_1)) N(x)$ depends only on the class of $x$, since a norm $u\sigma(u)$ changes neither factor. By facts 1 and 2, $\gamma \mapsto$ (class of a model) is an injective map from the conjugacy classes in the sum to $\mathrm{Cl}(B)$. By fact 3 its image contains every class with $N \ne 0$. By fact 4 the terms agree. So the two sums are equal. $\square$

### Level functions

Let $t \in \mathcal{O}$ be small, that is, $t \neq 0$, $t$ in the maximal ideal, and $c_K(t) = t$, and let $k = \mathrm{val}(t)$. For an algebra with involution $B$ put $u = 1 + t g$.

**Theorem 2.6.5 (Twisted orbital integrals of a level function).** Let $(K, e)$ be a model of $E_w$, and $t$, $k$ as above. Let $Q$ be monic, separable and conjugate self-reciprocal of degree $m$ over $E_w$. Let $B$ be an algebra with involution such that $u = 1 + tg$ generates $B$ over $E_K$, $Q^e(u) = 0$, $\dim B = m$ and $u\sigma(u) = 1$. Put
$$\varphi_k(y) = \mathbf{1}\big[\,y \in \mathrm{GL}_m(\mathcal{O}_w) \text{ and } \mathcal{N}(y) \in K_m(k)\,\big].$$
Then $SO^\theta_Q(\varphi_k) = S(B)$.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.TwOrbLevel.twOrb_level_eq_stSum` (also `GlobalTheory.T.twistedOrbitalIntegral_levelFunction_inert_eq_latticeCount`, a second name).

*Proof.* The function $\varphi_k$ is invariant under twisted conjugation by $K_G$, since $\theta(K_G) = K_G$ and $\mathcal{N}(k^{-1} y\, \theta(k)) = k^{-1} \mathcal{N}(y) k$ with $K_m(k)$ normal in $K_G$. So each twisted orbital integral is a sum of values of $\varphi_k$ over double cosets.

Suppose first that $u$ is integral over $\mathcal{O}$. Apply the construction of the unit case to $(B, \sigma, u)$: the characteristic polynomial of $u$ is $Q^e$, and there is a base point $\beta_0$ with $\sigma(\beta_0) = (-1)^{m-1}\beta_0 u$. The twisted classes of $y$ with $\det(X - \mathcal{N}(y)) = Q$ are parametrized by $\mathrm{Cl}(B)$ as before. For a lattice $L$ in the corresponding Hermitian space, $\mathcal{N}(y) \in K_m(k)$ for the matrix $y$ of $L$ means that $(u - 1)/t = g$ preserves $L$. So the lattices counted by $\varphi_k$ are the self-dual lattices stable under $g$, which are the lattices counted by $N$, and the sum is $S(B)$.

Suppose next that $u$ is not integral. A lattice stable under $g$ is stable under $u = 1 + tg$, so no lattice is stable under $g$, every $N(x)$ is $0$, and $S(B) = 0$. On the other side, if $\varphi_k$ were nonzero at a twisted conjugate of $y$, then $\mathcal{N}$ of that conjugate would be an integral matrix with characteristic polynomial $Q$, and $u$, a root of $Q^e$, would be integral. So every term is $0$. $\square$

**Theorem 2.6.6 (κ-orbital integrals of congruence indicators).** Let $(K, e)$ be a model of $E_w$, and $t$, $k$ as above. Let $Q_1, Q_2$ be polynomials over $E_w$ with $Q_1 Q_2$ monic, separable and of degree $n$. Let $B_1, B_2$ be algebras with involution and $u_i \in B_i$ such that $u_i$ generates $B_i$, $Q_i^e(u_i) = 0$, $\dim B_i = \deg Q_i$ and $u_i\sigma(u_i) = 1$. Suppose that $1 + t g = (u_1, u_2)$ in $B_1 \times B_2$. Then
$$O^\kappa_{Q_1, Q_2}\big(\mathbf{1}_{\mathrm{U}(n) \cap K_n(k)}\big) = \kappa(B_1, B_2).$$

Lean: `GlobalTheory.KapLat.kapOrb_eq_kapSum_of_fits`

*Proof.* Let $B = B_1 \times B_2$ and $u = (u_1, u_2)$. Since $Q_1 Q_2$ is separable, $u$ generates $B$. The argument of the unit case applies with $u$ in place of $g$: every $\gamma$ with characteristic polynomial $Q_1 Q_2$ has a Hermitian model $x$ for $u$, and conjugacy of $\gamma$ is equivalence of $x$ modulo norms. If $x$ is a model of $\gamma$, then so is $x u'\sigma(u')$ for every unit $u'$ of $B$. For $y \in \mathrm{U}(n)$, the condition $y^{-1}\gamma y \in K_n(k)$ says that $\gamma$ preserves the lattice $y\mathcal{O}_w^n$ and acts as $1$ on it modulo $t$. Through the model, this means that $(u - 1)/t = g$ preserves the corresponding lattice in $B$. So the orbital integral of $\gamma$ counts the self-dual lattices of $h_x$ stable under $g$, which is $N(x)$, and the signs are $\eta(\delta_1(x_1))$ as before. $\square$

**Corollary 2.6.7 (Congruence indicators with given degrees).** Let $(K, e)$ be a model of $E_w$, and $t$, $k$ as above. Let $n = a + b$, and let $Q_1, Q_2$ be monic and conjugate self-reciprocal of degrees $a$, $b$ over $E_w$, with $Q_1 Q_2$ separable. Let $B_1, B_2$ be algebras with involution such that each $u_i = 1 + t g_i$ generates $B_i$, $Q_i^e(u_i) = 0$, $\dim B_i = \deg Q_i$, and $u_i\sigma(u_i) = 1$. Then
$$O^\kappa_{Q_1, Q_2}\big(\mathbf{1}_{\mathrm{U}(n) \cap K_n(k)}\big) = \kappa(B_1, B_2).$$

Lean: `GlobalTheory.KapLat.kapOrb_congruence_eq_kapSum` (also `GlobalTheory.T.kappaOrbitalIntegral_congruence_inert_latticeCount`, a second name).

*Proof.* The product $Q_1 Q_2$ is monic of degree $a + b = n$, and $1 + t(g_1, g_2) = (u_1, u_2)$. Apply the Theorem. $\square$

**Remark 2.6.8 (Formalization).** The stable twisted orbital integral and the $\kappa$-orbital integral are sums over classes, and the twisted orbital integral is a sum over double cosets, all as `finsum`, which is $0$ when the support is infinite ([SimpleTwistedTraceFormulaStable.lean](lean:Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/Divisors/SimpleTwistedTraceFormulaStable.lean#L62)). The conjugation $c$ is chosen by `Classical.choose` among the continuous automorphisms that extend complex conjugation, with the identity as a fallback ([SimpleTwistedTraceFormulaStable.lean](lean:Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/Divisors/SimpleTwistedTraceFormulaStable.lean#L91)). The four conditions of a model appear as four separate hypotheses in the statements, and as one predicate in the general congruence theorem ([ConjRelEquiv.lean](lean:Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/DivisorProofs/ConjRelEquiv.lean#L17)); the conditions on $u_i$ are a second predicate in the same file. Several hypotheses are not used in the proofs: the conjugate self-reciprocal condition in the level theorem ([TwistedOrbitalIntegralLevelFunctionInertEqLattic.lean](lean:Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/DivisorProofs/TwistedOrbitalIntegralLevelFunctionInertEqLattic.lean#L33)); both conjugate self-reciprocal conditions in the unitary unit theorem ([InertPlaceKappaOrbitalUnitEqSignedLatticeSum.lean](lean:Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/DivisorProofs/InertPlaceKappaOrbitalUnitEqSignedLatticeSum.lean#L68)); and the unramified condition on $E$ and the two conjugate self-reciprocal conditions in the Corollary, whose proof is one application of the general theorem ([KappaOrbitalIntegralCongruenceInertLatticeCount.lean](lean:Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/DivisorProofs/KappaOrbitalIntegralCongruenceInertLatticeCount.lean#L120)). The proofs call named results for the facts on Hermitian models, the base point $\beta_0$ and the parametrization of twisted classes; we did not unfold them. The theorem on the unit function of the twisted side is in [InertPlaceStableTwistedOrbitalUnitEqLatticeSum.lean](lean:Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/DivisorProofs/InertPlaceStableTwistedOrbitalUnitEqLatticeSum.lean#L28), and the second names are restatements by `first | exact …`.
