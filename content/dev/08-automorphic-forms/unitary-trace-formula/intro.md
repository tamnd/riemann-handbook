This chapter proves the parts of the trace formula for a unitary group $U(n)$ that the comparison with the twisted trace formula of $\mathrm{GL}_n$ needs. Sections 1 to 5 treat the spectral side. They build [pseudo-coefficients](/learn/trace-formula/#arthurs-trace-formula-the-simple-trace-formula-and-pseudo-coefficients) of [discrete series](/learn/representations/#discrete-series) from data on the maximal compact subgroup, with [highest weights](/learn/representations/#highest-weights-and-branching) and a Koszul family, and they isolate the elliptic part of the trace formula for a [compact quotient](/learn/trace-formula/#the-trace-formula-for-a-compact-quotient). Then they separate automorphic multiplicities with traces of Iwahori Hecke operators, using [unramified representations](/learn/representations/#unramified-representations). Sections 6 and 7 compute the twisted and the $\kappa$-orbital integrals of unit and congruence functions at the inert places as counts of [lattices in Hermitian spaces](/learn/hermitian-lattices/#lattices-in-quadratic-and-hermitian-spaces), and prove that principal congruence functions match.

Sections 8 to 11 treat the geometric side of a definite unitary group. Section 8 writes a Hecke trace on algebraic modular forms as a finite sum over rational classes, with the masses of [the mass formula for unitary groups](/learn/hermitian-lattices/#the-mass-formula-for-unitary-groups). Section 9 descends orbital integrals to Levi subgroups by [constant terms](/learn/representations/#parabolic-induction-and-the-jacquet-module) and proves the matching at split places, as in [the twisted trace formula and base change](/learn/trace-formula/#the-twisted-trace-formula-and-base-change). Section 10 proves the local-global principle for [Hermitian forms](/learn/algebra/#hermitian-and-skew-hermitian-forms) invariant under an element, and the sign product for conjugacy classes. Section 11 stabilizes the rational geometric side. Section 12 proves Zelevinsky's theorem that the Grothendieck ring of $\mathrm{GL}_n$ over a $p$-adic field is a polynomial ring on the [segments](/learn/representations/#cuspidal-representations-and-segments). All these results are used in the step [every character](/step/every-character/).
