This chapter proves the archimedean and local inputs of base change from unitary groups to $\mathrm{GL}_n$ over a CM field $E$ with maximal real subfield $F$, and it sets up the [twisted trace formula](/learn/trace-formula/#the-twisted-trace-formula-and-base-change) that compares the two sides. At the archimedean places it computes twisted traces on $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{C})$-modules by highest weight vectors, and it builds test functions of compact support from the wave equation of the Casimir operator. At an inert finite place it finds Iwahori eigenvectors in unramified representations of unitary groups, and it proves the fundamental lemma for the unit elements and an Iwahori matching. It then matches test functions at all places of a frame. On the global side it truncates the twisted trace formula, shows that the non-elliptic terms vanish for very cuspidal test functions, and writes the elliptic terms as products of local twisted orbital integrals and archimedean character values. On the spectral side it separates finitely generated spaces of automorphic forms into spaces induced from the discrete spectrum, and it takes limits of such spaces whose infinitesimal characters converge.

The chapter uses, from Volume 1, the theory of [unramified representations](/learn/representations/#unramified-representations) and of [real reductive groups](/learn/real-groups/#real-reductive-groups), together with [highest weights and branching](/learn/representations/#highest-weights-and-branching), [parabolic induction](/learn/representations/#parabolic-induction-and-the-jacquet-module) and the [discrete spectrum](/learn/automorphic-forms/#the-discrete-spectrum). Its results feed the step [base change for $\mathrm{GL}_n$](/step/base-change-n/) and, through the limit statement for infinitesimal characters, the step [every character](/step/every-character/) of [the Galois row](/proof/galois-row/).
