This theorem proves the statement `Sep4`: a nonzero space of automorphic forms on $\mathrm{GL}_N$ over a CM field, with nonpositive exponents and regular infinitesimal character, maps nontrivially into a representation induced in stages from unitary twists of discrete automorphic representations. Its two callers feed it to [the limit statement EFinal](/entry/e-final-of/). The first step above it is [EveryCharacter from the Galois row](/step/every-character/).

## Statement

Let $E$ be a CM field and $\mathcal{S}_M$ the smooth functions on $\mathrm{GL}_M(\mathbb{A}_E)$, acted on by $\mathrm{GL}_M(\mathbb{A}_f)$, by the compact group $K_{\infty,M}$ at infinity and by the Lie algebra. *Stable* means preserved by all three. $\varphi$ has *type* $(K, W)$ if it is right $K$-invariant and $k \mapsto \varphi(gk)$ lies in $W$ for each $g$.

For $0 < r < N$, an *exponent* of $\varphi$ is a $c$ such that $e^{ct}$ occurs under $a_t = \mathrm{diag}(e^t I_r, I_{N-r})$ in the constant term along the parabolic of type $(r, N-r)$. Put $p(c) = \operatorname{Re} c / [E:\mathbb{Q}] - r(N-r)/2$.

A *tree* has leaves $(V, z)$ with $V \subseteq \mathcal{S}_M$, $z \in \mathbb{C}$. A leaf has space $V \otimes |\det|^z$. A node of sizes $r, s$ has as space the span of the $K_\infty$-finite functions, invariant under the unipotent radical, whose restrictions to the Levi lie in $\mathrm{space}(t_1)|\det|^{s/2} \otimes \mathrm{space}(t_2)|\det|^{-r/2}$. The tree is *good* if each leaf with $M > 0$ is irreducible, automorphic and square-integrable modulo the split centre (`IsDiscrete`), with $\operatorname{Re} z = 0$.

**Theorem.** Fix $E$, $N$ and a finite-dimensional $W$. There are finite sets $\Omega_M$ of such spaces $W'$ so that for each open $K$ there are finite sets $\Theta_M$ of open subgroups with this property. Let $U \subseteq \mathcal{S}_N$, $N > 0$, $U \neq 0$, be stable, automorphic and generated by finitely many vectors of type $(K, W)$. Suppose that $p(c) \le 0$ for all exponents in $U$, that the split centre acts by $e^{zt}$ with $\operatorname{Re} z = 0$, and that the infinitesimal character at each complex place has distinct entries. Then there are a good tree $t$ and a map $T$, intertwining on $U$, with $T(U) \subseteq \mathrm{space}(t)$ and $T|_U \neq 0$. Each leaf has $M > 0$ and a nonzero vector of type $(K', W')$ with $K' \in \Theta_M$, $W' \in \Omega_M$.

## Proof

The proof is `separation_of_six_inputs` applied to six statements:

- `SliceU` (`sliceU_of_noLog2 noLog_holds`): for each exponent $c$ of $\varphi$ there is a slice map $T$ with $T\varphi \neq 0$, whose image is invariant under the unipotent radical and restricts to automorphic forms on the two Levi blocks.
- `Blocks` (`blocks_holds`): if $p(c) = 0$, the image of a slice map is a finite sum of pieces induced from $W_1|\det|^{s/2} \otimes W_2|\det|^{-r/2}$, with $W_1, W_2$ under the same hypotheses.
- `Leaf` (`leaf_holds2`): if all exponents of $\varphi$ have $p(c) < 0$, the conclusion holds.
- `Node` (`node_holds`): the conclusion for $W_1$ and $W_2$ gives it for $U$.
- `TwConc` (`twConc_holds`): the conclusion survives a twist by $|\det|^z$, $\operatorname{Re} z = 0$.
- `Types` (`types_holds`): gives $\Omega$ and $\Theta$.

The lemma `core` is a strong induction on $N$. If all exponents have $p(c) < 0$, use `Leaf`. Otherwise one has $p(c) = 0$. Twist $U$ so that the split centre acts trivially, apply `SliceU` and `Blocks`, pick a piece where $\varphi$ survives, use induction on $W_1$ and $W_2$, then `Node`, and twist back with `TwConc`. Then `typeB_map` shows that $T$ keeps the type $(K, W)$, and `Types` bounds the leaves.
