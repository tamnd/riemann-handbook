This theorem proves `MultFor`, the one hypothesis of [multiplicity one for highest weights](/entry/mult-g-of-mult-for/). That result is step 2 of [the twisted trace on a K-type](/entry/core-g/). The Lean proof is one line, but it opens a proof by homology of the nilradical of a parabolic subalgebra.

## Statement

We use the notation of [multiplicity one for highest weights](/entry/mult-g-of-mult-for/): $\lambda \in \mathbb{Z}^n$ non-increasing, a level $\ell$, the weight $\mu_\ell$, the conditions `AdmFor` on $E^1, E^2$ acting on an inner product space $V$, and $V_\mu$.

**Theorem** (`MultFor`). Let $V$ satisfy `AdmFor` for $\lambda$ and $\ell$. Let $v, w \in V_{\mu_\ell}$ with $v \neq 0$ and $w = xv$ for some $x$ in the algebra generated by the $E^i_{ab}$. Then $w \in \mathbb{C}v$.

## Proof

The proof is `multFor_of_capCls` applied to `capelli_classification_of_action`, which is applied to the last part (`ActUU`) of [the injectivity, decomposition and action package](/entry/injective-decomposition-action-holds/). `multFor_of_capCls` passes `CapCls` and six proved statements to `multFor_of`, which calls `multiplicity_assembly_holds`. That proof runs as follows.

Put $K_{ab} = E^1_{ab} + E^2_{ab}$ and $P_{ab} = E^1_{ab} - E^2_{ab}$ for $a < b$. These are the *letters*. A letter is in $\mathfrak{u}_\ell$ if $\ell(a) < \ell(b)$. Let $C(V)$ be the space of $V$-valued functions on finite sets of letters, with a boundary $\partial$ of Chevalley-Eilenberg shape (`bdL`), and let $\omega_U$ be the set of $K$-letters in $\mathfrak{u}_\ell$. Let $B$ be the image under $\partial$ of the chains of degree $|\omega_U| + 1$ on letters in $\mathfrak{u}_\ell$ (`bdSub`).

1. `InvPick`: $w = av$ for a word $a$ in the $E^i_{ab}$ that commutes with all $K_{cd}$.
2. `HCdecLv`: $a$ is congruent, modulo $\mathfrak{u}_\ell$, to a word $b$ in the Levi letters.
3. `ActUU`: $(av) \otimes \omega_U - b \cdot (v \otimes \omega_U) \in B$. It needs that the $K$-letters above the diagonal kill $v$, which holds since $v$ is a highest weight vector.
4. On $C(V)/B$ two Levi families act (`LevStab`, `LevRel`). On the class $c$ of $v \otimes \omega_U$, the sum of the two families acts diagonally by known scalars (`SemiCls`), and the Capelli products act on $c$ by an explicit polynomial (`CapCls`). Then each Capelli element acts on $c$ by a scalar (`CapelliProductsScalar`). `LeviCommutantWordReduction` writes $b$ as a word in Capelli elements, on such vectors, so $b c = z c$ for a scalar $z$.
5. Thus $(w - zv) \otimes \omega_U \in B$. `InjUU` gives $w = zv$.
