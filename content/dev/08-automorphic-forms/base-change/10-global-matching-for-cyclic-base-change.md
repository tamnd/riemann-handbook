## Global matching for cyclic base change

This section passes from local to global matching for a cyclic extension $E/F$ of prime degree. First we show that product test functions that match at every place also match globally: their global twisted and ordinary orbital integrals agree at every rational element with irreducible characteristic polynomial, up to one constant $\kappa$ that depends only on the measures. Then we combine this with the [spherical matching](/entry/loc-all/) of the previous section and build, for each polynomial in the Hecke operators, matched global test functions with prescribed spectral action and controlled support. The result serves the step [every character](/step/every-character/) of [the Galois row](/proof/galois-row/). We keep the notation of the previous section: $E/F$ is cyclic of prime degree $l$ with generator $\sigma$, and $n > 0$.

Let $G^1_E$ be the subgroup of $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{A}_E)$ of elements with $|\det|_{\mathbb{A}_E} = 1$, and define $G^1_F$ in the same way. For a function $\Phi$ on $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{A}_E)$ let $\mathrm{avg}\,\Phi(g) = \int_{\mathbb{R}}\Phi(z(e^t)g)\,dt$ on $G^1_E$, where $t \mapsto z(e^t)$ is a fixed section of the positive real scalars. For local functions $\varphi_\infty$ on $\mathrm{GL}_n(E_\infty)$ and $\varphi_v$ on $\mathrm{GL}_n(E_v)$, equal to the unit for almost all $v$, let $\varphi_\infty \otimes \bigotimes_v\varphi_v$ be their product function on $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{A}_E)$.

**Definition 7.10.1 (Global matching).** Fix Haar measures $\mu_E$ on $G^1_E$ and $\mu_F$ on $G^1_F$, and let $c \in \mathbb{C}$. Functions $f_E$ on $G^1_E$ and $f_F$ on $G^1_F$ match globally with constant $c$ if the following holds for every $\gamma \in \mathrm{GL}_n(F)$ with irreducible characteristic polynomial and every Haar measure $\nu$ on the centralizer $Z_\gamma$ of $\gamma$ in $G^1_F$:

1. for every $\delta \in \mathrm{GL}_n(E)$ with $N\delta = \gamma$,
$$\int_{Z_\gamma\backslash G^1_E}f_E\big(x^{-1}\delta\sigma(x)\big)\,dx = c\int_{Z_\gamma\backslash G^1_F}f_F\big(x^{-1}\gamma x\big)\,dx,$$
where $Z_\gamma$ acts on $G^1_E$ through the inclusion $\mathbb{A}_F \to \mathbb{A}_E$ and both quotients carry the quotient measures of $\mu_E$, $\mu_F$ by $\nu$;
2. if $\gamma$ is not a norm from $\mathrm{GL}_n(E)$, the right side of 1 is 0.

An element with irreducible characteristic polynomial is elliptic, and its centralizer is the multiplicative group of the field $F[\gamma]$. Global matching is what the twisted trace formula needs on the elliptic part of the geometric side.

**Proposition 7.10.2 (Global comparison, conditional form).** Assume the following nine statements, which the companion development proves: three facts on integrals over quotients of locally compact groups; the descent of twisted centralizers (for $\delta$ with $N\delta = \gamma$ regular semisimple, the $x \in \mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{A}_E)$ with $x^{-1}\delta\sigma(x) = \delta$ are exactly the images of the elements of $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{A}_F)$ that commute with $\gamma$); the closed embedding $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{A}_F) \to \mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{A}_E)$, with $|\det|_{\mathbb{A}_E} = |\det|_{\mathbb{A}_F}^{l}$ on it and a Galois invariant modulus; two statements on the compactness of orbits modulo the centralizer; the twisted orbits of the units at almost all places; and the Hasse principle for twisted norms in prime degree. Then the following holds. Let Haar measures be fixed on $G^1_E$, $G^1_F$, $\mathrm{GL}_n(E_\infty)$, $\mathrm{GL}_n(F_\infty)$, and at every finite $v$ on $\mathrm{GL}_n(E_v)$ and $\mathrm{GL}_n(F_v)$ with volume 1 on $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathcal{O}_{E,v})$ and $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathcal{O}_v)$. There is $\kappa > 0$ such that: for every finite set $S$ of finite places of $F$, continuous compactly supported $\varphi_\infty$, $f_\infty$, $\varphi_v$, $f_v$, and constants $c_\infty$ and $c_v$, such that $\varphi_v$ and $f_v$ are the units and $c_v = 1$ for $v \notin S$, $\varphi_\infty$ matches $f_\infty$ with constant $c_\infty$, and $\varphi_v$ matches $f_v$ with constant $c_v$ for every $v$, the functions $\mathrm{avg}(\varphi_\infty \otimes \bigotimes_v\varphi_v)$ and $\mathrm{avg}(f_\infty \otimes \bigotimes_vf_v)$ match globally with constant $\kappa\,c_\infty\prod_{v \in S}c_v$.

Lean: `UpperTheory.ConvolutionTheory.G.stable_twisted_trace_formula_comparison`.

*Proof.* Fix $\gamma$ with irreducible characteristic polynomial. Its centralizer $T$ in $\mathrm{GL}_n$ is the torus $F[\gamma]^\times$, which is commutative, and $T(\mathbb{A}_F)$ is the restricted product of the $T(F_v)$. By the descent of twisted centralizers, the twisted centralizer of $\delta$ with $N\delta = \gamma$ in $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{A}_E)$ is the image of $T(\mathbb{A}_F)$. For a product function, Fubini's theorem and the structure of the restricted product write each side of the global identity as a product of local orbital integrals. The product converges absolutely: at almost every place the twisted orbit of $\delta$ meets the units, so the local factor of the units is 1, and the bound on orbits modulo the centralizer controls the finitely many other factors. At each $v$, local matching replaces the twisted factor by $c_v$ times the ordinary one; at the archimedean places it gives $c_\infty$. The constant $\kappa$ is the ratio between the global Haar measures and the product of the local ones, together with the central averaging; it does not depend on the test functions. If $\gamma$ is not a global norm, the Hasse principle shows that it is not a local norm at some place $v$, and condition 2 of local matching at $v$ makes the product 0. $\square$

**Theorem 7.10.3 (Global comparison).** The conclusion of the Proposition holds.

*Proof.* This is the Proposition applied to the nine statements, each proved in the companion development. $\square$

Now we build matched global test functions. Fix Haar measures, a twist of $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{A}_E)$ by $\sigma$, a split place $q$ of $F$ with a place $w_0$ of $E$ over it, and archimedean test functions $a_E$ and $a_F$ that are normalized and match with a constant $c_\infty > 0$. A *local datum* is a finite set $S$ of finite places of $F$ not containing $q$, with test functions $\varphi_v$ and $f_v$ at $v \in S$ that match with constants $c_v$. An exponent $\nu \in \mathbb{Z}^n$ is *good* if its entries are distinct, they sum to 0, and no proper nonempty subset of them sums to 0. For a local datum and a good $\nu$, the companion development builds two *sides*: a test function over $E$ and one over $F$ made of $a_E$, $a_F$, the functions of $S$, the units elsewhere, and the Iwahori-Hecke operator $U_q$ of $\nu$ at $q$, together with the operators $A$, $g$ through which these test functions act.

**Theorem 7.10.4 (Matched functions for one Hecke monomial).** Assume that the units match for every cyclic extension of totally complex number fields of prime degree. Then for every such $E/F$ and data as above there is $\kappa_0 > 0$ with the following property. For each local datum $L$, each good $\nu$, and the sides built from them, there is $Y_0 \ge 1$ such that, for every finite set $\kappa$ of places of $E$ outside the set of places attached to the side over $E$, multisets $t_0(w)$ of $n$ nonzero numbers for $w \in \kappa$, and $D \in \mathbb{N}$, there is a polynomial $\gamma$ in the variables $x_{w,i}$ ($w \in \kappa$, $1 \le i \le n$) with $\gamma(e_i(t_0(w))) \ne 0$, and for every monomial $x^\beta$ of degree at most $D$ there is $Y_1 \ge 1$ with this property: for every $j \ge 0$ and every word $\omega$ in the archimedean Hecke letters there are test functions $f_E$, $f_F$ that act as $A \circ (\gamma x^\beta m(\omega)) \circ U_q^j \circ g$ (with the base changed polynomial on $F$), whose supports at $w_0$ and $q$ are bounded by $Y_1Y_0^{|\omega|}$, and that match globally with constant $\kappa_0\prod_{v \in S}c_v$.

Lean: `UpperTheory.ConvolutionTheory.GM.termEx`.

*Proof.* The [global comparison](/entry/g4/) gives $k > 0$, and the Iwahori functions of $j\nu$ at the split place $q$ match with a constant $c_q > 0$. Put $\kappa_0 = k\,c_\infty\,c_q$; it depends on neither $L$ nor $\nu$. The two sides give constants $Y_E$ and $Y_F$; put $Y_0 = \max(Y_E, Y_F)$. The places of $\kappa$ lie over places outside $S \cup \{q\}$, which are unramified, so the [matching at all unramified places](/entry/loc-all/) gives $\gamma_w$ for $w \in \kappa$, and we put $\gamma = \prod_w\gamma_w$, each in its own variables. Split $x^\beta$ into polynomials $p_w$, one for each $w$, and choose spherical $h_w$ with transform $\gamma_wp_w$ and $f_v$ on $\mathrm{GL}_n(F_v)$ with the base changed transform. At $v \in S$ use the functions of $L$; at $q$ the Iwahori functions; at every other place the functions $\bigotimes_{w \mid v}h_w$ and $f_v$, which match with constant 1. The global comparison then gives the global matching with constant $k\,c_\infty\,c_q\prod_{v \in S}c_v$. $\square$

**Theorem 7.10.5 (Global matching from the units).** Assume that the units match for every cyclic extension of totally complex number fields of prime degree. Let $E/F$ be such an extension and fix data as above. There is $\kappa > 0$ such that, for each local datum $L$ and each good $\nu$, there are sides over $E$ and $F$ and numbers $p > 0$ and $C_0$ with the following property. For every finite set of unramified places with Satake parameters $t_0$ and every $D$, there is a polynomial $\gamma$ with $\gamma(t_0) \ne 0$ such that, for $j$ large, every Hecke polynomial $R$ of degree at most $D$, and of degree at most $j/p$ in the archimedean letters, gives test functions $f_E$ and $f_F$ that act as $A \circ \gamma R \circ U_q^j \circ g$, whose kernels vanish above height $e^{C_0j}$, and with
$$I_\sigma(f_E) = \kappa\prod_{v \in S}c_v\cdot I(f_F),$$
where $I_\sigma$ and $I$ are the twisted and ordinary integrals of the kernels over the automorphic quotients.

Lean: `UpperTheory.ConvolutionTheory.GM.gm_of_unit`.

*Proof.* Take $\kappa = \kappa_0$ from the [matched functions for one Hecke monomial](/entry/term-ex/). For $j$ large the test functions of the sides are elliptic, so their kernels see only elements with irreducible characteristic polynomial, and the kernels vanish above height $e^{Cj}$ for some $C$. For an elliptic pair that matches globally with constant $c$, the twisted integral equals $c$ times the ordinary one: the integrals of the kernels unfold into sums of global orbital integrals over the rational elliptic classes. Write $R$ as a sum of monomials. For each monomial the previous theorem gives matched functions, and the integrals are linear, so the identity holds for $R$. $\square$

**Theorem 7.10.6 (Global matching from the fundamental lemma).** Assume the fundamental lemma for the units: for every unramified cyclic extension $E/k$ of nonarchimedean local fields of characteristic 0, the characteristic function of $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathcal{O}_E)$ matches that of $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathcal{O}_k)$ with constant 1. Then the conclusion of the previous theorem holds.

Lean: `UpperTheory.ConvolutionTheory.GM.gm_of_bcfl`.

*Proof.* Let $E/F$ be cyclic of prime degree and $v$ a finite place of $F$ unramified in $E$. If $v$ splits, the units match by the split case, which the companion development proves. If not, there is one place $w$ over $v$, and the fundamental lemma for $E_w/F_v$ gives the matching. So the units match, and the [global matching from the units](/entry/gm-of-unit/) applies. $\square$
