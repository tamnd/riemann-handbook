This theorem builds matching pairs of global test functions on $\mathrm{GL}_n$ over $E$ and $F$ for cyclic base change, with a uniform constant. Its one hypothesis is the local matching of the units at unramified places. [The theorem from the fundamental lemma](/entry/gm-of-bcfl/) supplies that hypothesis, and the result goes on to [EveryCharacter from the Galois row](/step/every-character/).

## Statement

Let $E/F$ be a cyclic extension of prime degree of totally complex number fields, with $\mathrm{Gal}(E/F)$ generated by $\sigma$. Let $n > 0$.

$\mathrm{LocUnit}$ says: at each finite place $v$ of $F$ unramified in $E$, the unit $\mathbf{1}_{\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathcal{O}_E \otimes \mathcal{O}_v)}$ matches $\mathbf{1}_{\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathcal{O}_v)}$ with constant 1. Here $\varphi$ *matches* $f$ with constant $c$ (`MatchAt`) if, for Haar measures normalized on the maximal compact subgroups, the twisted orbital integrals of $\varphi$ are $c$ times the orbital integrals of $f$ at the norms, and those of $f$ vanish off the norms.

A local datum $L$ (`Loc`) is a finite set $S$ of places of $F$ outside a split place $q$, with test functions $\varphi_v$, $f_v$ at $v \in S$ that match with constants $c_v$, the Iwahori functions at $q$, and units elsewhere. An exponent $\nu \in \mathbb{Z}^n$ is good if it is strictly decreasing, has sum 0, and no proper nonempty partial sum is 0.

**Theorem.** If $\mathrm{LocUnit}$ holds, then $\mathrm{GM}$ holds. Fix Haar measures, a Galois twist $\theta$, a split place $q$ with a place $w_0$ over it, and archimedean test functions $a_E$, $a_F$ that are normalized (`AmpFn`) and match with a constant $c_\infty > 0$ (`ArchPair`). Then there is $\kappa > 0$ such that, for each local datum $L$ and each good $\nu$ there are sides $\mathfrak{e}$ over $E$ and $\mathfrak{f}$ over $F$, built from $L$, $a_E$, $a_F$ and the Iwahori-Hecke operator $U_q$ of $\nu$ at $q$, and $p > 0$ and $C_0$ with this property (`GeoPair`). For each finite set $\kappa'$ of unramified places, Satake parameters $t_0$ on $\kappa'$ and degree bound $D$, there is a polynomial $\gamma$ with $\gamma(t_0) \neq 0$ such that, for $j$ large, each Hecke polynomial $R$ of degree at most $D$ (and degree at most $j/p$ in the archimedean letters) gives test functions $f_E$, $f_F$ such that $f_E$ acts as $A \circ \gamma R \circ U_q^j \circ g$ for the data $A$, $g$ of $\mathfrak{e}$ (and $f_F$ likewise), their kernels vanish above height $e^{C_0 j}$, and
$$I_\theta(f_E) = \kappa \prod_{v \in S} c_v \cdot I(f_F).$$

We have not unfolded the global integrals $I_\theta$, $I$ (`twInt`).

## Proof

1. **The constant.** `termEx` gives $\kappa = k\, c_\infty\, c_q$, with $k$ from the global lemma `G.g4` and $c_q$ from `locQ`, the matching of Iwahori functions at $q$. Then it gives $\gamma$ and matched test functions for each monomial. At the places of $\kappa'$ it uses `locAll`, the only use of $\mathrm{LocUnit}$.
2. **The sides.** `sideE` and `sideF` build $\mathfrak{e}$ and $\mathfrak{f}$ from $L$ and $\nu$.
3. **Support.** The private lemma `sideCondition_split_place`, for $E/F$ and for $F/F$, makes the functions elliptic with kernels zero above height $e^{Cj}$, for $j$ large.
4. **Linearity.** Write $R$ as a sum of monomials, each with matched functions from step 1. `G.g3` turns each matching into an equality of global integrals, and `twLin` adds them up.
