This theorem is step 2 of the proof of [the twisted trace on a K-type](/entry/core-g/). It says that in a module as in `coreG`, at most one weight of the finite set $H$ has nonzero highest weight vectors, and that these vectors form a space of dimension at most 1. It has one hypothesis, `MultFor`, which `coreG` takes from [`multiplicity_formula_holds`](/entry/multiplicity-formula-holds/).

## Statement

We use the notation of [`coreG`](/entry/core-g/): $\lambda \in \mathbb{Z}^n$ non-increasing, $V$ with $E^1$, $E^2$, $\sigma$, $A$ (`IsCoreMod`), $K_{ab} = E^1_{ab} + E^2_{ab}$, and $V_\mu$ the space of $v$ with $K_{aa} v = \mu_a v$ and $K_{ab} v = 0$ for $a < b$. Let $\rho_a = \tfrac{n-1}{2} - a$.

A *level* is a monotone map $\ell \colon \{0, \dots, n-1\} \to \mathbb{N}$ with $\lambda_a = \lambda_b$ when $\ell(a) = \ell(b)$ (`IsLev`). Put $r_\ell(a) = \tfrac12(\#\{b : \ell(b) > \ell(a)\} - \#\{b : \ell(b) < \ell(a)\})$ and $\mu_\ell = 2\lambda + 2r_\ell$. Then $H$ is the set of $\mu_\ell$ for levels with values below $n$. The *cone* of $\ell$ is the set of sums $\sum c_{ab}(e_a - e_b)$ with $c_{ab} \in \mathbb{N}$ and $c_{ab} = 0$ unless $\ell(a) < \ell(b)$.

`MultFor` says: let $\ell$ be a level, and let $V$ satisfy `AdmFor` for $\ell$ (the relations of `IsCoreMod` without $\sigma$ and $A$, local finiteness under $K$, and $V_\nu = 0$ for each weight $\nu$ "of second type" for $\ell$). If $v, w \in V_{\mu_\ell}$, $v \neq 0$, and $w = xv$ for some $x$ in the algebra generated by the $E^i_{ab}$, then $w \in \mathbb{C}v$.

**Theorem.** If `MultFor` holds, then for each $V$ as in `coreG` there is $\mu_0 \in H$ with $V_\mu = 0$ for all $\mu \in H \setminus \{\mu_0\}$ and $\dim V_{\mu_0} \le 1$.

## Proof

The proof is `multG_of` with `MultFor` and six proved inputs. If all $V_\mu$ with $\mu \in H$ vanish, any $\mu_0$ works. Otherwise, choose $\mu_0 = \mu_{\ell_0}$ with $V_{\mu_0} \neq 0$ and $|\mu_0 + \rho|^2$ least.

1. `adm_least_holds` (`AdmLeast`): a least weight of this kind gives `AdmFor` for $\ell_0$. `real_compression` (`Compress`) lets the minimum over $H$ stand for the minimum over all levels.
2. **Other weights vanish.** Let $\mu \in H$, $\mu \neq \mu_0$, with a nonzero $x \in V_\mu$. Irreducibility gives $a$ with $a v = x$ for a nonzero $v \in V_{\mu_0}$. `real_model_exists` (`ModelEx`) gives a model module for $\ell_0$, and `cone_no_second_weights` shows that it has no weights of second type. Then `real_k_types_in_cone` puts $\mu - \mu_0$ in the cone of $\ell_0$, and `real_first_nilpotent` (`N1`) gives $\mu = \mu_0$, a contradiction.
3. **A line.** For $v, w \in V_{\mu_0}$ with $v \neq 0$, irreducibility gives $x$ with $xv = w$, and `MultFor` gives $w \in \mathbb{C}v$.
