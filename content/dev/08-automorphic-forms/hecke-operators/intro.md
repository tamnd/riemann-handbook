This chapter collects the results on Hecke operators and cusp forms that the later volumes use. Sections 1 to 6 are on classical and adelic forms. Section 1 proves strong multiplicity one for cuspidal representations of $\mathrm{GL}_a$ over totally real and totally complex fields, with [Whittaker functions](/learn/automorphic-forms/#fourier-coefficients-and-whittaker-functions) and [unramified representations](/learn/representations/#unramified-representations), and attaches classical newforms to cuspidal representations of $\mathrm{GL}_2$ over $\mathbb{Q}$. Section 2 bounds the cohomology of [arithmetic subgroups](/learn/trace-formula/#arithmetic-subgroups-and-siegel-sets) of $\mathrm{GL}_n$ with level. Section 3 builds the Hilbert [Eisenstein series](/learn/automorphic-forms/#eisenstein-series) of weight one from odd [Hecke characters](/learn/class-field-theory/#hecke-characters) of finite order, and Section 4 derives weight congruences from [the trace formula for a compact quotient](/learn/trace-formula/#the-trace-formula-for-a-compact-quotient). Section 5 finds harmonic representatives in relative Lie algebra cohomology for [cohomological representations](/learn/automorphic-forms/#regular-algebraic-cohomological-and-polarized-representations). Section 6 attaches [local Langlands parameters](/learn/galois-representations/#the-local-langlands-correspondence) to irreducible representations of $\mathrm{GL}_n$ over $p$-adic fields and counts [primes represented by norm forms](/learn/analytic-number-theory/#primes-represented-by-norm-forms) in short intervals. Sections 7 and 8 work with theta data: they conjugate the levels of rows, and prove that the good vectors on a [definite quaternion algebra](/learn/automorphic-forms/#definite-quaternion-algebras) have a free action and a fixed good share, using [the local theta correspondence](/learn/theta/#the-local-theta-correspondence). Sections 9 to 12 build the Hecke operator at a split prime on sections of a unitary moduli space, read it on the [adelic group](/learn/automorphic-forms/#automorphic-forms-on-adelic-groups) through [the Weil representation](/learn/theta/#the-weil-representation), and prove that it keeps integrality away from the prime.

The results are used in three steps of the proof. Strong multiplicity one and the existence of local parameters go into [EveryCharacter](/step/every-character/), the count of primes of norm form into [the step from theta integrals and local matching](/step/theta-local-matching/), and the parameters of cuspidal representations into [the local Langlands existence step](/step/rect-exists/). The other results go into later chapters, among them [the conjugate representations theorem](/entry/clozel-conj-c/), [twisted scalar conjugation](/entry/twisted-scalar-conjugation/) and [the Hecke integrality, transform and reading facts](/entry/hecke-int-transform-read-tie/).
