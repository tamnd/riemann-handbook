This theorem is an arithmetic Hilbert-Samuel formula for $L^2$ norms, on an arithmetic variety in a product of projective spaces, for a line bundle of degree $1$ on one factor. It also bounds the sup norm of a section by its $L^2$ norm. [Arithmetic volume and Hilbert-Samuel bounds from three inputs](/entry/hs-chain/) uses it twice: to count small sections along multiples, and for the $L^2$ degree of tensor powers.

## Statement

Use the language of [the parent entry](/entry/hs-chain/): a point $y$ with coordinates $y_{k,i}$ in a field $\Omega$ of characteristic $0$, its prime $P$ in $\mathbb{Z}[x_{k,i}]$, and a Hermitian line $\overline{A} = (a, h)$. A *block datum* is such data with a block $k_0$, and:

- $X$ has dimension $D+1$ with $D \geq 1$, and $\Omega$ is generated by quotients of forms of degree $n$ in the block $k_0$ only;
- $a$ is $1$ on $k_0$ and $0$ on the other blocks;
- $h$ is [[semipositive|FF2P7ER3QT]] relative to an ideal $P_0 \subseteq P$, and invariant under complex conjugation;
- a resolution $R$ of the complex points: a compact complex manifold of dimension $D$ with holomorphic local lifts to the cone over $P$, a bijection onto the complex points outside a closed set $V(J)$ up to scalars, with $V(J)$ of measure zero;
- a probability measure $dV$ on $R$ with smooth positive density in charts.

For $n \geq 1$ let $H_n$ be the forms of degree $n$ on block $k_0$, modulo $P$. Give $H_n$ the $L^2$ norm $\|f\|^2 = \tfrac12 \int (|f|^2/h^{2n})(z) + (|f|^2/h^{2n})(\bar z) \, dV$. Let $\widehat\chi(n) = -\tfrac12 \log \det G_n$, where $G_n$ is the Gram matrix of a $\mathbb{Z}$-basis of $H_n$.

**Theorem.** Assume `ArithIntersectionSystemStatement` and `ResKStatement`, as in the parent entry. Let $I$ be any intersection family that satisfies the axioms. Then for each block datum:

$$\widehat\chi(n) = I(\overline{A}^{D+1}) \frac{n^{D+1}}{(D+1)!} + o(n^{D+1}),$$

and there is $C$ with $|f(z)|/h(z)^n \leq C\, n^{D/2} \|f\|$ for all $n \geq 1$, $f \in H_n$ and complex points $z$.

The first part has the shape of the arithmetic Hilbert-Samuel theorem of Gillet and Soulé (Invent. Math. 110, 1992).

## Proof

`TheoremHSMBlock.proof` combines three statements:

1. reduction to projective space: the defect of $\widehat\chi$ minus the generic degree $\deg(X_{\mathbb{Q}})$ (`genDeg`) times the defect for $\mathbb{P}^D$ with the Fubini-Study metric is $o(n^{D+1})$;
2. the formula for $\mathbb{P}^D$ with the Fubini-Study metric;
3. the sup bound.

The defect is $\widehat\chi(n) - I(\overline{A}^{D+1}) n^{D+1}/(D+1)!$. Part 1 plus $\deg(X_{\mathbb{Q}})$ times part 2 gives the formula; the rest is ring arithmetic. [The open assembly of the block formula](/entry/theorem-hsmblock-of-open/) proves the three statements from 19 inputs. This theorem supplies them: graph and change of model lemmas, degree lemmas from `hAIS`, and resolution and integrability lemmas from `hResK`.

## In the Lean code

The theorem is in [HSMBlock.lean](lean:Development/WeilBound/CoreStatements/AutomorphicKernels/MoriwakiBigness/HilbertSamuelBlock/HSMBlock.lean#L42). The statement and the datum are in [StatementsArith.lean](lean:GroundTheory/HeightsAndTheta/Heights/HilbertSamuel/StatementsArith.lean#L273).

- $\widehat\chi(n)$ is set to $0$ when $H_n$ has no $\mathbb{Z}$-basis. The value does not depend on the basis, since a change of basis has determinant $\pm 1$.
- The conclusion holds for every intersection family, but `hAIS` asserts only that one system exists. It is used through degree lemmas (`degree_of_system`, `generic_degree_of_arith_system`), whose statements we have not unfolded.
- The field $P_0 \leq P$ of the datum is named `normalForm_baseLattice_le_dualSpan_sup_line`.
