This theorem gives the conclusion of [the Siegel-Weil identity at $s_0$](/entry/lit/) from three hypotheses that [`lit`](/entry/lit/) supplies. It proves three more inputs and calls [`top6`](/entry/top6/). It is the top of a chain of five steps (`top7`, `top6`, `top5`, `top3`, `top2`) that only pass hypotheses along.

## Statement

The data and the conclusion are those of [`lit`](/entry/lit/): under `Std`, `InRange` and $1 \le n$, for each $g$ the Siegel Eisenstein series $E(g, z)$ is holomorphic at $s_0$ with value the theta integral $I(g)$.

**Theorem.** This holds if:

- `GenA`: $G$ is generated by the upper unipotent, block diagonal and lower unipotent matrices;
- `GIdil`: theta integrals along a dilation at an archimedean place extend holomorphically in the parameter, with derivatives given by a dilation operator;
- `CoefND`: each nondegenerate Fourier coefficient of $E(g, z)$ is holomorphic at $s_0$ with value the same coefficient of $I$.

## Proof

The proof is `top6 fTfact hG schDeriv (derivOne wtc) hId hC`. The three added facts:

- `fTfact`: the Weyl element acts at one archimedean place by a constant times a partial Fourier transform;
- `schDeriv`: a polynomial times a Gaussian, moved by $\exp(tX)$, is differentiable in $t$ in the Schwartz space;
- `derivOne wtc`: the theta integral of a derivative along a one-parameter subgroup of $\mathrm{U}(V)$ is $0$. It uses `wtc`, the absolute convergence of theta integrals.
