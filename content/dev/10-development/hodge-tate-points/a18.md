This theorem computes the intersection number of $n$ line bundles on an $n$-dimensional projective variety $X$ from the multigraded Hilbert function of a model of $X$ in a product of projective spaces. It is a step of a chain on numerical ampleness that ends in [`k_twins`](/entry/k-twins/) and reaches [EveryCharacter from the literature and VWin](/step/every-character-of-literature/). Four theorems of the chain use it: `a19`, `a25`, `a28` and `a77`.

## Statement

Let $F \subseteq \Omega$ be fields. Let $K$ and each $\iota_k$ ($k \in K$) be finite sets. A point $y = (y_{k,i})$ with entries in $\Omega$ gives the prime ideal $V \subset F[x_{k,i}]$, the kernel of $x_{k,i} \mapsto y_{k,i} t_k$ into $\Omega[t_k : k \in K]$. It is the multihomogeneous ideal of the subvariety of $\prod_k \mathbb{P}^{\iota_k}$ with generic point $y$. Let $h_V(m)$, for $m \in \mathbb{Z}^K$, be the dimension of the part of degree $m$ of $F[x]/V$ (and $0$ if some $m_k < 0$). Let $\Gamma(m) \subseteq \Omega$, the sections of $\mathcal{O}(m)$ on $V$, be the $s$ with $s \prod_k y_{k,c_k}^{-m_k}$ in the chart ring $F[y_{k,i}/y_{k,c_k}]$ for every chart $c$.

Fix $k_0 \in K$, let $x = y_{k_0} \in \Omega^{N+1}$, and let $X \subseteq \mathbb{P}^N$ be the closure of $x$. Suppose that each block of $y$ has a nonzero entry and that the charts of $V$ and of $X$ give the same local rings in $\Omega$ (`IsModelWithBase`). So the projection $V \to X$ identifies the two varieties. Let $e_{k_0}$ be the unit vector at $k_0$.

**Theorem.** Suppose that $X$ has dimension $n$ (`IsSubvar`: $F[x_0, \dots, x_N]/I_X$ has Krull dimension $n+1$). Let $L_1, \dots, L_n$ be line bundles on $X$, given by invertible modules on the charts (`Kleiman.LineBundle`), and $\delta_1, \dots, \delta_n \in \mathbb{Z}^K$ such that $H^0(X, L_i(c)) = \Gamma(\delta_i + c\,e_{k_0})$ for all $c \in \mathbb{Z}$. Then
$$\lim_{c \to \infty} \sum_{T \subseteq \{1,\dots,n\}} (-1)^{n-|T|} \dim_F H^0\Bigl(X, \textstyle\bigotimes_{i \in T} L_i (c)\Bigr) = \text{the eventual value of } \Delta_{\delta_1} \cdots \Delta_{\delta_n} h_V(m),$$
where $\Delta_b h(m) = h(m) - h(m-b)$ and "eventual" means for $m$ large in every coordinate.

The left side, `interNum`, has the form of the intersection number $(L_1 \cdots L_n)$ of Snapper and Kleiman (Kleiman, *Toward a numerical theory of ampleness*, Ann. of Math. 84, 1966), with $h^0$ of large twists for the Euler characteristic. The right side is `intNum`, the mixed multidegree of $V$ in the directions $\delta_i$.

## Proof

1. `ModelDim.proof`: $\dim X = n$ if and only if $V$ has relative dimension $n$.
2. `MixedDiffConst.full`: then $\Delta_{\delta_1} \cdots \Delta_{\delta_n} h_V$ is eventually a constant $D$.
3. [`a17`](/entry/a17/): a polynomial $P$ equals $h_V$ eventually, and $\dim \Gamma(\beta + c\,e_{k_0}) = P(\beta + c\,e_{k_0})$ for large $c$.
4. `altSum_eq_of_eventually`: the mixed difference of $P$ is a polynomial that equals $D$ on a product of infinite sets, so it equals $D$ everywhere.
5. `a7` (`TensorRealise`): for large $c$, $H^0(X, \bigotimes_{i \in T} L_i(c)) = \Gamma(\sum_{i \in T} \delta_i + c\,e_{k_0})$.

So for large $c$ the sum is the mixed difference of $P$ at $\sum_i \delta_i + c\,e_{k_0}$ (after $T \mapsto T^c$), which is $D$.
