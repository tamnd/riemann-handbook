This theorem proves Tate's global Euler characteristic formula for the [[Galois cohomology|EESNTXFWHZ]] of a finite $\ell$-primary module, unramified outside a finite set of places. The proof of [EveryCharacter from Row0b](/step/every-character-of-row/) reaches it through two wrappers that ignore their argument: `global_euler_char_of_literature`, a helper of the [literature structure](/step/every-character-of-literature/), and `global_euler_char_of_open`, which takes the `OpenImageCondition` of [VWin from Row0b](/step/vwin-of-row/). Below this theorem are 1,190 declarations.

## Statement

Let $F$ be a number field with algebraic closure $\Omega$, $\ell$ a prime, $r > 0$, and $M$ a finite discrete $\mathbb{Z}/\ell^r$-module. Let $S$ be a finite set of finite places of $F$ that contains every place above $\ell$. Let $G_S$ be the quotient of $\operatorname{Gal}(\Omega/F)$ by the closed normal subgroup generated by the inertia groups at the finite places outside $S$. Let $G_S$ act continuously on $M$, and let $H^i = H^i(G_S, M)$ be continuous cochain cohomology. For each real place $w$, let $c_w$ be a complex conjugation at $w$.

**Theorem.** With $r_2$ the number of complex places,
$$\#H^0 \cdot \#H^2 \cdot \#M^{[F:\mathbb{Q}]} = \#H^1 \cdot \prod_{w \text{ real}} \#M^{c_w = 1} \cdot \#M^{r_2}.$$

This is Tate's formula $\#H^0 \#H^2 / \#H^1 = \prod_{v \mid \infty} \#H^0(F_v, M) / |\#M|_v$, without denominators. See Milne, *Arithmetic Duality Theorems*, Theorem I.5.1.

## Proof

The proof is one line: `globalEulerChar_of_lemmaX X.lemmaX`. The chain below it is:

1. `lemmaX` proves `GlobalEulerCharCoprime`. It is the case where $M$ is killed by $\ell$, and $G_S$ acts on $M$ through a finite Galois extension $L$ of degree prime to $\ell$. Also, $L$ contains $\mu_\ell$, and $\ell \neq 2$ or $L$ is totally complex. The proof turns $M$ into a $\mathbb{Z}/\ell$-module (`zmodRepOfTorsion`) and uses `altSum` and `ratio_eq_one_of_altSum`.
2. `W_assemble.ratioOneKilled_of_lemmaX` removes the conditions on $L$. This gives `RatioOneKilled`: the ratio is 1 for every $M$ killed by $\ell$. The proof uses `W_split.exists_split`, `ratio_eq_one_of_coprime_cyclic'` and `W_endx.ending_lemmaX`, which we have not opened.
3. `ratioOne_of_killed_l` is a dévissage from $M[\ell] = M$ to $M$ killed by a power of $\ell$ (`RatioOne`).
4. `globalEulerCharAt_of_ratioOne` translates the result into the form of the statement. The ratio lives on subfields of $\overline{\mathbb{Q}}$, with modules in `Rep ℤ`, and `W_dictfwd_a` carries it to $F$, $\Omega$ and $G_S$.

Here the ratio is `gsEulerRatio`, which is $\chi(M) \cdot \#M^{r_1 + r_2} / \prod_{w \text{ real}} \#H^0(c_w, M)$. We have not unfolded `eulerChar`.
