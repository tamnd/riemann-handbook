This theorem is the unitary case of step 1 in [Small compact open subgroups whose Hecke algebras satisfy a polynomial identity](/entry/loc-pi/), which belongs to the [Flath factorisation](/entry/flath-factorisation-of-literature/). The declaration itself only joins proved statements. The lattice argument is in `GlobalTheory.CompanionTheory.CartanU.final`.

## Statement

Let $L$ be a nontrivially normed field of characteristic $0$ with an ultrametric norm, and with closed balls compact. Let $s$ be a nontrivial involution of $L$ that keeps the norm, and let $J$ be an $n \times n$ matrix over $L$ with $(J^s)^{T} = J$ and $\det J \ne 0$. Put
$$U(s, J) = \{ g \in \mathrm{GL}_n(L) : (g^s)^{T} J g = J \}.$$

**Theorem** (`StCartanU`). Each open subgroup of $U(s, J)$ contains a compact open subgroup $K$ with a Cartan structure (`IsCartan K d l`). This means: there are finitely many elements $c_i$ that normalise $K$ and commuting elements $t_1, \dots, t_l$ such that $U(s, J)$ is the union of the sets $K c_i\, t\, c_j K$, with $t$ in the monoid generated by the $t_j$, and $t k t' \in K t t' K$ for $k \in K$ and such $t, t'$.

## Proof

`stCartanU` applies `GlobalTheory.T.isometric_lattice_congruence_subgroups` to seven proved inputs. Their types are in `StSandwich.lean`.

1. `StDelta` and `StExists`: a maximal integral lattice $\Lambda$ for the Hermitian form exists. `StExists` comes from the sandwich statement.
2. `StFrame`: a Witt frame $e_i, f_i$ with anisotropic part $W$ that is adapted to $\Lambda$. The local proof `stFrame` builds it from a Hensel statement, a sandwich statement, a plane statement and a splitting statement.
3. `StTor`: for units $u_i$ there is one isometry that is $u_i$ on $e_i$, $s(u_i)^{-1}$ on $f_i$ and $1$ on $W$.
4. `StDecomp`: each isometry is $k\,t\,k'$ with $k, k'$ fixing $\Lambda$ and $t$ the torus element of $(p^{r_i})$, $r$ antitone. It comes from the frame, Witt and torus statements.
5. `StIwahori`: an element of the congruence subgroup of level $\pi$ factors as $a b$, and $t a t^{-1}$, $t^{-1} b t$ stay in it for dominant $t$.
6. The congruence subgroups of $\Lambda$ are compact and open, normal in the stabiliser of $\Lambda$, and cofinal among open subgroups.

`CartanU.final` takes $K$ as a congruence subgroup inside the given open subgroup, the $c_i$ as coset representatives of $K$ in the stabiliser, and the $t_j$ as torus elements. Steps 4 and 5 give `IsCartan`. The result has the form of the Cartan decomposition $G = K_0 A^{+} K_0$ of Bruhat and Tits (Publ. Math. IHES 41, 1972), written with lattices.
