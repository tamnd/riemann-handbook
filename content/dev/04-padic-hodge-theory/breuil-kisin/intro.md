This chapter attaches Breuil-Kisin modules to lattices in semistable representations of $G_K$, for a finite extension $K$ of $\mathbb{Q}_\ell$, and uses them to control Hodge-Tate jumps in $\ell$-adic limits. A module is written as a *frame*: a matrix $T$ over $W(C^\flat)$ whose columns span a module over $\mathfrak{S} = W[[X]]$ with a Frobenius of bounded poles along $E$, and whose span over $A_{\mathrm{inf}}$ is stable under $G_K$. The first sections build a logarithm of $[\pi^\flat]$ in $B_{\mathrm{st}}$, then period matrices over $A_{\mathrm{cris}}$, then a frame from these periods. The main theorem gives a de Rham frame whose height is bounded by the Hodge-Tate jumps alone, and a crystalline one when the representation is crystalline. Because the bound does not depend on the lattice, the frames of a tower of lattices have a limit. The last theorem uses this, with Hodge-Tate operators attached to one element of $G_K$, to prove that a representation covered by semistable representations with fixed jumps is de Rham with the same jumps, and crystalline when they are.

The chapter needs from Volume 1 the [period rings](/learn/galois-representations/#period-rings) with the tilt, $A_{\mathrm{inf}}$, $\theta$, $B_{\mathrm{cris}}$, $B_{\mathrm{st}}$ and $B_{\mathrm{dR}}$, the [crystalline, de Rham and semistable representations](/learn/galois-representations/#crystalline-de-rham-and-potentially-semistable-representations) with their Hodge-Tate weights, and the [cyclotomic character](/learn/galois-representations/#the-cyclotomic-character-and-tate-modules). The theorem on frames of bounded height and the theorem on limits of frames are inputs of [the theorem on the core of the Galois row](/entry/core-all-of/), and the theorem on covers is an input of [the theorem on closedness at $\ell$](/entry/closed-at-l/). Both are used in the step [the Galois row holds](/step/galois-row-holds/) of [the Galois row](/proof/galois-row/).
