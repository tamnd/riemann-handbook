This chapter has two parts. The first part studies [abelian schemes](/learn/abelian-varieties/#abelian-schemes) and their [formal groups](/learn/formal-groups/#formal-group-laws) over $A_{\mathrm{inf}}/(\xi^2)$ and over the ring of integers $\mathcal{O}$ of an algebraically closed valued field. It builds a period datum from the $p$-power torsion, the Weil pairing and the [period rings](/learn/galois-representations/#period-rings) of the Background, turns it into a Hodge-Tate datum through a Hopf order, and bounds the colength of the Hodge-Tate periods at split places and at unramified places, first for linear data and then for the formal groups of degenerating abelian schemes. The second part proves that the $l$-adic character of an algebraic [Hecke character](/learn/class-field-theory/#hecke-characters) is [de Rham](/learn/padic-hodge/#de-rham-representations) at the places above $l$, with the Hodge-Tate jump $-n(\iota \circ \tau)$ at each embedding $\tau$, from periods of Lubin-Tate characters in $B_{\mathrm{dR}}^+$ and [local class field theory](/learn/class-field-theory/#local-class-field-theory).

Both parts are used in the step [every character of the literature](/step/every-character-of-literature/). The length bounds and the Hodge-Tate data enter it directly, and the de Rham property of Hecke characters enters it through [the unconditional form](/entry/field-outright/) of the last theorem.
