## The top power of the first Chern class

Let $X$ be a connected smooth projective variety of dimension $d$ over a field $K$ of characteristic $0$, and let $c$ be the first Chern class of $\mathcal{O}(1)$ in de Rham cohomology. This section proves that $c^d \neq 0$ in $H^{2d}_{\mathrm{dR}}(X)$. The Hodge-Tate comparison uses this fact twice. It gives a nonzero class in the top degree, which makes the Hodge-Tate map injective there, and the crystalline period map of a later section uses it to find a functional $\varphi$ with $\varphi(c^d) = 1$. The proof is a residue computation on the open set where all coordinates of a finite projection to $\mathbb{P}^d$ are nonzero.

**Definition 2.2.1 (De Rham cohomology on the thickening site).** Let $q : X \to \operatorname{Spec} K$ be a scheme over $K$. We write $H^n_{\mathrm{dR}}(X)$ for the $n$-th cohomology of the sheaf $T \mapsto \mathcal{O}(T)$ on the site of divided-power thickenings of $X$ (see the [Theorem 2.1.4](/entry/hodge-affine-iso-sign/)), with its divided-power ideal $J$ and filtration $J^{[r]}$. It has a cup product and a unit $1 \in H^0_{\mathrm{dR}}(X)$. We put $c^0 = 1$ and $c^{k+1} = c^k \cup c$.

For $X$ smooth over a field of characteristic $0$ this cohomology is de Rham cohomology, and the filtration by $J^{[r]}$ is the Hodge filtration. That is why the comparison theorems are stated with it.

**Definition 2.2.2 (Tautological line).** Let $X$ be a scheme over $K$. A *line datum* on $X$ is an étale map $f : Y \to X$ together with local sections and transition units. It is *tautological* if there are $N$ and a closed immersion $i : X \to \mathbb{P}^N_K$ over $K$ such that $f$ has a section over each chart $i^{-1}\{x_j \neq 0\}$, and the transition units between the charts $j$ and $k$ are the ratios $x_k / x_j$ of the homogeneous coordinates. So a tautological line is a model of $\mathcal{O}(1)$ pulled back along a projective embedding, and the existence of one says that $X$ is projective.

**Definition 2.2.3 (De Rham first Chern class).** Let $D$ be a line datum on $X$. A class $c \in H^2_{\mathrm{dR}}(X)$ is a *de Rham first Chern class* of $D$ if the following holds for every $s \geq 1$. Restrict to thickenings $T$ on which the divided powers $J^{[s]}$ vanish and over which $D$ has local sections. Choose lifts $\Lambda$ of the transition units of $D$ to the thickenings. Then there is a $2$-cocycle $z$ on Čech simplices of thickenings with
$$z(\sigma) = \operatorname{plog}_s\big(\Lambda(\sigma) - 1\big), \qquad \operatorname{plog}_s(x) = \sum_{k < s} (-1)^k\, k!\, \gamma_{k+1}(x),$$
whose class is the restriction of $c$. Here $\gamma_{k+1}$ is the divided power, so $\operatorname{plog}_s$ is the truncated logarithm $\log(1 + x)$ written with divided powers. This is the right definition because $d \log$ of the transition units is the classical Čech cocycle of $c_1$, and on a thickening the logarithm replaces $d \log$.

The main theorem of the section is the following.

**Theorem 2.2.4 (Nonvanishing of the top power).** Let $K$ be a field of characteristic $0$ and $X$ a connected scheme, smooth of relative dimension $d$ over $K$. Let $D$ be a tautological line on $X$ and $c \in H^2_{\mathrm{dR}}(X)$ a de Rham first Chern class of $D$. Then $c^d \neq 0$ in $H^{2d}_{\mathrm{dR}}(X)$.

The proof needs a projection of $X$ to projective space and an algebraic statement about residues. We state both first.

**Definition 2.2.5 (Torus charts).** Let $R$ be a $K$-algebra and $t_0, \dots, t_n \in R^\times$. For a nonempty set $S \subseteq \{0, \dots, n + 1\}$ let $E(S) \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^{n+1}$ be the set of exponents $m$ with $m_k \geq 0$ when $k + 1 \notin S$, and with $\sum_k m_k \leq 0$ when $0 \notin S$. Put $T(S) = K[t^m : m \in E(S)] \subseteq R$. If $t_k = y_{k+1}/y_0$ for homogeneous coordinates $y_0, \dots, y_{n+1}$ of $\mathbb{P}^{n+1}$, then $T(S)$ is the ring of functions on the open set of $\mathbb{P}^{n+1}$ where $y_j \neq 0$ for $j \in S$.

**Definition 2.2.6 (Jacobian forms).** Let $B \subseteq R$ be a subalgebra and $D_0, \dots, D_n$ derivations of $R$ over $K$. For $\kappa : \{0, \dots, m - 1\} \to \{0, \dots, n\}$ injective, let $\mathrm{Jac}(B, \kappa)$ be the additive group generated by the elements $b \cdot \det\big(D_{\kappa(k)}(b_l)\big)_{k, l < m}$ with $b, b_0, \dots, b_{m-1} \in B$.

If the $D_k$ are dual to coordinates $t_k$, an element of $\mathrm{Jac}(B, \mathrm{id})$ is the coefficient of a top form $b\, db_0 \wedge \dots \wedge db_n$ with entries in $B$. The coefficient of $d\log t_0 \wedge \dots \wedge d\log t_n$ is $(t_0 \cdots t_n)^{-1}$.

**Lemma 2.2.7 (Projection to projective space).** Let $K$ be a field of characteristic $0$ and $X$ connected and smooth of relative dimension $n + 1$ over $K$, with a closed immersion $i : X \to \mathbb{P}^N_K$ over $K$. Then there are $e > 0$ and homogeneous polynomials $y_0, \dots, y_{n+1}$ of degree $e$ with these properties.

1. The opens $U_k = i^{-1}\{y_k \neq 0\}$ cover $X$, and each intersection $U_S = \bigcap_{k \in S} U_k$ with $S \neq \emptyset$ is affine.
2. There is an open $V \subseteq \bigcap_k U_k$ with étale coordinates $t_0, \dots, t_n$ such that $\mathcal{O}(V)$ is a domain and $t_k = y_{k+1}/y_0$. The $t_k$ are units and algebraically independent over $K$.
3. Let $B(S)$ be the image of restriction $\mathcal{O}(U_S) \to \mathcal{O}(V)$ for $S \neq \emptyset$. Restriction is injective. $B(S)$ is a finitely generated $K$-algebra, contains $T(S)$ and is integral over $T(S)$. Also $B(S \setminus \{j\}) \subseteq B(S)$ for $S$ the full set, and for every $r \in \mathcal{O}(V)$ there are $b, b'$ in $B$ of the full set with $b' \neq 0$ and $r b' = b$.

Lean: `CrystallineSemistableComparison.Pb.geo_setup`.

*Proof.* A homogeneous form of Noether normalization gives $y_0, \dots, y_{n+1}$ of a common degree $e$ with no common zero on $X$, so the $U_k$ cover $X$, and the map $X \to \mathbb{P}^{n+1}$ given by the $y_k$ is finite. Finiteness gives the integrality of $B(S)$ over $T(S)$, since $T(S)$ is the ring of the chart of $\mathbb{P}^{n+1}$ below $U_S$. Since $i$ is a closed immersion and the $U_S$ are preimages of affine opens of $\mathbb{P}^N$, they are affine. Since $X$ is connected and smooth, it is integral, so restriction to a dense open $V$ is injective and $\mathcal{O}(V)$ is a domain. Choose $V$ small enough that the $t_k$ are étale coordinates on it. The $t_k$ are algebraically independent because the map to $\mathbb{P}^{n+1}$ is finite and so dominant. Fractions come from the fact that $\mathcal{O}(V)$ is a localization of a finite extension of the function field of $U_{\text{all}}$. $\square$

The second input is the following statement of commutative algebra.

**Theorem 2.2.8 (Residue obstruction).** Let $K$ be a field of characteristic $0$, $R$ a domain over $K$, $t_0, \dots, t_n \in R^\times$ algebraically independent over $K$, and $D_0, \dots, D_n$ derivations of $R$ over $K$ with $D_k(t_j) = \delta_{kj}$. Let $B(S)$, for $S \subseteq \{0, \dots, n+1\}$, be subalgebras of $R$ such that, for $S$ nonempty, $B(S)$ is finitely generated, contains $T(S)$ and is integral over it, with $B(S \setminus \{j\}) \subseteq B(S)$ for $S$ the full set, and such that each $r \in R$ satisfies $r b' = b$ for some $b, b'$ in $B$ of the full set with $b' \neq 0$. Let $\omega_j \in \mathrm{Jac}(B(\text{all} \setminus \{j\}), \mathrm{id})$ and $\eta_k \in \mathrm{Jac}(B(\text{all}), \kappa_k)$, with $\kappa_k$ the increasing map that omits $k$. Then
$$(t_0 \cdots t_n)^{-1} \neq \sum_{j=0}^{n+1} \omega_j + \sum_{k=0}^{n} (-1)^k D_k(\eta_k).$$

Lean: `CrystallineSemistableComparison.Pb.resid_alg`.

*Proof.* We take this as given. It is the algebraic form of the residue theorem at the torus fixed point: the form $d\log t_0 \wedge \dots \wedge d\log t_n$ has residue $1$ there, while forms that are regular on one of the large charts and exact forms have residue $0$. $\square$

The next lemma turns a relation between cohomology classes on the charts into a relation between functions on $V$. It takes three statements on the graded pieces as hypotheses.

**Lemma 2.2.9 (Transfer to coordinates).** Assume the following three statements.

1. (Graded pieces on a frame.) On a thickening site with $d$ coordinates and constant graded pieces, for $j > r$ the group $H^j(J^{[r]}/J^{[r+1]})$ is zero, and for $r = j + q$ each class in it is represented by a sum $\sum_I x_I\, \omega_I$ over the $j$-element sets $I$ of coordinates, with $x_I \in J^{[q]}$; such a sum represents $0$ only if all $x_I \in J^{[q+1]}$.
2. (Polynomial thickenings.) The truncated thickening sites on which $J^{[s]} = 0$ have enough polynomial thickenings for the divided-power filtrations of steps $r \leq s$.
3. (Affine vanishing.) For $X$ smooth over a field of characteristic $0$, an affine open $V$ and $r < s$, the group $H^j(J^{[r]}/J^{[r+1]})$ on the thickenings of $V$ with $J^{[s]} = 0$ is zero for $j \neq r$.

Let $K$ be a field of characteristic $0$, $X$ smooth of relative dimension $n + 1$ over $K$, $i : X \to \mathbb{P}^N_K$ and $y_0, \dots, y_{n+1}$ such that each $U_S$ with $S \neq \emptyset$ is affine. Let $V \subseteq U_{\text{all}}$ have étale coordinates $t_0, \dots, t_n$, and let $D_0, \dots, D_n$ be derivations of $\mathcal{O}(V)$ with $D_k(t_j) = \delta_{kj}$. Let $g_0, \dots, g_n$ be units on $U_{\text{all}}$, and $\hat g_k$ lifts of the $g_k$ to the thickenings over $U_{\text{all}}$ with $J^{[n+2]} = 0$. Let $c_0 \in H^{n+1}(J^{[n+1]}/J^{[n+2]})$ on these thickenings be the class of $d\log \hat g_0 \cup \dots \cup d\log \hat g_n$. Let $\omega_j$ be classes in the same group over $U_{\text{all} \setminus \{j\}}$, and $f$ an $n$-cochain with values in $J^{[n]}$ whose coboundary has values in $J^{[n+1]}$ and represents $c_0 - \sum_j \omega_j|_{U_{\text{all}}}$. Then there are $x_j \in \mathrm{Jac}(B(\text{all} \setminus \{j\}), \mathrm{id})$ and $\eta_k \in \mathrm{Jac}(B(\text{all}), \kappa_k)$ with
$$\pm\, (g_0 \cdots g_n)^{-1} \det\big(D_k(g_l)\big) = \sum_j x_j + \sum_k (-1)^k D_k(\eta_k)$$
in $\mathcal{O}(V)$, for one of the two signs.

Lean: `CrystallineSemistableComparison.Pb.cone_transfer` (a second name for `GlobalTheory.T.projective_chart_affine_coords`).

*Proof.* This is the [Theorem 1.6.4 of Volume 3](/entry/projective-chart-affine-coords/) on affine coordinates of projective charts. By statement 3 the graded piece $n + 1$ has cohomology only in degree $n + 1$ on each $U_S$, so the Čech spectral sequence of the cover reduces the relation to degree $n + 1$ on $U_{\text{all}}$. By statements 1 and 2, on the coordinate open $V$ the classes are sums of the forms $\omega_I$ with coefficients, so the relation is an identity of top forms. Its coefficient of $dt_0 \wedge \dots \wedge dt_n$ is the displayed identity, and the classes $\omega_j$ and $f$ give the $x_j$ and the $\eta_k$. $\square$

**Lemma 2.2.10 (Nonvanishing from three inputs).** Assume the following three statements.

1. The conclusion of the [Lemma 2.2.7](/entry/geo-setup/) on projection to projective space, for all $K$, $X$, $n$, $N$ and $i$ as there.
2. The conclusion of the theorem on the residue obstruction above.
3. (Local exactness.) For $K$ of characteristic $0$, a line datum $D$ that is tautological for $i : X \to \mathbb{P}^N_K$, homogeneous $y_0, \dots, y_{m-1}$ of degree $e$, $s \geq 0$ and lifts $\Lambda$ of the transition units to the thickenings with $J^{[s]} = 0$ over the opens $U_k \cap i^{-1}\{x_j \neq 0\}$: there are $1$-cochains $b_k$ with values in $J^{[1]}$ on the thickenings over $U_k$ with $e \cdot z|_{U_k} = \partial b_k$, and for all $k, k'$ a lift $\hat g_{kk'}$ of $y_k / y_{k'}$ over $U_k \cap U_{k'}$ with $b_k - b_{k'} = d\log \hat g_{kk'}$.

Then the conclusion of the [Theorem 2.2.4](/entry/pow-k-ne-zero/) on the top power holds.

Lean: `CrystallineSemistableComparison.Pb.powK_ne_zero_of`.

*Proof.* If $d = 0$, then $c^0 = 1$, and $1 \neq 0$ because $X$ is nonempty. Let $d = n + 1$ and suppose that $c^{n+1} = 0$. Let $i : X \to \mathbb{P}^N_K$ be the closed immersion of the tautological line $D$.

1. Statement 1 gives $y_0, \dots, y_{n+1}$, the cover $U_k$, the open $V$ with coordinates $t_k$ and the algebras $B(S)$. Choose derivations $D_k$ of $\mathcal{O}(V)$ dual to the $t_k$.
2. Restrict to the thickenings over the $U_k$ on which $J^{[n+2]} = 0$. Choose a lift $\Lambda$ of the transition units. The definition of a de Rham first Chern class gives the cocycle $z$ of truncated logarithms, and $c^{n+1}$ restricts to the class of the cup power $z^{n+1}$. Since $c^{n+1} = 0$, this class is $0$, and so is the class of $(e z)^{n+1}$.
3. Statement 3 writes $e z$ on each $U_k$ as a coboundary $\partial b_k$, with $b_k - b_{k'} = d\log \hat g_{kk'}$ on $U_k \cap U_{k'}$.
4. The graded pieces on the intersections $U_S$ have cohomology only in the diagonal degree, and none in negative degrees or above the dimension. With these vanishing results, a Čech computation turns the vanishing of $(e z)^{n+1}$ into the hypothesis of the [Lemma 2.2.9](/entry/cone-transfer/) on transfer for $g_k = y_k / y_{k+1}$.
5. The transfer lemma gives $\pm (g_0 \cdots g_n)^{-1} \det(D_k g_l) = \sum_j x_j + \sum_k (-1)^k D_k(\eta_k)$. On $V$, $g_0 = t_0^{-1}$ and $g_k = t_{k-1} t_k^{-1}$ for $k \geq 1$. A direct computation of the logarithmic Jacobian of these ratios gives $(g_0 \cdots g_n)^{-1} \det(D_k g_l) = \pm (t_0 \cdots t_n)^{-1}$. After a change of sign of the $x_j$ and $\eta_k$, this is an identity that statement 2 forbids.

So $c^{n+1} \neq 0$. $\square$

We now prove the [Theorem 2.2.4](/entry/pow-k-ne-zero/) on the top power.

*Proof.* We check the three statements of the last lemma. Statement 1 is the [Lemma 2.2.7](/entry/geo-setup/) on projection to projective space, and statement 2 is the theorem on the residue obstruction above. Statement 3 we take as given. It holds because on the thickenings over $U_k$ the lifted transition units differ from the ratios $y_k / x_j$ by elements of $1 + J$, so $e$ times their truncated logarithm is the coboundary of the truncated logarithms of $y_k / x_j^e$, and on $U_k \cap U_{k'}$ these cochains differ by $d\log$ of $y_k / y_{k'}$. $\square$
