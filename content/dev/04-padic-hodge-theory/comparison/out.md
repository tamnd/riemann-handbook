This theorem proves the statement `Out`: three functors on perfect algebras over $\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{C}_\ell}^\flat$ satisfy descent to test algebras, up to each element of $W(\mathfrak{m}^\flat)$. It only feeds the proved hypotheses of [out_of_rin](/entry/out-of-rin/) into that theorem, which has the real content. Two lemmas of the crystalline comparison use it, and both lead to the [local steps](/entry/steps-holds/). Below this theorem are 412 declarations.

## Statement

Let $\mathcal{O}^\flat$ be the tilt of $\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{C}_\ell}$, let $\pi \in \mathcal{O}^\flat$ be the element with $\pi^\sharp = \ell$, and let $A_{\inf} = W(\mathcal{O}^\flat)$. For a perfect $\mathcal{O}^\flat$-algebra $A$ of the kind in [out_of_rin](/entry/out-of-rin/), put $U_n(A) = (W(A)/\xi)/\ell^n$, let $A_{\mathrm{cris},n}(A)$ be the $W(A)$-subalgebra of $W(A)[1/\ell]$ generated by the $\xi^k/k!$, modulo $\ell^n$, and let $\mathrm{Fil}^r_n(A)$ be its ideal generated by the $\xi^k/k!$ with $k \geq r$. Here $\xi = \ell - [\pi]$.

**Theorem.** Let $s \in A_{\inf}$ be such that no Witt coordinate of $s$ is a unit of $\mathcal{O}^\flat$. Then for each $n \geq 0$ and $r \geq 0$, the functors $U_n$, $A_{\mathrm{cris},n}$ and $\mathrm{Fil}^r_n$ satisfy descent to test algebras up to $s$ (up to the image of $s$ in $A_{\mathrm{cris}}$ for the last two).

The condition on $s$ says that $s \in W(\mathfrak{m}^\flat)$, with $\mathfrak{m}^\flat$ the maximal ideal of $\mathcal{O}^\flat$. "Descent up to $s$" is defined in [out_of_rin](/entry/out-of-rin/).

## Proof

The proof is one line: `out_of_rin l (CrystallineSemistableComparison.R.forD l)`. `R.forD` proves the five hypotheses `RIn` from lemmas `RL.d_ob`, `RL.d_seq`, `RL.gr` and others, which we have not opened.
