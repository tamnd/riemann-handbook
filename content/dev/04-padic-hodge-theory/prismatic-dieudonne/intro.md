This chapter has two parts. The first part works with a $p$-divisible group $G$ over the ring of integers $O$ of an algebraically closed field of characteristic $0$, its [Tate module](/learn/galois-representations/#the-cyclotomic-character-and-tate-modules) and the [period ring](/learn/galois-representations/#period-rings) $A_{\inf}$. Sections 1 to 3 build quasi-logarithms, prove that a basis of the Tate module can be adapted to them, show that a symmetric cocycle modulo an ideal is a coboundary, and prove that the Frobenius matrix in a lifted adapted basis is invertible. Sections 4 and 5 give the period matrices of $G$ over a frame and lift them to $A_{\inf}$. Section 6 reduces to groups $G$ such that $G$ and its dual are connected, and builds the prismatic Dieudonné functor: a free $A_{\inf}$-module with a Frobenius, a reduction modulo $\xi$ and a Hodge filtration. The second part compares crystalline and [de Rham cohomology](/learn/geometry/#de-rham-cohomology-and-deformations) of a smooth, separated and quasi-compact scheme $X$ over a ring $R$ with a nilpotent divided power ideal, in degrees one and two. Sections 7 and 8 build free divided power thickenings and coproducts with an affine lift, Section 9 shows that restriction to an affine lift is bijective in degrees one and two, and Section 10 gives the comparison isomorphisms with cup products and naturality. The chapter needs from Volume 1 the [rings, ideals and modules](/learn/algebra/#rings-ideals-and-modules).

The first part proves [the theorem on prismatic Dieudonné modules](/entry/prismatic-dieudonne-hodge/), which is a literature input of the proof ([step](/step/cm-abelian-inputs-from-literature/)). The second part proves [the comparison of crystalline and de Rham cohomology](/entry/crystalline-smooth-lift-of-relations/), which the files on abelian schemes use below the step on characters ([step](/step/every-character/)). The cohomology in the second part is the cohomology of a category of thickenings, not of the crystalline site.
