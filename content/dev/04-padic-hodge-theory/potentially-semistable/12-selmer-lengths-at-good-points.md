## Selmer lengths at good points and the signature choice

This section compares two Selmer lengths. The first is the length of the Selmer group $\mathrm{Sel}_\pi$ of a point $\pi$ of a Hecke algebra, as in [Theorem 6.2.2](/entry/selmer-bound-at-point-main/). The second is the length of a Selmer group of a twist of a representation that comes from a signature in the twisting data. We show that at every good point there is a good signature whose twisted Selmer length bounds $\lambda(\mathrm{Sel}_\pi)$, and that the tame local terms of the bound are controlled by a choice of characters. This is the signature choice condition, an input of the step [EveryCharacter from theta integrals and local matching](/step/theta-local-matching/). The key steps are Chebotarev density and the fact that a residually absolutely irreducible representation over a discrete valuation ring is fixed up to conjugation by its trace. We use [[Selmer groups|36YNSTESMQ]] and the [[Galois cohomology|9MPCZ3EAJM]] of [the Background](/learn/galois-cohomology/#selmer-and-tate-shafarevich-groups).

We keep the notation of the section on the Selmer bound at a point, and $\lambda$ is the normalised length there. A datum has a finite set of *local indices* $a$. Each $a$ comes with the data of that section: a prime $\ell_a$, a ring $\mathcal{O}_a$, a finite set $X_a$ of points with subsets $Z^{\mathrm{good}}_a \subseteq Z_a \subseteq X_a$, a finite set $Q_a$ of places with the elements $\zeta_{a,v}$, characters $\chi_{a,1}, \dots, \chi_{a,t}$ and a place $w_a$. The *twisting data* give *twisting indices* $j$, each with a prime $\ell_j$, rings $\mathcal{O}_{0,j} \to \mathcal{O}_j \to \mathcal{O}_{j,k}$ for $k \in \mathbb{N}$ and a set of *signatures* $s$. A signature has a weight $j(s)$, a side $\epsilon(s) \in \{+, -\}$ and a representation $\rho_s : G_E \to \mathrm{GL}_r(\mathcal{O}_{0,j})$. Each local index $a$ has a twisting index $j_a$, a side $\epsilon_a$, a map $\tau_a : \mathcal{O}_{0, j_a} \to \mathcal{O}_a$ and a character $\psi_a : G_E \to \mathcal{O}_a^\times$, trivial on the decomposition groups at the places above $\ell_{j_a}$. For a side $\epsilon$ and a character $\psi$ put $\psi^\epsilon = \psi$ if $\epsilon = +$ and $\psi^\epsilon = \psi^{-1}$ if $\epsilon = -$.

For a signature $s$ of $j$, $k \in \mathbb{N}$, a finite set $Q$ of places and a character $\psi : G_E \to \mathcal{O}_{j,k}^\times$, let $\mathrm{Sel}(s, k, Q, \psi)$ be the Bloch-Kato Selmer group of $\rho_s$ twisted by $\psi$ with coefficients in $\mathcal{O}_{j,k}$, with conditions at the places above $\ell_j$, at the auxiliary places and at $Q$. Let $\mathrm{sLen}(s, k, Q, \psi) \in [0, \infty]$ be its length divided by $v(\ell_j)$. A signature is *good* if a nonzero automorphic vector of its weight and side is fixed by the given group algebra element and has the given Hecke eigenvalues at the split places outside a finite set.

**Definition 6.12.1 (Chebotarev condition).** The *Chebotarev condition* says: for every number field $F$, every open normal subgroup $U$ of $G_F$, every $g \in G_F$ and every finite set $A$ of finite places of $F$, there is a place $v \notin A$ of degree one and unramified over $\mathbb{Q}$, and a place $B$ of $\Omega$ above $v$ whose inertia group lies in $U$, with a Frobenius $\sigma$ at $B$ such that $\sigma g^{-1} \in U$.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.TwSys.Cheb`.

This is the Chebotarev density theorem in the form used here.

**Definition 6.12.2 (Point representation).** Let $\pi \in X_a$. A continuous $\rho : G_E \to \mathrm{GL}_r(\mathcal{O}_a)$ is a *point representation at $\pi$* if there is a Hecke-Galois representation $r : G_E \to \mathrm{GL}_{n_V}(\mathcal{O}_a)$ of $\pi$ with $r \otimes L_a \cong (\rho \otimes L_a) \oplus \chi_{a,1} \oplus \cdots \oplus \chi_{a,t}$.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.IndefiniteRowSideFullHecke.LatticeConditions.IsRhoPt`.

**Definition 6.12.3 (Same character).** Let $f_1 : \mathcal{O}_0 \to \mathcal{O}_1$ and $f_2 : \mathcal{O}_0 \to \mathcal{O}_2$ be ring maps and $\psi_i : \Gamma \to \mathcal{O}_i^\times$ characters. Then $(f_1, \psi_1)$ and $(f_2, \psi_2)$ *give the same character* if for every finitely supported $F : \Gamma \to \mathcal{O}_0$,
$$\sum_g f_1(F(g))\, \psi_1(g) = 0 \iff \sum_g f_2(F(g))\, \psi_2(g) = 0.$$

Lean: `GlobalTheory.IndefiniteRowSideFullHecke.LatticeConditions.SameChar`.

With $F = \delta_g - \delta_1$ we see: if $\psi_1(g) = 1$, then $\psi_2(g) = 1$.

**Definition 6.12.4 (Conditions on the datum).** The datum *has good triples* if, for every local index $a$, every row type $\sigma$ that is good for the weight of $a$ and every list $\Sigma$ of parameters of $\sigma$ at $a$, there is a good signature $s$ of $j_a$ such that $(a, s, \Sigma)$ is a *triple*: $j(s)$ is the weight of $a$, $\epsilon(s) = \epsilon_a$, $\rho_s$ is semisimple over the fraction field, and at almost all split places the characteristic polynomial of Frobenius on $\rho_s$, mapped by $\tau_a$, is the Galois polynomial of the twisted parameter. It *has good rows at points* if every $\pi \in Z^{\mathrm{good}}_a$ has a good row type $\sigma$ and a list $\Sigma$ of parameters of $\sigma$ at $a$ that are also parameters of $\pi$. It is *residually absolutely irreducible* if every point representation at every $\pi \in Z^{\mathrm{good}}_a$ is residually absolutely irreducible. A signature $s$ is *attached* to $\pi$ if some $\sigma$ and $\Sigma$ as above make $(a, s, \Sigma)$ a triple.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.PtCore.TriplesGood` (also `GlobalTheory.PtCore.GoodPtRow`, `GlobalTheory.PtCore.ResAbsIrr` and `GlobalTheory.PtCore.AttachedIn`).

**Lemma 6.12.5 (Selmer length at a good point).** Assume the Chebotarev condition, and that the datum has good triples, good rows at points and is residually absolutely irreducible. Let $a$ be a local index, $\rho_x$ for $x \in X_a$ a family, and $\pi \in Z^{\mathrm{good}}_a$ with $\rho_\pi$ a point representation at $\pi$. Let $j = j_a$, $k \in \mathbb{N}$ and $\psi : G_E \to \mathcal{O}_{j,k}^\times$, and assume that $(\tau_a, \psi_a)$ and $(\mathcal{O}_{0,j} \to \mathcal{O}_{j,k}, \psi)$ give the same character. Then there is a good signature $s$ of $j$, attached to $\pi$, with $\epsilon(s) = \epsilon_a$, such that:

1. if $\mathrm{sLen}(s, k, Q_a, \psi) < \infty$, then $\mathrm{Sel}_\pi$ has finite length;
2. $\lambda(\mathrm{Sel}_\pi) \leq \mathrm{sLen}(s, k, Q_a, \psi)$.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.PtSel.sel_at`.

*Proof.* By good rows at points, $\pi$ has a good row type $\sigma$ and a list $\Sigma$ of parameters shared with $\pi$. By good triples there is a good signature $s$ such that $(a, s, \Sigma)$ is a triple; so $s$ is attached to $\pi$ and $\epsilon(s) = \epsilon_a$. The next step is a result proved elsewhere in this development, which uses the Chebotarev condition. Since $(a, s, \Sigma)$ is a triple, the traces of $\rho_\pi$ and of $\tau_a(\rho_s)$ agree on $G_E$. Since $\rho_\pi$ is residually absolutely irreducible, the two representations are conjugate over $\mathcal{O}_a$. So there is $U \in \mathrm{GL}_r(\mathcal{O}_a)$ that conjugates the coefficient representation $W_\pi$ of $\mathrm{Sel}_\pi$ into the lattice of $\tau_a(\rho_s)$ twisted by $\psi_a^{-1}$. Hence $\mathrm{Sel}_\pi$ has the same length as the Selmer group of that twisted lattice. Since the two pairs give the same character, the Selmer group of the twisted lattice has the same length as the one with coefficients $\mathcal{O}_{j,k}$ and the character $\psi$. The character $\psi_a$ is trivial on the decomposition groups at the places $v$ above $\ell_j$, so $\psi$ is trivial there too, by the remark after the definition of the same character. Under this triviality, a third result proved elsewhere bounds that length by $\mathrm{sLen}(s, k, Q_a, \psi)$, and gives finiteness when $\mathrm{sLen}$ is finite. This proves 1. For 2, if $\mathrm{Sel}_\pi$ has infinite length, then by 1 $\mathrm{sLen} = \infty$ and the bound holds. Otherwise the bound is the comparison of lengths. $\square$

**Definition 6.12.6 (Choice of characters).** A *choice of characters* assigns to each place $w$ of the global data a twisting index $j(w)$ with $\ell_{j(w)}$ the residue characteristic of $w$, a side $\epsilon(w)$, a finite set $Q(w)$ of places, $k(w) \in \mathbb{N}$ and a character $\psi'_w$ of a ray class group with values in $\mathcal{O}_{j(w), k(w)}^\times$. Put $\psi_{G,w} = \psi'_w \circ \mathrm{Art}$ and $\psi_{W,w} = (\psi'_w)^{\epsilon(w)} \circ \mathrm{Art}$. The choice is *tied* to the datum if $j_a = j(w_a)$, $\epsilon_a = \epsilon(w_a)$ and $Q_a = Q(w_a)$ for every local index $a$, the characters $\psi_a$ and $\psi'_{w} \circ \mathrm{Art}$ come from one family of characters with values in the global coefficient field, invariant under conjugation of the places, and a compatibility of embeddings of the base field holds. It has the *same twist* if, for every $a$, $j_a = j(w_a)$ and $(\tau_a, \psi_a)$ and $(\mathcal{O}_{0,j_a} \to \mathcal{O}_{j_a, k(w_a)}, \psi_{G, w_a})$ give the same character.

Lean: `Formalism.BasicNotions.NChoice` (also `Formalism.BasicNotions.NChTie` and `GlobalTheory.PtCore.SameTwist`).

**Theorem 6.12.7 (Selmer core).** Assume the Chebotarev condition. Let a choice of characters be tied to the datum and have the same twist, and assume that the datum has good triples, good rows at points and is residually absolutely irreducible. Let $\rho_x$, for each $a$ and $x \in X_a$, be a family such that $\rho_\pi$ is a point representation at $\pi$ for every $\pi \in Z^{\mathrm{good}}_a$. Then for every $a$ and $\pi \in Z^{\mathrm{good}}_a$ there is a good signature $s$ of $j(w_a)$, attached to $\pi$, with $\epsilon(s) = \epsilon(w_a)$, such that, with $\mathrm{sLen} = \mathrm{sLen}(s, k(w_a), Q(w_a), \psi_{G, w_a})$, the group $\mathrm{Sel}_\pi$ has finite length if $\mathrm{sLen} < \infty$, and $\lambda(\mathrm{Sel}_\pi) \leq \mathrm{sLen}$.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.PtSel.selCore`.

*Proof.* Fix $a$ and $\pi$. The tie gives $j_a = j(w_a)$ and $\epsilon_a = \epsilon(w_a)$, and the same twist gives the hypothesis of the Lemma with $k = k(w_a)$ and $\psi = \psi_{G, w_a}$. The Lemma gives $s$ with the bound for $Q_a$, and $Q_a = Q(w_a)$ by the tie. $\square$

**Definition 6.12.8 (Signature choice condition).** A choice of characters satisfies the *signature choice condition* for a family $\rho$ if, for every local index $a$ with a twist at $w_a$:

1. for every $\pi \in Z^{\mathrm{good}}_a$ there is a good signature $s$ of $j(w_a)$ such that, with $\mathrm{sLen} = \mathrm{sLen}(s, k(w_a), Q(w_a), \psi_{W, w_a}^{\epsilon(s)})$, the group $\mathrm{Sel}_\pi$ has finite length if $\mathrm{sLen} < \infty$, and $\lambda(\mathrm{Sel}_\pi) \leq \mathrm{sLen}$;
2. $\displaystyle\sum_{v \in Q_a} \lambda\big(\mathcal{O}_a/(\zeta_{a,v} - 1)\big) \leq \sum_{P \in Q(w_a)} t(\psi_{W, w_a}, P)$, where $t(\psi, P)$ is the infimum of $v(\psi(\sigma) - 1)/v(\ell)$ over the $\sigma$ in an inertia group at $P$ with $\psi(\sigma) \neq 1$, and $t(\psi, P) = 0$ if there is no such $\sigma$. Here $v$ is the valuation of the coefficient ring of $\psi$ and $\ell = \ell_{j(w_a)}$.

Lean: `Formalism.MainNotions.N.KillingConditions.SignatureChoiceCondition` (also `Formalism.MainNotions.BlochKatoReduction.Gen.tameVal`, the number $t$).

**Theorem 6.12.9 (Signature choice condition).** Assume the Chebotarev condition. Let $r_x$ and $\rho_x$ be families of representations such that, at every point of $Z_a$, $\rho_x$ is a continuous complement of the characters in $r_x$ and $r_x$ satisfies the conditions on Hecke-Galois families. Let a choice of characters be tied to the datum and have the same twist, and assume that the datum has good triples, good rows at points and is residually absolutely irreducible. Then the choice satisfies the signature choice condition for $\rho$.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.JointSix.main`.

*Proof.* At every point of $Z_a$, the splitting of $r_x$ makes $\rho_x$ a point representation, by a result proved elsewhere in this development; in particular this holds on $Z^{\mathrm{good}}_a \subseteq Z_a$. So the Selmer core Theorem applies. Let $a$ be a local index and $\pi \in Z^{\mathrm{good}}_a$, and take $s$ from it, with $\epsilon(s) = \epsilon(w_a)$. Then
$$\psi_{W, w_a}^{\epsilon(s)} = \big((\psi'_{w_a})^{\epsilon(w_a)} \circ \mathrm{Art}\big)^{\epsilon(w_a)} = \psi'_{w_a} \circ \mathrm{Art} = \psi_{G, w_a},$$
since taking the inverse twice gives back the character. This gives 1. For 2, $Q_a = Q(w_a)$ by the tie, and the sum is bounded term by term: for $v \in Q_a$, $\lambda(\mathcal{O}_a/(\zeta_{a,v} - 1)) \leq t(\psi_{W, w_a}, v)$ by a result proved elsewhere, which uses the tie. $\square$

**Remark 6.12.10 (Formalization).** The conclusion of the last Theorem quantifies over the local indices $a$ with `TwLoc D a`, a twist at $w_a$; the intro at [PsiAtPsiAt.lean](lean:Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/InertiaEigenvalues/PsiAtPsiAt.lean#L28) discards this hypothesis, so both parts hold at every local index. Of the family hypothesis `PtFam` only the field used by `PtFam.pt` matters, and $r$ is not used otherwise. The file ends with an `example` at [line 39](lean:Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/InertiaEigenvalues/PsiAtPsiAt.lean#L39) that restates the theorem and is proved by `main`; it adds nothing. The identity $\psi_{W}^{\epsilon} = \psi_G$ is `psiAt_psiW`, and the termwise bound is `GlobalTheory.T.conductorRoot_sub_one_length_le_tameValue`, a second name of `GlobalTheory.CondTieChoice.conductor_le_tameVal`, which we did not open. The Lemma uses `GlobalTheory.PtCore.twistedRho_conj_latTpsi` (from `GlobalTheory.T.trace_rankRPart_eq_trace_baseChangeTriple` and `GlobalTheory.T.exists_conj_of_trace_eq_residually_absIrreducible_DVR`), `GlobalTheory.ConjLatticeSelmer.sPiTop_eq_sTLoc`, `GlobalTheory.SelmerSameChar.sTLoc_eq`, `sameChar_eq_one` and `GlobalTheory.SelTwistLe.sT_le_sLen`; we read their statements and the first proof, but not the proofs of the rest. The Selmer group of a signature is `selT`, with the length `lenN` in $[0, \infty]$, while $\lambda(\mathrm{Sel}_\pi)$ is the rational `sPi`, computed by `Module.length (...).toNat`; this is why part 1 is needed for part 2. We did not unfold `goodE`, `HasParamW`, `HasParamX`, `GoodRowOf`, `IsHeckeGalois`, `GoodR`, `IsComplement` or the Selmer conditions `bk`, so the definitions above give them by their role. The number $t$ is an `sInf` of a set of reals, so it is $0$ when $\psi$ is trivial on the inertia group; the convention in the definition records this. The field `psi_eq` of the tie, the compatibility of the characters, is used only inside `conductor_le_tameVal`.
