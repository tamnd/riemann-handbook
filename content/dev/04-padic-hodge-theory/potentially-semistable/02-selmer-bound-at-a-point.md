## The Selmer bound at a point

This section proves that the congruence length of a point of a Hecke algebra is bounded by the length of a Bloch-Kato [[Selmer group|36YNSTESMQ]], by Tate-Shafarevich terms, by the $\ell$-part of the class number and by explicit local terms. The bound is an input of the step [EveryCharacter from theta integrals and local matching](/step/theta-local-matching/). The proof adds two finiteness statements and the local facts of [Theorem 6.1.5](/entry/local-duality-class-field-facts-of-artin-tate-euler/) to the inputs of the closed form of the bound, [Theorem 2.1 of Volume 15](/entry/theorem-n5-from-inputs/), which does the main work. The Selmer groups are those of [the Background](/learn/galois-cohomology/#selmer-and-tate-shafarevich-groups).

We fix the following data. Let $E$ be a number field with algebraic closure $\Omega$, $G_E = \mathrm{Gal}(\Omega/E)$, and let $g_0$, $g_1$ be two fixed primes. Let $\ell$ be a prime, and let $\mathcal{O}$ be a complete discrete valuation ring, finite over $\mathbb{Z}_\ell$, with fraction field $L$. Let $X$ be a finite set, $\mathbb{T} \subseteq \mathcal{O}^X$ an $\mathcal{O}$-subalgebra (the Hecke algebra), and $Z \subseteq X$ a subset. Let $S$ and $Q$ be finite sets of finite places of $E$. Let $\chi_1, \dots, \chi_t : G_E \to \mathcal{O}^\times$ be characters, fix for each $v \in S \cup Q$ an element $\gamma_v$ of inertia, and put $\zeta_v = \chi_1(\gamma_v)$. Let $s_0$ be a natural number. Finally let $r_x : G_E \to \mathrm{GL}_{n_V}(\mathcal{O})$ and $\rho_x : G_E \to \mathrm{GL}_r(\mathcal{O})$, for $x \in X$, be two families of representations, and let $\pi \in X$.

**Definition 6.2.1 (Congruence length).** For an $\mathcal{O}$-module $M$ of finite length put $\lambda(M) = \mathrm{length}_{\mathcal{O}}(M)/\mathrm{length}_{\mathcal{O}}(\mathcal{O}/\ell)$, so that $\lambda(\mathcal{O}/\ell^k) = k$. The *congruence ideal* of $\pi$ is
$$\eta_\pi = \{ f(\pi) : f \in \mathbb{T},\ f(y) = 0 \text{ for all } y \notin Z \} \subseteq \mathcal{O},$$
and the *congruence length* of $\pi$ is $v_{cc}(\pi) = \lambda(\mathcal{O}/\eta_\pi)$.

If $\pi \in Z$, then $\eta_\pi$ measures how far the eigensystem $\pi$ is congruent to eigensystems outside $Z$. If $\mathbb{T} = \mathcal{O}^X$, then $\eta_\pi = \mathcal{O}$ for $\pi \in Z$ and $v_{cc}(\pi) = 0$: there are no congruences. The bound below says that congruences force classes in a Selmer group or in the local terms.

The places of $E$ above $\ell$ are split into two disjoint sets, the *row places* and the *column places*. At the row places one has a local length bound $\mathrm{len}_{\mathrm{row}}(w) \in \mathbb{Q}$, and at the column places a lift exponent $e'(w) \in \mathbb{N}$. Put $W_\pi = \rho_\pi \otimes \chi_1^{-1}$ with coefficients $L/\mathcal{O}$, and let $\mathrm{Sel}_\pi \subseteq H^1(G_E, W_\pi)$ be its Bloch-Kato Selmer group, with conditions at $\ell$, $S$ and $Q$. For a place $w$ above $\ell$, let $d_w = [E_w : \mathbb{Q}_\ell]$ and let $h_{0,w}$ be $\lambda$ of the invariants of the decomposition group in $W_\pi$. Let $h_0$ be the largest $\lambda$ of the invariants of a decomposition group at a column place in $(L/\mathcal{O})(e)$, for $0 < e < t$. Put
$$\delta = \sum_{0 < k < t} \big(s_0 + v_\ell(k)\big).$$
At a column place $w$ put $\mathrm{len}_w = e'(w) + j_w(e'(w) + \delta) + 2\delta + h_{0,w}$, where $j_w$ is a local integer attached to $W_\pi$ at $w$. At a row place put $\mathrm{len}_w = \mathrm{len}_{\mathrm{row}}(w)$. Let $\text{Ш}(e)$ be the Tate-Shafarevich group of the twist by the $e$-th power of the cyclotomic character, and $h_E$ the class number of $E$.

**Theorem 6.2.2 (Selmer bound at a point).** Assume global Artin reciprocity, local Tate duality and the local Euler characteristic formula. Assume the following conditions on the data.

1. The coefficients of the reverse characteristic polynomial of $r_x(g)$, as functions of $x$, lie in $\mathbb{T}$ for every $g \in G_E$.
2. Each $r_x \otimes L$ satisfies an absolute irreducibility condition. Residually, the characteristic polynomial of $r_x$ is that of $\bar\rho_\pi$ times $\prod_{c=1}^{t} (X - \bar\chi_c)$, and the image of $\bar\rho_\pi$ spans the matrices over the residue field.
3. Each $r_x$ is continuous, and unramified outside $\ell$, $S$ and $Q$.
4. At each place $v$ of $S \cup Q$, the element $\gamma_v$ is a tame generator of inertia, the characters $\chi_c$ agree with $\chi_1$ on inertia, and $\zeta_v \neq 1$ if $v \in Q$. At the places of $Q$ and at some places of $S$, inertia acts on $r_x \otimes L$ by a matrix conjugate to $\mathrm{diag}(1, \dots, 1, \chi_1, \dots, \chi_1)$ with $r$ entries $1$.
5. If $r_x \otimes L$ has a nonzero vector on which $G_E$ acts by $\chi_1$, then $x \in Z$.
6. $r_\pi \otimes L \cong \rho_\pi \otimes L \oplus \chi_1 \oplus \cdots \oplus \chi_t$ as representations of $G_E$.
7. At the places above $\ell$: crystalline and gain conditions on the twists of $r_x$, Bloch-Kato dimension and annihilator conditions, Fontaine's conditions, finiteness of local $H^1$, compatibility of the local coefficient fields, and a congruence object condition at $\pi$.
8. At the column places the unramified part of $H^1_f$ is trivial, and the lift condition holds with exponent $e'(w)$.
9. At the row places the length condition holds with $\mathrm{len}_{\mathrm{row}}(w)$, and the local torsion bound holds with a constant $c_B$.

Assume also $\ell \neq g_0$, $\ell \neq g_1$, $\pi \in Z$, $\mathrm{Sel}_\pi$ has finite length, and $\text{Ш}(e)$ has finite length for $0 < e < t$. Then
$$\begin{aligned} v_{cc}(\pi) \leq {}& \sum_{w \mid \ell} \mathrm{len}_w + \lambda(\mathrm{Sel}_\pi) + \sum_{0 < e < t} \lambda(\text{Ш}(e)) + v_\ell(h_E) \\ &+ t \, c_B \sum_{w \in \mathrm{row}} (d_w + 2) + 4 n_V \big(|\mathrm{col}| + 1\big) \delta \\ &+ t \, |\mathrm{col}| \, h_0 + t \sum_{v \in Q} \lambda\big(\mathcal{O}/(\zeta_v - 1)\big). \end{aligned}$$

*Proof.* The three published inputs give the thirteen local facts, by [Theorem 6.1.5](/entry/local-duality-class-field-facts-of-artin-tate-euler/). Two finiteness statements, proved elsewhere in this development, hold. For each place $w$ above $\ell$, given the crystalline conditions of 7, the invariants of the decomposition group in $W_\pi$ have finite length. For $e > 0$, the invariants of the decomposition group in $(L/\mathcal{O})(e)$ have finite length. The second holds because the cyclotomic character has infinite image on the decomposition group, so the invariants of $(L/\mathcal{O})(e)$ are killed by $\chi^e(g) - 1 \neq 0$ for some $g$. The triviality of the unramified part of $H^1_f$ in 8 is the unramified input in the form of the closed theorem. With these, the bound is [Theorem 2.1 of Volume 15](/entry/theorem-n5-from-inputs/) applied to the remaining hypotheses. $\square$
