This chapter is about the crystalline and de Rham period rings of $\mathbb{Q}_\ell$. The chapter title refers to the map $\theta$, not to theta series. The proof builds $B_{\mathrm{cris}}$, $B_{\mathrm{dR}}^+$ and $B_{\mathrm{dR}}$ twice, from two definitions of the ring of integers of $\mathbb{C}_\ell$ and with two ways of inverting the period $t = \log[\epsilon]$. The chapter proves that the two constructions are isomorphic, compatibly with the Galois action, the Frobenius, $\theta$, the filtration, the logarithm and $A_{\mathrm{inf}}$. It deduces that abstract period data tied to the first construction are also tied to the second. Then it collects forty basic properties of $B_{\mathrm{cris}}$ in the second construction: the action of the period $t$ and of the Kummer period $u$, the valuation of $B_{\mathrm{dR}}$, the fundamental exact sequence, the Galois invariants of $B_{\mathrm{cris}}$, and the fact that the Kummer cocycle has no period in $B_{\mathrm{cris}}$. The proof of the last fact is where the transport of period data is used.

The chapter needs from Volume 1 the [period rings](/learn/galois-representations/#period-rings) with the tilt, $A_{\mathrm{inf}}$ and $\theta$, and the [cyclotomic character](/learn/galois-representations/#the-cyclotomic-character-and-tate-modules). Its results feed [the local steps](/entry/steps-holds/), the crystalline, Hodge-Tate and Frobenius steps for the cohomology of a tower. These are used in the step [geometric realization of weight one](/step/geo-one-holds/) of [the Galois row](/proof/galois-row/).
