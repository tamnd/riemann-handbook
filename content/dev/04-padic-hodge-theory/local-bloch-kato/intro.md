This chapter is about Galois cohomology at one prime $\ell$, with coefficients in lattices of crystalline representations. Its first part proves the *exact local conditions*: for a crystalline lattice $T_0$ with prescribed Hodge-Tate jumps, twisted by a crystalline character, the cohomology classes whose extensions are *admissible* (quotients of lattices in crystalline representations with given jumps and given residual semisimplification) are exactly the classes of the Bloch-Kato finite subgroup $H^1_f$. For the twisting characters the admissible classes are zero or unramified. The proof uses local Tate duality and the local Euler characteristic formula, the Bloch-Kato dimension formula for $H^1_f$, an operator $\psi$ on $(\varphi, \Gamma)$-modules over $k((X))$, and a reduction of Wach modules modulo $\lambda$. The same part proves Theorems B and C, two bounds on the colength of the map that a surjection of crystalline lattices induces on the finite parts of $H^1$, and an identity for the congruence lengths $\eta_j$ at twisted places.

The chapter needs from Volume 1 the [continuous cohomology](/learn/galois-cohomology/#continuous-cohomology) of a profinite group, the cohomology of [local fields](/learn/galois-cohomology/#local-fields) with Tate duality and the Euler characteristic, the [period rings](/learn/galois-representations/#period-rings), [crystalline representations](/learn/padic-hodge/#crystalline-and-semistable-representations), [Wach modules](/learn/padic-hodge/#lattices-wach-breuil-kisin-and-prismatic-f-crystals) and [local Bloch-Kato theory](/learn/padic-hodge/#local-bloch-kato-theory). Its results feed three theorems of the third-place series of the theta lifts: [Theorem 8.9.2 of Volume 15](/entry/nlink-e-main-alt-alt/), [the inert step](/entry/inert-step-main-alt-alt/) and [the $\eta_j$ identity with local data](/entry/eta-j-with-local-data-eq-alt/). These are used in the step [the hypotheses at the third place of the unitary group](/step/unitary-group-third-place-hypotheses-def/) of [the unitary hypotheses](/proof/unitary-hypotheses/).
