## Level steps and the arc data at a cut

The final form of Theorem E is a statement about modules over $\mathcal{O}[[X]]$ with no Galois group in sight. Its last hypothesis asks, at each cut, for *arc data*: a model of the levels $s = 0, 1, \dots, \ell_c - 1$ of the arc for each of the two lattices, and a map between the models. This section builds the arc data for a map of crystalline lattices that is onto. The result is used in [the lifting theorem for crystalline lattices](/entry/crystalline-lifting-second-form/), through [the local lifting theorem from local inputs](/entry/local-theorem-of-inputs/), and it feeds [the final form of Theorem E](/entry/theorem-e-final-form/).

We keep the [Wach datum](/entry/theorem-of-local-lemmas/) at $(\ell, K, L)$, in case A, with $p = \ell$, $q = \pi_0 + p$, and the [kinds of letters](/entry/split-case-a-final/).

**Definition 8.4.1 (Coordinates of a lattice).** Let $T$ be a lattice. *Coordinates* of $T$ are an $\mathcal{O}[[X]]$-module $V$ with, for each letter $i$, an additive bijection $\theta_i$ from $V$ onto the part of $D(T)$ at the letter $i$, such that $\theta_i(a x)$ is the image of $a$ in $\Lambda$ times $\theta_i(x)$, for $a \in \mathcal{O}[[X]]$. A *map of coordinates* over a map of lattices $F \colon T_1 \to T_2$ is an $\mathcal{O}[[X]]$-linear map $V_1 \to V_2$ compatible with the maps $D(T_1) \to D(T_2)$.

Coordinates turn the Wach module at a letter into a module over the single ring $\mathcal{O}[[X]]$. Write $N_a \subseteq V$ for the preimage of the Wach module at the letter $a$.

**Definition 8.4.2 (Arc data at a cut).** Let $h \colon V_1 \to V_2$ be a map of coordinates, $c$ a cut with length $\ell_c$, and $H \colon \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$. Put $c' = c - \ell_c$ and $\Lambda_H = \prod_{s < \ell_c} \varphi^s(q)^{H(s)}$. Fix a uniformizer $\varpi$ of $\mathcal{O}$, the unit $u = \chi_\Gamma(\gamma)$, numbers $j, M, g, G_0, S, \eta$, and for each step $s < \ell_c$ a kind and a size $b_s$. *Arc data* consist of, for $k = 1, 2$:

1. level data for $V_k$ at $c$ with the element $\Lambda_H$, and a level model with an $L$-vector space $D_k$, a filtration $\mathrm{Fil}_k(s)$ at each step $s$, and level maps at each step;
2. vectors $e_{k,1}, \dots, e_{k,r_k}$ of $V_k$ and $\lambda_{k,1}, \dots, \lambda_{k,r_k}$ of $D_k$, and submodules $U_k(s) \subseteq \mathcal{O}^{r_k}$;

together with a level map from the first model to the second, with a linear part $P \colon \mathcal{O}^{r_1} \to \mathcal{O}^{r_2}$. The arc data are *final* if:

1. for each $k$ the models have the given kinds, sizes and $\eta$; each $\mathrm{Fil}_k(s)$ is a filtration whose jumps lie in the window $W(s)$ below; the vectors $e_k$ form a basis of $N_{c'}$ over $\mathcal{O}[[X]]$; the $\lambda_k$ form a basis of $D_k$ over $L$; and $v \in U_k(s)$ if and only if $\sum_m v_m \lambda_{k,m} \in U(s)$, where $U(s)$ is $D_k$, $\mathrm{Fil}_k(s)^{b_s}$ or $\mathrm{Fil}_k(s)^{-\eta}$ for $s$ thin, up or down;
2. the level maps at each step fix the elements of finite order, commute with $\varphi$ and $\Gamma$, and satisfy conditions on units, completeness for $\varpi$, membership, local regularity, the localization of constants and an equivalence;
3. the level map satisfies its filtration condition, and at each step of kind up, $P$ maps $U_1(s)$ to $U_2(s)$ as required;
4. for each $m$, $h(e_{1,m}) - \sum_l P_{lm} e_{2,l} \in X N_{c'}(V_2)$.

Here a jump of a filtration $\mathrm{Fil}$ is an integer $u$ with $\mathrm{Fil}^u \ne \mathrm{Fil}^{u+1}$, and the window of the step $s$ is
$$W(s) = \begin{cases} [-G_0, G_0] & s \text{ thin}, \\ [b_s, b_s + S] \cup [-\eta, \eta] & s \text{ up}, \\ [-b_s - S, -b_s] \cup [-\eta, \eta] & s \text{ down}. \end{cases}$$
The last condition says that $P$ is the matrix of $h$ in the two bases, read modulo $X$.

**Lemma 8.4.3 (Level steps from jumps).** Let $T$ be a crystalline lattice with coordinates $V$, let $c$ be a cut with length $\ell_c \le n$, and let $m$ bound the numbers $M(i)$ attached to the jumps of $T$ at the letters $i$. Assume the level hypotheses for $V$ at $c$ with the element $\Lambda_H$ and the element $\kappa = \Lambda_H \prod_{s < \ell_c} \varphi^s(q)^m$. Assume the Wach properties P3, P4, P5 and P9 for $T$, the level maps on the Hodge filtration, the saturation property, and the compatibility of the level maps with $\varphi$, $\Gamma$ and the comparison map. Assume that $\mathcal{O}$ is a complete discrete valuation ring, $\varpi$ is irreducible in $\mathcal{O}$, $v \ge 0$ on $\mathcal{O}$, $p$ lies in the maximal ideal, $L$ contains a primitive $p^n$-th root of unity, $K_0 \to K$ is a bijection, $s = 1$ and $p \ge 5$. Assume the identities
$$\begin{aligned} q &= w \cdot \Phi_p(1 + X) \text{ for a unit } w \in \mathcal{O}[[X]]^\times, \\ \varphi_0(X) &= (1 + X)^p - 1, \\ 1 + \rho_\zeta(X) &= \zeta \exp(X) \text{ for } \zeta \text{ a primitive } p^{s+1}\text{-th root of unity}, s < \ell_c, \end{aligned}$$
with $\Phi_p$ the $p$-th cyclotomic polynomial, and that a unit $u \in \mathbb{Z}_p^\times$ with $v_p(u - 1) = 1$ maps to $\chi_\Gamma(\gamma)$, and that the Teichmüller set $\Delta$ maps onto $(\mathbb{Z}/p)^\times$. Let $e$ be a basis of $N_{c'}$ over $\mathcal{O}[[X]]$, $j \le \ell_c$ and $3g \le (p - 1) M$. If at each step $s < \ell_c$ the jumps of the filtration of $D(T)$ at $(c, s)$ lie in the window $W(s)$, then the level steps hold: the level model of $V$ satisfies conditions 1 and 2 of final arc data.

Lean: `Formalism.MainNotions.N.Ln03.Pf.levSteps_of_jumps'`.

*Proof.* Since $p \ge 5$ we have $p \ne 2$, and since $K_0 \to K$ is a bijection, $[K : K_0] = 1$. With $s = 1$, property P9 then says that the Wach module $N(T)$ is free over the ring of letters, with a basis in $N_\Delta(T)$, and that $(\gamma - 1) N_\Delta(T) \subseteq \pi_0 N_\Delta(T)$. Through the coordinates this makes each $N_a$ free. The ring steps follow from the identities for $q$, $\varphi_0$ and $\rho_\zeta$, the unit $u$, the root of unity and the Teichmüller set. Since $T$ is crystalline, $D_{\mathrm{cris}}(T)$ is finite-dimensional over $L$, so its filtrations are finite. Property P5 with the bound $m$ gives a local form of P5 at every letter. The membership condition comes from the level maps on the Hodge filtration, the saturation property, P3 and the freeness. The localization of constants comes from the compatibilities of the level maps with $\varphi$, $\Gamma$ and the comparison map, P4, $j \le \ell_c$ and $3g \le (p - 1)M$. With these steps, the level steps follow from the jump condition. $\square$

**Theorem 8.4.4 (Arc data at one cut).** Let $F \colon T_1 \to T_2$ be a map of crystalline lattices that is onto, with a map of coordinates $h \colon V_1 \to V_2$ over $F$. Assume the hypotheses of the last lemma that do not refer to a single lattice, the equality $D_{\mathrm{dR}} = D_{\mathrm{cris}}$, and that every jump of $T_2$ is a jump of $T_1$ for every map onto. Let the jumps of $T_1$ at each letter $i$ lie in the window of a kind $\mathrm{ty}(i)$ for $G_0, S, \eta$. Let $c$ be a cut with $\ell_c \le n$, $H \colon \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ any function, $j \le \ell_c$ and $3 g \le (p-1) M$. Let $e_1, e_2$ be bases of $N_{c'}(V_1)$ and $N_{c'}(V_2)$, and let $P$ be the matrix of constant terms of $h$ in these bases. Then final arc data exist at $c$ for $H$, with the kinds and sizes of the letters $c - s$ and with this $P$.

Lean: `Formalism.MainNotions.N.Ln03.Pf.finalArc_holds`.

*Proof.* Since $D_{\mathrm{dR}} = D_{\mathrm{cris}}$ and $K_0 = K$, the jumps of each $T_k$ are bounded, by numbers $m_k$. The level hypotheses for $V_k$ at $c$ follow, with the element $\Lambda_H \prod_{s < \ell_c} \varphi^s(q)^{m_k}$. The level steps for $V_1$ follow from [the lemma on level steps](/entry/lev-steps-of-jumps-prime/), since the jumps of $T_1$ lie in the windows. For $V_2$ the same lemma applies: $F$ is onto, so the jumps of $T_2$ are jumps of $T_1$ and lie in the same windows. The map steps follow from the two level hypotheses and the matrix $P$. The level steps and the map steps together are final arc data. $\square$

**Theorem 8.4.5 (Realisation of the arc data).** Assume the compatibilities of the level maps with $\varphi$, $\Gamma$ and the comparison map, $s = 1$, the Wach properties P3, P4, P5 and P9, the saturation property, the level maps on the Hodge filtration, the length lemma for Wach lattices, $D_{\mathrm{dR}} = D_{\mathrm{cris}}$, and that jumps of a quotient are jumps of the source. Assume that $\mathcal{O}$ is a complete discrete valuation ring with $p$ in its maximal ideal, $K_0 \to K$ is a bijection, $L$ contains a primitive $p^n$-th root of unity, $p \ge 5$, and a unit $u \in \mathbb{Z}_p^\times$ with $v_p(u-1) = 1$ maps to $\chi_\Gamma(\gamma)$. Let $e$ generate $D(\mathbf{1})$ and let $\mathbf{1}$ be crystalline. Let $F \colon T_1 \to T_2$ be a map of crystalline lattices that is onto, with good coefficients. Take $j, G_0, S, \eta, h$, kinds $\mathrm{ty}$ and cuts with lengths that satisfy the conditions on cuts, with the jumps of $T_1$ in the windows of $\mathrm{ty}$ and in $[-h, h]$, with $g = \max(G_0, S, \eta, 1) \le h$, the sizes bounded by $h$, and at most two thick letters on each arc. Then there are coordinates $V_1$, $V_2$, $V_0$ of $T_1$, $T_2$, $\mathbf{1}$ with a map $V_1 \to V_2$ over $F$, and $e_0 \in V_0$ with $\theta_i(e_0) = e_i e$ for each letter $i$, such that for every uniformizer $\varpi$ and every cut $c$ final arc data exist at $c$ with
$$H(s) = g + \begin{cases} G_0 & \text{thin}, \\ b + S & \text{up of size } b, \\ \eta & \text{down}, \end{cases} \qquad M = \Bigl\lfloor \frac{h + 2g}{p - 1} \Bigr\rfloor + 1,$$
the kind taken at the letter $c - s$.

Lean: `Formalism.MainNotions.N.Ln03.realised2_pf`.

*Proof.* The coordinate steps give $V_1$, $V_2$, $V_0$, the map, and $e_0$; they use the ring condition, $s = 1$, P9, the length lemma, $D_{\mathrm{dR}} = D_{\mathrm{cris}}$, the generator $e$, the crystalline unit lattice $\mathbf{1}$ and $K_0 = K$. Fix $\varpi$ and a cut $c$. By P9 each Wach module is free over the ring of letters, so $N_{c'}(V_1)$ and $N_{c'}(V_2)$ have bases $e_1$, $e_2$ over $\mathcal{O}[[X]]$. Write $h(e_{1,m}) = \sum_l a_{lm} e_{2,l}$ and let $P$ be the matrix of constant terms $a_{lm}(0)$. The identities for $q$, $\varphi_0$ and $\rho_\zeta$ hold for the Wach datum, the Teichmüller set maps onto $(\mathbb{Z}/p)^\times$, and $\ell_c \le n$ and $j \le \ell_c$ hold by the conditions on cuts. From $g \le h$ we get $3g \le (p - 1)M$. Then the [theorem on arc data at one cut](/entry/final-arc-holds/) gives the final arc data. $\square$

**Remark 8.4.6 (Formalization).** The arc data are `ArcData` and `FinalArcProps` ([FinalArcProps.lean](lean:Formalism/DistinguishedUnitIdeals/FinalArcProps.lean#L219)); the realisation is `Realised2` (CrystallineLiftingEveryOrdering.lean line 51). Not unfolded here: `JumpsLet` (which gives the numbers $M(i)$), `LevelData`, `LevelModel`, `LvHyp`, `K1b`, `UnitsAt`, `Compl`, `Mem`, `LocReg`, `LocalizesConst`, `Equiv`, `FlDef`, `LocLIs`, `MapFl`, `CMap`, `GoodCoeffs`, `UnitGen`, and the meaning of $\rho_\zeta$; conditions 2 and 3 of final arc data list their names only. In `finalArc_holds` the height function $H$ is a free parameter ([PfAssemble.lean](lean:Formalism/SharpRestriction/LevelLetterCocycles/LevelKernelDetection/PfAssemble.lean#L521)), so the theorem holds for every $H$. `realised2_pf` does not use four premises of `Realised2`: good coefficients, the bound $[-h, h]$, the size bound and the bound of two thick letters per arc (`hG`, `hJ`, `hS`, `hT` at Realised2Pf.lean line 77). The fields `Ks` and `Δ` of the coordinates are the fixed choices `KsW W` and `DeltaT p`.
