## The congruence length through the blocks

The last three sections of the chapter turn from lattices in crystalline representations to congruences between families of matrix representations over a discrete valuation ring. This section defines the valuation of a congruence ideal at a point, the column module of a family of cocycle data, and the bounds on the cohomology of the blocks of a block-triangular reduction. Its theorem bounds the valuation of the congruence ideal by a sum of local terms, one group of terms for each block, once the valuation is known to be at most the total length of the maps from the column module to the blocks. The next section proves that last inequality for ring data built from a representation, and so gives [the display-free bound](/entry/display-free/) that [the normalized ring data](/entry/normalized-ring-data/) use.

Throughout, $\mathcal{O}$ is a discrete valuation ring with valuation $v$ and fraction field $L$, as in [the final form of Theorem E](/entry/theorem-e-final-form/). We write $L/\mathcal{O}$ for the quotient $\mathcal{O}$-module and $e_{\mathcal{O}}(\ell) = \operatorname{length}_{\mathcal{O}} \mathcal{O}/\ell\mathcal{O}$ for $\ell \in \mathbb{N}$. Lengths take values in $\mathbb{N} \cup \{\infty\}$.

**Definition 8.17.1 (Congruence ideal).** Let $X$ be a set, $T$ an $\mathcal{O}$-subalgebra of the ring $\mathcal{O}^X$ of functions $X \to \mathcal{O}$, and $Z \subseteq X$. The *congruence ideal* $\mathfrak{c}(T, Z)$ is the ideal of $f \in T$ with $f(y) = 0$ for every $y \notin Z$, that is, the kernel of the restriction $T \to \mathcal{O}^{X \setminus Z}$. For $x \in X$ put
$$v_{T,Z}(x) = \inf_{f \in \mathfrak{c}(T, Z)} v(f(x)) \in \mathbb{N} \cup \{\infty\}.$$

The number $v_{T,Z}(x)$ measures how far the points of $Z$ are congruent to the points outside $Z$ inside $T$. For example, let $X = \{x, y\}$, $Z = \{x\}$, and $T$ the algebra of pairs $(a, b)$ with $a \equiv b \bmod \varpi^k$. Then $\mathfrak{c}(T, Z)$ is the set of pairs $(a, 0)$ with $a \in \varpi^k \mathcal{O}$, and $v_{T,Z}(x) = k$.

**Definition 8.17.2 (Block data).** *Block data* consist of: a second set $X'$ with a map $p \colon X' \to X$; a finite set $\{1, \dots, d\}$ of indices; an $\mathcal{O}$-subalgebra $R'$ of the ring of maps $X' \to M_d(L)$ that is noetherian as a module over itself; a commutative ring $T_c$ with an algebra map $T_c \to R'$; a group $G$ with a homomorphism $r \colon G \to R'^{\times}$ and a character $\chi \colon G \to T_c^{\times}$; elements $E, e_1 \in R'$ and $\delta \in T_c$; a point $\pi' \in X'$, an integer $t \ge 1$, a map $\mathrm{bl} \colon \{1, \dots, d\} \to \{0, \dots, t\}$ with fibres of sizes $d_k$, and $\mathcal{O}$-algebra maps $\rho_k \colon R' \to M_{d_k}(\mathcal{O})$ for $0 \le k \le t$. For $x' \in X'$ let $V_{x'} = L^d$ with $R'$ acting by the value at $x'$, and for each $k$ let $M_k = (L/\mathcal{O})^{d_k}$ with $R'$ acting through $\rho_k$. Put $u_g = \chi(g)^{-1} r(g)$ for $g \in G$.

The modules $M_k$ are the blocks of a lattice at $\pi'$ with its torsion divided out, and $u_g$ is the representation $r$ twisted by $\chi^{-1}$. For a subgroup $D \subseteq G$ and a module $V$ with an action of $R'$, let $\partial_D \colon V \to V^D$ be the map $v \mapsto (g \mapsto u_g v - v)$, so that the image of $\partial_D$ is the space of coboundaries of $D$ in $V$. For a module $M$ with an action of $R'$ we write $H^1(G, M)$ for the first cohomology of $G$ acting on $M$ through $g \mapsto u_g$, $H^1(D, M)$ for that of $D$, and $H^1_{\mathrm{ur},I}$ for the kernel of the restriction to a subgroup $I$.

**Definition 8.17.3 (Column module).** *Column data* consist of a finite set $\iota$ with an injective map $l \mapsto w_l$ to $\mathbb{N}$, subgroups $D_w \subseteq G$, numbers $j_w, e'_w \in \mathbb{N}$, and elements $K_{l,a}(g) \in R'$ for $a < j_{w_l}$ and $k_0(l, g) \in R'$, for $l \in \iota$ and $g \in G$. Let $Y \subseteq R'$ be the left ideal generated by the elements $y_g = (u_g - 1) E$. The *column module* $N$ is the quotient of $Y \times \prod_{l \in \iota} (R' \times R'^{j_{w_l}} \times R')$ by the left submodule generated by the vectors with $E$ in the first place of the factor $l$, the vectors with $1 - e_1$ in a place $a$ of the middle part, the vectors with $\delta - E$ in the last place, and, for $l \in \iota$ and $g \in D_{w_l}$, the vectors
$$\bigl(y_g;\ u_g - 1,\ (-\delta K_{l,a}(g))_a,\ -\delta\, k_0(l, g)\bigr)$$
in the factor $l$. Each map of $R'$-modules $\varphi \colon N \to M$ gives a cocycle $\Psi(\varphi)$ of $G$ in $M$, its value at $g$ being $\varphi$ of the class of the generator attached to $g$.

The column module encodes, column by column, the cocycles that a congruence must carry. The last relation says that $y_g$ and the twisted correction $u_g - 1$ agree up to $\delta$ times the column entries.

**Definition 8.17.4 (Generic column condition).** The *generic column condition* holds if, at each $x' \in X'$ with $p(x') \notin Z$:

1. for every nonzero $\eta \in E V_{x'}$ there is $g \in G$ with $u_g \eta \ne \eta$;
2. for each $l \in \iota$, with $w = w_l$, there is a subspace $P$ of $V_{x'}^{D_w}$ that contains the image of $\partial_{D_w}$, the maps $g \mapsto K_{l,a}(g) s$ for $s \in e_1 V_{x'}$ and every $a$, and the maps $g \mapsto k_0(l, g) s$ for $s \in E V_{x'}$, with $\dim_L P/\operatorname{im} \partial_{D_w} \le j_w + \dim_L \ker \partial_{D_w}$.

**Definition 8.17.5 (Block conditions).** Fix $\ell, c_B \in \mathbb{N}$, finite sets $W_2, Q \subseteq \mathbb{N}$, degrees $\deg(w)$, constants $s, h_E, H_0 \in \mathbb{N} \cup \{\infty\}$, and numbers $\mathrm{Sha}(c)$. Let $C = \{w_l : l \in \iota\}$ be the set of columns. Each block $M_c$ comes with submodules $H_G$ of $H^1(G, M_c)$ and $H_w$, $F_w$ of $H^1(D_w, M_c)$.

1. The *condition on block $0$ with constant $s$* asks for: an $\mathcal{O}$-module $P_0$ with an action of $R'$, a map $\mathrm{tens} \colon P_0 \to \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{O}}(L/\mathcal{O}, M_0)$ that is $R'$-linear, an element $v_1$ with every $m = e_1 m$ of the form $\mathrm{tens}(v_1)(x)$, and elements $\kappa_{l,a}(g)$ with $K_{l,a}(g) v_1 = \ell^{e'_{w_l}} \kappa_{l,a}(g)$ on $D_{w_l}$; cocycles built from the $\kappa_{l,a}$ lie in $F_{w_l}$, and such a cocycle is a coboundary only for zero coefficients; the classes of all $\Psi(\varphi)$ lie in $H_G$, which restricts into $H_w$ for $w \in W_2$; $r$ and $\chi$ are trivial on given subgroups $I_o(v)$; a twisted row representation $\rho_1$ with a map into $R'$ and conditions on each row $w \in W_2$; and the length of the part of $H_G$ that is unramified at all $I_o(v)$ and lies in $F_w$ at all $w \in W_2 \cup C$ is at most $s$.
2. The *condition on block $c$ with constant $s'$* asks for: at $w \in W_2$, $F_w \subseteq H_w$, a number $c_T(w) \le c_B$ such that $\ell^{c_T(w)}$ moves every class $g \mapsto \psi(y_g)$, for $\psi \colon Y \to M_c$ linear over $\mathcal{O}$ and $D_w$-equivariant, into $F_w$, and the length of $(H_w \cap \ell^{-c_T(w)} F_w)/F_w$ at most $e_{\mathcal{O}}(\ell)\, c_T(w) (\deg(w) + 1)$; at $w \in Q$, $F_w = H^1_{\mathrm{ur}, I_w}$ for a subgroup $I_w \subseteq D_w$ with an element $\gamma_w \in I_w$ such that $(r(\gamma_w) - 1)(r(\gamma_w) - \zeta_w) = 0$, $\chi(\gamma_w) = \zeta_w$, every $g \in I_w$ has $r(g) = r(\gamma_w)^{n}$ and $\chi(g) = \chi(\gamma_w)^{n}$ for some $n$, the elements $u_g$ for $g \in I_w$ act trivially on $M_c$, and $r(\gamma_w)$ acts by a scalar $\zeta_{\mathcal{O}}(w)$; the classes of all $\Psi(\varphi)$ lie in $H_G$, which restricts into $H_w$ for $w \in W_2$; $r$ and $\chi$ are trivial on the $I_o(v)$; for $g$ in given subgroups $I_S(v)$ some $a \in R'$ that is injective on $M_c$ has $a (r(g) - \chi(g)) E = 0$; and the length of the part of $H_G$ unramified at all $I_o(v)$ and $I_S(v)$ and in $F_w$ for all $w \in W_2 \cup Q \cup C$ is at most $s'$.

The block conditions are the local inputs of the bound: a bound on a Selmer-type group of each block, and bounds on the local conditions at each row and at each place of $Q$.

**Theorem 8.17.6 (Congruence length through the blocks).** Take block data and column data as above, and let $\delta_{\mathcal{O}} \in \mathcal{O}$. Assume:

1. the functions of an $\mathcal{O}$-subalgebra $T' \subseteq \mathcal{O}^{X'}$ are the sums $\sum_{k < q} (a_k \circ p)\, b_k$ with $a_k \in T$, for fixed $b_0, \dots, b_{q-1}$; at $x \notin Z$ the $b_k$ are linearly independent on the fibre $p^{-1}(x)$; and the determinant of each square matrix over $R'$, evaluated at the points of $X'$, is given by a function of $T'$;
2. $E^2 = \delta E$ and $e_1^2 = e_1$;
3. at each $x'$ with $p(x') \notin Z$ there is $\delta_x \ne 0$ in $L$ by which $\delta$ acts on $V_{x'}$, the spaces $E V_{x'}$ and $e_1 V_{x'}$ have dimension $1$, and the generic column condition holds at $x'$;
4. the values $\rho(a)$ at $\pi'$ lie in $M_d(\mathcal{O})$, are block upper triangular for $\mathrm{bl}$ and have the diagonal blocks $\rho_k(a)$; each $\rho_k$ is onto; and $d_c = 1$ for $c \ne 0$;
5. $v_{T,Z}(p(\pi')) \le \sum_{k=0}^{t} \operatorname{length}_{\mathcal{O}} \operatorname{Hom}_{R'}(N, M_k)$;
6. $E = \sum_{g \in S_E} t_E(g)\, r(g)$ for a finite $S_E \subseteq G$ and $t_E(g) \in T_c$, and each $k_0(l, g)$ lies in $R' E$;
7. on $M_0$: $E$ acts by $0$, both $\delta$ and $\tau = \sum_{g \in S_E} t_E(g) \chi(g)$ act by $\delta_{\mathcal{O}}$, and $e_1 M_0 \cong L/\mathcal{O}$; the condition on block $0$ with constant $s$ holds;
8. for $2 \le c \le t$: $e_1$ and $E$ act on $M_c$ by $0$, $\delta$ and $\tau$ act by $\delta_{\mathcal{O}}$, $\operatorname{length} \ker \partial_{D_w} \le H_0$ on $M_c$ for $w \in C$, and the condition on block $c$ with constant $\mathrm{Sha}(c - 1)$ holds;
9. on $M_1$: each $u_g$ acts trivially, $e_1$ acts by $0$, $E$ and $\delta$ act by $\delta_{\mathcal{O}}$, and $k_0(l, g)$ acts by $\ell^{e'_{w_l}} \delta_{\mathcal{O}}\, u(l, g)$ for $g \in D_{w_l}$, with $u(l, \cdot) \colon G \to \mathcal{O}$ zero on $I_{w_l}$ and equal to $1$ somewhere on $D_{w_l}$; $H^1_{\mathrm{ur}, I_{w_l}} \subseteq F_{w_l}$; and the condition on block $1$ with constant $h_E$ holds.

Put $v_\delta = v(\delta_{\mathcal{O}})$, $e_{\mathcal{O}} = e_{\mathcal{O}}(\ell)$, $N_0 = d_0$, and $h_0(w) = \operatorname{length} \ker \partial_{D_w}$ on $M_0$. Then
$$\begin{aligned} v_{T,Z}(p(\pi')) \le{} & \sum_{w \in W_2} \operatorname{length}(F_s(w)/\operatorname{Im}(w)) + \sum_{w \in C} \bigl( e_{\mathcal{O}} e'_w + j_w (e_{\mathcal{O}} e'_w + v_\delta) + 2 v_\delta + h_0(w) \bigr) + s \\ & + \sum_{0 < c < t} \mathrm{Sha}(c) + h_E + t\, e_{\mathcal{O}}\, c_B \sum_{w \in W_2} (\deg(w) + 2) \\ & + 4 (N_0 + t)(|C| + 1)\, v_\delta + t\, |C|\, H_0 + t \sum_{w \in Q} v(\zeta_{\mathcal{O}}(w) - 1), \end{aligned}$$
where $F_s(w) \supseteq \operatorname{Im}(w)$ are the row modules of the condition on block $0$.

*Proof.* By hypothesis 5 it suffices to bound each length $\ell_k = \operatorname{length} \operatorname{Hom}_{R'}(N, M_k)$ and to add the bounds. On a block $M_c$ with $c \ne 0$, which is $L/\mathcal{O}$ since $d_c = 1$, the kernel of multiplication by $\zeta_{\mathcal{O}}(w) - 1$ has length $v(\zeta_{\mathcal{O}}(w) - 1)$. With this, the conditions on the blocks give, for each block, a submodule of local classes at the rows and at $Q$ and a global submodule that contain the classes of all $\Psi(\varphi)$, with lengths bounded by $s$, $h_E$ or $\mathrm{Sha}(c - 1)$. The bounds on the lattices of the blocks then give
$$\begin{aligned} \ell_0 &\le \sum_{w \in W_2} \operatorname{length}(F_s(w)/\operatorname{Im}(w)) + \sum_{w \in C} \bigl( j_w (e_{\mathcal{O}} e'_w + v_\delta) + h_0(w) \bigr) + s + 4 N_0 (|C| + 1) v_\delta, \\ \ell_1 &\le h_E + \sum_{w \in C} (e_{\mathcal{O}} e'_w + 2 v_\delta) + e_{\mathcal{O}} c_B \sum_{w \in W_2} (\deg(w) + 2) + 4 (|C| + 1) v_\delta + \sum_{w \in Q} v(\zeta_{\mathcal{O}}(w) - 1), \\ \ell_c &\le \mathrm{Sha}(c - 1) + e_{\mathcal{O}} c_B \sum_{w \in W_2} (\deg(w) + 2) + 4 (|C| + 1) v_\delta + |C| H_0 + \sum_{w \in Q} v(\zeta_{\mathcal{O}}(w) - 1) \quad (2 \le c \le t). \end{aligned}$$
We add these $t + 1$ bounds. The terms $\mathrm{Sha}(c - 1)$ for $2 \le c \le t$ give $\sum_{0 < c < t} \mathrm{Sha}(c)$. The terms $e_{\mathcal{O}} c_B \sum (\deg(w) + 2)$, $4(|C| + 1) v_\delta$ and $\sum_Q v(\zeta_{\mathcal{O}}(w) - 1)$ occur $t$ times, the term $|C| H_0$ occurs $t - 1 \le t$ times, and $4 N_0 (|C| + 1) v_\delta + 4t (|C| + 1) v_\delta = 4(N_0 + t)(|C| + 1) v_\delta$. The column sums of $\ell_0$ and $\ell_1$ add up to the column sum of the theorem. $\square$

For $t = 1$ there are only the blocks $M_0$ and $M_1$, the sum over $0 < c < t$ is empty, and the bound has the terms of block $0$ and of block $1$ only.
