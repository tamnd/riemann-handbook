## Ring data and the display bound

This section proves the display bound: for ring data built from a family of representations $r_0$ of a group over a finite set of points, the valuation of the congruence ideal at a point $\pi$ is bounded by the local terms of [the bound through the blocks](/entry/top-length-bound-via-splitting/). The bound holds with no condition on the size of the residue field of $\mathcal{O}$, and this is what *free* means in its name. It is used in [the normalized ring data](/entry/normalized-ring-data/). The proof has two steps. First, a list of conditions on block data at one point, the *ring conditions*, reduces the display bound to the inequality $v_{T,Z} \le \sum_k \operatorname{length} \operatorname{Hom}(N, M_k)$ of the previous section. Second, for ring data this inequality is a lemma on modules of finite type.

We keep the notation of the previous section: the congruence ideal and $v_{T,Z}$, block data with $V_{x'} = L^d$ and the blocks $M_k = (L/\mathcal{O})^{d_k}$, $u_g = \chi(g)^{-1} r(g)$, the column module $N$, the generic column condition and the conditions on the blocks.

**Definition 8.18.1 (Display bound).** Block data with $\delta_{\mathcal{O}} \in \mathcal{O}$ satisfy the *display bound* if the following holds for all column data and all data of the block conditions. Assume the generic column condition at every $x'$ with $p(x') \notin Z$, $k_0(l, g) \in R' E$ for all $l, g$, the condition on block $0$ with constant $s$, for $2 \le c \le t$ the bound $\operatorname{length} \ker \partial_{D_w} \le H_0$ on $M_c$ for $w \in C$ and the condition on block $c$ with constant $\mathrm{Sha}(c - 1)$, and on $M_1$ the action of $k_0(l, g)$ by $\ell^{e'_{w_l}} \delta_{\mathcal{O}} u(l, g)$, the conditions on $u(l, \cdot)$, $H^1_{\mathrm{ur}, I_{w_l}} \subseteq F_{w_l}$, and the condition on block $1$ with constant $h_E$. Then $v_{T,Z}(p(\pi'))$ is at most the right side of [the bound through the blocks](/entry/top-length-bound-via-splitting/).

A second form of the display bound also assumes that $N$ is generated by $g_0$ elements over $R'$ and that $g_0 \sum_k d_k$ is less than the number of elements of the residue field. The display bound implies this second form, since it has fewer hypotheses.

**Definition 8.18.2 (Ring conditions).** Let $T' \subseteq \mathcal{O}^{X'}$ be an $\mathcal{O}$-subalgebra, $b_0, \dots, b_{q-1} \in \mathcal{O}^{X'}$, $\rho \colon R' \to M_d(\mathcal{O})$ an $\mathcal{O}$-algebra map, $S_E \subseteq G$ finite, $t_E \colon G \to T_c$ and $\delta_{\mathcal{O}} \in \mathcal{O}$. Block data satisfy the *ring conditions* if:

1. every $f \in T'$ is $\sum_k (a_k \circ p)\, b_k$ with $a_k \in T$; for every $x \in X$ the $b_k$ are linearly independent over $\mathcal{O}$ on the fibre $p^{-1}(x)$; and the determinant of each square matrix over $R'$, evaluated at the points of $X'$, is the value of a function of $T'$;
2. $E^2 = \delta E$ and $e_1^2 = e_1$; at each $x' \in X'$, $\delta$ acts on $V_{x'}$ by some $\delta_x \ne 0$, and $E V_{x'}$, $e_1 V_{x'}$ have dimension $1$;
3. $\rho(a)$ is the value of $a$ at $\pi'$, it is block upper triangular for $\mathrm{bl}$ with diagonal blocks $\rho_k(a)$, each $\rho_k$ is onto, and $d_c = 1$ for $c \ne 0$;
4. $E = \sum_{g \in S_E} t_E(g)\, r(g)$; put $\tau = \sum_{g \in S_E} t_E(g) \chi(g)$;
5. on $M_0$, $E$ acts by $0$, $\delta$ and $\tau$ act by $\delta_{\mathcal{O}}$, and $e_1 M_0 \cong L/\mathcal{O}$;
6. on $M_c$ for $2 \le c \le t$, $e_1$ and $E$ act by $0$ and $\delta$, $\tau$ act by $\delta_{\mathcal{O}}$;
7. on $M_1$, every $u_g$ acts trivially, $e_1$ acts by $0$, and $E$, $\delta$ act by $\delta_{\mathcal{O}}$.

These are the hypotheses 1 to 4 and the scalar parts of 6 to 9 of the bound through the blocks, with the linear independence asked at every point.

**Theorem 8.18.3 (Display bound from block lengths).** Let block data satisfy the ring conditions. Assume that for all column data such that, at every $x'$ with $p(x') \notin Z$, there is a nonzero $R'$-linear map $N \to V_{x'}$, we have
$$v_{T,Z}(p(\pi')) \le \sum_{k=0}^{t} \operatorname{length}_{\mathcal{O}} \operatorname{Hom}_{R'}(N, M_k).$$
Then the block data satisfy the display bound.

Lean: `Formalism.RepresentationBoundsCohomology.ringDataObjects.free_of_bound`

*Proof.* Take column data and block-condition data as in the display bound. We apply [the bound through the blocks](/entry/top-length-bound-via-splitting/). Its hypotheses 1, 2, 4 and 6 and the scalar parts of 7 to 9 are ring conditions. Hypothesis 3 at $x'$ with $p(x') \notin Z$ is ring condition 2 at $x'$ together with the generic column condition. The rest of 7 to 9 are hypotheses of the display bound. For hypothesis 5 we use the assumption of the theorem, so we need a nonzero map $N \to V_{x'}$ at each $x'$ with $p(x') \notin Z$. On $V_{x'}$ we have $E^2 = \delta E$, $e_1^2 = e_1$, $\delta$ acts by $\delta_x \ne 0$, $E V_{x'}$ and $e_1 V_{x'}$ are lines, and the generic column condition holds. Under these conditions on a finite-dimensional $L$-space $V$ with an action of $R'$, there is a nonzero $R'$-linear map from the column module to $V$. $\square$

The last fact is the existence of a column map. Its two parts of the generic column condition say that $E V$ is not fixed by all $u_g$, and that the span of the column cocycles in $V$ is small enough for a nonzero solution of the relations of $N$ to exist.

**Definition 8.18.4 (Primitive family).** Let $X$ be a finite set, $T \subseteq \mathcal{O}^X$ an $\mathcal{O}$-subalgebra, $G$ a group, $r_0 \colon G \to (M_d(\mathcal{O}))^X$ a homomorphism, $\pi \in X$, $t \ge 1$, $\mathrm{bl} \colon \{1, \dots, d\} \to \{0, \dots, t\}$, and $\chi_1, \varepsilon \colon G \to \mathcal{O}^{\times}$ characters. The family is *primitive* if $T$ is a local ring, $T$ separates the points of $X$, the coefficients of the reversed characteristic polynomial of $r_0(g)$, as functions on $X$, lie in $T$, the matrices $r_0(g)(\pi)$ have zero entries between different blocks, $d_c = 1$ for $c \ne 0$, and the diagonal entry of $r_0(g)(\pi)$ in the block $c \ne 0$ is $\chi_1(g)\, \varepsilon(g)^{-(c-1)}$. The family is *residually absolutely irreducible* if the reduction of block $0$ of $r_0(\cdot)(\pi)$ to the residue field $k$ has no invariant subspaces other than $0$ and $k^{d_0}$, and every matrix that commutes with it is a scalar.

Let $T[r_0]$ be the set of finite sums $\sum_g c_g\, r_0(g)$ with $c_g \in T$. For an idempotent $e$ and a matrix $a$ put $P_e(a) = \det(X e - a + (1 - e))$, the characteristic polynomial of $a$ on the image of $e$ when $a = e a e$.

**Definition 8.18.5 (Idempotents and a corner perturbation).** Idempotents $e_B, e_1 \in T[r_0]$ are *adapted* if $e_B e_1 = e_1 e_B = 0$, $e_B(x)$ has rank $t$ and $e_1(x)$ has rank $1$ at every $x$, $e_B(\pi)$ is the diagonal matrix with $0$ on block $0$ and $1$ elsewhere, and $e_1(\pi)$ vanishes outside block $0$. For $g_0 \in G$, a *corner perturbation* is $s \in T[r_0]$ with $e_B s = s e_B = s$, $s(\pi) = 0$, and such that at every $x$ the polynomial $P_{e_B(x)}(e_B r_0(g_0) e_B + s)(x)$ has $t$ distinct roots in $L$.

**Definition 8.18.6 (Ring data).** *Ring data* for such a family are block data, with $T_c = T'$, together with $T' \subseteq \mathcal{O}^{X'}$, $b_0, \dots, b_{q-1}$, $\rho$, $S_E$, $t_E$, elements $e_B, X_0 \in R'$ and functions $r_i \in T'$, such that, with $X_0(x) = e_B r_0(g_0) e_B + s$:

1. at each $x' \in X'$, $P_{e_B}(X_0)(p(x')) = \prod_{i < t} (X - r_i(x'))$, and for each $x$ and each list $\alpha_0, \dots, \alpha_{t-1}$ in $\mathcal{O}$ with $P_{e_B}(X_0)(x) = \prod_{i<t} (X - \alpha_i)$ there is exactly one $x'$ over $x$ with $r_i(x') = \alpha_i$ for all $i$;
2. $T'$ is generated by the functions $f \circ p$, $f \in T$, and the $r_i$; $q = t!$; and the $b_k$ form a basis of $T'$ over $T$;
3. $p(\pi') = \pi$ and $r_i(\pi') = \chi_1(g_0)\, \varepsilon(g_0)^{-i}$;
4. $r(g)$, $\chi(g)$, $e_B$, $e_1$ and $X_0$ are the pullbacks along $p$ of $r_0(g)$, $\chi_1(g)$, $e_B$, $e_1$ and $X_0$, and $R'$ is the $T'$-span of the $r(g)$; $T'$ acts on $R'$ and $V_{x'}$ by the value at $x'$, and on the blocks by the value at $\pi'$;
5. $E = e_B \prod_{i=1}^{t-1} (X_0 - r_i e_B) = \sum_{g \in S_E} t_E(g)\, r(g)$;
6. $\rho(a) = a(\pi')$ and $\rho_k$ is the block $k$ of $\rho$.

Put $\delta = \prod_{0 < i < t} (r_0 - r_i)$ and $\delta_{\mathcal{O}} = \delta(\pi')$.

So $X'$ is the set of points of $X$ with an ordering of the $t$ eigenvalues of the corner $X_0$. On the image of $e_B$ these eigenvalues are distinct, so $E$ is $\delta$ times the projector onto the eigenline of $X_0$ for the eigenvalue $r_0$, which gives $E^2 = \delta E$. At $\pi'$ the eigenvalues are $\chi_1(g_0) \varepsilon(g_0)^{-i}$, the diagonal entries of the blocks $c = i + 1$. Hence $\delta_{\mathcal{O}} = \chi_1(g_0)^{t-1} \prod_{0 < i < t} (1 - \varepsilon(g_0)^{-i})$, and $v(\delta_{\mathcal{O}})$ is the valuation of $\prod_{0<i<t}(\varepsilon(g_0)^{i} - 1)$.

**Lemma 8.18.7 (Colength by block lengths).** Let a primitive, residually absolutely irreducible family carry adapted idempotents, a corner perturbation and ring data with $R'$ noetherian. Let $Z \subseteq X$, and let $N$ be an $R'$-module generated by finitely many elements, with a nonzero $R'$-linear map $N \to V_{x'}$ at every $x'$ with $p(x') \notin Z$. Then
$$v_{T,Z}(\pi) \le \sum_{k=0}^{t} \operatorname{length}_{\mathcal{O}} \operatorname{Hom}_{R'}(N, M_k).$$

*Proof.* There is a finite free extension $\mathcal{O}'$ of $\mathcal{O}$, with ramification index $1$, whose residue field has more than $g d$ elements, where $g$ is the number of generators of $N$. Over $\mathcal{O}'$ the bound holds by the same lemma with a large residue field, and the base change keeps $v_{T,Z}(\pi)$ and the lengths. $\square$

**Theorem 8.18.8 (Display bound).** Let a primitive, residually absolutely irreducible family carry adapted idempotents, a corner perturbation and ring data with $R'$ noetherian, and assume that the ring data satisfy the ring conditions. Then for every $Z \subseteq X$ the ring data satisfy the display bound.

Lean: `Formalism.RepresentationBoundsCohomology.ringDataObjects.displayFree`

*Proof.* By the theorem on the display bound from block lengths, it suffices to show $v_{T,Z}(p(\pi')) \le \sum_k \operatorname{length} \operatorname{Hom}_{R'}(N, M_k)$ for column data with a nonzero map $N \to V_{x'}$ at every $x'$ off $p^{-1}(Z)$. The ring data give $p(\pi') = \pi$. Since $R'$ is noetherian and $N$ is a quotient of a submodule of a finite free $R'$-module, $N$ is generated by finitely many elements. The lemma on the colength by block lengths gives the bound. $\square$

**Example 8.18.9.** Let $t = 1$. Then $E = e_B$, the product in $\delta$ is empty, so $\delta = 1$ and $v_\delta = 0$. There are two blocks, $M_0$ and $M_1 = L/\mathcal{O}$, and the bound of the display bound has only the terms of these two blocks, with no term in $v_\delta$.
