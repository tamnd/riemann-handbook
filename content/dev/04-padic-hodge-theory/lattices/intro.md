This chapter studies Galois-stable lattices in crystalline representations of $G_K$, for a finite extension $K$ of $\mathbb{Q}_p$, mostly unramified over $\mathbb{Q}_p$, and bounds how far a map of lattices is from being onto on finite cohomology classes. For a map $F \colon T_1 \to T_2$ of lattices that is onto, every finite class of $T_2$ lifts to $T_1$ after multiplication by a power $p^e$, and the colength of the image is at most a constant times the length of $\mathcal{O}/p\mathcal{O}$. The bounds depend only on the Hodge-Tate jumps of the two lattices, read letter by letter along the embeddings of $K$ into the coefficient field, and not on the lattices themselves. The chapter proves the local Theorem B (letters of two kinds, thick and thin, with an alternating or a constant pattern), the local Theorem C (windows of jumps that alternate with the parity of the letter), the split and the universal parts of the lifting along arcs in case A, the local lifting theorem E with its corollary, and the uniform export bound for families of twisted places. Most proofs here assemble results that the proof takes as admitted inputs from the theory of Wach modules (Wach, Berger) and from the comparison theorems of Fontaine. Sections 1 to 7 state these inputs as theorems with their sources and show how the main bounds follow from them. Sections 8 to 20 prove what these bounds rest on: [the bound at one point](/entry/point-bound/) behind the export bound; Theorem E over power series, from its [full form](/entry/theorem-e-full-form/) through its [primitive form](/entry/theorem-e-primitive-form/) to its [final form](/entry/theorem-e-final-form/); [the lifts along alternating arcs](/entry/sum-alt/) for Theorem B; a second [colength bound from frames](/entry/integrality-from-metric-of-large-constant/), for ramified $K$ and every constant $C \ge 160$; [the congruence length through blocks](/entry/top-length-bound-via-splitting/) and [the display bound](/entry/display-free/) for ring data; and [the local theory](/entry/local-theory-of-admitted/), a list of statements on Wach modules and lifting exponents with explicit constants such as $10$, $23$ and $32$.
