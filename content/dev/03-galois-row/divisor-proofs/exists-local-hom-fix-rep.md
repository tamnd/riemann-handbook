This theorem proves the local step of the representability of homomorphisms that respect polarizations. Near each point $p$ of $\operatorname{Spec} R$ it finds a basic open $D(f)$ over which the sections of a fixed power of each bundle form a free module, compatible with base change, and there it builds the representing scheme. Its statement form is [the local representability](/entry/exists-local-representing-scheme-hom-fixed-pullback/), which [the representability over an affine base](/entry/exists-hom-fix-rep-finite-type/) glues.

## Statement

The statement is that of [the local representability](/entry/exists-local-representing-scheme-hom-fixed-pullback/): with $A, B, L, M$ over $\operatorname{Spec} R$, $R$ noetherian, as in [the affine theorem](/entry/exists-hom-fix-rep-finite-type/), each point $p$ has an open neighbourhood $U$ over which the homomorphisms $A_U \to B_U$ that carry $M$ to $L$ are represented by a scheme that is quasi-compact, separated and locally of finite type over $U$.

## Proof

1. `GlobalTheory.RelAmpleFixed.veryAmpleAt_baseChange` gives $k \geq 1$ such that $M^{\otimes k}$ is very ample (`Out.VeryAmpleAt`) on each base change of $B$ along a ring map $R \to R'$. The proof takes for $k$ the exponent $N$ that relative ampleness gives on the affine open $\operatorname{Spec} R$ itself.
2. `GlobalTheory.T.exists_basicOpen_baseChangeStable_sectionBasis`, for $(A, L, k, p)$, gives $f_1 \in R$ with $p \in D(f_1)$ and this property. For each $g$ with $D(g) \subseteq D(f_1)$ and each base change $A_g$ of $A$ to $\operatorname{Spec} R_g$, there are sections $b_1, \dots, b_m$ of $L^{\otimes k}$ on $A_g$ whose pullbacks along each map $\operatorname{Spec} R'' \to \operatorname{Spec} R_g$ are a basis of the sections of $L^{\otimes k}$ over $R''$. The same lemma for $(B, M, k, p)$ gives $f_2$.
3. Let $f = f_1 f_2$. Then $p \in D(f) = D(f_1) \cap D(f_2)$.
4. `GlobalTheory.HomFixTransport.homFixRep_basicOpen` moves a representing scheme over $\operatorname{Spec} R_f$, for every choice of base changes $A_f, B_f$, to one over the open $D(f)$ of $\operatorname{Spec} R$. So it suffices to work over $R_f$.
5. Let $A_f, B_f$ be base changes. `GlobalTheory.isCommMonObj_of_grpPullback` makes them commutative. `GlobalTheory.GrpPullbackPol.isAbelianScheme_and_pull_of_isGrpPullback` makes them abelian schemes, with the pulled back bundles rigidified and (for $A_f$) relatively ample.
6. `GlobalTheory.T.exists_fixedPullbackHomScheme_of_sectionBases` over the noetherian ring $R_f$ takes $k$, the very ampleness of $M_f^{\otimes k}$ from step 1, and the two bases from step 2 with $g = f$. It gives the representing scheme.

The rest is bookkeeping.

The exponent $k$ comes from $B$ and $M$ only, and the same $k$ serves for $L$. The relative ampleness of $M_f$ in step 5 is dropped, since only very ampleness at $k$ is used for $M$. In `Out.RelAmple` and `Out.VeryAmpleAt` the very ampleness is stated by charts: nonvanishing loci of sections that are affine, cover, and whose rings are generated by the ratios of the sections.
