This theorem builds, for a finite extension $K$ of $\mathbb{Q}_\ell$, a tower of truncations of $B_{\mathrm{dR}}^+$, each with a divided power subring that contains $\mathcal{O}_K$ and the image of $A_{\mathrm{cris}}$. The comparison of crystalline and de Rham first Chern classes uses it, and this leads to the [local steps](/entry/steps-holds/). Below this theorem are 303 declarations.

## Statement

Let $t \in B_{\mathrm{dR}}^+$ be the usual period and $\xi = \ell - [\pi] \in A_{\inf}$. For $k \geq 1$ and $M \geq 0$ put:

- $B_k = B_{\mathrm{dR}}^+/t^k$, with the ideal $I_B = (\xi)$ and its divided powers $x^n/n!$ (they exist because $B_k$ is a $\mathbb{Q}$-algebra);
- $A_k \subset B_k$, the subring generated by the image of $A_{\inf}$ and the elements $\eta^i/i!$, where $\eta = \xi/\ell^M$;
- $I = I_B \cap A_k$.

A *level* (`Kap.Lev`) is such data in general: rings $A \to B$ with divided power ideals, maps $\mathrm{toB} \colon B_{\mathrm{dR}}^+ \to B$ and $\lambda \colon A_{\mathrm{cris}} \to A$, and Galois actions. A level of index $m$ is *good* if, for example, $\mathrm{toB}$ is onto with kernel $(t^m)$, $A \to B$ is injective with $I = A \cap I_B$ and $I_B = I B$, all maps respect $\mathcal{O}_K$, $K$, divided powers and the Galois action, and the $r$-th divided power ideal of $I_B$ is $(t^r)$. A *system* (`LevSys`) is a level $L_m$ of index $m + 1$ for each $m$, with maps $L_{m+1} \to L_m$. It is good if each level is good, the maps respect all the structure, and each compatible family $(y_m)$ comes from exactly one element of $B_{\mathrm{dR}}^+$. A level is `CrisOK` if $\lambda(\xi) \in I$, and $a - \lambda(w) \in I$ whenever $a \in \mathcal{O}_K$ and $\theta(w) = a$.

**Theorem.** If $[K : \mathbb{Q}_\ell]$ is finite, there is a good system of levels whose levels are all `CrisOK`.

## Proof

1. `levHyp_ex`: there is an $M$ with `LevHyp`. Its first field, `exists_lattice_bound_integers`, gives one $M$ with $\mathcal{O}_K \subset A_k$ for all $k$. Five more lemmas say that $A_{\mathrm{cris}}$ lies in $A_k$, that $A_k$ is closed under the divided powers of $B_k$, that the Galois group keeps $(t^k)$ and $A_k$, and that $B_{k+1} \to B_k$ maps $A_{k+1}$ into $A_k$.
2. `levSys h` takes $L_m$ to be the data above for $k = m + 1$, and `levSys_good` proves the conditions, mostly with one `GlobalTheory.T.level_*` lemma per field (through `lev_good`). `dp_of_char` proves the divided power conditions on $B$ from those on $A$, because over $\mathbb{Q}$ one has $\gamma_n(x) = x^n/n!$. The condition on compatible families is `existsUnique_period_of_compatible`.
3. `CrisOK` for each level is `level_xi_mem_ideal_and_theta_compat`.
