This theorem is the unit law of the cup product on étale cohomology with coefficients $\mathbb{Z}/q(i)$. It is the first of the three laws that [the ring laws](/entry/s41/) put together. It is one line: the lemma `LeafRingUnit_of` applied to seven earlier lines of the same file. Below this theorem are 308 declarations.

## Statement

The spaces $E$, the groups $H^n(E, \mathbb{Z}/q(i))$, the generators $g_i(\zeta)$, the twisted pairings and the cup product are as in [the associativity entry](/entry/cup-product-tensor-holds-of/). The unit class $1 \in H^0(E, \mathbb{Z}/q(0))$ is the class of a $0$-cocycle $\alpha$ with value $g_0(\zeta)$ on each $0$-simplex over each scheme $U$, for each primitive root $\zeta$ on $U$. It is $0$ if there is no such cocycle.

**Theorem.** Let $q \geq 1$, and let $q$ be invertible on $E$. Then $1 \cup a = a$ for each $a \in H^n(E, \mathbb{Z}/q(i))$, after the identification of $H^{0+n}(E, \mathbb{Z}/q(0+i))$ with $H^n(E, \mathbb{Z}/q(i))$.

## Proof

Write $a = [\beta]$ for a cocycle $\beta$.

1. By $\mathrm{s34}$ there is a twisted pairing $\mu \colon I_0 \times I_i \to I_i$ that is a left unit for sections: $\mu(s, b) = b$ when $s$ is étale locally of the form $g_0(\zeta)$.
2. By $\mathrm{s16}$ there is a cocycle $\alpha$ as in the definition of $1$, and $1 = [\alpha]$.
3. By $\mathrm{s36}$ the values of $\alpha$ on $0$-simplices are étale locally of the form $g_0(\zeta)$.
4. By $\mathrm{s37}$ the Alexander-Whitney product $\gamma$ of $\alpha$ and $\beta$ through $\mu$ is a cocycle with $[\gamma] = [\beta]$.
5. The cup product uses the chosen twisted pairing, not $\mu$. By $\mathrm{s2}$ the chosen pairing is twisted, and by $\mathrm{s12}$ two twisted pairings give the same cup product.
6. By $\mathrm{s1}$, the cup product through $\mu$ of $[\alpha]$ and $[\beta]$ is $[\gamma]$.

So $1 \cup a = [\gamma] = [\beta] = a$. We have not unfolded the proofs of $\mathrm{s34}$, $\mathrm{s36}$ and $\mathrm{s37}$.
