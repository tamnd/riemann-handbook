This chapter builds the multiplicative structure of [étale cohomology](/learn/geometry/#tale-cohomology) with coefficients in the Tate twists $\mathbb{Z}/q(i)$, on simplicial spaces over schemes. It proves the Kummer sequence $0 \to \mu_q \to \mathbb{G}_m \to \mathbb{G}_m \to 0$ in the form the Galois row needs, and it identifies $\mu_q$ with $\mathbb{Z}/q(1)$ through [roots of unity](/learn/algebra/#roots-of-unity). Then it constructs the cup product from Alexander-Whitney cochains and proves the ring laws: existence, uniqueness, associativity, the unit law and graded commutativity. It defines Kummer classes and first Chern classes of [line bundles](/learn/geometry/#line-bundles-and-divisors) by explicit cocycles and proves that all these operations commute with pullback. The last section uses the projective bundle formula to define Chern classes, proves the Whitney sum formula, and turns Chern classes into power sum classes with Newton's formulas in a ring of [formal power series](/learn/algebra/#polynomials-and-power-series). The result is the axiom D2: natural, additive classes $s_k(V)$ with $s_k(L) = c_1(L)^k$ for line bundles.

From Volume 1 the chapter needs the basic facts on [rings and modules](/learn/algebra/#rings-ideals-and-modules), [tensor products](/learn/algebra/#tensor-products-and-coinvariants), roots of unity and power series, and the definition of étale cohomology. The Kummer sequence goes to [the top theorem of the étale topology](/entry/etale-topology-top/), and the power sum classes go to [the axiom D2 of étale cohomology](/entry/a0/). Both reach [the finiteness of the representation](/entry/v-fin-holds/), which the step [geometric realization of weight one](/step/geo-one-holds/) uses.
