This theorem makes, for each $q \geq 1$, the commutative ring $\bigoplus_{k \geq 0} H^{2k}(E, \mathbb{Z}/q(k))$ of a space $E$ on which $q$ is invertible, with cup product as product and pullback as ring maps. The construction of [power-sum classes](/entry/s57/) uses this ring to state the relation of Chern classes on a projective bundle and to apply Newton's identities. Below this theorem are 1,049 declarations.

## Statement

The spaces $E$, the groups $H^n(E, \mathbb{Z}/q(i))$ and the condition "$q$ is invertible on $E$" are as in [the entry above](/entry/s57/).

**Theorem.** For each $q \geq 1$ there is a family of data, one for each space $E$ on which $q$ is invertible:

1. a commutative ring $R(E)$;
2. for each $k \geq 0$, additive maps $\iota_k \colon H^{2k}(E, \mathbb{Z}/q(k)) \to R(E)$ and $\pi_k \colon R(E) \to H^{2k}(E, \mathbb{Z}/q(k))$ with $\pi_k \iota_k = \mathrm{id}$;
3. $\iota_0(1) = 1$, $\iota_{k+l}(a \cup b) = \iota_k(a)\, \iota_l(b)$, and $\iota_k(x^{\cup k}) = \iota_1(x)^k$ for $x \in H^2(E, \mathbb{Z}/q(1))$;
4. for each map $f \colon E \to E'$, a ring map $f^* \colon R(E') \to R(E)$ with $f^* \iota_k = \iota_k f^*$ and $\pi_k f^* = f^* \pi_k$.

In Lean this is `LeafRing`: for each $q$, the structure `Collected q` is nonempty.

## Proof

The proof is `LeafRing_of s0 s41`, which is `etale_even_cohomology_graded_commRing_of_cup_ring_axioms`. It takes two inputs.

- `s0`, `DivisorNaturality`: pullback commutes with cup product, keeps the unit class $1 \in H^0(E, \mathbb{Z}/q(0))$ when $q$ is invertible, and commutes with $c_1$ of line bundles and with Kummer classes of units.
- [`s41`](/entry/s41/), `DivisorRingCompatibility`: the cup product has $1$ as a left unit when $q$ is invertible, is associative, and is graded commutative, $a \cup b = (-1)^{nm}\, b \cup a$ for $a$ in degree $n$ and $b$ in degree $m$.

Put $A_k = H^{2k}(E, \mathbb{Z}/q(k))$. Cup product maps $A_k \times A_l$ to $A_{k+l}$. Since all degrees are even, the sign $(-1)^{(2k)(2l)}$ is $1$, and the three ring laws make $A_\bullet$ a graded commutative ring (`gcr`). Then $R(E) = \bigoplus_k A_k$ with $\iota_k$ the inclusion of a summand and $\pi_k$ the projection. A right unit law follows from the left unit law and commutativity. Pullback is additive and multiplicative by `DivisorNaturality`, so it gives the ring map `mapOf`. The identity for $x^{\cup k}$ is an induction on $k$. The rest is bookkeeping with casts between equal degrees.
