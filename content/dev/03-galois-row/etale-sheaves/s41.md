This theorem puts three laws of the cup product on étale cohomology with coefficients $\mathbb{Z}/q(i)$ into one conjunction. It only packages them: the proof makes a triple of three earlier results. The [even cohomology ring](/entry/s42/) uses the conjunction. Below this theorem are 1,025 declarations, nearly all of them below the three laws.

## Statement

The spaces $E$ and the groups $H^n(E, \mathbb{Z}/q(i))$ are as in [the power-sum entry](/entry/s57/). For each $q \geq 1$ and each space $E$:

1. if $q$ is invertible on $E$, then $1 \cup a = a$ for each class $a$, where $1 \in H^0(E, \mathbb{Z}/q(0))$ is the unit class;
2. $(a \cup b) \cup c = a \cup (b \cup c)$;
3. $a \cup b = (-1)^{nm}\, b \cup a$ for $a \in H^n(E, \mathbb{Z}/q(i))$ and $b \in H^m(E, \mathbb{Z}/q(j))$.

The equalities hold after the identification of groups with equal indices, such as $H^{0+n}(E, \mathbb{Z}/q(0+i))$ with $H^n(E, \mathbb{Z}/q(i))$.

## Proof

The proof is `LeafA6ringCut.goal_of s38 s39 s40`, which is `fun q _ E => ⟨h5 q E, h6 q E, h7 q E⟩`. The three facts that do the work are:

- `s38`, the unit law, from `LeafRingUnitPf.LeafRingUnit_of`. It uses seven earlier lines, among them the uniqueness of the twisted pairing of resolutions that defines the cup product (`s12`) and a cocycle for the unit class (`s16`).
- `s39`, associativity, `Fix.CupProductTensor.ringAssoc`.
- `s40`, graded commutativity, `RingCommutativity.goal_of`, which is `Fix.CupProductTensor.ringComm'`.

We have not unfolded the proofs of these three.
