This theorem makes, for each $k > 0$, a characteristic class $s_k$ of vector bundles in étale cohomology with coefficients $\mathbb{Z}/q(k)$. The class is natural, additive in direct sums, and equal to $c_1(L)^k$ on a line bundle $L$. Its input is the projective bundle formula. [a0](/entry/a0/) and [intersectionNumber_baseChange](/entry/d2-intersection-number-base-change/) are copies of it, and [the theorem above them](/entry/etale-duality-trace-isomorphism/) supplies the formula. Below this theorem are 1,783 declarations.

## Statement

The spaces are simplicial presheaves $E$ of sets on schemes. $H^n(E, \mathbb{Z}/q(i))$ is étale sheaf cohomology of the chain complex of $E$ with coefficients in the Tate twist. $q$ is invertible on $E$ if it is a unit on each scheme $U$ where $E$ has a $0$-simplex. A rank $N$ vector bundle is a map $V \colon E \to B\mathrm{GL}_N$, the nerve of the groupoid $\mathrm{GL}_N(\Gamma(U, \mathcal{O}))$. Let $P_N$ be the nerve of the groupoid whose objects are unimodular vectors $v \in \Gamma(U, \mathcal{O})^N$ and whose maps $v \to w$ are pairs $(g, \lambda) \in \mathrm{GL}_N \times \mathbb{G}_m$ with $gv = \lambda w$. The projective bundle $\pi \colon \mathbb{P}(V) \to E$ is the pullback of $P_N \to B\mathrm{GL}_N$ along $V$. The factor $\lambda$ gives a line bundle on $\mathbb{P}(V)$, and $\xi \in H^2(\mathbb{P}(V), \mathbb{Z}/q(1))$ is its Kummer class $c_1$.

Let $\mathrm{D1}$ be the projective bundle formula: if $q$ is invertible on $E$, then for all $n, i$ the map
$$\bigoplus_{j=0}^{N-1} H^{n-2j}(E, \mathbb{Z}/q(i-j)) \to H^n(\mathbb{P}(V), \mathbb{Z}/q(i)), \qquad (a_j) \mapsto \sum_j \pi^* a_j \cup \xi^j$$
is bijective. Let $\mathrm{D2}$ say: for each $q \geq 1$ and $k > 0$ there are classes $s_k(V) \in H^{2k}(E, \mathbb{Z}/q(k))$ with $f^* s_k(V) = s_k(f^* V)$, $s_k(V \oplus W) = s_k(V) + s_k(W)$ and $s_k(L) = c_1(L)^k$ for a line bundle $L$, each when $q$ is invertible on the space.

**Theorem.** If 22 statements $\mathrm{A1}, \dots, \mathrm{C5}, \mathrm{D1}$ hold, then $\mathrm{D2}$ holds.

Only $\mathrm{D1}$ is used. The other 21 are axioms of étale cohomology that the proof never reads.

## Proof

This is the construction of Chern classes by Grothendieck (La théorie des classes de Chern, Bull. Soc. Math. France 86, 1958), followed by Newton's power sums, in six facts.

1. [`s42`](/entry/s42/): a commutative ring $R(E)$ with each $H^{2k}(E, \mathbb{Z}/q(k))$ as a summand, cup product as product, and pullback as ring map.
2. `s43`, `LeafChern`: given $\mathrm{D1}$, each $V$ on a space where $q$ is invertible has a unique sequence $\gamma_j(V) \in H^{2j}(E, \mathbb{Z}/q(j))$ with $\gamma_0 = 1$, $\gamma_j = 0$ for $j > N$, and $\sum_{j=0}^{N} \pi^* \gamma_j(V)\, \xi^{N-j} = 0$ in $R(\mathbb{P}(V))$.
3. `s44`, `LeafNat`: this relation pulls back, so $f^* \gamma_j(V) = \gamma_j(f^* V)$ by uniqueness.
4. `s54`, `LeafWhitney`: the Whitney formula $\gamma(V \oplus W) = \gamma(V)\, \gamma(W)$ for $\gamma(V) = \sum_j \gamma_j(V) t^j$.
5. `s55`, `LeafLine`: for a line bundle, $\gamma_1(L) = -c_1(L)$. So $\gamma(L) = 1 - c_1(L)\,t$.
6. `s56`, `LeafNewton`: for $c(t) = \sum_j c_j t^j$ with $c_0 = 1$, let $\mathrm{newton}_k(c)$ be the coefficient of $t^k$ in $-t\, c'(t)/c(t)$. It commutes with ring maps, is additive on products, and gives $x^k$ for $c = 1 - xt$.

Put $s_k(V) = \mathrm{newton}_k(\gamma(V))$, read in degree $k$ of $R(E)$. Then 3 and 6 give naturality, 4 and 6 give additivity, and 5 and 6 give $s_k(L) = c_1(L)^k$.
