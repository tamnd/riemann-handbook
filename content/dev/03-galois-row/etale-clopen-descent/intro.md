This chapter collects the results of the Galois row that come from descent along finite étale maps and from fixed points of finite étale correspondences. It proves that the clopen subsets of a finite étale scheme are the points of a finite étale scheme, and from this it builds the finite étale scheme of subgroups of the torsion of an [abelian scheme](/learn/elliptic-curves/#abelian-varieties-and-abelian-schemes) and the isogenies of a Harris and Taylor [Shimura variety](/learn/geometry/#shimura-varieties-and-toroidal-compactifications) with a given kernel. In étale cohomology it proves that a class that vanishes at all strictly henselian points vanishes on an étale neighbourhood, and the projective bundle formula for the trivial bundle. Its longest part is the holomorphic Lefschetz formula for a correspondence given by two finite étale maps to a smooth projective variety over $\mathbb{C}$, with the weighted count of fixed points as a sum over marked pairs. The chapter needs from Volume 1 the [schemes and finite étale maps](/learn/geometry/#schemes-and-varieties) and the [complex manifolds and their cohomology](/learn/geometry/#complex-manifolds), where the Atiyah and Bott formula is stated.

The results feed [the representability of isogenies of type $T_j$](/entry/isogeny-rep/), [the finiteness of étale cohomology](/entry/etale-topology-top/) and [the projective bundle formula on discrete spaces](/entry/pbf-disc/). The Lefschetz formula and the count of fixed points are the two inputs of [the theorem that Hecke eigensystems occur in Dolbeault cohomology](/entry/ks-hodge-of-geo/). That theorem is used in the step [GeoOne holds](/step/geo-one-holds/) of [the Galois row](/proof/galois-row/).
