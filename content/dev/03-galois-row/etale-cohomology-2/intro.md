This chapter collects two groups of results of the Galois row. The first group is in étale cohomology. It proves that the tensor product with a bounded below complex of flat sheaves keeps quasi-isomorphisms, that a unit has a Kummer class that is natural and additive, and that the cohomology of a filtered limit of schemes is the colimit of the cohomology groups. The second group is about division in compact groups. It proves a key criterion in linear algebra for a group of matrices with a lacunary Lie element. From this criterion it shows that a continuous semisimple representation $\rho$ of a compact group, whose elements are killed by polynomials of degree $n$ on a dense set, has a subrepresentation $r$ of dimension $n$ such that $\rho$ embeds into a sum of copies of $r$. The chapter needs from Volume 1 the [tensor products of modules](/learn/algebra/#tensor-products-and-coinvariants), [flat modules](/learn/algebra/#rings-ideals-and-modules), [matrices and characteristic polynomials](/learn/algebra/#matrices), [schemes and étale maps](/learn/geometry/#schemes-and-varieties), [topological groups](/learn/analysis/#topological-groups-and-covering-maps) and [Lie algebras](/learn/analysis/#lie-groups-and-lie-algebras).

The tensor product result and the Kummer class are inputs to [the ring laws of the cup product](/entry/s41/) and [the naturality of the Kummer class](/entry/nat-kum/), and so to [the power-sum classes of vector bundles](/entry/s57/). The continuity of cohomology feeds [the finiteness of étale cohomology](/entry/etale-topology-top/) in the step [GeoOne holds](/step/geo-one-holds/). The small subrepresentation feeds [the division of a Galois representation](/entry/div-set-holds/), which is used in the step [local-global compatibility from a geometric lift](/step/galois-local-global-strong-of-geometry-lift/) of [the Galois row](/proof/galois-row/).
