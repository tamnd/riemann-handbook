## Class groups in cyclotomic towers

This section proves that the $l$-rank of the class group stays bounded along the cyclotomic $\mathbb{Z}_l$-tower of an abelian number field. This is the theorem of Ferrero and Washington in the form of a bound for the $l$-rank. The proof follows Sinnott: a Stickelberger element kills the minus part of the class group, Sinnott's nonvanishing lemma shows that the Stickelberger element generates a large ideal modulo $l$, and a reflection argument passes from the minus part to the whole group. The result is used in the next section for the part at $l = 2$ and for the parts over cyclotomic layers of the [window bound](/entry/vwin-of-hypotheses-h-e-alt/), and directly by the count of [Theorem 8.16.7](/entry/of-four-alt/). The background is in [Iwasawa theory](/learn/galois-cohomology/#iwasawa-theory) and [ideals and the class group](/learn/number-fields/#ideals-and-the-class-group).

### Notation

For a finite abelian group $C$ (written multiplicatively) and a prime $l$, put $C^l = \{x^l : x \in C\}$ and $C[l] = \{x \in C : x^l = 1\}$. The index $[C : C^l]$ is $l^{r}$, where $r$ is the $l$-rank of $C$. For an endomorphism $\phi$ of $C$ we write $(1 - \phi)C$ for the subgroup of the elements $x \phi(x)^{-1}$. For a number field $K$, $\mathrm{Cl}(K)$ is the class group of its ring of integers.

Let $M > 2$ and let $L = \mathbb{Q}(\zeta_M)$ be a [[cyclotomic field|NN77VYSGXR]]. For $t \in (\mathbb{Z}/M)^\times$ let $\sigma_t$ be the automorphism with $\sigma_t(\zeta_M) = \zeta_M^t$. We let $t$ act on $\mathrm{Cl}(L)$ by the automorphism $\sigma_t^{-1}$. This makes $\mathrm{Cl}(L)$ a module over the group ring $\mathbb{Z}[(\mathbb{Z}/M)^\times]$. We write $[t]$ for the group element $t$ in the group ring and $J = [-1]$. The field $L$ is a CM field, and $\sigma_{-1}$ is its complex conjugation.

**Definition 4.1.1 (Half Stickelberger element).** Let $M > 2$ and let $c > 1$ be odd and prime to $M$. For $s \in \mathbb{Z}/M$ let $v(s) \in \{0, \dots, M - 1\}$ be its representative. Put
$$g_c(M, s) = \Big\lfloor \frac{c \, v(c^{-1} s)}{M} \Big\rfloor - \frac{c - 1}{2},$$
and
$$\theta_H(M, c) = \sum_{s \in (\mathbb{Z}/M)^\times, \ 2 v(s) < M} g_c(M, s) \, [s] \in \mathbb{Z}[(\mathbb{Z}/M)^\times].$$

The sum runs over half of the units, one from each pair $\{s, -s\}$. The full Stickelberger element with the factor $c - [c]$ is $\theta_H(M, c)(1 - J)$ up to the sign convention, so the next statement is the theorem of Stickelberger in a form with integral coefficients.

**Theorem 4.1.2 (Stickelberger).** Let $M > 2$, let $c > 1$ be odd and prime to $M$, and let $L = \mathbb{Q}(\zeta_M)$. Then $\theta_H(M, c)(1 - J)$ kills $\mathrm{Cl}(L)$.

Lean: `UpperTheory.V.FW.stickH`.

*Proof.* This is Stickelberger's theorem for the field $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_M)$ (Washington, Theorem 6.10), written with the half sum. $\square$

**Lemma 4.1.3 (Sinnott's nonvanishing).** Let $l$ be a prime, let $f > 1$ be prime to $l$, and let $c > 1$ be odd and prime to $fl$. Put $e_l = 2$ if $l = 2$ and $e_l = 1$ if $l$ is odd. There are $N \geq 0$ and $n_0 \geq 2$ with this property. For each $n \geq n_0$, put $M = f l^n$. There is $\gamma \in (\mathbb{Z}/M)^\times$ with $\gamma = 1 + f l^{e_l}$, the subgroup $\langle \gamma \rangle$ has index $\varphi(f l^{e_l})$, and
$$([\gamma] - 1)^N \in \big(\theta_H(M, c), \ 1 + J, \ l\big) \subset \mathbb{Z}[(\mathbb{Z}/M)^\times].$$

Lean: `UpperTheory.V.FW.memZ` (also `UpperTheory.V.FW.nonvan`, `UpperTheory.V.FW.sinnott`).

*Proof.* The quotient of $\mathbb{F}_l[(\mathbb{Z}/M)^\times]$ by $1 + J$ splits, after an extension of scalars, into components indexed by the odd characters $\chi$ of the part of $(\mathbb{Z}/M)^\times$ of order prime to $l$. Each component is a group ring of an $l$-group, which is a local ring with maximal ideal generated by the augmentation ideal. So the claim holds if, for each odd $\chi$ with values in a field of characteristic $l$, the $\chi$-component of $\theta_H(M, c)$ is nonzero already at a fixed finite level $l^{n_0}$, with $n_0$ independent of $n$. The coefficients of $\theta_H$ are values of the function $s \mapsto \lfloor c v(c^{-1}s)/M \rfloor$, which is a sum of fractional parts. Sinnott's lemma states the following. Let $\eta_1, \dots, \eta_k \in \mathbb{Z}_l^\times$ be such that no ratio $\eta_i / \eta_j$ with $i \neq j$ is rational, and let $N \neq 0$. Then there is $n_0$ such that for $n \geq n_0$, each commutative ring $k$, each $P : \{0, \dots, N - 1\} \to k$ and each $\alpha : \mathbb{Z}/l^n \to k$ with $\alpha(y) - \alpha(y - N) = P(y)$ for $y$ in the range of $P$ (and $0$ elsewhere) and $\sum_i \alpha(\eta_i^{-1} y) = 0$ for all $y$, the function $P$ is zero. Applied to the twisted sums of the coefficients of $\theta_H$, it gives the nonvanishing. $\square$

**Lemma 4.1.4 (Counting in the minus part).** Let $C$ be a finite abelian group, let $J$ and $\gamma$ be commuting endomorphisms of $C$ that come from a group action, with $J^2 = 1$, and let $\theta$ be an element of the group ring with $\theta(1 - J) C = 1$. If $(\gamma - 1)^N \in (\theta, 1 + J, l)$, then
$$\#\big((1 - J)C \cap C[l]\big) \leq [C : C^l (1 - \gamma) C]^N.$$

Lean: `UpperTheory.V.FW.core`.

*Proof.* Write $(\gamma - 1)^N = u \theta + v(1 + J) + l z$. Multiply by $1 - J$. Since $(1 + J)(1 - J) = 1 - J^2 = 0$ and $\theta(1 - J)$ kills $C$, the element $(1 - J)(1 - \gamma)^N$ maps $C$ into $((1 - J)C)^l$. Put $A = (1 - J)C$. Then $(1 - \gamma)^N A \subset A^l$. For a finite abelian group, $\#A[l] = \#(A/A^l)$, and $A[l] = A \cap C[l]$. The group $A / A^l$ is killed by $(1 - \gamma)^N$, so the filtration by the images of $(1 - \gamma)^i$ has $N$ steps, each a quotient of $A / A^l (1 - \gamma) A$. The map $1 - J$ from $C$ onto $A$ commutes with $\gamma$, so it maps $C / C^l (1 - \gamma) C$ onto $A / A^l (1 - \gamma) A$. These steps give the bound. $\square$

**Lemma 4.1.5 (Ambiguous classes).** Let $l$ be a prime and let $f$ be prime to $l$. There is $B$ with this property. For each $n \geq 2$, each $L = \mathbb{Q}(\zeta_{f l^n})$ and each $\gamma \in (\mathbb{Z}/f l^n)^\times$ with $\gamma = 1 + f l^{e_l}$,
$$[\mathrm{Cl}(L) : \mathrm{Cl}(L)^l (1 - \gamma) \mathrm{Cl}(L)] \leq B.$$

Lean: `UpperTheory.V.FW.ambCyc`.

*Proof.* Let $C = \mathrm{Cl}(L)$ and $L_0 = \mathbb{Q}(\zeta_{f l^{e_l}})$. Then $\sigma_\gamma$ generates the cyclic group $G = \mathrm{Gal}(L / L_0)$ of order a power of $l$. The quotient $C / C^l (1 - \gamma) C$ is the group of $G$-coinvariants of $C / C^l$. By [global class field theory](/learn/class-field-theory/#global-class-field-theory), $C / C^l$ is the Galois group of the maximal unramified abelian extension $H$ of $L$ of exponent $l$, and the coinvariants are the Galois group over $L$ of the largest subextension $H' \subset H$ on which $G$ acts trivially. Then $\mathrm{Gal}(H' / L_0)$ is a central extension of a cyclic group, so it is abelian. The extension $H' / L_0$ is unramified outside $l$ and the infinite places, since $L / L_0$ is ramified only over $l$. So $\mathrm{Gal}(H'/L)$ is a subgroup of a quotient of the Galois group of the maximal abelian pro-$l$ extension of $L_0$ unramified outside $l$ and $\infty$. That group is a finitely generated $\mathbb{Z}_l$-module, generated by $d$ elements with $d$ independent of $n$. A subgroup of a quotient of it is generated by $d$ elements, and $\mathrm{Gal}(H'/L)$ has exponent $l$. So the index is at most $B = l^d$. $\square$

**Lemma 4.1.6 (Reflection).** Let $K$ be a CM field with complex conjugation $J$, let $l$ be an odd prime, and suppose $K$ contains a primitive $l$-th root of unity. Let $C = \mathrm{Cl}(K)$ and $C^-[l] = \{x \in C[l] : Jx = x^{-1}\}$. Then
$$[C : C^l (1 - J) C] \leq l \cdot \# C^-[l], \qquad [C : C^l] \leq l \cdot \#\big((1 - J) C \cap C[l]\big)^2 .$$

Lean: `UpperTheory.V.FW.reflOdd`, `UpperTheory.V.FW.pOdd`.

*Proof.* The first inequality is a reflection theorem of Leopoldt type: Kummer theory over $K$ pairs the plus part of $C / C^l$ with the minus part of $C[l]$ through the unramified Kummer extensions, and the plus part of $C / C^l$ is $C / C^l (1 - J) C$. Washington, Chapter 10, §10.2 gives the theorem in the form $r^+ \leq r^-$ of $l$-ranks. For the second, $l$ is odd, so $C / C^l$ splits into the eigenspaces of $J$, and $(1 - J)C$ meets $C[l]$ in the minus part of $C[l]$, whose order is the order of the minus part of $C / C^l$. So $[C : C^l]$ is the product of $[C : C^l (1 - J)C] \leq l \cdot \#C^-[l]$ and $\#C^-[l]$, and $C^-[l] = (1 - J)C \cap C[l]$. $\square$

**Lemma 4.1.7 (The case $l = 2$).** Let $f > 1$ be odd, let $n \geq 2$ and let $L = \mathbb{Q}(\zeta_{f 2^n})$, with $C = \mathrm{Cl}(L)$. Let $c > 1$ be odd and prime to $f 2^n$, and let $\gamma$ with $([\gamma] - 1)^N \in (\theta_H(f 2^n, c), 1 + J, 2)$. Let $K^- = \{x : Jx = x^{-1}\}$. Then
$$[K^- : (1 - J) C] \leq \big(2^{[(\mathbb{Z}/f 2^n)^\times : \langle \gamma \rangle]}\big)^N, \qquad [C : C^2] \leq \big([K^- : (1 - J)C] \cdot \#((1 - J)C \cap C[2])\big)^2.$$

Lean: `UpperTheory.V.FW.sgnCyc`, `UpperTheory.V.FW.pTwo`.

*Proof.* For the first bound, the quotient $K^- / (1 - J) C$ is a Tate cohomology group of $\langle J \rangle$ acting on $C$. Since $f > 1$ and $n \geq 2$, the extension $L / L^+$ is unramified at all finite places, and this group is controlled by the signs at the real places of units of the real subfield $L^+$. A unit attached to $\theta_H$ shows that the quotient is a module over $\mathbb{F}_2[(\mathbb{Z}/f 2^n)^\times]$ killed by $([\gamma] - 1)^N$, with $\gamma$-coinvariants of order at most $2$ to the index of $\langle \gamma \rangle$. For the second bound, let $A = (1 - J)C \subset K^-$ and let $r$ denote the $2$-rank. The map $1 - J$ sends $C[2]$ into $A \cap C[2]$ with kernel $C[2] \cap K^-$, since $x = x^{-1}$ on $C[2]$. So $r(C) \leq r(K^-) + r(A)$. From $A \subset K^-$ we get $r(K^-) \leq r(A) + r(K^-/A)$, and $2^{r(K^-/A)} \leq [K^- : A]$. Since $2^{r(A)} = \#(A \cap C[2])$, this gives $[C : C^2] \leq [K^- : A] \cdot \#(A \cap C[2])^2$, which is at most the square in the statement. $\square$

### The bound in the cyclotomic fields

**Theorem 4.1.8 (Bounded $l$-rank in the tower of $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_f)$).** Let $l$ be a prime and let $f > 1$ be prime to $l$. There are $B$ and $n_0$ such that for each $n \geq n_0$ the field $L = \mathbb{Q}(\zeta_{f l^n})$ is totally complex and
$$[\mathrm{Cl}(L) : \mathrm{Cl}(L)^l] \leq B.$$

Lean: `UpperTheory.V.FW.cyc`.

*Proof.* Put $c = 2fl + 1$. It is odd, greater than $1$ and prime to $fl$, and so prime to $M = f l^n$ for every $n$. Take $N$ and $n_0 \geq 2$ from the Lemma on Sinnott's nonvanishing, and $B_0$ from the Lemma on ambiguous classes. Let $n \geq n_0$ and $C = \mathrm{Cl}(L)$. Then $M > 2$, so $L$ is a CM field and hence totally complex, and $J$ acts as complex conjugation. Take $\gamma$ from Sinnott's lemma. By the Theorem of Stickelberger, $\theta_H(M, c)(1 - J)$ kills $C$. The Lemma on counting in the minus part and the Lemma on ambiguous classes give
$$\#\big((1 - J) C \cap C[l]\big) \leq [C : C^l (1 - \gamma) C]^N \leq B_0^N .$$
If $l$ is odd, $L$ contains $\zeta_l$, and the Lemma on reflection gives $[C : C^l] \leq l \, B_0^{2N}$. If $l = 2$, the index of $\langle \gamma \rangle$ is $\varphi(4f)$, and the Lemma on the case $l = 2$ gives
$$[C : C^2] \leq \big(2^{\varphi(4f) N} B_0^N\big)^2 .$$
Both bounds are independent of $n$. $\square$

### Descent to an abelian field

**Lemma 4.1.9 (Embedding in a cyclotomic tower).** Let $l$ be a prime and let $K$ be a finite abelian extension of $\mathbb{Q}$. There are $f > 1$ prime to $l$ and $D \geq 1$ such that for all $n, n_0$ and each field $K' = K(\zeta_{l^n})$ there are $m \geq n_0$ and a field $L = \mathbb{Q}(\zeta_{f l^m})$ containing $K'$ with $[L : K'] \leq D \, l^{n_0}$.

Lean: `UpperTheory.V.FW.embed`.

*Proof.* By the theorem of Kronecker and Weber, $K$ lies in $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_{N})$ for some $N$. Write $N = f_0 l^a$ with $f_0$ prime to $l$, and enlarge $f_0$ to $f > 1$. Take $m = \max(n, a, n_0)$. Then $K' \subset \mathbb{Q}(\zeta_{f l^m})$, and the degree $[L : K']$ is bounded by $[\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_f) : \mathbb{Q}]$ times the part of $l^m$ that $K'$ does not already contain, which is at most a constant times $l^{n_0}$. $\square$

**Lemma 4.1.10 (Descent of the $l$-rank).** Let $K \subset L$ be number fields with $L$ totally complex. Then
$$[\mathrm{Cl}(K) : \mathrm{Cl}(K)^l] \leq [L : K] \cdot [\mathrm{Cl}(L) : \mathrm{Cl}(L)^l].$$

Lean: `UpperTheory.V.FW.desc`.

*Proof.* Let $\mathrm{N} : \mathrm{Cl}(L) \to \mathrm{Cl}(K)$ be the norm. By class field theory its cokernel is the Galois group of $L \cap H_K$ over $K$, where $H_K$ is the Hilbert class field, so the cokernel has order at most $[L : K]$. Then $[\mathrm{Cl}(K) : \mathrm{Cl}(K)^l]$ is at most the index of $\mathrm{N}(\mathrm{Cl}(L)) \mathrm{Cl}(K)^l$ times the order of $\mathrm{N}(\mathrm{Cl}(L)) / \mathrm{N}(\mathrm{Cl}(L)) \cap \mathrm{Cl}(K)^l$, and the second factor is a quotient of $\mathrm{Cl}(L) / \mathrm{Cl}(L)^l$. $\square$

**Theorem 4.1.11 (Ferrero and Washington, bounded rank form).** Let $l$ be a prime and let $K$ be a finite abelian extension of $\mathbb{Q}$ that contains a primitive root of unity of order $4$ if $l = 2$ and of order $l$ if $l$ is odd. There is $r \geq 0$ such that for each $n \geq 0$ and each extension $K'$ of $K$ generated by the $l^n$-th roots of unity,
$$[\mathrm{Cl}(K') : \mathrm{Cl}(K')^l] \leq l^r .$$

Lean: `UpperTheory.V.FW.ferreroWashington`.

*Proof.* Take $f$ and $D$ from the Lemma on embedding, and $B$ and $n_0$ from the Theorem on bounded $l$-rank in the tower of $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_f)$. Put $r = D l^{n_0} B$. Let $K' = K(\zeta_{l^n})$, and take $m \geq n_0$ and $L = \mathbb{Q}(\zeta_{f l^m}) \supset K'$ with $[L : K'] \leq D l^{n_0}$. The field $L$ is totally complex with $[\mathrm{Cl}(L) : \mathrm{Cl}(L)^l] \leq B$. The Lemma on descent gives
$$[\mathrm{Cl}(K') : \mathrm{Cl}(K')^l] \leq D \, l^{n_0} B = r < l^r . \qquad \square$$

**Remark 4.1.12.** The classical theorem says that the Iwasawa invariant $\mu$ of the cyclotomic $\mathbb{Z}_l$-extension of an abelian field is $0$. By the formula of Iwasawa for the orders of the $l$-parts of the class groups of the layers, this is equivalent to a bound for the $l$-ranks of these class groups that is independent of the layer. The statement above is this bound, for the fields $K(\zeta_{l^n})$.

**Remark 4.1.13 (Formalization).** The theorem of Ferrero and Washington is a hypothesis of the window bound, and [FerreroWashington.lean](lean:Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/FerreroWashington.lean#L104) proves it, so the published input is discharged. The root of unity hypothesis is introduced in the proof and never used (the seventh variable of the `intro` at line 106). The bound $r = D l^{n_0} B$ is crude: the proof shows $[\mathrm{Cl}(K') : \mathrm{Cl}(K')^l] \leq r$ and then uses $r < l^r$. The definitions of the twist and the projection in `UpperTheory.V.FW.nonvan` and the proofs of `UpperTheory.V.FW.ambCyc`, `UpperTheory.V.FW.sgnCyc` and `UpperTheory.V.FW.sinnott` are not unfolded here; the proofs given for these lemmas are the classical arguments, not a transcription.
