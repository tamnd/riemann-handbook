This chapter proves the Iwasawa theory inputs of the window bound and the deformation inputs of the symplectic and unitary cases. It proves the theorem of Ferrero and Washington in the form of a bound for the $l$-rank of class groups in cyclotomic towers, and from it the part at $2$ of the window bound. For odd $l$ it bounds the minus part by the Euler system of Gauss sums: Kolyvagin's induction bounds a Selmer group of a twisting character by a Stickelberger value, and this value is a generalized Bernoulli number. Then it builds frames and even presentations for polarized ordinary lifts of rank three, proves that every component of type TI of the space of lifts is good when one seed component is, constructs graded models of symplectic and unitary deformation rings with the trace inequality of the Ribet method, and gives block extensions and the space of lifts in rank four. From Volume 1 the chapter uses [profinite groups](/learn/galois-representations/#profinite-groups), [decomposition groups, inertia and Frobenius](/learn/galois-representations/#decomposition-groups-inertia-and-frobenius), [the cyclotomic character and Tate modules](/learn/galois-representations/#the-cyclotomic-character-and-tate-modules), [continuous cohomology](/learn/galois-cohomology/#continuous-cohomology), [Dirichlet characters](/learn/l-functions/#dirichlet-characters), [local rings and dimension](/learn/commutative-algebra/#local-rings-and-dimension), [deformation functors](/learn/deformations-patching/#deformation-functors) and [symplectic groups](/learn/groups/#general-linear-and-symplectic-groups).

The results go to the modularity chapter: to [the window bound from the hypotheses](/entry/vwin-of-hypotheses-h-e-alt/), to [the window bound from four counts](/entry/of-four-alt/), to [the main theorem for big images](/entry/main-big/), to [the unramified one-character theorem](/entry/one-char-unram-u/), to [the rank four theorem](/entry/main4-alt/) and to [the theorem on finiteness and irreducibility](/entry/fin0-no-end-all-irr-of-good-con-purity-aut/). Through the window bound they are used in the step [VWin from the rank n inputs](/step/v-window-of-depth-zero-residual-bc-cm/) of the proof, which gives [VWin from Row0b](/step/vwin-of-row/).
