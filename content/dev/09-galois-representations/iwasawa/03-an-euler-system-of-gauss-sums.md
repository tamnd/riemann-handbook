## An Euler system of Gauss sums

This section builds the Euler system that bounds the eigenspaces of Tate-Shafarevich groups by Bernoulli numbers. The system is the system of Gauss sums of Kolyvagin and Rubin: for each squarefree product $d$ of admissible primes, an element $X(d)$ of $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_{Fd})$ whose ideal is a Stickelberger combination of the conjugates of one prime over a fixed prime $r \equiv 1 \pmod F$. The norm and congruence relations of the system hold up to roots of unity. Kolyvagin's derivative operators then turn the system into a family of Kummer classes with values in $O \otimes \mathbb{Q}_l/\mathbb{Z}_l$, ramified in a controlled way. These classes drive the induction of the next section, which proves the bound of [Theorem 4.4.4](/entry/ml-count/). The background is in [Euler systems and Kato](/learn/galois-cohomology/#euler-systems-and-kato), [roots of unity](/learn/algebra/#roots-of-unity) and [the splitting of primes](/learn/number-fields/#the-splitting-of-primes).

### Notation

Fix an algebraic closure $\overline{\mathbb{Q}}$ and put $G_\mathbb{Q} = \mathrm{Gal}(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}/\mathbb{Q})$. For $N \geq 1$ let $G_N \subset G_\mathbb{Q}$ be the subgroup that fixes the $N$-th roots of unity. An element $g \in G_\mathbb{Q}$ *lifts* $t \in (\mathbb{Z}/N)^\times$ if $g(\zeta) = \zeta^t$ for every $N$-th root of unity $\zeta$. A place $A$ of $\overline{\mathbb{Q}}$ (a valuation ring of $\overline{\mathbb{Q}}$) *lies over* $n$ if $n$ is not a unit at $A$. For such a place, $D_A$ and $I_A$ are its [decomposition and inertia groups](/learn/galois-representations/#decomposition-groups-inertia-and-frobenius) in $G_\mathbb{Q}$, and a Frobenius at $A$ over $q$ is an element of $D_A$ that acts as $x \mapsto x^q$ on the residue field.

For $q$ prime and $\tau \in G_\mathbb{Q}$, put
$$\mathrm{N}_{q, \tau}(u) = \prod_{i = 0}^{q - 2} \tau^i(u), \qquad \mathrm{D}_{q, \tau}(u) = \prod_{i = 0}^{q - 2} \tau^i(u)^i .$$
These are the norm and Kolyvagin's derivative for the cyclic group of order $q - 1$ generated by $\tau$. For a list $L = (q_1, \dots, q_k)$ of primes and a choice $\tau_q$ for each $q$, the iterated derivative is $\mathrm{D}_L = \mathrm{D}_{q_1, \tau_{q_1}} \circ \cdots \circ \mathrm{D}_{q_k, \tau_{q_k}}$, with $\mathrm{D}_{()} = \mathrm{id}$.

**Definition 4.3.1 (Stickelberger exponents).** For $M \geq 1$, $b \geq 0$ and $t \in \mathbb{Z}/M$, let $v(t) \in \{0, \dots, M - 1\}$ be the representative and put
$$\vartheta_{M, b}(t) = \Big\lfloor \frac{v(t) \, b}{M} \Big\rfloor - \Big\lfloor \frac{v(-t) \, b}{M} \Big\rfloor .$$
Then $\vartheta_{M, b}(-t) = -\vartheta_{M, b}(t)$.

With $\theta_M = \sum_t \{t / M\} \sigma_t^{-1}$, a direct computation gives $\sum_t \vartheta_{M, b}(t) \sigma_t^{-1} = (1 - \sigma_{-1})(b - \sigma_b)\theta_M$. So $\vartheta_{M, b}$ is the exponent vector of a Stickelberger element with integral coefficients, made odd.

**Definition 4.3.2 (Gauss sum element).** Let $K = \mathbb{Q}(\zeta_M)$, let $b \geq 0$, and let $\mathfrak{a}$ be an ideal of $\mathcal{O}_K$. An element $X \in K$ is a *Gauss sum element* for $(M, b, \mathfrak{a})$ if
$$X \cdot \sigma_{-1}(X) = 1, \qquad (X) = \prod_{t \in (\mathbb{Z}/M)^\times} \sigma_t(\mathfrak{a})^{\vartheta_{M, b}(t)} .$$

For a prime ideal $\mathfrak{a}$ the product ideal is principal by Stickelberger's theorem, and a Gauss sum $g^{b - \sigma_b}$ divided by its complex conjugate gives such an element.

**Definition 4.3.3 (Admissible primes).** Let $l$, $F$ and $r$ be integers. A prime $q$ is *admissible* for $(l, F, r)$ if $q \equiv 1 \pmod F$, $q \neq r$, and $l$ does not divide the order of $r$ modulo $q$. An integer $d$ is *admissible* if it is squarefree and each of its prime factors is admissible. An *admissible list* is a list of distinct admissible primes.

**Definition 4.3.4 (Euler system up to roots of unity).** Let $X$ be a function from the positive integers to $\overline{\mathbb{Q}}$ and $s$ a function of two integers. Then $(X, s)$ is an *Euler system up to roots of unity* for $(l, F, r)$ if for all admissible $d$:

1. $X(d) \neq 0$ and $X(d)$ is fixed by $G_{Fd}$;
2. $X(d)$ is a unit at every place not over $r$;

and for each prime $q$ with $qd$ admissible:

3. $l$ does not divide $s(d, q)$;
4. (norm relation) for each $\tau \in G_{Fd}$ that lifts a generator of $(\mathbb{Z}/q)^\times$ and each $\phi$ that lifts $q$ in $(\mathbb{Z}/Fd)^\times$, there is $\zeta$ with $\zeta^{2Fqd} = 1$ and
$$\mathrm{N}_{q, \tau}\big(X(qd)\big) = \zeta \Big(\frac{X(d)}{\phi^{-1} X(d)}\Big)^{s(d, q)} ;$$
5. (congruence) for each such $\phi$ there is $\zeta$ with $\zeta^{2Fqd} = 1$ and $X(qd) \equiv \zeta \, \phi^{-1}(X(d))^{s(d, q)}$ modulo every place over $q$.

An *Euler system* is the same with $\zeta = 1$ in 4 and 5.

The relations are those of the classical Euler system of Gauss sums. The factor $X(d)/\phi^{-1}X(d)$ is the Euler factor $1 - \mathrm{Frob}_q^{-1}$ in multiplicative form, and the exponent $s(d, q)$ prime to $l$ is harmless after tensoring with $\mathbb{Z}_l$.

**Definition 4.3.5 (Order at the base place).** Let $A_0$ be a place over $r$, let $u \in \overline{\mathbb{Q}}$ and let $k : (\mathbb{Z}/F)^\times \to \mathbb{Z}$. Then $u$ *has order $k$ at $A_0$* if $v_{A_0}(\sigma u) = k(t) \, v_{A_0}(r)$ for every $t$ and every $\sigma$ that lifts $t$.

**Theorem 4.3.6 (Existence of the Gauss sum system).** Let $l$, $r$ be primes and $F \geq 1$, $b < F$ integers with $r \equiv 1 \pmod F$, and let $A_0$ be a place over $r$. There is an Euler system up to roots of unity $(X, s)$ for $(l, F, r)$ such that $X(1)$ has order $\vartheta_{F, b}$ at $A_0$.

Lean: `UpperTheory.V.ML.laxEx`.

*Proof.* For each $d \geq 1$, let $\mathfrak{a}_d$ be the prime of $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_{Fd})$ below $A_0$, and let $X(d)$ be a Gauss sum element for $(Fd, b, \mathfrak{a}_d)$; it exists by Stickelberger's theorem, as above. Since $X(d) \sigma_{-1}(X(d)) = 1$ and its ideal is supported on primes over $r$, $X(d)$ is a nonzero unit away from $r$ and lies in $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_{Fd})$, which gives 1 and 2. The order at $A_0$ of $X(1)$ is read off from the exponents of its ideal. For the relations, let $qd$ be admissible and put $M = Fd$. The prime $q$ is prime to $M$ and $r \nmid qM$. The relative norm from $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_{qM})$ to $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_M)$ of the prime over $r$ is a power $\mathfrak{a}^{s}$, where $s$ is the order of $r$ modulo $q$ up to the factor of the residue degree. Since $q$ is admissible, $l \nmid s$, which gives 3. Comparing ideals, $\mathrm{N}_{q, \tau}(X(qd))$ and $(X(d)/\phi^{-1}X(d))^{s}$ have the same ideal and both have absolute value $1$ at every complex place, so their ratio is a root of unity in $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_{qM})$, of order dividing $2qM$. This gives 4. The congruence 5 comes from the congruence of Gauss sums modulo the primes over $q$, in which the additive character of order $q$ becomes trivial. $\square$

### From the system to Kummer classes

Let $l$ be an odd prime and let $O$ be a complete discrete valuation ring, finite over $\mathbb{Z}_l$. Put $W = O \otimes \mathbb{Q}_l/\mathbb{Z}_l$, and let $\chi_O : G_\mathbb{Q} \to O^\times$ be the cyclotomic character.

**Definition 4.3.7 (Twisting data).** Let $N_0 \geq 1$ be prime to $l$, let $m \geq 1$ and $F = N_0 l^m$. A character $\alpha : G_\mathbb{Q} \to O^\times$ is a *twisting character* for $(N_0, m)$ if $\alpha(g) \equiv 1 \pmod{l^m}$ for $g \in G_F$, $\alpha(g) \equiv -1 \pmod{l^m}$ for every $g$ that lifts $-1 \in (\mathbb{Z}/F)^\times$, and there is $g_0$ with $\alpha(g_0) - \chi_O(g_0)$ a unit. Its *dual character* is $\alpha^* = \chi_O \alpha^{-1}$.

The last condition says that $\alpha$ and $\chi_O$ are distinct modulo the maximal ideal. It gives the element $g_0 - a_0$ of the group ring, with $a_0 \equiv \chi_O(g_0)$, which projects Kummer classes to the $\alpha^*$-part.

**Definition 4.3.8 (Kolyvagin classes).** Let $\beta : G_\mathbb{Q} \to O^\times$, let $M = l^m$, $n \geq 1$, and let $V$ be an $O$-module. A function $\nu : G_\mathbb{Q} \to V$ is a *Kolyvagin class* for $(M, F, n, \beta)$ if on $G_F$ it is additive and locally constant, $\nu(g u g^{-1}) = \beta(g)\nu(u)$ for $g \in G_\mathbb{Q}$ and $u \in G_F$, $M \nu(u) = 0$, and $\nu$ vanishes on the inertia groups of all places not over $n$.

So the restriction of $\nu$ to $G_F$ is a homomorphism with the equivariance of a class in $H^1(\mathbb{Q}, V(\beta))$, unramified outside $n$.

**Definition 4.3.9 (Kolyvagin system).** Let $A_0$ be a place over $r$ and $w_1 \in W$. A family $\nu_L$ indexed by the admissible lists $L$ is a *Kolyvagin system* for $(l, M, F, r, \beta, A_0, w_1)$ if:

1. each $\nu_L$ is a Kolyvagin class for $(M, F, F r \prod_{q \in L} q, \beta)$;
2. (step) for each admissible list $q :: L$, each place $A$ over $q$ and each Frobenius $\phi$ at $A$, there are $\tau \in I_A$ and a unit $u$ with $\nu_{q :: L}(\tau) = u \, \nu_L(\phi)$;
3. (start) there are $\tau \in I_{A_0}$ and a unit $u$ with $\nu_{()}(\tau) = u \, w_1$.

The step relation is the key property of Kolyvagin's derivative classes: the ramified part of the class at $q$ equals, up to a unit, the value at Frobenius of the previous class.

**Definition 4.3.10 (The Stickelberger value).** Let $\alpha : G_\mathbb{Q} \to O^\times$, let $\sigma_t$ be a lift of $t$ for each $t \in (\mathbb{Z}/F)^\times$, and let $b \geq 0$. Put
$$z(\alpha, \sigma, b) = \sum_{t \in (\mathbb{Z}/F)^\times} \vartheta_{F, b}(t) \, \alpha(\sigma_t)^{-1} \in O .$$

This is the image of the odd Stickelberger element under the character $\alpha^{-1}$. For a Dirichlet character it is $(b - \alpha^{-1}(b))$ times a multiple of a generalized Bernoulli number $B_{1, \alpha^{-1}}$; Section 5 relates it to $B_{k, \psi}$.

**Theorem 4.3.11 (Kolyvagin system of Gauss sums).** Let $l$ be an odd prime, let $\alpha$ be a twisting character for $(N_0, m)$ with $F = N_0 l^m$, let $r$ be a prime with $r \equiv 1 \pmod F$, let $b < F$, and let $A_0$ be a place over $r$. Let $\sigma_t$ lift $t$ for each $t \in (\mathbb{Z}/F)^\times$, and let $w \in W$ with $l^m w = 0$. Then there is a Kolyvagin system $(\nu_L)$ for $(l, l^m, F, r, \alpha^*, A_0, z(\alpha, \sigma, b) w)$.

Lean: `UpperTheory.V.ML.nuSysEx`.

*Proof.* Take the Gauss sum system $(X, s)$ of the Theorem on existence. First remove the roots of unity: the roots of unity in the relations have order dividing $2Fqd$, and raising $X(d)$ to the power $2 N_0 d$ and applying $g_0 - a_0$, where $g_0$ is the element with $\alpha(g_0) - \chi_O(g_0)$ a unit and $a_0 \equiv \chi_O(g_0) \pmod{l^m}$, kills them modulo $l^m$ and turns the system into a true Euler system. For each prime $q$ choose $\tau_q$ that fixes the roots of unity of order prime to $q$ and lifts a generator of $(\mathbb{Z}/q)^\times$; it generates the tame inertia at $q$ in $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_q)$. For an admissible list $L$, the derivative $y_L = \mathrm{D}_L$ of the modified element of level $\prod L$ is fixed by $G_F$ modulo $l^m$-th powers, by the usual telescoping identity $(\tau - 1)\mathrm{D}_{q, \tau} = (q - 1) - \mathrm{N}_{q, \tau}$ and the norm relation. So the Kummer character $\kappa_L$ of an $l^m$-th root of $y_L$ is defined on $G_F$ with values in $\mathbb{Z}/l^m$. Put
$$\nu_L(u) = \sum_{t \in (\mathbb{Z}/F)^\times} \kappa_L(\sigma_t^{-1} u \sigma_t) \, \alpha^*(\sigma_t) \, w .$$
The sum over $t$ is the projection to the $\alpha^*$-eigenspace, so $\nu_L$ has the equivariance for $\alpha^*$. It is additive on $G_F$, killed by $l^m$, and unramified outside $F r \prod L$, since $y_L$ is a unit away from these primes. The step relation comes from the congruence of the system: at a place over $q$, the valuation of $y_{q :: L}$ is the derivative of the norm relation, and the congruence identifies the ramified part of the Kummer class at $q$ with the value of $\kappa_L$ at Frobenius, up to the unit $-q \, s(d, q)$. The start relation comes from the order of $X(1)$ at $A_0$: on the inertia at $A_0$, the Kummer character of $X(1)$ is read off from its valuations, which are $\vartheta_{F, b}(t)$ at the conjugates $\sigma_t A_0$, and the projection gives $z(\alpha, \sigma, b) w$ up to a unit. $\square$
