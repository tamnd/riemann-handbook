This chapter collects the Hecke side of the Kato argument. It first builds towers of [modular functions](/learn/automorphic-forms/#modular-forms) with algebraic coefficients, a lower bound for spaces of cusp forms of odd weight, the Eichler and Shimura decomposition and the semisimplicity of the Hecke action at a newform. From these it builds Hecke projectors onto the part of the Kummer module that belongs to an odd-weight newform, proves the Eichler and Shimura relation modulo prime powers there, and identifies the image as the [Galois representation](/learn/galois-representations/#galois-representations) of the newform. It then turns to Eisenstein series: Rankin values give a Hecke eigenfunctional that does not vanish on a depleted Eisenstein series, and an ordinary Eisenstein measure gives the ordinary eigenfunctional. The last sections show that certain cochains on the geometric Galois group are coboundaries, and compute the edge class of two Siegel units modulo $p^n$ as a product of two Eisenstein series of weight $1$.

These results feed the [Euler system of Kato](/learn/galois-cohomology/#euler-systems-and-kato) through [the existence of a tower](/entry/tower-ex/), [the joint theta operator theorem](/entry/th-op/), [the admissibility of admissible classes](/entry/kato-global-admissibility-of-admissible/), [the unit law of smoothed classes](/entry/kato-global-unit-law-of-smoothed-classes/) and [the edge congruence for translated Siegel units](/entry/one/). Through these, the chapter supports the bound KatoTwoW in case I of [the window bound](/proof/vwin/).
