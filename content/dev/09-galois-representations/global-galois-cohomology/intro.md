This chapter proves the global results of Galois cohomology and class field theory that the proof uses. Sections 1 and 2 compute Herbrand quotients of idele groups, prove the first inequality for cyclic extensions and prove the global reciprocity law for the idele symbol. Section 3 proves the main theorem of complex multiplication for elliptic curves with a uniformization of given level. Sections 4 to 7 prove Poitou-Tate duality for the Galois group $G_S$ of the maximal extension unramified outside a finite set $S$ of places: first for the roots of unity of prime power order, then for all finite modules through a base case, then with real places through the vanishing of Tate cohomology in high degrees. Section 8 bounds the length of the unramified cokernel of a lattice with Hodge-Tate data by a Frobenius length bound. Sections 9 and 10 construct Selmer presentations, choose auxiliary places by the Chebotarev density theorem, and build a ray class character all of whose powers prime to $\ell$ are good twists.
