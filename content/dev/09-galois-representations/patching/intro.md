This chapter collects the results on deformation rings and patching that the modularity argument of Volume 9 uses, together with the stability and counting results that the same argument takes from algebraic $K$-theory and from the geometry of locally symmetric spaces. Sections 1 and 2 prove that complexes of split unimodular sequences are exact in a stable range, and from this the homological stability of $\mathrm{GL}_n$ over rings of finite stable rank. Sections 3 to 5 bound the rank of the odd homology of arithmetic groups through real chains, Hecke operators, heights and a spectral gap. Section 6 builds a function that measures the distance to the cusp and regularizes cochains. Section 7 bounds the relations of each weight of a rank three deformation ring with a torus action, and the lengths of Selmer groups that these relations control. Sections 8 to 10 prove the two Selmer bounds of a point of a [universal ordinary determinant ring](/learn/deformations-patching/#determinants-and-residually-reducible-representations) at Taylor-Wiles level, and choose the auxiliary group elements that they need. Sections 11 to 13 turn these bounds into a presentation of the completion at a point, patch in the manner of Taylor, Wiles and Kisin, and prove that automorphy implies the core properties of the universal ring. The reader needs from Volume 1 the [deformation functors](/learn/deformations-patching/#deformation-functors), [Galois cohomology of global fields](/learn/galois-cohomology/#global-fields) and [local rings and dimension](/learn/commutative-algebra/#local-rings-and-dimension).

The core properties and the presentation at an ordinary point feed [the vanishing step](/entry/vwin-of-hypotheses-h-e-alt/) of the modularity chapter. The stability theorem feeds [Lemma K](/entry/lemma-k-lit-n-alt/), the rank bound of Section 5 feeds [the odd homology bound](/entry/b-odd/), and the count in rank three feeds [the count in case V](/entry/count-v-of-finiteness-duality-euler-host/). The cusp geometry results feed [the relation lemma](/entry/rel-onto/) and [the published hypotheses from patching](/entry/published-hypotheses-from-patching-alt/).
