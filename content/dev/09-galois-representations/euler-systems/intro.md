This chapter proves the Euler system results of the [window bound](/proof/vwin/). Sections 1 to 3 prove the coefficient law for zeta classes made from Siegel units: the logarithmic derivative of each term is a coefficient of a product of two smoothed Eisenstein series of weight one, and the law passes to sums over representatives, to normalized traces and to diamond translates. Section 4 builds Kato's norm-compatible system of zeta classes for a newform-type representation, with its integrality and its tame classes. Section 5 proves with Coleman power series that the resulting class does not vanish on inertia at $\ell$. Section 6 proves that ordinary cocycles of the adjoint of an automorphic representation of dimension 2 to 4 are coboundaries, from a Selmer bound at Taylor-Wiles levels and a Hecke ideal that vanishes at automorphic points. Section 7 builds Hilbert modular forms of level one congruent to one from the theta series of an even lattice with a fixed point free isometry of order $p$.

From Volume 1 the chapter needs these results. Sections 1 and 2 use [modular forms](/learn/automorphic-forms/#modular-forms) and [Eisenstein series](/learn/automorphic-forms/#eisenstein-series) for the $q$-expansions of Siegel units and their logarithmic derivatives. Sections 3 and 4 use [the cyclotomic character and Tate modules](/learn/galois-representations/#the-cyclotomic-character-and-tate-modules) for the diamond operators and the Galois action on the tower, [decomposition groups, inertia and Frobenius](/learn/galois-representations/#decomposition-groups-inertia-and-frobenius) for the tame classes, and [Euler systems and Kato](/learn/galois-cohomology/#euler-systems-and-kato) for the shape of the norm relations. Section 5 uses [local fields](/learn/galois-cohomology/#local-fields), [p-adic numbers and local fields](/learn/number-fields/#p-adic-numbers-and-local-fields) and [Iwasawa theory](/learn/galois-cohomology/#iwasawa-theory) for the Kummer presentations and the Coleman power series. Section 6 uses [continuous cohomology](/learn/galois-cohomology/#continuous-cohomology) for cocycles and coboundaries, [Selmer groups](/learn/galois-cohomology/#selmer-and-tate-shafarevich-groups) for the Greenberg-Wiles count, and [Hida theory](/learn/galois-cohomology/#hida-theory) for the universal ordinary ring. Section 7 uses [theta series of lattices](/learn/theta-functions-cm/#theta-series-of-lattices) for the seed, [automorphic forms on adelic groups](/learn/automorphic-forms/#automorphic-forms-on-adelic-groups) for the lift to a level one form, and [places and completions](/learn/number-fields/#places-and-completions) for the conditions at the primes above $p$.
