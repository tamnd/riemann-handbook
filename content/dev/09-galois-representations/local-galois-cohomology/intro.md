This chapter proves the results on [[local field|J85FZEQG38]]s that the main proof uses: [[local class field theory|SHEN158T3M]] for finite extensions of $\mathbb{Q}_p$, and the local Euler characteristic formula of Tate in [[Galois cohomology|9MPCZ3EAJM]]. Sections 1 and 2 build the Lubin-Tate tower of a local field from [[Lubin-Tate formal group laws|77QT4B80V8]] and compute the reciprocity map on it: an element that acts on the $\pi^n$-torsion by $[u^{-1}]_f$ and on the maximal unramified extension by the $k$-th power of Frobenius corresponds to $u\pi^k$. Sections 3 to 5 build the local Artin map. Section 3 defines the local symbol through the invariant map of the Brauer group and proves that, for each finite abelian extension $L/K$, it gives an isomorphism $K^\times/N_{L/K}L^\times \cong \operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$. Section 4 proves the existence theorem: every subgroup of finite index of $K^\times$ is a norm group, and the universal norms are trivial. Section 5 glues the finite layers into the Artin map $K^\times \to G_K^{\mathrm{ab}}$ and proves local reciprocity for every local field of characteristic $0$. Sections 6 and 7 prove that for a finite discrete $G_K$-module $M$ of order invertible in $K$,
$$\frac{\# H^0(K, M)\, \# H^2(K, M)}{\# H^1(K, M)} = \frac{1}{\#(\mathcal{O}_K/\# M\,\mathcal{O}_K)}.$$
Section 6 treats modules killed by a prime $l$ that become trivial on a subgroup of index prime to $l$ by a direct computation with Kummer theory, and Section 7 reduces the general case to this one by the ramification filtration and induction on the order of $M$. The proof does not use local duality.

From Volume 1 the chapter uses [profinite groups](/learn/galois-representations/#profinite-groups), [decomposition groups, inertia and Frobenius](/learn/galois-representations/#decomposition-groups-inertia-and-frobenius), [the cyclotomic character and Tate modules](/learn/galois-representations/#the-cyclotomic-character-and-tate-modules), [local class field theory](/learn/class-field-theory/#local-class-field-theory), [formal group laws](/learn/formal-groups/#formal-group-laws), [continuous cohomology](/learn/galois-cohomology/#continuous-cohomology), and the cohomology of [local fields](/learn/galois-cohomology/#local-fields). The local reciprocity map feeds the norm index computation of the statement that the product of two non-norms in a quadratic extension is a [norm](/entry/mul-mem-range-norm/), and the Euler characteristic formula is used for [every character](/step/every-character/) of the main proof. The local Artin map is also the abelian case of [the local Langlands correspondence](/learn/galois-representations/#the-local-langlands-correspondence).
