## Generic forms are dense

The congruence statement of Section 25 asks for spaces $U'$ that are generic at a given finite set of places. This section proves the analytic fact behind it: every algebraic form of a fixed level is an $\ell$-adic limit, in a relative sense, of sums of forms that lie in automorphic spaces generic at the given places. The method raises the level at $w_0$ along a tower of $\ell$-groups. The non-generic forms of the larger level are few, so translation-stable spaces of functions on the quotient group pick out generic forms close to the given one. One step gains a factor $\lVert \ell \rVert^{1/2}$, and iteration gives the density. The results are [the one step theorem](/entry/step-holds/) and [the density theorem](/entry/dense-gen-holds/), an input of [the congruence theorem at generic places](/entry/cong-uwg-of/).

We keep the notation of Section 25. Let $E$ be a CM field, $\ell$ a prime, $\iota : \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell \cong \mathbb{C}$, and $a \geq 1$. Let $G = \mathrm{U}(a)(\mathbb{A})$ be the group of adelic points of the definite unitary group in $a$ variables, $G_f$ and $G_\infty$ its finite and infinite parts, and $R$ the action of $G$ on functions $G \to \mathbb{C}$ by right translation. For a split place $w$ we write $c_w : G \to \mathrm{GL}_a(E_w)$ for the component at $w$, and $G_w \subset G$ for the subgroup of elements whose other components are trivial. On $\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell$ we use the norm that extends the $\ell$-adic norm, so $\lVert \ell \rVert = \ell^{-1} < 1$.

### Levels, algebraic forms and parts

**Definition 3.29.1 (Level away from $w_0$).** Let $w_0$ be a split place. A subgroup $K \subset G_f$ is a *level away from $w_0$* if it is compact, its components at the places not apart from $w_0$ are trivial, and it contains $N \cap G'$ for some open $N \ni 1$, where $G'$ is the subgroup of $G_f$ of elements with trivial components at the places not apart from $w_0$. A *level* is a compact subgroup $C \subset G_f$ that contains $N \cap G_f$ for some open $N \ni 1$. For a subgroup $J \subset \mathrm{GL}_a(E_{w_0})$, let $K_J$ be the subgroup generated by $K$ and by the $x \in G_{w_0}$ with $c_{w_0}(x) \in J$.

Lean: `UpperTheory.Gal.Gen.lev` (also `GlobalTheory.Cong.IsLevelAway`, `UpperTheory.Gal.Gen.IsLevel`).

So $K_J$ is $K$ with the level at $w_0$ set to $J$.

**Definition 3.29.2 (Algebraic forms).** A *weight* $W$ assigns a dominant weight of $\mathrm{GL}_a$ to each infinite place of $E$. Let $V_W$ be the corresponding irreducible representation of $G_\infty$. An *algebraic form of weight $W$* is a linear map $f$ from $V_W$ to functions on $G$ such that each $f(v)$ is an automorphic form and $f(W(x) v) = R(x) f(v)$ for $x \in G_\infty$. For a subgroup $C$, the form $f$ is *fixed by $C$* if $f(v)(gk) = f(v)(g)$ for all $v$, $g$ and $k \in C$.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.DefU.algForms` (also `UpperTheory.Gal.Gen.fixedBy`, `GlobalTheory.DefU.Wt`).

**Definition 3.29.3 (Parts).** Let $P$ be a property of automorphic spaces. For an automorphic space $\pi$, the *$\pi$-part* consists of the algebraic forms $f$ with $f(v) \in \pi$ for all $v$. The *$P$-part of level $C$*, written $\mathrm{part}_C(P)$, is the sum, over the automorphic spaces $\pi$ with $P(\pi)$, of the forms in the $\pi$-part that are fixed by $C$.

Lean: `UpperTheory.Gal.Gen.part` (also `GlobalTheory.DefU.AutSub.finPart`).

**Definition 3.29.4 (Approximation by a property).** Let $K$ be a subgroup and $P$ a property of automorphic spaces. *Forms with $P$ approximate the forms of weight $W$ fixed by $K$* if, for each $f$ of weight $W$ fixed by $K$ and each $\varepsilon > 0$, there are $F \in \mathrm{part}_K(P)$ and $e : G \to \mathbb{C}$ with $\lVert \iota^{-1} e(g) \rVert \leq \varepsilon$ for all $g$ and
$$f(v)(g) - F(v)(g) = e(g)\, f(v)(g) \quad \text{for all } v, g.$$

Lean: `UpperTheory.Gal.Gen.Dense`.

The error is relative: $F = (1 - e) f$ pointwise, with a factor $1 - e$ that is $\ell$-adically close to $1$.

**Definition 3.29.5 (Generic at a place).** Let $w$ be a split place and $\Phi \in \mathbb{C}[X_1, \dots, X_a]$. An automorphic space $U$ is *$\Phi$-generic at $w$* if $\Phi(z) \neq 0$ for every $z \in (\mathbb{C}^\times)^a$ such that $U$ has a local component at $w$ with Satake parameter $\{z_1, \dots, z_a\}$. *Genericity data* for $(w_0, K)$ are a finite set $S$ of split places apart from $w_0$ and polynomials $\Phi_w \neq 0$ for $w \in S$, such that for $w \in S$ and $x \in G_w$, $x \in K$ if and only if $c_w(x) \in \mathrm{GL}_a(\mathcal{O}_w)$. Then $U$ is *generic at $S$* if it is $\Phi_w$-generic at each $w \in S$.

Lean: `UpperTheory.Gal.Gen.GenAt` (also `UpperTheory.Gal.Gen.SatAt`, `UpperTheory.Gal.Gen.HypAt`, `UpperTheory.Gal.Gen.GenData`).

### The inputs

**Proposition 3.29.6 (Inputs).** Let $w_0$ be a split place.

1. *Smoothness.* For each algebraic form $f$ there is a neighbourhood $N$ of $1$ in $\mathrm{GL}_a(E_{w_0})$ with $f(v)(gx) = f(v)(g)$ for all $v$, $g$ and all $x \in G_{w_0}$ with $c_{w_0}(x) \in N$.
2. *Towers.* If $w_0 \mid \ell$ and $N$ is a neighbourhood of $1$ in $\mathrm{GL}_a(E_{w_0})$, there are open compact subgroups $J_0 \supset J_1 \supset \cdots$ such that each $J_n$ is normalized by $J_0$, each neighbourhood of $1$ contains some $J_n$, $J_0 \subset N$, $J_0$ has no element of finite order other than $1$, and $x^{\ell^n} \in J_n$ for $x \in J_0$.
3. *Levels.* Let $K$ be a level away from $w_0$ and $J$ open and compact. Then $K_J$ is a level; its elements are the products $kx$ with $k \in K$, $x \in G_{w_0}$ and $c_{w_0}(x) \in J$; $c_{w_0}(K_J) \subset J$; an element $c \in K_J$ with $c_{w_0}(c) = 1$ lies in $K$; $c_{w_0}$ maps $G_{w_0}$ onto $\mathrm{GL}_a(E_{w_0})$; and $c \in K_J$ with $c_{w_0}(c) \in J'$ lies in $K_{J'}$.
4. *Maps to finite groups.* Let $C$ be a level and $\lambda : C \to H$ a homomorphism that is trivial on an open neighbourhood of $1$ and on the elements of finite order. Then there is $L : G \to H$ with $L(gx) = L(g)$ for $x \in G_\infty$, $L(gc) = L(g) \lambda(c)$ for $c \in C$, and $\varphi \circ L$ automorphic for every $\varphi : H \to \mathbb{C}$.
5. *Counting.* Let $K$ be a level away from $w_0$, $(J_n)$ as in item 2 without the last three conditions, $W$ a weight and $S, \Phi$ genericity data. Then for some $n$ the part of level $K_{J_n}$ of the spaces that are not generic at $S$ is finite dimensional, of dimension at most $\frac{1}{2} [J_0 : J_n]$.
6. *Parts.* Let $C$ be a subgroup and $P$ a property. Every form fixed by $C$ is in $\mathrm{part}_C(P) + \mathrm{part}_C(\neg P)$; every element of $\mathrm{part}_C(P)$ is fixed by $C$; $\mathrm{part}_C(P) \subset \mathrm{part}_K(P)$ for $K \subset C$; $R(x)$ preserves $\mathrm{part}_C(P)$ for $x \in G_f$ that normalizes $C$; and every element of $\mathrm{part}_C(P)$ is a finite sum of nonzero forms $F_\pi$ in the $\pi$-parts, fixed by $C$, with $P(\pi)$.
7. *Products.* If $u$ is an automorphic form with $u(gx) = u(g)$ for $x \in G_\infty$ and $f$ is an algebraic form, then $v \mapsto u \cdot f(v)$ is an algebraic form.
8. *Functions on $\ell$-groups.* Let $H$ be a finite $\ell$-group and $\mathcal{P}$ a space of functions $H \to \mathbb{C}$, stable under right translation, with $|H| \leq 2 \dim \mathcal{P}$. Then some $c \in \mathcal{P}$ has $\lVert \iota^{-1}(1 - c(h)) \rVert^2 \leq \lVert \ell \rVert$ for all $h \in H$.

Lean: `UpperTheory.Gal.Gen.SmoothAt` (also `UpperTheory.Gal.Gen.Tower`, `UpperTheory.Gal.Gen.LevFacts`, `UpperTheory.Gal.Gen.Ell`, `UpperTheory.Gal.Gen.CountGen`, `UpperTheory.Gal.Gen.PartFacts`, `GlobalTheory.T.exists_algebraicModularForm_mul_archInvariant`, `UpperTheory.Gal.Gen.AlgC`).

These facts are proved elsewhere in the development; we take them as given here. Item 5 is the deep one: the forms of level $K_{J_n}$ that are not generic at $S$ grow more slowly than the index $[J_0 : J_n]$.

We also use a fact of linear algebra. Let $\Psi : V \to M$ be linear with $V$ finite dimensional, and $A, B \subset M$ subspaces with $B$ finite dimensional and $\Psi(V) \subset A + B$. Then $\dim V \leq \dim \Psi^{-1}(A) + \dim B$. Indeed, the composite $V \to (A + B)/A$ has kernel $\Psi^{-1}(A)$, and $(A + B)/A$ is a quotient of $B$.

### One step

**Lemma 3.29.7 (Core step).** Let $C \subset G_f$ be a subgroup, $H$ a finite $\ell$-group, $\lambda : C \to H$ onto with kernel $C'$, and $L : G \to H$ with $L(gx) = L(g)$ for $x \in G_\infty$, $L(gc) = L(g) \lambda(c)$ for $c \in C$, and $\varphi \circ L$ automorphic for every $\varphi$. Let $P$ be a property such that $\mathrm{part}_{C'}(\neg P)$ is finite dimensional with $2 \dim \mathrm{part}_{C'}(\neg P) \leq |H|$. Then for each $f$ of weight $W$ fixed by $C$ there are $F \in \mathrm{part}_{C'}(P)$ and $e : G \to \mathbb{C}$ with $\lVert \iota^{-1} e(g) \rVert^2 \leq \lVert \ell \rVert$ and $f - F = e f$.

Lean: `UpperTheory.Gal.Gen.stepCore_holds` (also `UpperTheory.Gal.Gen.stepCore_pf`, `UpperTheory.Gal.Gen.StepCore`).

*Proof.* For $\varphi : H \to \mathbb{C}$, let $\Psi(\varphi)$ be the form $v \mapsto (\varphi \circ L) \cdot f(v)$, an algebraic form by item 7. It is fixed by $C'$: for $k \in C'$, $L(gk) = L(g)$ and $f(v)(gk) = f(v)(g)$. So by item 6, $\Psi$ maps the space $\mathbb{C}^H$ into $\mathrm{part}_{C'}(P) + \mathrm{part}_{C'}(\neg P)$. Let $\mathcal{P} = \Psi^{-1}(\mathrm{part}_{C'}(P))$. By the fact of linear algebra, $|H| \leq \dim \mathcal{P} + \dim \mathrm{part}_{C'}(\neg P)$, so $|H| \leq 2 \dim \mathcal{P}$. The space $\mathcal{P}$ is stable under right translation by $u = \lambda(c)$: $\Psi(\varphi(\cdot\, u)) = R(c) \Psi(\varphi)$, because $L(gc) = L(g) u$ and $f$ is fixed by $c$, and $R(c)$ preserves $\mathrm{part}_{C'}(P)$ by item 6, since $c$ normalizes the kernel $C'$. Item 8 gives $c \in \mathcal{P}$ with $\lVert \iota^{-1}(1 - c(h)) \rVert^2 \leq \lVert \ell \rVert$. Put $F = \Psi(c)$ and $e = 1 - c \circ L$. Then $f - F = e f$. $\square$

**Theorem 3.29.8 (One step).** Let $w_0 \mid \ell$ be split, $K$ a level away from $w_0$, $W$ a weight and $S, \Phi$ genericity data for $(w_0, K)$. Then for each form $f$ of weight $W$ fixed by $K$ there are $F$ in the part of level $K$ of the spaces generic at $S$ and $e : G \to \mathbb{C}$ with
$$\lVert \iota^{-1} e(g) \rVert^2 \leq \lVert \ell \rVert \text{ for all } g, \qquad f - F = e f.$$

Lean: `UpperTheory.Gal.Gen.step_holds` (also `UpperTheory.Gal.Gen.step_pf`, `UpperTheory.Gal.Gen.Step`).

*Proof.* Item 1 gives a neighbourhood $N$ for $f$, and item 2 gives a tower $(J_n)$ with $J_0 \subset N$. Item 5 gives $n$ such that the non-generic part of level $K_{J_n}$ has dimension at most $\frac{1}{2}[J_0 : J_n]$. Let $C = K_{J_0}$ and $C' = K_{J_n}$.

The group $H = J_0 / J_n$ is finite, as $J_n$ is open in the compact $J_0$, and it is an $\ell$-group, as $x^{\ell^n} \in J_n$. By item 3, $c_{w_0}(C) \subset J_0$, so $\lambda(c) = c_{w_0}(c) \bmod J_n$ is a homomorphism $C \to H$. It is onto, since $c_{w_0}$ maps $G_{w_0}$ onto $\mathrm{GL}_a(E_{w_0})$ and $G_{w_0} \cap c_{w_0}^{-1}(J_0) \subset C$. Its kernel is $C'$ by the first and last clauses of item 3. It is trivial on the open set $c_{w_0}^{-1}(J_n)$. If $c \in C$ has finite order, then $c_{w_0}(c) \in J_0$ has finite order, so it is $1$, and $\lambda(c) = 1$. Item 4 gives $L$.

The form $f$ is fixed by $C$: an element of $C$ is $kx$ with $k \in K$ and $c_{w_0}(x) \in J_0 \subset N$, and $f(v)(gkx) = f(v)(gk) = f(v)(g)$. The core step, for the property of being generic at $S$, gives $F \in \mathrm{part}_{C'}$ and $e$. Since $K \subset C'$, item 6 puts $F$ in the part of level $K$. $\square$

### Density

**Theorem 3.29.9 (Density of generic forms).** Let $w_0 \mid \ell$ be split, $K$ a level away from $w_0$, $W$ a weight and $S, \Phi$ genericity data for $(w_0, K)$. Then forms in spaces generic at $S$ approximate the forms of weight $W$ fixed by $K$.

Lean: `UpperTheory.Gal.Gen.denseGen_holds` (also `UpperTheory.Gal.Gen.denseGen_pf`, `UpperTheory.Gal.Gen.DenseGen`).

*Proof.* Let $q = \lVert \ell \rVert^{1/2} < 1$, and write $\mathcal{G}$ for the part of level $K$ of the spaces generic at $S$. We show by induction on $m$ that for each $f$ fixed by $K$ there are $F \in \mathcal{G}$ and $e$ with $\lVert \iota^{-1} e \rVert \leq q^m$ and $f - F = e f$. For $m = 0$ take $F = 0$ and $e = 1$. For the step, take $F, e$ for $m$. Then $F$ is fixed by $K$ (item 6), so $f - F$ is fixed by $K$, and the one step theorem gives $F', e'$ with $\lVert \iota^{-1} e' \rVert \leq q$ and $(f - F) - F' = e'(f - F) = e' e f$. So $F + F' \in \mathcal{G}$ and the error $e' e$ has norm at most $q^{m+1}$. Given $\varepsilon > 0$, choose $m$ with $q^m < \varepsilon$. $\square$
