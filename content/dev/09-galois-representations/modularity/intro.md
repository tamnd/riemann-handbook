This chapter proves the automorphic part of the [window bound](/proof/vwin/). The window bound is a bound for the lengths of Tate-Shafarevich groups of cyclotomic twists over a family of abelian fields, and it is the last step that the [Galois row](/proof/galois-row/) feeds. Section 1 shows how the row, the base change theorem for unitary groups and the published inputs give the window bound, through Lemma K and four case counts. Sections 2 to 6 prove the input for the first count: two Bloch-Kato Selmer groups of a newform-type representation vanish, by Kato's Euler system of zeta classes, its Hodge-Tate law and its Euler factors at tame primes. Section 7 builds the seed for the symplectic case of the second and third counts, from Hilbert modular forms congruent to Eisenstein series. Sections 8 and 9 prove automorphic induction from $\mathrm{GL}_2$ over a CM field to $\mathrm{GL}_4$ over a subfield of index two, from a trace identity with an Eisenstein term. Later sections prove four-dimensional symplectic automorphy and potential modularity, three-dimensional unitary automorphy with Hida theory, Lemma K, the remaining counts and the extended hypotheses, which include rank two descent and quadratic base change.
