This chapter builds the level-four objects of the construction and the data that the membership statement needs at that level. Sections 1 and 2 conjugate the symmetric level into the level of a row, find a finite adelic point that carries the row level, and give the spectral objects over the replaced stages, with the level-four saturation of a discrete series. Sections 3 and 4 compare the two-step measures on the unitary stabilizers, prove that the result does not depend on the path, and give a gauge for each CM extension. Section 5 proves the kernel entry conditions for the true column choices, and Section 6 gives the levels, the classes, the comparison maps and the kernel data at the served places, and from them a lattice layer with a kernel datum. Section 7 proves that the upward Hecke operator is rational and invertible on the ramified forms and derives the column layer conditions at the later stages. Section 8 puts these results into a constructed core and into the membership statement from the construction steps with a second pass. The later sections give the divisor data, the theta data on the true rows and columns, and the extension of the adelic data to larger fields.
