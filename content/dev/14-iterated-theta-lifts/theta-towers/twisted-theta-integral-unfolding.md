This theorem proves the unfolding step `StepUnfold` of the row test. The pairing of a column vector with an iterated theta lift becomes a finite sum over cosets of local coefficients times periods. Its one user is the renaming [`twisted_theta_integral_unfolds`](/entry/twisted-theta-integral-unfolds/), and through it the lift formula of [the five properties of the twisted theta lifts](/entry/theta-lift-row-properties-conj/).

## Statement

Assume three statements: `ThetaLocalInputs`, the [record of named literature statements](/step/theta-local-inputs-def/); `LiftsCuspidal`, which says that along each arrow of the grid the theta kernel lifts cusp forms to cusp forms; and `SumOKK`, which says that the finite sums of the row test are meaningful. Then for all large admissible choices $\mathrm{ch}$ (in the sense of `Big`, see [the five properties](/entry/theta-lift-row-properties-conj/)), the statement `UnfoldAt ch` holds ([StepUnfold.lean](lean:Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Statements/HeckeThetaIntegrals/StepUnfold.lean#L106)).

`UnfoldAt` says this. For each finite combination $\tau_i$ of polynomial data on the row and the descending path, there are a finite combination $\sigma$ of polynomial data on the column and the row, and units $z_t \in \mathbb{C}^\times$, with the following property. Each $z_t$ is in the subgroup generated by the local values of a character $\xi_t$. Let $t$ and $a$ be indices. Let $\tau$ be a finite expansion of the product over the local places of the flip functions at $\Phi_a$ (`IsFlipFin`). Let $M$ be a volume at $t$, and for each coset $\beta$ in the support $\operatorname{supp}_t(\Phi_a)$ let $M_\beta$ be a volume and $\tau_\beta$ an expansion, with the conditions `IsTermVol` and `IsExpansion`. Then for each $y$:

$$B^+\bigl(\varphi_a,\ \Lambda_M(\tau_i \otimes \tau)(F_y)\bigr) = z_t^{-1}\, s \sum_{\beta \in \operatorname{supp}_t(\Phi_a)} \Bigl(\prod_w c_w(\beta)\Bigr)\, P_{M_\beta}(\sigma, \tau_\beta; \varphi_a, F_y).$$

Here $\varphi_a$ is the column function of $a$, $F_y$ the function at the grid point $(0, m)$ made from $y$ (`formTop`), $\Lambda_M$ the iterated theta lift along the opposite path, $s$ a fixed constant of the sum choice, and $c_w(\beta)$ the local coefficients of the term at $\beta$. The term $P$ (`defTot`) is a finite combination of periods `defPer`. Each period is the integral over a fundamental domain of a lift along the column times a lift along the row.

## Proof

The proof collects 17 facts, each a statement $\mathrm{Big}(Q)$, joins them with `Big.and`, and applies one pointwise lemma with `big_mono`.

The pointwise lemma is `lastRow_real_iterated_integral_eq_const_mul_finite`. Given the vanishing statement `CellVan` (from `cellSumRelationsPassToIntegrals_proof`), it gives `UnfoldAt ch` from these facts at $\mathrm{ch}$: the row period groups split into real and finite parts (`GrpAt`); the left side is an iterated integral and is linear in $\tau_i$ (`IterAt`, `LinAt`); the integrand is bi-invariant (`InvAt`); for a sign $e = \pm 1$, the integrand is supported on cells, the cells have representatives, and the cell values are known (`SuppOn`, `RepAt`, `ValAt`); a real line condition holds (`RealLine`); and `SumOK` holds.

The facts `SuppOn` and `ValAt` come from further facts: `FlatAt`, `OffAt`, `TensAt`, `VolAt`, `WtAt`, `FockAt`, `BasicAt` and `OneAt`. The rest is bookkeeping.

- The pointwise lemma has the name of an identity for the last row, "iterated integral equals a constant times a finite sum". Its conclusion is the whole of `UnfoldAt`.
