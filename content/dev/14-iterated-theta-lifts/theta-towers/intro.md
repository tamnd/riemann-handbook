This chapter studies the twisted theta lifts along a grid of Hermitian spaces on the definite side, and it proves three groups of results. First, it shows that the lifts keep cusp forms and that a cusp form is close to a finite sum of lifts of Fock vectors, so that the lifts along a path through the grid can be flipped square by square. With this it proves the five row properties of the lifts: a sum condition, constants for each square, a convergence statement, an unfolding formula with explicit constants, and rationality of the sums. Second, it proves that the rows are separated by real functionals, from unramified Howe duality and the Siegel-Weil formula. Third, it computes the Petersson norms of the bottom theta lifts by the Rallis inner product formula as products of Hecke $L$-values, and it joins these norm formulas with the row properties into the bound that makes a constant function fit. The chapter needs from Volume 1 the [Hermitian and skew-Hermitian forms](/learn/algebra/#hermitian-and-skew-hermitian-forms), the [Haar measure](/learn/analysis/#haar-measure), the [cusp forms](/learn/automorphic-forms/#constant-terms-and-cusp-forms), the [theta kernels](/learn/theta/#theta-kernels) and the [global theta lift](/learn/theta/#the-global-theta-lift), the [Siegel-Weil formula](/learn/theta/#the-siegel-weil-formula), the [Rallis inner product formula](/learn/theta/#the-rallis-inner-product-formula), [Tate's thesis](/learn/class-field-theory/#tates-thesis-and-hecke-l-functions) and the [slopes of Hermitian lattices](/learn/arakelov/#hermitian-lattices-and-slopes).

The results are used in the step [the hypotheses are not empty](/step/nonempty-hyps/). Its first item is [the separation of rows from Howe duality and Siegel-Weil](/entry/functionals-separate-of-howe-unram-of-siegel-weil/). Its second and third items use [the row properties of the twisted lifts](/entry/theta-lift-row-properties-conj/), and its third item ends with [the fit of constant functions](/entry/fourier-coefficient-fit-of-join-alt/).
