This chapter proves the quotient index bound for a lattice $M$ of cusp forms: if forms in $M$ with small sup norms span the space of cusp forms together with the subspace $S^\flat$ of forms with vanishing low coefficients at the maximal cusps, then the index of their span in $M / (M \cap S^\flat)$ is bounded by a polynomial in the size of the weight. The tool is a family of linear functionals built from CM points. Each one evaluates a form along a formal arc through a CM point and reads off one coefficient. Sections 1 to 3 show that these functionals detect $S^\flat$: a form lies in $S^\flat$ exactly when all its low Fourier-Jacobi coefficients vanish, and a nonzero low coefficient is seen at a split point of the fundamental domain, which gives a CM point and a member of the family. Section 4 bounds the functionals at the finite places by an integral model, and Section 5 bounds them at the infinite places by the Cauchy inequalities, so that each functional is numerically good. Section 6 turns these bounds into a bridge, applies the bridge theorem of Volume 13, and supplies the constants from the data of a page.
