This chapter computes the scalars of a chain of theta lifts by the doubling method and shows that the total up map of the chain is injective. Let $\Theta$ be the total theta map of a row of the chain. Then $\Theta \Theta^*$ acts on each Hecke eigenpart of the first stage by a positive constant times a product of $L$-values, one for each step. Section 1 stands apart: it proves that a CM elliptic curve has a good model at a prime, by global Artin reciprocity, and that at an inert prime its formal group has height two. Sections 2 and 3 compute the local doubling integral at a twist place on generic vectors of a parahoric level, and show that the flattened integrand has a local factor there. Section 4 peels the twist places off the global doubling integral, one at a time. Section 5 computes the Hecke parameters of the lifts along the chain by a parameter transfer. Section 6 combines these results with the Rallis inner product formula and proves that each step of the chain acts by a positive multiple of an $L$-value. Section 7 composes the steps into the scalar formula and deduces the injectivity. Sections 8 and 9 prove the double step of a see-saw at finitely many split places and derive from it the see-saw laws at the twist places, for decorations that are products of local data up to roots of unity.

The chapter uses from Volume 1 [complex multiplication](/learn/elliptic-curves/#complex-multiplication), [the height of a formal group](/learn/formal-groups/#height) and [global class field theory](/learn/class-field-theory/#global-class-field-theory) in Section 1, [the doubling method](/learn/theta/#the-doubling-method), [depth zero representations and types](/learn/representations/#depth-zero-representations-and-types) and [Haar measure](/learn/analysis/#haar-measure) in Sections 2 to 4, [the Weil representation](/learn/theta/#the-weil-representation), [the global theta lift](/learn/theta/#the-global-theta-lift) and [theta towers](/learn/theta/#theta-towers) in Sections 5 to 7, [the Rallis inner product formula](/learn/theta/#the-rallis-inner-product-formula) in Section 6, and [see-saw pairs](/learn/theta/#seesaw-pairs) in Sections 8 and 9. The injectivity enters [the theorem on large unramified exponents](/entry/member-bundles-large-unramified-exponents-alt/) in branch 1 of the [unitary hypotheses](/proof/unitary-hypotheses/), below the step [HypsAlt is nonempty](/step/nonempty-hyps/), and the see-saw laws enter [the rescaled laws](/entry/laws-resc-alt/) of the open orbit chapter in branch 3 of the same step.
