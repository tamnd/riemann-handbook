This theorem proves the automorphic induction statement $\mathrm{IndCs}$ of [`indCs`](/entry/ind-cs/) from one local hypothesis, the base change fundamental lemma for unit elements. The proof only passes it to [`indCs_of_gm`](/entry/ind-cs-of-gm/), which has the real content.

## Statement

Let $\mathrm{BCFLUnit}$ be the following statement. Let $E/k$ be a finite Galois extension of non-archimedean local fields of characteristic 0, unramified, with cyclic Galois group generated by $\sigma$. Let $1_E$ and $1_k$ be the characteristic functions of $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathcal{O}_E)$ and $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathcal{O}_k)$, with Haar measures that give them volume 1. Then:

1. if $\delta \in \mathrm{GL}_n(E)$ is $\sigma$-regular semisimple and its norm $N\delta$ (the Lean `normGL σ δ`, which we have not unfolded) is conjugate to $\gamma \in \mathrm{GL}_n(k)$, then the twisted orbital integral of $1_E$ at $\delta$ equals the orbital integral of $1_k$ at $\gamma$, for compatible measures on the centralizers;
2. if $\gamma$ is regular semisimple and is not a norm, then the orbital integral of $1_k$ at $\gamma$ is 0.

This is the fundamental lemma for base change of the unit element (Kottwitz, *Base change for unit elements of Hecke algebras*, Compositio Math. 60, 1986).

**Theorem.** If $\mathrm{BCFLUnit}$ holds, then $\mathrm{IndCs}$ holds.

## Proof

The proof is `indCs_of_gm (GM.gm_of_bcfl h)`. The theorem `gm_of_bcfl` turns $\mathrm{BCFLUnit}$ into $\mathrm{GM}$, the comparison of geometric sides. First `locUnit_of` proves `LocUnit`: at each unramified place $v$, the unit elements on the two sides match. At a split place this is `L1.unitSplit`. At a place with one place above it, it is $\mathrm{BCFLUnit}$, applied to the completions. Then `gm_of_unit` turns `LocUnit` into $\mathrm{GM}$. We have not unfolded `gm_of_unit`.
