This theorem proves the statement `SymplecticFourImageDense` from the published hypotheses `HypothesesH'`. It only passes hypotheses along to [`sp4In_alt`](/entry/sp4-in-alt/). Its result goes to [`vwin_of_hypotheses_H_E_alt`](/entry/vwin-of-hypotheses-h-e-alt/), where it feeds the counts in cases II and III (see [the chapter on VWin](/proof/vwin/)). The content is in [`main4_alt`](/entry/main4-alt/) and in [the seed of case II](/entry/seed-iis-alt/), with [`indCs`](/entry/ind-cs/) below it.

## Statement

**Theorem.** Assume `HypothesesH'`. Let $\ell$ be an odd prime, $0 < e < t$ with a host that is big for $t$, and $\psi$ a character of the host. Let $D$ be a residually reducible deformation datum for $\mathrm{GSp}_4$, whose residual representation is diagonal with characters $\theta, \Psi, \alpha, 1$ and weights $e+1, e, 1, 0$. Suppose $D$ meets the conditions `Good` and is in case II or case III. Then the algebra generated by the point lifts of $D$ is finite over $\mathcal{O}$, no point of block shape spans the whole block, and there is $\iota$ such that each irreducible point lift is automorphic.

We have not unfolded `Good` (about 25 clauses) or the cases.

## Proof

The proof is one call to `sp4In_alt` with these five inputs:

- the cohomology input, the field `coh` of `adjoint_lift_of_prints`;
- `FW.ferreroWashington`, the bounded $\ell$-rank of class groups in cyclotomic towers;
- [`seedIIs_alt`](/entry/seed-iis-alt/), which proves `SeedIIAlt`;
- `fourth_initial_of_prints_h`, which proves `SeedIVAlt`;
- `pmEx_of_printsH`, which proves `PMExAlt`, the existence of a potential modularity tower.
