This theorem proves `ModelPlaceBuilt2` from four hypotheses: `JfSmoothDS`, `GenTransfer`, `JDHatOpQQ` and `JABddOpQQ`. It turns the last two into the two analytic model conditions on the transferred function, square integrability (`JDecay`) and the adjoint relation (`JAdj`), and then hands everything to `modelPlaceBuilt2_of_genTransfer`. Its only user is [model places from the bounded operator realization](/entry/model-place-built2-of-bdd/), which proves `JDHatOpQQ`.

## Statement

**Theorem.** If `JfSmoothDS`, `GenTransfer`, `JDHatOpQQ` and `JABddOpQQ` hold, then `ModelPlaceBuilt2` holds.

`JDHatOpQQ` is stated in the entry for [`modelPlaceBuilt2_of_bdd`](/entry/model-place-built2-of-bdd/), `JABddOpQQ` in the entry for [`jABddOpQQ_of_ssnRep`](/entry/j-abdd-op-qq-of-ssn-rep/).

## Proof

At a built place, let $J_f$ be the function `B.JF` made from $f_v$, and let $j = \mathrm{trJ}(J_f)$ be its image under a frame $e$. Let $m = 2N + 2$, where $N$ is `S.Ntop`.

1. `jDecayTrQQ_of_open` proves `JDecayTrQQ` from `JDHatOpQQ`: there is $C_c$ such that, at each place built with $C_c$, if $J_f$ lies in `Q.FFl`, then $j$ satisfies `JDecay` with $m$. That is, for each word $w$ of length at most $m$ in the hat set of $L$, each component of $w \cdot j$ is square integrable on the model domain.
2. `jAdjTrQQ_of_three` proves `JAdjTrQQ` from `JDHatOpQQ`, `JABddOpQQ` and the proved `star_operator_transfer_holds`. Its conclusion is `JAdj` for $j$ with $m$: for $b$ in the monoid generated by the bounded set, $X$ in the hat set, words $v, w$ in the hat set with $|v| + |w| + 1 \le m$, and a smooth cutoff $\varphi$ of $G$ that vanishes near $0$,
$$\langle X(\varphi \cdot b\, v j),\, w j \rangle = \langle \varphi \cdot b\, v j,\, X^* w j \rangle.$$
3. `modelPlaceBuilt2_of_genTransfer` takes `JfSmoothDS`, `GenTransfer` and the two facts. It gets `GCore` from `GenTransfer` by `gCore_of_genTransfer`, adds the proved `energy_trace_identity_holds` and `energy_reality_holds`, and calls `modelPlaceBuilt2_of_openQQ`.

The hypothesis `JABddOpQQ` is used only in step 2. The work is in `jDecayTrQQd_of_leaves` and `jAdjTrQQ_of_leaves`, which we have not unfolded.
