This theorem proves the admissibility hypothesis `AdmAtEnt2` from the condition `CM2` on lattices with complex multiplication. Its user is [`servH_row_of_artin`](/entry/serv-h-row-of-artin/), which gets `CM2` from global Artin reciprocity and passes `AdmAtEnt2` to [`servH_row`](/entry/serv-h-row/). There it is used at each joint site by [`admissible_at_each_site`](/entry/admissible-at-each-site/). The proof only passes the hypothesis along. The content is in [`admissible_entries_small_chain`](/entry/admissible-entries-small-chain/).

## Statement

Let $d$ be a squarefree positive integer with $d \equiv 3 \pmod 4$ and let $\omega_d = (1 + i\sqrt d)/2$. A lattice $L \subset \mathbb C$ has CM by $\omega_d$ if $\omega_d L \subseteq L$. Let $g_2(L)$, $g_3(L)$ be its Weierstrass invariants. For a prime $\ell$, let $\mathbb Q_\ell^{\mathrm{ur}}$ be the closure in $\mathbb C_\ell$ of the field generated by the roots of unity of order prime to $\ell$.

`CM2` says: for each such $d$ and $L$, each prime $\ell \ge 5$ with $\ell \nmid d$ and each field isomorphism $\iota \colon \mathbb C \to \mathbb C_\ell$, there are $g_2', g_3' \in \mathbb Q_\ell^{\mathrm{ur}}$ and $\lambda \neq 0$ with
$$g_2' = \lambda^4 \iota(g_2(L)), \quad g_3' = \lambda^6 \iota(g_3(L)), \quad \lVert g_2' \rVert \le 1, \quad \lVert g_3' \rVert \le 1, \quad \lVert g_2'^3 - 27 g_3'^2 \rVert = 1.$$
So the curve of $L$ has a model with integral coefficients and unit discriminant over $\mathbb Q_\ell^{\mathrm{ur}}$.

`AdmAtEnt2` is stated in [`servH_row`](/entry/serv-h-row/): under the hypotheses `CurveIn`, `ThetaSeriesDataHypothesesBelowMaximalLevel`, `XiIn`, `LawInLt` and the ties `TiesAt`, the pending system at a place is `admitted`.

**Theorem.** `CM2` implies `AdmAtEnt2`.

## Proof

Make `CM2` an instance `Fact CM2`. Then apply `admissible_entries_small_chain`, which proves `AdmAtEnt2` under this instance.
