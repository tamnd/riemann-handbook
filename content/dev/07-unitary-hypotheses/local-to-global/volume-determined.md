This theorem proves `VolumeOddGeneric`: the volume of a fundamental domain for a real stabilizer, under a pinned Haar measure, is a ratio of two indices of arithmetic groups. It is one line that applies `volume_generic_odd_of_rep` to two results proved elsewhere. Its only user is [the nineteen hypotheses of Theorem S](/entry/theorem-s-leaves-of-carrier-g/), which uses it for the conjuncts `RowsSumC` and `RowFormAllP`.

## Statement

**Theorem.** Let $D$ be a datum with $g_i \ge 3$ for all $i$ (the Lean `D.host.g i`, which we did not unfold), $X$ a Hida setting, $Y$ full data over $X$, and $u, u'$ words with the prime hypothesis. Let $t$ be a relevant generic term with $G(t) \neq 0$ at a point $w$, and $(F', L, U)$ a normal representative of $t$ at $w$: $L$ is lower and $U$ upper unitriangular, and $Z(F') = L \, W_w \, U$ for the matrix $W_w$ of $w$. Let $\mu_R$ be a measure on $G_\infty$ that is pinned at $(t, w)$: it is the conjugate by an element $s$ of a Haar measure on a stabilizer set, normalized so that a coordinate map sends it to a fixed product measure $\mu_{\mathrm{stab}}$. Let $R(t)$ be the set of $y \in N_u$ with $x_t^{-1} y x_t \in N_{u'}$, where $x_t$ is the point of $t$ in $G_\infty$. Let $R_O(t)$ be the set `ROinf`, which we did not unfold. Let $K$ be a measurable set such that each $r \in R(t)$ has exactly one $s \in R_O(t)$ with $s r \in K$. Then

$$\mu_R(K) = \frac{[\Gamma_{\mathrm{int}} : \Gamma_{\mathrm{int}} \cap \Gamma_{\mathrm{aut}}]}{[\Gamma_{\mathrm{aut}} : \Gamma_{\mathrm{int}} \cap \Gamma_{\mathrm{aut}}]}.$$

Here $\Gamma_{\mathrm{int}}$ (`stabInt`) is generated by the lower unitriangular $a$ with $a W_w b = W_w$ for an upper unitriangular $b$, with integrality conditions at every finite place. $\Gamma_{\mathrm{aut}}$ (`stabAut`) is generated by the matrices $L^{-1} u^* L$, where $u$ is the matrix of an element that stabilizes the lattice of $t$ and both flags. The right side is the Lean `volIdx`.

## Proof

`volume_generic_odd_of_rep` takes two inputs.

- `dictC_holds` (`DictC`): an injective homomorphism $\Phi$ from the stabilizer of $W_w$ into $G_\infty$, such that $c^{-1} \Phi c$ lands in $R(t)$ and $\Phi(\Gamma_{\mathrm{aut}}) = c \, R_O(t) \, c^{-1}$ for some $c$.
- `covolOne3C_holds` (`CovolOne3C`): a fundamental domain $K_A$ for $\Phi(\Gamma_{\mathrm{int}})$ of measure $1$.

From these, `volume_generic_odd_of` compares the two fundamental domains. Both groups lie in the stabilizer of $W_w$, and both indices are finite and nonzero (`volIdxFinP`). Then the measure of $K$ is the measure of $K_A$ times the index ratio. We did not open the comparison lemma.
