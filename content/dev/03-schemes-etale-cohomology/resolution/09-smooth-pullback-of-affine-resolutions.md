## Smooth pullback of affine resolutions

The affine resolution of Section 8 depends on a chosen embedding of $Y$ into a smooth affine scheme. To glue the affine resolutions to a resolution of an arbitrary scheme, we need that they are compatible with open immersions, and more generally with smooth maps $u \colon V \to Y$. This section proves that compatibility. The difficulty is that the chosen embeddings of $V$ and of $Y$ are not related. We construct, étale locally on $V$, a smooth ambient space over the ambient space of $Y$ that contains $V$ as a fibre product. Then functoriality of the principalization for smooth maps and the independence of the embedding give the result. Section 10 uses it for open immersions and étale covers.

Throughout, $k$ has characteristic $0$, and $\mathbb{A}^M$ is affine space over $k$ with coordinates indexed by $\{0, \dots, M - 1\}$.

**Definition 8.9.1 (Coordinate codimension).** Let $t \colon X \to Y$ be a closed immersion of schemes over $k$ and $c \geq 0$. Then $t$ has *smooth coordinate codimension $c$* if each point of $X$ has an open neighbourhood $U$ in $Y$ with a smooth map $q \colon U \to \mathbb{A}^c$ over $k$ such that the ideal of $X$ on $U$ is the inverse image under $q$ of the ideal of the origin.

So, locally, $X$ is cut out by $c$ functions that are part of a smooth coordinate system on $Y$.

**Definition 8.9.2 (Presented embedding).** An embedding datum $(A, e)$ for $Y$ is *presented in affine space* if $A$ comes with a closed immersion $\rho \colon A \to \mathbb{A}^M$ over $k$, with $M \geq 1$, of smooth coordinate codimension $c \geq 1$. A *presented selection* chooses, for each reduced affine $Y$ of finite type over $k$, an embedding datum presented in affine space, such that its base change to each field extension of characteristic $0$ is again presented.

An *embedded resolution rule* $E$ gives a sequence of blow-ups $E(Y, D)$ of $Y$ for each reduced affine $Y$ of finite type over $k$ and each embedding datum $D$ for $Y$. The rule of Section 8 is an example. We use three conditions on $E$:

1. *independence of the embedding*: if $D$ and $D'$ are presented in affine space, then $E(Y, D)$ and $E(Y, D')$ are isomorphic;
2. *compatibility with smooth ambient maps*: if $D' = (A', e')$ and $D = (A, e)$ are embedding data for $Y'$ and $Y$, $\varphi \colon A' \to A$ is smooth over $k$, and the square with $\psi \colon Y' \to Y$ is cartesian, then $E(Y', D')$ is a pullback of $E(Y, D)$ along $\psi$;
3. *proper centres*: every centre of $E(Y, D)$ is nonempty.

The first step is a local form of the Jacobian criterion, adapted to a smooth map.

**Lemma 8.9.3 (Graph chart).** Let $X$ be a closed subscheme of a smooth affine scheme $A$ of finite type over $k$, and let $\rho \colon A \to \mathbb{A}^M$ be a closed immersion over $k$. Let $h \colon Y \to X$ be smooth of relative dimension $r$, and let $y \in Y$. Then there are:

1. an affine open $V \ni y$ of $Y$, a smooth affine $A'$ of finite type over $k$, a smooth map $g \colon A' \to A$ over $k$, and a closed immersion $j \colon V \to A'$ such that $V = A' \times_A X$;
2. numbers $n = q + r$, a closed immersion $\sigma \colon A' \to \mathbb{A}^{M + n}$ that lifts $\rho g$ along the projection to $\mathbb{A}^M$, functions $F_1, \dots, F_q$ and $\Delta$ on $\mathbb{A}^{M+n}$;

such that the ideal of $\sigma$ is generated by the ideal of $\rho$ and $F_1, \dots, F_q$, the image of $\sigma$ lies in $D(\Delta)$, and the map $D(\Delta) \to \mathbb{A}^{M+q}$, $(x, t) \mapsto (x, F(x, t))$, is smooth.

Lean: `FunctorialResolution.EmbeddedResolution.smoothPullbackHasGraphChart`.

*Proof.* Near $y$, the smooth map $h$ is the pullback of a standard smooth algebra $B' = \mathcal{O}(A)[x_1, \dots, x_n] / (f_1, \dots, f_q)$, where a $q \times q$ minor of the Jacobian matrix $(\partial f_j / \partial x_{s(i)})$ is invertible in $B'$. The relative dimension is $n - q = r$. Put $A' = \operatorname{Spec} B'$ and let $g$ be the structure map; it is smooth. Lift the $f_j$ along the surjection $\mathcal{O}(\mathbb{A}^M) \to \mathcal{O}(A)$ to polynomials $F_j$ on $\mathbb{A}^{M+n}$, and let $\Delta$ be the corresponding minor. The Jacobian determinant commutes with the lift and with evaluation, so $\Delta$ maps to the invertible minor in $B'$. This gives the image condition and the smoothness of the graph map. $\square$

**Theorem 8.9.4 (Local ambient embedding).** Assume that affine spaces are smooth over $k$. Let $X$, $A$ and $h \colon Y \to X$ be as in the lemma, and assume that $\rho$ has smooth coordinate codimension $c$. Then for each $y \in Y$ there are $V$, $A'$, $g$ and $j$ as in part 1 of the lemma, and $N_0 \geq r$, such that for every $N \geq N_0$ there is a closed immersion $\sigma \colon A' \to \mathbb{A}^{M + N}$ over $k$ of smooth coordinate codimension $c + (N - r)$.

Lean: `FunctorialResolution.EmbeddedResolution.smoothPullbackHasLocalAmbientEmbedding_of_affineSpaceIsSmooth`.

*Proof.* Take the graph chart. Locally $A$ is cut out in $\mathbb{A}^M$ by $c$ coordinates, and $A'$ is cut out in $D(\Delta)$ by these and the $q$ functions $F_j$. The smooth graph map makes the $F_j$ part of a coordinate system, so $\sigma$ has smooth coordinate codimension $c + q = c + (n - r)$. Padding with extra coordinates set to $0$ gives the same for every $N \geq n$. $\square$

Now we compare two presented embeddings along a smooth map.

**Theorem 8.9.5 (Étale cover with compatible embeddings).** Assume that affine spaces are smooth over $k$, that the conclusion of the previous theorem holds, and that the empty list is in strict normal crossing position on smooth schemes. Let $Y$ and $V$ be reduced, affine, of finite type and quasi-compact over $k$, with embedding data $G$ for $Y$ and $H$ for $V$, both presented in affine space. Let $u \colon V \to Y$ be smooth of relative dimension $r$ over $k$. Then there is a reduced affine $V''$ of finite type over $k$ with an étale surjective map $c \colon V'' \to V$ over $k$, and:

1. an embedding datum $G'$ for $V''$, presented in affine space, with a smooth map $f$ from its ambient space to that of $G$ such that $V''$ is the fibre product of the ambient space of $G'$ and $Y$, via $u c$;
2. an embedding datum $H'$ for $V''$, presented in affine space, with a smooth map $g$ from its ambient space to that of $H$ such that $V''$ is the fibre product of the ambient space of $H'$ and $V$, via $c$.

Lean: `FunctorialResolution.EmbeddedResolution.exists_etaleCover_closedEmbeddingIntoAffineSpace_of_smooth`.

*Proof.* In outline: for each point of $V$, the previous theorem applied to $u$ and to the identity of $V$ gives local charts, with thresholds $N_0$. $V$ is quasi-compact, so finitely many chart domains cover $V$. Take $N$ at least all thresholds. Let $V''$ be the disjoint union of the chosen basic opens. The map $c$ is étale and surjective. The ambient spaces of the charts all embed into one affine space of dimension $M + N + 1$, by the coordinates of the charts and one extra coordinate for the basic opens. This gives the two presented embedding data $G'$ and $H'$ with the smooth maps $f$ and $g$. $\square$

**Theorem 8.9.6 (Smooth pullback of affine resolutions).** Let $E$ be an embedded resolution rule that is independent of the embedding, compatible with smooth ambient maps, and has proper centres. Let $C$ be a presented selection. Assume the hypotheses of the previous theorem. Let $Y$ and $V$ be reduced, affine, of finite type and quasi-compact over $k$, and let $u \colon V \to Y$ be smooth of relative dimension $r$ over $k$. Then $E(V, C(V))$ is a pullback of $E(Y, C(Y))$ along $u$.

Lean: `FunctorialResolution.EmbeddedResolution.isPullbackBlowupSequence_presentedSelection_of_smooth`.

*Proof.* Take $c \colon V'' \to V$, $G'$ and $H'$ from the previous theorem. By compatibility with smooth ambient maps, $E(V'', G')$ is a pullback of $E(Y, C(Y))$ along $u c$, and $E(V'', H')$ is a pullback of $E(V, C(V))$ along $c$. Since $c$ is surjective and the centres are nonempty, the second pullback is strict. By independence of the embedding, $E(V'', G')$ and $E(V'', H')$ are isomorphic.

Let $s$ be the base change of $E(Y, C(Y))$ along $u$, with the blow-ups along empty centres removed. It is a pullback of $E(Y, C(Y))$ along $u$. The base change of $s$ along $c$ is a strict pullback of $s$, so it is a pullback of $E(Y, C(Y))$ along $u c$. Two pullbacks of the same sequence along the same map are isomorphic, so $E(V'', G')$ is isomorphic to this base change. So $E(V'', H')$ is a strict pullback along $c$ of both $E(V, C(V))$ and $s$, up to isomorphism. A strict pullback along a surjective flat map determines the sequence up to isomorphism, so $E(V, C(V))$ is isomorphic to $s$. Hence $E(V, C(V))$ is a pullback of $E(Y, C(Y))$ along $u$. $\square$

For an open immersion $u$ the relative dimension is $0$. This is the case used for gluing.

**Remark 8.9.7 (Formalization).** The graph chart and the local ambient embedding are the propositions `SmoothPullbackHasGraphChart` ([GraphChart.lean](lean:Development/ResolutionOfSingularities/EmbeddedResolution/ResolutionDescent/GraphChart.lean#L77)) and `SmoothPullbackHasLocalAmbientEmbedding`, and both are proved. The hypotheses "affine spaces are smooth" (`AffineSpaceIsSmooth`) and "the empty list is in strict normal crossing position on smooth schemes" are passed as arguments, though the project proves both. A presented embedding is `ClosedEmbeddingIntoAffineSpace`, which carries its dimension and codimension as data. In the Lean proof of the étale cover theorem, the local charts are chosen with `Nonempty.some` and the finite subcover comes from compactness. The last theorem is stated for an arbitrary embedded resolution rule and presented selection, so its three conditions on $E$ are arguments. We read the proof of the last theorem in full. For the étale cover theorem we read the statement and the choice of the cover, not the construction of the embedding into one affine space, which is the content of the steps from line 301 of [EtaleCoverClosedEmbedding.lean](lean:Development/ResolutionOfSingularities/EmbeddedResolution/ResolutionDescent/EtaleCoverClosedEmbedding.lean#L301).
