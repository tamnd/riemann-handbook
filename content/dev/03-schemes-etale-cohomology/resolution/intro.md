This chapter proves Hironaka's theorem on [[resolution of singularities|MB52NDA6WN]] over a field of characteristic $0$, in its strong and functorial form, together with the principalization of ideals on smooth schemes from which it comes. The proof follows the route of Włodarczyk and Kollár. A marked ideal $(X, I, m, E)$ on a smooth scheme is resolved by [[blow-ups|4SFS82N88K]] in smooth centres, by induction on the dimension of $X$: divisor reduction moves the problem to a boundary divisor, a hypersurface of maximal contact moves it to a hypersurface, and the dimension drops. Then an affine scheme $Y$ is embedded in affine space, the ideal of $Y$ is principalized, and the strict transforms of $Y$ give a resolution of $Y$. Independence of the embedding and compatibility with smooth maps let the affine resolutions glue to a resolution of every reduced scheme of finite type. The chapter needs from Volume 1 the [schemes and the smooth, étale and proper morphisms](/learn/geometry/#schemes-and-varieties) and the statements of [resolution and principalisation](/learn/geometry/#resolution-of-singularities).

The two top results of the chapter are the correctness of the principalization rules and functorial resolution in characteristic $0$. Both reach the step [EveryCharacter from the literature and VWin](/step/every-character-of-literature/). There resolution and principalisation in characteristic $0$ are two of the inputs of the bound on covolumes of [the Weil bound](/proof/weil-bound/), so these results are proved here and are not taken from the literature.
