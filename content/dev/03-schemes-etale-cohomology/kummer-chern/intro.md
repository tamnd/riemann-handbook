This chapter builds the multiplicative structure of [étale cohomology](/learn/geometry/#tale-cohomology) with coefficients in the Tate twists $\mathbb{Z}/q(i)$, on simplicial spaces over schemes. It proves the Kummer sequence $0 \to \mu_q \to \mathbb{G}_m \to \mathbb{G}_m \to 0$ in the form the Galois row needs, and it identifies $\mu_q$ with $\mathbb{Z}/q(1)$ through [roots of unity](/learn/algebra/#roots-of-unity). It proves that the tensor product with a bounded below complex of flat sheaves keeps quasi-isomorphisms, and with this it constructs the cup product from Alexander-Whitney cochains and proves the ring laws: existence, uniqueness, associativity, the unit law and graded commutativity. It defines the Kummer class of a unit and the first Chern class of a [line bundle](/learn/geometry/#line-bundles-and-divisors) by explicit cocycles, and proves that they are natural and that the Kummer class is additive. It then uses the projective bundle formula to define Chern classes, proves the Whitney sum formula, and turns Chern classes into power sum classes with Newton's formulas in a ring of [formal power series](/learn/algebra/#polynomials-and-power-series). The result is the axiom D2: natural, additive classes $s_k(V)$ with $s_k(L) = c_1(L)^k$ for line bundles. The last sections prove that the cohomology of a filtered limit of schemes is the colimit of the cohomology groups, and a result on division in compact groups: from a key criterion for a group of matrices with a lacunary Lie element, a continuous semisimple representation $\rho$ of a compact group, whose elements are killed by polynomials of degree $n$ on a dense set, has a subrepresentation $r$ of dimension $n$ such that $\rho$ embeds into a sum of copies of $r$.

From Volume 1 the chapter needs [rings and modules](/learn/algebra/#rings-ideals-and-modules), [tensor products](/learn/algebra/#tensor-products-and-coinvariants), [matrices and characteristic polynomials](/learn/algebra/#matrices), roots of unity and power series, [schemes and étale maps](/learn/geometry/#schemes-and-varieties), [topological groups](/learn/analysis/#topological-groups-and-covering-maps), [Lie algebras](/learn/analysis/#lie-groups-and-lie-algebras) and the definition of étale cohomology. The Kummer sequence and the continuity of cohomology go to [the top theorem of the étale topology](/entry/etale-topology-top/), and the power sum classes go to [the axiom D2 of étale cohomology](/entry/a0/). Both reach [the finiteness of the representation](/entry/v-fin-holds/), which the step [geometric realization of weight one](/step/geo-one-holds/) uses. The small subrepresentation feeds [the division of a Galois representation](/entry/div-set-holds/), which is used in the step [local-global compatibility from a geometric lift](/step/galois-local-global-strong-of-geometry-lift/) of [the Galois row](/proof/galois-row/).
