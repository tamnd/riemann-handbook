## Kummer classes, first Chern classes and naturality

This section defines two kinds of classes. The Kummer class of a unit $u$ lies in $H^1(X, \mathbb{Z}/q(1))$. The first Chern class of a line bundle $L$ lies in $H^2(E, \mathbb{Z}/q(1))$. Both are given by explicit cocycles. Then the section proves that the four operations of the chapter commute with pullback: the cup product, the unit class, the first Chern class and the Kummer class. The first Chern class and the naturality are used for the Chern classes of vector bundles in [the last section](/entry/s42/).

The classical picture is the [Kummer sequence](/entry/kum-seq/) $0 \to \mu_q \to \mathbb{G}_m \to \mathbb{G}_m \to 0$. Its coboundary $H^0(X, \mathbb{G}_m) \to H^1(X, \mu_q)$ sends a unit $u$ to the class of the torsor of $q$-th roots of $u$. Its coboundary $H^1(X, \mathbb{G}_m) \to H^2(X, \mu_q)$ sends the class of a line bundle to its first Chern class (Milne, *Étale Cohomology*, Chapter III, Proposition 4.9 for $H^1(X, \mathbb{G}_m) = \mathrm{Pic}(X)$, and Chapter VI, §10). We do not use the coboundary maps. We write down cochains that represent the two coboundaries, and we prove the needed facts from these cochains.

We keep the notation of the earlier sections: $\mathcal{I}_i$ is the chosen injective resolution of $\mathbb{Z}/q(i)$, $\iota$ is its augmentation, and $\langle \zeta \rangle_i$ is the generator of $\mathbb{Z}/q(i)$ for $\zeta \in \mathrm{Prim}_q$. For a scheme $U$ we write $\Gamma(U)^\times$ for the units of $\Gamma(U, \mathcal{O}_U)$.

**Definition 4.7.1 (Kummer section).** Let $q \geq 1$. A *Kummer section* is a family of maps $t_U : \Gamma(U)^\times \to \mathcal{I}_1^0(U)$, one for each scheme $U$, such that:

1. $t$ commutes with restriction along each map $g : V \to U$, when $q$ is a unit on $U$;
2. $t_U(\zeta v) = t_U(v) + \iota \langle \zeta \rangle_1$ for each $\zeta \in \mathrm{Prim}_q(\Gamma(U, \mathcal{O}_U))$ and each $v \in \Gamma(U)^\times$.

The *chosen Kummer section* $t$ is $\mathrm{pick}$ of this property.

The second condition says that $t$ is a lift of the map "$v \mapsto$ the class of $v$ modulo $\mu_q$". Since $d \iota = 0$, the section $d t_U(v) \in \mathcal{I}_1^1(U)$ does not change when $v$ is multiplied by a root of unity. So $d t_U(v)$ depends only on $v^q$, at least where $\mu_q$ is generated by a primitive root. We take as given that a Kummer section exists. Then the chosen $t$ satisfies the two conditions.

**Definition 4.7.2 (Kummer class).** Let $X$ be a scheme and let $u \in \Gamma(X)^\times$. A *Kummer cocycle* of $u$ is a $1$-cocycle $\gamma$ of the space $\mathrm{sch}(X)$ with values in $\mathcal{I}_1$ such that, for each scheme $U$ and each map $x : U \to X$:

1. the component of $\gamma$ on $x$ as a $1$-simplex, in $\mathcal{I}_1^0(U)$, is $0$;
2. the component of $\gamma$ on $x$ as a $0$-simplex, in $\mathcal{I}_1^1(U)$, is $d t_U(v)$ for each $v \in \Gamma(U)^\times$ with $v^q = x^* u$.

The *Kummer class* $\mathrm{kum}(u) \in H^1(X, \mathbb{Z}/q(1))$ is $\mathrm{pick}$ of the property "is the class of a Kummer cocycle of $u$".

A space $E$ has a set of $N$-simplices over each scheme $U$. A map $L : E \to B\mathrm{GL}_N$ is a *vector bundle of rank* $N$ on $E$. Here $B\mathrm{GL}_N$ is the space whose $N'$-simplices over $U$ are the strings of $N'$ composable matrices in $\mathrm{GL}_N(\Gamma(U, \mathcal{O}_U))$: the nerve of the group, seen as a category with one object. A vector bundle of rank $1$ is a *line bundle*. For a line bundle $L$ and a $1$-simplex $x$ of $E$ over $U$, the *transition unit* $g_L(x) \in \Gamma(U)^\times$ is the determinant of the $1 \times 1$ matrix $L(x)$. For a $2$-simplex $x$ with edges $x_{01}$, $x_{12}$ and $x_{02}$, the nerve gives $g_L(x_{02}) = g_L(x_{01})\, g_L(x_{12})$. For [line bundles on schemes](/learn/geometry/#line-bundles-and-divisors) this is the usual cocycle condition of transition functions.

**Definition 4.7.3 (First Chern class).** Let $L$ be a line bundle on a space $E$. A *Chern cocycle* of $L$ is a $2$-cocycle $\gamma$ of $E$ with values in $\mathcal{I}_1$ with these components:

1. on each $0$-simplex, $\gamma_0(x) = 0$ in $\mathcal{I}_1^2(U)$;
2. on each $1$-simplex $x$ over $U$, $\gamma_1(x) = d t_U(v)$ in $\mathcal{I}_1^1(U)$ for each $v$ with $v^q = g_L(x)$;
3. on each $2$-simplex $x$ over $U$, $\gamma_2(x) = t_U(v) + t_U(w) - t_U(vw)$ in $\mathcal{I}_1^0(U)$ for each $v$ and $w$ with $v^q = g_L(x_{01})$ and $w^q = g_L(x_{12})$.

The *first Chern class* $c_1(L) \in H^2(E, \mathbb{Z}/q(1))$ is $\mathrm{pick}$ of the property "is the class of a Chern cocycle of $L$".

The components follow the coboundary of the Kummer sequence. On an edge we take a $q$-th root $v$ of the transition unit. On a triangle, $vw$ is a $q$-th root of $g_L(x_{02})$, and the value measures the failure of $t$ to be multiplicative.

**Lemma 4.7.4 (Chern cocycles).** Let $q \geq 1$, let $E$ be a space on which $q$ is invertible, and let $L$ be a line bundle on $E$. Then $L$ has a Chern cocycle, and $c_1(L) = [\gamma]$ for each Chern cocycle $\gamma$ of $L$.

*Proof.* First, $q$-th roots exist étale locally: if $q$ is a unit on $U$ and $u \in \Gamma(U)^\times$, then there is an étale covering of $U$ on whose members $u$ is a $q$-th power. Indeed, the algebra $\Gamma(U, \mathcal{O}_U)[X]/(X^q - u)$ is étale, since the derivative $qX^{q-1}$ is a unit modulo $X^q - u$, and it is finite and free over $\Gamma(U, \mathcal{O}_U)$, so its spectrum maps onto $U$. So the values in the definition are prescribed on the members of an étale covering. Two choices of roots give the same value, by the second condition on $t$. Sections of a sheaf are separated and glue, so there is exactly one section with the prescribed values on each edge and on each triangle. This gives a cochain $\gamma$ of degree $2$.

The cochain $\gamma$ has components only on simplices of dimension at most $2$, since $\mathcal{I}_1$ is $0$ in negative degrees. So $\delta \gamma$ has components only on simplices of dimension at most $3$. We check that these components vanish. On a vertex, the component is $d$ of the component $0$. On an edge, it is $d d t_U(v) = 0$, up to the face terms, which are $0$. On a triangle, the three edge terms and $d \gamma_2(x)$ cancel. On a tetrahedron, the alternating sum of the four face values $t(v) + t(w) - t(vw)$ is $0$, because of the cocycle condition of the transition units. So $\gamma$ is a cocycle, and a Chern cocycle exists.

The second claim follows from the definition by $\mathrm{pick}$. Two Chern cocycles of $L$ have the same values on each simplex, by the uniqueness of the glued sections. So the class of the property is unique, and $c_1(L)$ is the class of any Chern cocycle. $\square$

**Lemma 4.7.5 (Kummer classes are natural).** Let $q \geq 1$, let $f : Y \to X$ be a map of schemes, and let $u \in \Gamma(X)^\times$. If $q$ is a unit on $X$, then
$$
f^* \mathrm{kum}(u) = \mathrm{kum}(f^* u) \quad \text{in } H^1(Y, \mathbb{Z}/q(1)).
$$

Lean: `LateralTheory.E.LeafA6nat_xy7V2Cut.natKum`.

*Proof.* Kummer cocycles exist when $q$ is a unit on $X$, by the argument of the previous proof: the values are prescribed étale locally through local $q$-th roots, they do not depend on the roots by the second condition on $t$, and they glue. Let $\gamma$ be a Kummer cocycle of $u$. The pullback $f^* \gamma$ has the component $\gamma(f \circ x)$ on a simplex $x : U \to Y$. Since $(f \circ x)^* u = x^*(f^* u)$, the cocycle $f^* \gamma$ satisfies the two conditions of a Kummer cocycle of $f^* u$. The values of a Kummer cocycle are determined étale locally, so two Kummer cocycles of the same unit are equal. Hence $f^* \mathrm{kum}(u) = [f^* \gamma] = \mathrm{kum}(f^* u)$. $\square$

**Theorem 4.7.6 (Naturality).** Let $q \geq 1$.

1. For each map of spaces $f : E \to E'$ and classes $a \in H^n(E', \mathbb{Z}/q(i))$, $b \in H^m(E', \mathbb{Z}/q(j))$: $f^*(a \cup b) = f^* a \cup f^* b$.
2. For each map $f : E \to E'$ with $q$ invertible on $E'$: $f^* 1_{E'} = 1_E$.
3. For each map $f : E \to E'$ with $q$ invertible on $E'$ and each line bundle $L$ on $E'$: $f^* c_1(L) = c_1(L \circ f)$.
4. For each map of schemes $f : Y \to X$ with $q$ a unit on $X$ and each $u \in \Gamma(X)^\times$: $f^* \mathrm{kum}(u) = \mathrm{kum}(f^* u)$.

Lean: `LateralTheory.E.LeafA6nat_xy7V2Cut.goal_of` (also `LateralTheory.Fix.EtaleChapterDTwo.s0`, a second name).

*Proof.* For all four parts, $f^*$ is induced by the map of free complexes $C_\bullet(E) \to C_\bullet(E')$. On cochains it sends $\alpha$ to the cochain with components $\alpha_N(f(x))$. The map $f$ commutes with the faces of simplices.

Part 1. Let $a = [\alpha]$ and $b = [\beta]$, and let $\gamma$ be an Alexander-Whitney product of $\alpha$ and $\beta$ for the chosen twisted pairing. Such a $\gamma$ exists by [the existence theorem](/entry/cup-product-tensor-holds-of/). The Alexander-Whitney formula is evaluated on the front and back faces of $x$. Since $f$ commutes with faces, $f^* \gamma$ is an Alexander-Whitney product of $f^* \alpha$ and $f^* \beta$. By the defining property of the cup product, $f^*(a \cup b) = [f^* \gamma] = f^* a \cup f^* b$.

Part 2. If $E$ has a $0$-simplex $x$ over $U$, then $f(x)$ is a $0$-simplex of $E'$ over $U$. So $q$ is invertible on $E$. Let $\alpha$ be a unit cocycle of $E'$. Then $f^* \alpha$ has the values $\iota \langle \zeta \rangle_0$ on $0$-simplices, so it is a unit cocycle of $E$. By the fact (U1) of [the section on the unit](/entry/s38/), $f^* 1_{E'} = [f^* \alpha] = 1_E$.

Part 3. As in Part 2, $q$ is invertible on $E$. Let $\gamma$ be a Chern cocycle of $L$, from [the lemma on Chern cocycles](/entry/etale-first-chern-class-represented-by-chern-cocycle/). The transition units satisfy $g_{L \circ f}(x) = g_L(f(x))$, by the definition. So $f^* \gamma$ is a Chern cocycle of $L \circ f$, and the same lemma gives $f^* c_1(L) = [f^* \gamma] = c_1(L \circ f)$.

Part 4 is [the naturality of Kummer classes](/entry/nat-kum/). $\square$

**Remark 4.7.7.** Part 1 of the theorem has no hypothesis on $q$. Parts 2 and 3 need $q$ invertible on the target $E'$ only. The lemma on Chern cocycles needs $q$ invertible on $E$. Without it, $c_1(L)$ can be $0$ by the choice convention.

**Remark 4.7.8 (Formalization).** The classes $t$, $\mathrm{kum}(u)$, $c_1(L)$ and $1_E$ are defined with $\mathrm{pick}$ ([PullHlComp.lean](lean:Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/Divisors/PullHlComp.lean#L130)), so each fact of this section rests on an existence result. The existence of a Kummer section is a theorem named `intersectionNumber_baseChange` in the namespace `EtaleKummerTriangleExists`; it has nothing to do with intersection numbers, and we did not unfold its proof. The naturality of Kummer classes is the second part of `EtaleKummerClassExplicitCocycle.holds_of`, applied to this input. The theorem has the type `LeafA6nat`, a second name of `DivisorNaturality` ([InstCommRingR.lean](lean:Development/EtaleCohomology/EtaleSheaves/InstCommRingR.lean#L46), [DivisorSupportCondition.lean](lean:Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/Divisors/DivisorSupportCondition.lean#L26)), but it does not mention divisors.
