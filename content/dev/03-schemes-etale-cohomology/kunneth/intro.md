title: Introduction
---
This chapter proves the results of the Lean folder `BlockDecomposition` that the $\ell$-adic Hodge comparison takes as inputs. Despite the name of the folder, they are not about block decompositions. The first three sections work with period rings: they build a lattice in $B_{\mathrm{dR}}^+ / t^k$ with a base of divided powers, prove the universal property of the algebra of continuous functions from $\mathbb{Z}_\ell$ to a perfect $\ell$-adic algebra, and deduce a line object for which a filtration of the crystalline period ring is acyclic. The other five sections work on the site of thickenings of a smooth scheme over a field of characteristic $0$. They prove Čech descent for relative differential forms on the charts of a blow-up, the vanishing of graded cohomology on affine opens in all degrees but one, the transfer of a cohomological identity on a projective chart to an identity of functions in coordinates, and a Künneth formula for proper smooth products, glued by a Mayer-Vietoris argument, with slant maps as a consequence.

From Volume 1 the chapter uses [period rings](/learn/galois-representations/#period-rings) and [de Rham cohomology](/learn/geometry/#de-rham-cohomology-and-deformations). Its results enter the chapter on the $\ell$-adic Hodge comparison as [the level systems](/entry/lev-sys-ex/), [the continuous line](/entry/cont-line/) of [the local comparison](/entry/local-of/), [the parts of the Hodge-Tate comparison](/entry/parts/) behind [the bijectivity of the Hodge-Tate map](/entry/ht-bij-of/), [the vanishing on affine opens](/entry/gr-aff-pf/), and [the transfer lemma](/entry/cone-transfer/) in the proof that [the top power of the first Chern class is nonzero](/entry/pow-k-ne-zero-of/). Through them it enters the step [GeoOne holds](/step/geo-one-holds/) of [the Galois row](/proof/galois-row/).
