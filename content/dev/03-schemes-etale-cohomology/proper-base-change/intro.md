---
title: Introduction
---
This chapter proves the results on étale cohomology that the Galois row uses. We work with a model of étale cohomology with coefficients $\mathbb{Z}/q(i)$, given by a list of axioms, and proves the deep axioms from the simple ones: the power-sum classes of vector bundles, the proper base change theorem of M. Artin for these coefficients, and the finiteness of the cohomology of proper schemes over an algebraically closed field. The last part leaves the model and proves a Frobenius relation of Eichler-Shimura type on crystalline periods, from a congruence relation on points with values in perfect rings. From it we get that a Galois representation cut out of the cohomology of a tower is crystalline at the places above $\ell$, with the Frobenius of its Weil-Deligne representation controlled by the Hecke polynomial.

The chapter needs the basic facts on [étale cohomology](/learn/geometry/#tale-cohomology), on [period rings](/learn/galois-representations/#period-rings), on [crystalline representations](/learn/galois-representations/#crystalline-de-rham-and-potentially-semistable-representations) and on [Weil-Deligne representations](/learn/galois-representations/#weil-deligne-representations) from Volume 1. The step [Steps holds](/entry/steps-holds/) of the [Galois row](/proof/galois-row/) uses the proper base change theorem and the Frobenius at the places above $\ell$, and the step [VFin holds](/entry/v-fin-holds/) uses the finiteness theorem. Both feed the step [GeoOne holds](/step/geo-one-holds/), the Galois row in weight one.
