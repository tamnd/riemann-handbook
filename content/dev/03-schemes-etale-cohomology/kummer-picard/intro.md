This chapter proves the inputs from étale cohomology, coherent cohomology and commutative algebra that the Galois row uses. Its first seven sections are étale. The first is a moduli space for the clopen subschemes of fixed rank of a finite étale scheme. The next three give the projective bundle formula for spaces (simplicial presheaves on schemes) on which $q$ is invertible: a Künneth formula for $\mathbb{G}_m \times E$, the case of the projective line by a cover with two affine lines, and the induction on the rank by the blowup of $\mathbb{P}^N$ at a point. The next two show, without Jacobians, that the $q$-torsion of the Picard group of an affine curve over an algebraically closed field is finite: Kummer classes on a proper smooth curve do not change when the algebraically closed base field becomes larger, and a Selmer group of functions with divisors divisible by $q$ is finite modulo $q$-th powers. The last constructs objects of the derived category of étale sheaves on a scheme $B$ that represent relative cohomology, naturally over all étale $U \to B$. Sections 6.8 to 6.10 prove that coherent cohomology on a proper scheme over a Noetherian ring is finitely generated: Serre's theorem on projective space, Chow's lemma with a relative Serre theorem, and Noetherian induction. Sections 6.11 to 6.13 are commutative algebra for deformation rings: a multiplicity symbol on a Rees algebra, a bound for the length of a cotangent lattice of a graded algebra by valuations at its two-signed points, and graded presentations of framed deformation rings with numbers of variables and relations given by framed cohomology.

The chapter uses from Volume 1 [schemes and finite étale morphisms](/learn/geometry/#schemes-and-varieties), [line bundles and divisors](/learn/geometry/#line-bundles-and-divisors), [étale cohomology](/learn/geometry/#tale-cohomology), the [Leray-Hirsch theorem in topology](/learn/geometry/#complex-manifolds) as a model, [rings, ideals and modules](/learn/algebra/#rings-ideals-and-modules), [fields and extensions](/learn/algebra/#fields-and-extensions), [roots of unity](/learn/algebra/#roots-of-unity) and [ideals and the class group](/learn/number-fields/#ideals-and-the-class-group), together with [coherent cohomology](/learn/commutative-algebra/#coherent-cohomology), [deformation functors](/learn/deformations-patching/#deformation-functors) and [local deformation problems and global deformation rings](/learn/deformations-patching/#local-deformation-problems-and-global-deformation-rings). The results enter the proof of [GeoOne](/step/geo-one-def/), the Galois row in weight one, through the cohomology of a tower of Shimura varieties: the projective bundle formula through [Lemma 5.5.3](/entry/pbf-all/), the Picard torsion and the relative cohomology objects through the finiteness theorem [Theorem 3.7.1](/entry/etale-topology-top/), and the clopen subschemes through [Theorem 5.1.4](/entry/exists-finite-etale-representing-clopen-subschemes/). The coherent finiteness theorem, [Theorem 6.10.6](/entry/ground-theory-arith-scheme-coh-finite-h-of-is-proper/), feeds [EveryCharacter](/step/every-character/), where the proof splits into its two large parts. The length bound, [Theorem 6.12.8](/entry/main-holds/), and the graded presentations, [Theorem 6.13.8](/entry/lemma-p/), enter the window bound [Theorem](/entry/vwin-of-hypotheses-h-e-alt/) below [VWin](/step/vwin-def/), and the length bound also enters [Theorem 7.3 of Volume 9](/entry/abstract-count/).
