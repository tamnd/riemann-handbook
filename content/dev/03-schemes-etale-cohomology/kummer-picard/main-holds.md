This theorem proves $\mathrm{LemmaM}$, a statement of commutative algebra. [The abstract count](/entry/abstract-count/) uses it through `Abs.coord_count`, below the [main fork](/proof/vwin/). The proof normalizes the equations and then calls [`core_holds`](/entry/core-holds/), which has the main argument.

## Statement

Let $\mathcal{O}$ be a complete discrete valuation ring of characteristic zero. Let $K$ be an algebraically closed field over $\mathcal{O}$ with a valuation $v$ that extends the normalized valuation of $\mathcal{O}$ (`IsNVal`). Let $A = \bigoplus_{j \in \mathbb{Z}} A_j$ be a [[graded|8KB4SQGC0C]] $\mathcal{O}$-algebra with an augmentation $x \colon A \to \mathcal{O}$. Suppose that $A_0$ is a complete Noetherian local $\mathcal{O}$-algebra with the residue field of $\mathcal{O}$, that $A$ is generated by $A_0$ and finitely many elements of weights $\pm 1, \pm 2$, and that $x$ kills $A_j$ for $j \ne 0$.

Let $\mathfrak{s}$ be the set of $f$ with $cf \in (\ker x)^2$ for some $c \ne 0$. For each $j$, let $C_j$ be the image of $A_j \cap \ker x$ in $A/\mathfrak{s}$. Let $F_0, \dots, F_r \in A_{-1}$, $G_i \in A_{-2}$, $E_i \in A_1$, $E'_i \in A_2$ and $Z_i \in A_0 \cap \ker x$, and let $I$ be the ideal that they generate. Let $\ell$ be the length of $C_{-1}$ modulo the $\mathcal{O}$-span of the $F_i$.

A point is an $\mathcal{O}$-algebra map $\xi \colon A \to K$ that kills $I$. It is two-signed if it is nonzero on some $A_j$ with $j < 0$ and on some $A_j$ with $j > 0$. The torus $K^\times$ acts on points by $t \cdot \xi(f) = t^j \xi(f)$ for $f \in A_j$. A finite set $T$ of two-signed points covers if it meets the orbit of each two-signed point.

**Theorem.** Suppose $M_1$ to $M_5$:

1. $r \ge 1$; $x$ is a smooth point with $r$, $s$, $r'+1$, $s'+1$ and $m$ coordinates of weights $-1$, $-2$, $1$, $2$ and $0$; the $F_i$, $G_i$ and $Z_i$ span $C_{-1}$, $C_{-2}$ and $C_0$ up to nonzero scalars;
2. the image of $A_0$ in $A/I$ is a finite $\mathcal{O}$-module;
3. a condition on points that vanish in all nonzero weights (`M3`);
4. at each two-signed point, any two [[derivations|3J89REV9WQ]] that kill $I$ are linearly dependent;
5. $a \in A_0$, $x(a) = 0$, and $\xi(a) \ne 0$ at each two-signed point.

Then a covering set exists, $\ell$ is finite, and $\ell \le 2 \sum_{\xi \in T} v(\xi(a))$ for each covering set $T$.

## Proof

1. `rk_holds`: $C_{-1}$ is free of rank $r$.
2. `sm_holds`: replace the $F_i$ by $F'_i$ with the same $\mathcal{O}$-span, such that $F'_i \equiv \varpi^{c_i} y_i \bmod \mathfrak{s}$ for $i < r$, with a uniformizer $\varpi$ and $y_i \in A_{-1}$, and $F'_r \in \mathfrak{s}$. Then $\ell = \sum c_i$. The ideal $I$ does not change.
3. `cut_holds` gives a chart $\varphi$ of $A$ in power series over the fraction field, with $r$, $s$, $r'+1$ and $s'+1$ variables of weights $-1$, $-2$, $1$ and $2$, none of weight $0$, and $\varphi(Z_i) = 0$.
4. `nz_holds` replaces $a$ by $a' \equiv a \bmod I$ with $\varphi(a') \ne 0$. Points kill $I$, so $\xi(a') = \xi(a)$.
5. `fin_holds` with `hom_holds` gives a covering set.
6. `core_holds` gives $\sum c_i \le 2 \sum_{\xi \in T} v(\xi(a'))$.
