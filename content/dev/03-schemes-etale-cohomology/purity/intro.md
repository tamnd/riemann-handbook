This chapter proves results of étale cohomology and of the theory of schemes that the proof takes as inputs. Sections 1 to 3 prove the projective bundle formula: for a vector bundle $V$ of rank $N$ on a space (a simplicial presheaf on schemes) on which $q$ is invertible, the cohomology of the projective bundle $\mathbb{P}(V)$ with coefficients $\mathbb{Z}/q(i)$ is free over the cohomology of the base, with basis $1, c_1(L), \dots, c_1(L)^{N-1}$. The proof goes from schemes to constant spaces by a local to global argument, and from constant spaces to all spaces by the Eilenberg-Zilber theorem and a filtration by columns. Sections 4 and 5 prove a vanishing theorem for the generic fibre of a strict henselisation of the affine line over an absolutely integrally closed local domain: its $\mathbb{Z}/q$-torsors are trivial, its Picard group has no $q$-torsion, its units are $q$-th powers, and so its $H^1$ with coefficients $\mathbb{Z}/q(i)$ is zero. Sections 6 to 8 prove Abhyankar's lemma and the purity of the branch locus, and with them the étale enlargement of a model by roots of uniformisers. Sections 9 to 13 compute the cohomology of affine curves, of the punctured line and of fields of transcendence degree one, prove the Kummer sequence on spaces, and prove homotopy invariance. Sections 14 to 16 construct the Hilbert scheme of points of a finite flat morphism.

The chapter uses from Volume 1 the [étale cohomology](/learn/geometry/#tale-cohomology) of schemes, [line bundles and the Picard group](/learn/geometry/#line-bundles-and-divisors), the [projective bundle formula in topology](/learn/geometry/#complex-manifolds) as a model, [roots of unity](/learn/algebra/#roots-of-unity), [localization and integral closure](/learn/algebra/#rings-ideals-and-modules), and [flat and étale algebras](/learn/commutative-algebra/#flat-and-tale-algebras). Most of the results enter the proof below [the theorem on the local steps for the cohomology of a tower](/entry/steps-holds/) of [the Galois row](/proof/galois-row/): the projective bundle formula as one of the results of étale cohomology that the proof collects into one package, the vanishing of $H^1$ through two proofs of local acyclicity for the relative affine line, and the results of sections 9 to 13 as further conditions of that package. The étale enlargement is [the last step](/entry/etale-enlarge-by-uniformiser-roots/) of the enlargement to a good model. The Hilbert scheme gives [the classification of clopen subschemes of fixed rank](/entry/exists-clop-rep-of-rank-of-locally-noetherian/) of a finite étale cover.
