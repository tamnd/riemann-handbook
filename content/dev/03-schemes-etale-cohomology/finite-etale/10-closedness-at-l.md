## Closedness at ℓ

The Galois representations of weight one are built as limits: their traces are limits of linear combinations of traces of representations that come from geometry. This section proves that the local properties at a place above $\ell$ pass to such a limit. If every representation in the approximating set is semistable at the place with fixed Hodge-Tate jumps, then the limit is de Rham with the same jumps, and it is crystalline when all of them are crystalline (Theorem 5.10.5). The proof restricts everything to a decomposition group, which is the Galois group of a finite extension of $\mathbb{Q}_\ell$, and then applies [the theorem on Hodge-Tate jumps through a cover](/entry/closed-of-cover/) of the chapter on Breuil-Kisin modules. The work of this section is to set up the global data and to move the local hypotheses to the form that theorem needs.

Let $E$ be a number field, $\Omega$ an algebraic closure of $E$ and $G_E = \mathrm{Gal}(\Omega/E)$ with its profinite topology. Let $\ell$ be a prime and $\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell$ an algebraic closure of $\mathbb{Q}_\ell$, with the topology of the $\ell$-adic absolute value. (The chapter on Breuil-Kisin modules writes $\Omega$ for $\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell$.) A *finite extension* of $\mathbb{Q}_\ell$ is a subfield of $\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell$ of finite degree over $\mathbb{Q}_\ell$, and $G_K = \mathrm{Gal}(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell/K)$ for such a $K$. We use the [period rings](/learn/galois-representations/#period-rings) ([[Fontaine's period rings|K6HW8YWCQJ]]) and the semistable, de Rham and crystalline representations of $G_K$ with the multiplicities $m_\tau(V, n)$, as in the chapter on Breuil-Kisin modules and [the Background](/learn/galois-representations/#crystalline-de-rham-and-potentially-semistable-representations). For a valuation ring $A$ of $\Omega$ that contains the ring of integers of $E$, let $D_A \subseteq G_E$ be its [[decomposition group|7X92G8CQ33]], the subgroup of $g$ with $g(A) = A$.

**Definition 5.10.1 (Local bridge).** Let $A$ be a valuation ring of $\Omega$. A *local bridge* at $A$ consists of:
1. a finite extension $K_w$ of $\mathbb{Q}_\ell$ and a ring map $j : \Omega \to \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell$ such that $j(E) \subseteq K_w$ and $j(E)$ is dense in $K_w$;
2. an isomorphism of groups $e : D_A \to G_{K_w}$ such that $e$ and $e^{-1}$ are continuous and $e(g)(j(x)) = j(g(x))$ for all $g \in D_A$ and $x \in \Omega$;
3. a finite extension $L_w$ of $\mathbb{Q}_\ell$ that contains $\tau(K_w)$ for every $\mathbb{Q}_\ell$-embedding $\tau : K_w \to \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell$;
4. a topological ring $O$ over $\mathbb{Z}_\ell$ with an isomorphism of topological $\mathbb{Z}_\ell$-algebras from $O$ to the ring of integers of $L_w$.

We say that the bridge *induces* $A$ if $A = \{x \in \Omega : |j(x)| \leq 1\}$.

**Definition 5.10.2 (Models and local properties).** Fix a local bridge at $A$, and let $r : D_A \to \mathrm{GL}_a(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell)$ be a homomorphism. A *model* of $r$ over a finite extension $L \supseteq L_w$ is a homomorphism $r' : D_A \to \mathrm{GL}_a(L)$ that equals $r$ in $\mathrm{GL}_a(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell)$. Its *representation* $V_{r'}$ is $L^a$, with $g \in G_{K_w}$ acting by $r'(e^{-1}(g))$. We say that $r$ is *semistable at $A$* if it has a model $r'$ with $V_{r'}$ semistable. In the same way $r$ is *de Rham at $A$* or *crystalline at $A$* if some model has a de Rham or a crystalline representation.

**Definition 5.10.3 (Hodge-Tate jumps at an embedding).** Fix a local bridge at $A$. Let $\tau : E \to \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell$ be an embedding, and let $L$ be a finite extension of $\mathbb{Q}_\ell$. A $\mathbb{Q}_\ell$-embedding $\tau' : K_w \to L$ *induces* $\tau$ if $\tau(x) = \tau'(j(x))$ for all $x \in E$. Let $J$ be a finite multiset of integers and $r : D_A \to \mathrm{GL}_a(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell)$ a homomorphism. We say that $r$ *has the jumps $J$ at $\tau$* if there are a model $r'$ of $r$ over some $L \supseteq L_w$ with $V_{r'}$ de Rham and an embedding $\tau' : K_w \to L$ that induces $\tau$, such that $m_{\tau'}(V_{r'}, n)$ is the multiplicity of $n$ in $J$ for every $n \in \mathbb{Z}$.

**Definition 5.10.4 (Trace approximation).** Let $\Gamma$ be a group, $r : \Gamma \to \mathrm{GL}_a(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell)$ a homomorphism and $\mathcal{C}$ a set of homomorphisms $\Gamma \to \mathrm{GL}_a(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell)$. We say that $\mathcal{C}$ *approximates $r$ in trace* if for every $\varepsilon > 0$ there are $\rho_1, \dots, \rho_s \in \mathcal{C}$ and $c_1, \dots, c_s \in \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell$ with
$$
\Big| \mathrm{tr}\, r(g) - \sum_{k=1}^{s} c_k \, \mathrm{tr}\, \rho_k(g) \Big| \leq \varepsilon \quad \text{for all } g \in \Gamma.
$$
The homomorphism $r$ is *semisimple* if every subspace of $\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell^a$ that is stable under $r(\Gamma)$ has a stable complement.

**Theorem 5.10.5 (Closedness at $\ell$).** Let $E$, $\Omega$ and $\ell$ be as above, let $a \geq 1$, and use the period rings above. Let $w$ be a finite place of $E$ and $A$ a valuation ring of $\Omega$ over $w$, so that an integer $x$ of $E$ lies in the maximal ideal of $A$ if and only if $x \in w$. Fix a local bridge at $A$ that induces $A$. Let $J$ assign to each embedding $\tau : E \to \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell$ a finite multiset $J(\tau)$ of integers. Let $r : G_E \to \mathrm{GL}_a(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell)$ be continuous and semisimple, and let $\mathcal{C}$ be a set of homomorphisms $G_E \to \mathrm{GL}_a(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell)$ that approximates $r$ in trace. Assume that every $\rho \in \mathcal{C}$ is continuous, that $\rho|_{D_A}$ is semistable at $A$, and that $\rho|_{D_A}$ has the jumps $J(\tau)$ at every $\tau$ that is induced by an embedding $\tau' : K_w \to L'$ into some finite extension $L'$ of $\mathbb{Q}_\ell$. Then:
1. $r|_{D_A}$ is de Rham at $A$, and it has the jumps $J(\tau)$ at every $\tau$ that is induced by an embedding $\tau' : K_w \to L'$ into some finite extension $L'$;
2. if $\rho|_{D_A}$ is crystalline at $A$ for every $\rho \in \mathcal{C}$, then $r|_{D_A}$ is crystalline at $A$.

*Proof.* We give the proof in five steps. It uses five facts that we take as given. (a) If $r$ is semistable at $A$, then $V_{r'}$ is semistable for every model $r'$ of $r$. The same holds for crystalline. (b) If $r$ has the jumps $J$ at $\tau$, then $m_{\tau'}(V_{r'}, n)$ is the multiplicity of $n$ in $J$ for every model $r'$ over $L$ with $V_{r'}$ de Rham and every $\tau' : K_w \to L$ that induces $\tau$. (c) A continuous semisimple homomorphism of a compact group that is approximated in trace by a set of continuous homomorphisms has a cover over $L_w$ with members in that set, in the sense of Definition 3.6.3 of the chapter on Breuil-Kisin modules. (d) A cover pulls back along a continuous homomorphism of groups, with the members composed with that homomorphism. (e) If $\Gamma$ is compact and $r : \Gamma \to \mathrm{GL}_a(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell)$ is continuous, then for each finite extension $L_0$ there are a finite extension $L \supseteq L_0$ and a homomorphism $r_1 : \Gamma \to \mathrm{GL}_a(L)$ that equals $r$ in $\mathrm{GL}_a(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell)$.

First, let $\iota : G_{K_w} \to G_E$ be $e^{-1}$ followed by the inclusion of $D_A$. It is continuous, because $e^{-1}$ is. Let $\rho : G_E \to \mathrm{GL}_a(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell)$, and let $R$ be a realization (Definition 3.6.2 of that chapter) of $\rho \circ \iota$ over $L \supseteq L_w$, given by $r'' : G_{K_w} \to \mathrm{GL}_a(L)$. Then $r'' \circ e$ is a model of $\rho|_{D_A}$, and its representation is $R$.

Second, $G_E$ is compact. By (c), $r$ has a cover over $L_w$ with members in $\mathcal{C}$. By (d), $r \circ \iota$ has a cover over $L_w$ with members $\rho \circ \iota$ for $\rho \in \mathcal{C}$.

Third, we check the hypotheses of [the theorem on covers](/entry/closed-of-cover/) for $K = K_w$, $L_0 = L_w$ and the function $J'$ given by $J'(\tau'') = J(\tau'' \circ j|_E)$ for $\tau'' : K_w \to \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell$. Property 3 of the bridge gives $\tau''(K_w) \subseteq L_w$. Let $\rho \in \mathcal{C}$ and let $R$ be a realization of $\rho \circ \iota$ over $L \supseteq L_w$. By the first step, $R$ is the representation of a model of $\rho|_{D_A}$, so $R$ is semistable by (a). Let $\tau'' : K_w \to L$, and put $\tau = \tau'' \circ j|_E$. Then $\tau''$ induces $\tau$, so $\rho|_{D_A}$ has the jumps $J(\tau)$ at $\tau$, and (b) gives that $m_{\tau''}(R, n)$ is the multiplicity of $n$ in $J(\tau) = J'(\tau'')$. If every $\rho|_{D_A}$ is crystalline at $A$, then every such $R$ is crystalline by (a).

Fourth, let $L_0$ be a finite extension. By (e), applied to $G_E$ and to the compositum of $L_0$ and $L_w$, there are a finite extension $L$ that contains $L_0$ and $L_w$ and $r_1 : G_E \to \mathrm{GL}_a(L)$ equal to $r$. Then $r_1|_{D_A}$ is a model of $r|_{D_A}$, and the realization of $r \circ \iota$ given by $r_1 \circ \iota$ is its representation $V$. The theorem on covers shows that $V$ is de Rham, that $m_{\tau''}(V, n)$ is the multiplicity of $n$ in $J'(\tau'')$ for every $\tau'' : K_w \to L$, and that $V$ is crystalline if every member of $\mathcal{C}$ is crystalline at $A$.

Fifth, we assemble the result. The fourth step with $L_0 = L_w$ shows that $r|_{D_A}$ is de Rham at $A$. Let $\tau$ be induced by $\tau' : K_w \to L'$. The fourth step with $L_0 = L'$ gives $L \supseteq L'$ and a model with a de Rham representation $V$. Let $\tau''$ be $\tau'$ followed by the inclusion $L' \subseteq L$. Then $\tau''$ induces $\tau$, and $\tau'' \circ j|_E = \tau$, so $J'(\tau'') = J(\tau)$. So $r|_{D_A}$ has the jumps $J(\tau)$ at $\tau$, which is 1. Under the hypothesis of 2, the fourth step gives a model with a crystalline representation, which is 2. $\square$

**Remark 5.10.6 (Formalization).** The period rings are an abstract model, tied to the concrete rings by a datum `LocTied`. The proof does not use the hypotheses that $A$ lies over $w$ and that the bridge induces $A$, and the place $w$ enters only through the first of them ([ClosedAtL.lean](lean:Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/SatakeParameters/ClosedAtL.lean#L26)). The ring $O$ of the bridge plays no role in the proof. In the hypothesis on the jumps of $\rho$, the embedding $\tau'$ and the field $L'$ are bound outside the property "has the jumps $J(\tau)$ at $\tau$", which chooses its own model and embedding; so $\tau'$ only expresses that $\tau$ is induced. The function $J$ is defined on all embeddings of $E$, but only the induced ones matter. The de Rham property of the theorem on covers is a statement on `Module.finrank` of $L^a$, which the proof unfolds to the dimension count of the definition (lines 98 to 101 of the same file). Fact (a) is `GlobalTheory.SstModel.main` with `IsModel.isCrysAt_iff`, (b) is `IsJumpsAt.count_eq`, (c) is `GlobalTheory.TraceConjugationCover.cover_exists`, (d) is `GlobalTheory.BKLim.cover_pull`, and (e) is `GlobalTheory.CompactRepField.exists_finExt_entries_mem` with `GlobalTheory.DeRhamRead.exists_model_of_mem`; they were read as statements, not proofs. A second proof, `closedAtL_of`, derives the theorem from two separate statements, one for the de Rham property and one for the jumps ([ClosedAtLConclusionOfMem.lean](lean:Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Statements/EllipticCurveLevels/ClosedAtLConclusionOfMem.lean#L147)).

## Notes and references

The chapter feeds three parts of the Galois row. The scheme of subgroups of the torsion of an abelian scheme is used in the first step of [the representability of isogenies of type $T_j$](/entry/isogeny-rep/), and the isogenies with a given kernel are used for the quotient objects in the chapter on Galois representations. The neighbourhood lemma is an input to [the finiteness of étale cohomology](/entry/etale-topology-top/) of proper schemes, and the projective bundle formula is an input to [the formula on discrete spaces](/entry/pbf-disc/). The holomorphic Lefschetz formula and the count of marked fixed points are the two inputs of [the theorem that Hecke eigensystems occur in Dolbeault cohomology](/entry/ks-hodge-of-geo/), in [the geometric realization of weight one](/entry/geometric-realization-of-weight-one/). This is the step [GeoOne holds](/step/geo-one-holds/) of [the Galois row](/proof/galois-row/), which builds the Galois representations of weight one from the cohomology of a tower of unitary Shimura varieties. The finite cover with reduced fibre of Section 5.9 is the third step of the proof of Theorem 5.6.4. The closedness at $\ell$ of Section 5.10 is an input of [the theorem on the core of the Galois row](/entry/core-all-of/), under the step [the Galois row holds](/step/galois-row-holds/).
