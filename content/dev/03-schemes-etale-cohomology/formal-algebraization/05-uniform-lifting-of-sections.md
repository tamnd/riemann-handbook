## Uniform lifting of sections

Sections 5 and 6 prove the algebraization of formal schemes with an ample line bundle, EGA III_1, §5.4. Let $(Y_e)$ be a compatible system of proper schemes over the rings $R/I^{e+1}$, with a compatible system of line bundles $N_e$ whose first member is ample. To embed the whole system in one projective space, we need sections of a fixed power of $N_e$ for each $e$ that are compatible under restriction. This section proves the key step: for all large multiples $D$ of a fixed $d$, each section of $N_e^{\otimes D}$ lifts to a section of $N_{e+1}^{\otimes D}$, with a bound on $D$ that does not depend on $e$.

Let $R$ be a ring and $I \subseteq R$ an ideal. For $e \geq 0$ let $R_e = R/I^{e+1}$. A *formal system of proper schemes* is a sequence of schemes $Y_e$, proper over $\operatorname{Spec} R_e$, with morphisms $\beta_e : Y_e \to Y_{e+1}$ such that the square formed by $\beta_e$, the structure maps and $\operatorname{Spec} R_e \to \operatorname{Spec} R_{e+1}$ is a pullback square. So $Y_e = Y_{e+1} \times_{R_{e+1}} R_e$. A *formal line bundle* on it is a sequence of [line bundles](/learn/geometry/#line-bundles-and-divisors) $N_e$ on $Y_e$ with isomorphisms $v_e : N_e \cong \beta_e^* N_{e+1}$. For $D \in \mathbb{Z}$ we write $N_e^D = N_e^{\otimes D}$, and $v_e$ induces $N_e^D \cong \beta_e^* N_{e+1}^D$. A section $\sigma' \in \Gamma(Y_{e+1}, N_{e+1}^D)$ *maps to* $\sigma \in \Gamma(Y_e, N_e^D)$ if its pullback along $\beta_e$ corresponds to $\sigma$ under this isomorphism.

**Definition 7.5.1 (Very ample family of sections).** Let $Y$ be a scheme over a ring $B$ and $L$ a line bundle on $Y$. A family $s_0, \dots, s_m$ of sections of $L$ is *very ample* if the open sets $Y_{s_i}$ where $s_i$ does not vanish cover $Y$, each $Y_{s_i}$ is affine, and for each $i$ the map $B[x_0, \dots, x_m] \to \Gamma(Y_{s_i}, \mathcal{O})$ with $x_j \mapsto s_j / s_i$ is surjective.

So a very ample family defines a closed immersion $Y \to \mathbb{P}^m_B$ whose pullback of $\mathcal{O}(1)$ is $L$.

**Theorem 7.5.2 (Uniform lifting of sections).** Let $R$ be Noetherian and $I \subseteq R$ an ideal. Let $(Y_e, \beta_e)$ be a formal system of proper schemes and $(N_e, v_e)$ a formal line bundle on it. Let $d \geq 1$, and let $s_0, \dots, s_m$ be a very ample family of sections of $N_0^d$ on $Y_0$. Then there is $t_0 \geq 0$ such that, for each $t \geq t_0$, each $e \geq 0$ and each $\sigma \in \Gamma(Y_e, N_e^{dt})$, there is $\sigma' \in \Gamma(Y_{e+1}, N_{e+1}^{dt})$ that maps to $\sigma$.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.CompanionTheory.FormalAlg.Lift.exists_uniform_lift` (also `GlobalTheory.T.formal_line_bundle_very_ample_system`, a second name).

The theorem does not need $R$ to be $I$-adically complete. Each statement is about two adjacent levels, and the uniformity in $e$ comes from the finiteness of the graded objects.

*Proof.* Since $R$ is Noetherian, $I$ is generated by finitely many elements $\rho_1, \dots, \rho_g$. We take as given the following inputs, which build an affine cover of each $Y_e$ and the lifted sections on it.

1. *Affine opens on the thickenings.* For each $e$ and each nonempty $S \subseteq \{0, \dots, m\}$ there is an open $U_e(S) \subseteq Y_e$, which on $Y_0$ is the intersection of the sets $Y_{s_i}$ for $i \in S$. These opens are compatible with $\beta_e$, decrease in $S$, and are affine.
2. *Lifted sections.* There are sections $\tilde s_{e,j,S}$ of $N_e^d$ over $U_e(S)$ that reduce to $s_j$ on $Y_0$, are compatible with $\beta_e$, and do not vanish on $U_e(S)$ for $j \in S$.

For $e \geq 0$, $t \in \mathbb{Z}$ and $S$ as above, let $T_e(t, S) = \Gamma(U_e(S), N_e^{dt})$. These are $R$-modules with these maps:

- restriction $T_e(t, S) \to T_e(t, S')$ for $S \subseteq S'$;
- reduction $T_{e+1}(t, S) \to T_e(t, S)$, through $\beta_e$ and $v_e$;
- multiplication by $\tilde s_{e,j,S}$, from $T_e(t, S)$ to $T_e(t+1, S)$;
- a unit element $1 \in T_e(0, \{i\})$.

More inputs give the properties of these maps. The reduction commutes with restriction, with multiplication and with the unit. Multiplication commutes with restriction up to an element of $I \cdot T$. The reduction is surjective with kernel $I^{e+1} T_{e+1}(t, S)$, because $U_{e+1}(S)$ is affine and $U_e(S)$ is its inverse image in $Y_e$. The ideal $I^{e+1}$ kills $T_e(t, S)$. Multiplication by $\tilde s_{e,i,S}$ is bijective for $i \in S$, since this section does not vanish. Two localization properties hold, which say that the modules over the smaller open sets are the localizations at $x_j$ of the graded modules over the larger ones. Last, a finiteness property for the graded module over a single chart holds.

With these data, an abstract input, a uniform vanishing statement for the first Čech cohomology, applies. It gives $t_0$ such that, for each $t \geq t_0$ and each $e$, each Čech $1$-cocycle $(c_{ij})$ with $c_{ij} \in T_{e+1}(t, \{i, j\})$ and reduction $0$ is a coboundary of a family $b_i \in T_{e+1}(t, \{i\})$ with reduction $0$. In the classical argument one uses that the cocycles with reduction $0$ are cocycles of $I^{e+1} N_{e+1}^{dt}$, which is a module over $Y_0$ graded by the powers of $I$. The graded ring $\bigoplus_e I^e / I^{e+1}$ is Noetherian, so Serre's vanishing for large twists holds uniformly in $e$ ([coherent cohomology](/learn/commutative-algebra/#coherent-cohomology)).

Now let $t \geq t_0$, $e \geq 0$ and $\sigma \in \Gamma(Y_e, N_e^{dt})$. On each $U_{e+1}(\{i\})$ the reduction is surjective, so $\sigma$ lifts there to $\sigma'_i$. The differences $\sigma'_j - \sigma'_i$ on the pairs form a Čech $1$-cocycle with reduction $0$. By the choice of $t_0$ it is the coboundary of a family $(b_i)$ with reduction $0$. Then the sections $\sigma'_i - b_i$ agree on the overlaps, so they glue to a global $\sigma' \in \Gamma(Y_{e+1}, N_{e+1}^{dt})$, and $\sigma'$ maps to $\sigma$. A final input carries out this gluing. $\square$

The lifting property, applied for $e = 0, 1, 2, \dots$ in turn, gives for each $t \geq t_0$ and each section on $Y_0$ a compatible system of sections on all the $Y_e$. This is the use in Section 6.

**Remark 7.5.3 (Formalization).** The theorem is in [UniformLiftingOfSections.lean](lean:Development/AutomorphicSpectrum/Proofs/BlockDecomposition/UniformLiftingOfSections.lean#L25). The modules $T_e(t, S)$ are `Tm`, with the maps `resT`, `red`, `mulT` and `oneT`. The abstract input is `T.uniform_first_cohomology_kernels_exist`; it is stated for a general family of modules indexed by $e$, $t$ and finite sets $S$, and it returns $t_0$ as an integer, which the proof converts with `toNat`. The isomorphisms $v_e$ carry a generation condition `IsGen`. The name `formal_line_bundle_very_ample_system` is a wrapper that closes with `first | exact ...`.
