This chapter collects the results on formal algebraization and the local and cohomological facts that sit next to them in the proof. Its first part proves Grothendieck's existence theorem for closed subschemes: over a complete noetherian local ring, a compatible system of closed subschemes of the thickenings of a proper scheme comes from one closed subscheme. The proof treats projective space first, passes to proper schemes by Chow's lemma and a proper pushforward, and closes with noetherian induction. With a uniform lifting of sections of an ample line bundle, it then gives the algebraization of formal schemes with an ample line bundle. This part needs from Volume 1 the [theorem on formal functions and the existence theorem](/learn/commutative-algebra/#formal-functions-and-grothendiecks-existence-theorem), [completion](/learn/commutative-algebra/#completion), [coherent cohomology](/learn/commutative-algebra/#coherent-cohomology) and [ample line bundles](/learn/positivity-resolution/#ample-nef-and-big-line-bundles). The second part works over $\mathbb{C}$. It proves that each branch of an affine variety at a point is proper there, relates branches to the normalization of a curve, computes the local order of a hyperplane section by generic slices, proves a Crofton formula for the Chern integral of a Fubini and Study metric, and bounds the regularized mass near a small set. It needs [local rings and dimension](/learn/commutative-algebra/#local-rings-and-dimension), [holomorphic functions](/learn/complex-analysis/#holomorphic-functions), [complex manifolds](/learn/geometry/#complex-manifolds) and [Chern forms](/learn/positivity-resolution/#chern-weil-forms). The last part proves two facts of Hopf type on the cochains of a functor on a finite meet semilattice, the bound $\dim H^1 \leq g$ and the isomorphism $\Lambda^2 H^1 \cong H^2$, and it compares these cochains with the cohomology of the structure sheaf.

The existence theorem and the algebraization feed [the theorem on formal thickenings](/entry/formal-thickening-formal-algebraisation/). The facts on branches, local orders and masses are inputs of the [literature step](/step/every-character-of-literature/), through the Poincaré and Lelong formula. The Crofton formula feeds [the Chern degree over the complex numbers](/entry/chern2-chern-degree-complex/). The Hopf bound and the comparison with the structure sheaf are inputs of the [row step](/step/every-character-of-row/), and the exterior square is an input of the [list of chapters of the Hodge and de Rham proof](/entry/abelian-hodge-de-rham-prime/).
