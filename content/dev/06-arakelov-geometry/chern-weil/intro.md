---
title: Introduction
---
This chapter proves the Chern-Weil theorems that the development takes from the literature, and the Chern number relations for families of lattices on models over a discrete valuation ring. We first show that the integral of the top power of the first Chern form of a smooth weight on a line bundle is the self intersection number of the bundle, and from it the same equality for every polynomial in first Chern forms. A fibre integral over the bundle of full flags and the splitting principle then give the Chern numbers of a vector bundle with a smooth metric as integrals of its Chern forms. A transgression argument extends this to metrics that are good in the sense of Mumford, and an antisymmetrization over the Weyl group computes the push forward from the flag bundle. Two further results use the same intersection numbers. Each sub-bundle of the Lie algebra of a semi-abelian scheme over a projective curve in characteristic zero has a determinant of degree at most zero. On a model over a discrete valuation ring, the Chern numbers of the bundles of a lattice family satisfy linear relations twisted by a permutation of the labels; we reduce these relations, by a chain of refinements of the model, to [the last link of the chain](/entry/stage5/). The later sections of the chapter prove that link and the results on flag models, cuts of divisors and Mahler sections that it uses.

The chapter uses from Volume 1 the [Chern-Weil forms, the splitting principle and Mumford's good metrics](/learn/positivity-resolution/#chern-weil-forms), the [metrics and curvature of line bundles](/learn/positivity-resolution/#metrics-curvature-and-positivity), the [hermitian line bundles](/learn/arakelov/#hermitian-line-bundles), the [arithmetic varieties and intersection numbers](/learn/arakelov/#arithmetic-varieties-and-intersection-numbers), the [line bundles and divisors](/learn/geometry/#line-bundles-and-divisors), and the [semi-abelian degenerations](/learn/abelian-varieties/#semi-abelian-degenerations-and-mumfords-construction) with the [Lie algebra of the Hodge bundle](/learn/arakelov/#the-hodge-bundle-and-zarhins-trick). The theorem on good metrics is the field `GoodChernWeil` of the step [published results in arithmetic geometry](/step/published-results-arithmetic/), where it replaces the theorem of Mumford that the development took from the literature; it then enters [Mumford's proportionality theorem](/learn/abelian-varieties/#mumfords-proportionality-theorem). The degree formula for line bundles is part of the input `ArDegree`, and the seminegativity of Lie algebras is the input `SemiAbelianLieSeminegative`; both are used in the step [every character from the literature](/step/every-character-of-literature/). The Chern number relations are used in [the Hodge lattice flag bound from lattice families](/entry/model-on-hodge-of-family-pos/).
