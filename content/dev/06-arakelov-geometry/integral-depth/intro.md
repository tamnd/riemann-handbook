---
title: Introduction
---
This chapter proves the local and lattice estimates that the chapter on [canonical forms near the boundary](/entry/sum-pt-of-sizes-alt/) quotes. The first estimates are $p$-adic. At an integral point of the moduli space, the formal group of the universal abelian scheme has Kodaira-Spencer coordinates: for each complex embedding $\tau$ of the CM field, the Lie vectors on which the field acts through $\tau$. The depth of a determinant along these vectors measures how far a differential operator on integral functions is divisible by $p$. At a prime that is unramified in the CM field, the formal group splits into typed parts on which the roots of unity act, and at an odd inert prime this gives a lower bound for the depth with a small oscillation. Summed over all primes, the oscillations form a prime sum, and a theta series argument turns the oscillation at a prime that is not a host prime into a bound for the reference exponent. The other estimates are global: a peak section of a cusp form on the thick part of the frames, the degree of the Lie eigenlattices of a CM abelian variety of definite signature, the slope of the eigenlattices of cusp forms in the definite case, and the arithmetic Hilbert-Samuel bound on a component. The later sections prove that Hilbert-Samuel bound from a Bertini theorem over number fields and a slope bound for lattices of sections, and further results on Kodaira-Spencer coordinates, canonical bundles and covolumes on integral models.

The chapter needs [formal group laws](/learn/formal-groups/#formal-group-laws) and [their logarithms](/learn/formal-groups/#the-invariant-derivation-and-the-logarithm), [the splitting of primes](/learn/number-fields/#the-splitting-of-primes) and [completions](/learn/number-fields/#places-and-completions) of number fields, [abelian varieties with complex multiplication](/learn/theta-functions-cm/#abelian-varieties-with-complex-multiplication), [hermitian lattices and slopes](/learn/arakelov/#hermitian-lattices-and-slopes), [heights](/learn/arakelov/#heights), [arithmetic volume](/learn/arakelov/#arithmetic-volume-and-bigness), [$L^2$ estimates](/learn/positivity-resolution/#l2-estimates) and [toroidal compactifications of Shimura varieties](/learn/geometry/#shimura-varieties-and-toroidal-compactifications). Its results enter the reference exponent bound, the Bergman kernel bound, the Lie degree at the last level, the low defect bound and the Hilbert-Samuel estimate of the previous chapter, and so [the covolume bound step](/step/covolume-bound-of-x-def/) of the proof.
