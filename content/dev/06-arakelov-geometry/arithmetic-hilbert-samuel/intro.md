---
title: Introduction
---
This chapter proves the arithmetic Hilbert-Samuel formula for nef hermitian line bundles, and the lower bound of Moriwaki for the arithmetic volume. The first proof assumes only principalisation of ideals. From it we get the Poincaré-Lelong formula for a section, a reciprocity law for archimedean integrals, mixed heights by induction on the dimension, and so the arithmetic intersection calculus. The ample case comes from a change of metric and a count of lattice points, and the nef case from an upper bound and a lower bound for the number of small sections. We then show that the volume of a bundle nef on the fibres is at least its self intersection. The last two sections give a second proof in coordinates: an $L^2$ form of the formula for a semipositive metric, and from it Moriwaki bigness, the arithmetic Kleiman inequality and two upper bounds of Hilbert-Samuel type.

The chapter uses from Volume 1 the [hermitian line bundles](/learn/arakelov/#hermitian-line-bundles), the [arithmetic varieties and intersection numbers](/learn/arakelov/#arithmetic-varieties-and-intersection-numbers), the [heights](/learn/arakelov/#heights) and the [arithmetic volume and bigness](/learn/arakelov/#arithmetic-volume-and-bigness). The Hilbert-Samuel formula and the volume bound are used in the step [published results in arithmetic geometry](/step/published-results-arithmetic/), where they replace two theorems taken from the literature. Moriwaki bigness and the Hilbert-Samuel bounds are used in the step [every character from the literature](/step/every-character-of-literature/).
