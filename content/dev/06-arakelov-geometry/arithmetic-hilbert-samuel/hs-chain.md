This theorem proves five bounds on the [arithmetic volume](/learn/arakelov/#arithmetic-volume-and-bigness) of a Hermitian line bundle from three inputs. Two corollaries in the same file give `MoriwakiBigStatement` and `HSUse`. `Outright.lean` proves the three inputs and gets both with no hypothesis (`moriwakiBig_outright`, `hsUse_outright`), for the step [EveryCharacter from the literature and VWin](/step/every-character-of-literature/).

## Statement

An [[arithmetic variety|YXWGV80Z2V]] $X$ of dimension $D+1$ is given by a point $y$ with coordinates $y_{k,i}$ in a field $\Omega$: its ideal $P$ is the kernel of evaluation at $y$ on the multigraded ring $\mathbb{Z}[x_{k,i}]$, and the quotient has Krull dimension $D+1$ plus the number of blocks. A Hermitian line $\overline{A}$ is a multidegree $a$ and a metric $h$ on the complex points with $h(cz) = |c|^a h(z)$. A section of $\overline{A}^{n}$ is an element $s$ of $\Omega$ that is integral on each chart in degree $na$. It is small if $|s| \leq h^n$ on the complex points. Put $\widehat{h}^0(n) = \log \#\{\text{small sections}\}$ and

$$\widehat{\mathrm{vol}}(\overline{A}) = \limsup_{n \to \infty} \frac{\widehat{h}^0(n)}{n^{D+1}/(D+1)!}.$$

The number $\overline{A}^{D+1}$ comes from any function $II$ that satisfies the axioms `IsIntersectionSystem`: symmetry, additivity, and the inductive formula of Bost, Gillet and Soulé (J. AMS 7, 1994) for an [[arithmetic intersection number|4HV4WTAX9P]].

**Theorem.** Assume resolution of singularities for closed subvarieties of $\mathbb{P}^M$ in characteristic $0$ (`ResKStatement`), an intersection system that is multilinear and has a projection formula (`ArithIntersectionSystemStatement`), and a Zariski dense set of points of bounded height on each arithmetic variety (`DenseBoundedHeight.Statement`). Let $\overline{A}$ be [[semipositive|FF2P7ER3QT]] and invariant under complex conjugation, and let $\Omega$ be generated by quotients of forms of equal degree at $y$ (the function field). Then:

1. if $D \geq 1$ and $A$ is nef on the fibres, $\overline{A}^{D+1} \leq \widehat{\mathrm{vol}}(\overline{A})$;
2. the same for $D = 0$, with $A$ also nef on the generic fibre;
3. if $A$ is nef on the fibres and $\overline{A}$ has height $\geq 0$ on each arithmetic curve, then $\overline{A}^{D+1} \geq 0$;
4. if $A$ is generated by small sections of degree $1$ and has positive degree, then $\widehat{\mathrm{vol}}(\overline{A}) \leq \overline{A}^{D+1}$;
5. if each monomial of degree $a$ has norm at most $h$, the same upper bound holds for the lattices of integrally closed sections.

## Proof

The Lean proof is a chain of 180 steps. The main ones:

- a Kleiman criterion over a field, nef plus ample is ample (`KleimanScheme.proof`), and a Kodaira lemma: a multiple of a nef and big $\overline{A}$, minus $\mathcal{O}(1)$, has a bounded section (`KodairaSection.proof`);
- lifting to a resolution (`LiftBlockDatum.proof`, with `ResKStatement`) and counting along multiples (`LiftCountAlongMultiples.proof`), with the block Hilbert-Samuel formula [`theoremHSMBlock`](/entry/theorem-hsmblock/);
- the separation of shifted sections (`ShiftSectionSeparation.proof`), with the dense set of bounded height;
- conclusions 1 and 2 (`ArithmeticVolumeBig.proof`, `MoriwakiBigCurve.proof`), and 3 from a mixed Kleiman bound (`ArithKleiman.proof`);
- conclusion 4 (`G7.proof`) from the degree of $L^2$ norms on tensor powers (`TensorPowerL2Degree.proof`, again with `theoremHSMBlock`);
- conclusion 5 (`ArithmeticVolumeNef.proof`) from 4 by a shift of monomials.

## In the Lean code

The theorem is in [HSChain.lean](lean:Development/WeilBound/CoreStatements/AutomorphicKernels/MoriwakiBigness/MoriwakiBigTheorem/HSChain.lean#L180). We have read the five conclusions and the main steps above, not all 180 steps.

- Conclusions 1 and 2 are only the lower bound. Moriwaki's theorem (J. Algebraic Geom. 18, 2009) gives equality; the [Weil bound](/proof/weil-bound/) chapter notes this.
- The count uses `Nat.card`, so it is $0$ when the set of small sections is infinite.
- The density hypothesis is used once (`s81`).
- Conclusion 5 is named `ArithmeticVolumeNef`, but its statement has no nef hypothesis. The bound on monomials takes its place.
