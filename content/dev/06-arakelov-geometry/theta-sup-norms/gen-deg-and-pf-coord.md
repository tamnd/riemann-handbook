This theorem proves two forms of the projection formula for heights on arithmetic varieties in products of projective spaces. It assumes that complex-analytic log resolutions exist. Its only user is its second name, [the projection formula for heights, from resolution](/entry/degree-and-coordinates-of-resolution/). The first form carries the content. The second form follows from the first in a few lines.

## Statement

Let $K$ be a finite set of blocks, with a finite set $\iota_k$ of coordinates for each $k \in K$. Let $R = \mathbb{Z}[x_{k,i}]$ be the polynomial ring in all coordinates, graded by $\mathbb{Z}^K$. An *arithmetic variety of dimension $d$* (`IsArithVar P d`) is a prime ideal $P$ of $R$ that is multihomogeneous and relevant, with $P \cap \mathbb{Z} = 0$ and $\dim R/P = d + |K|$. It defines an integral closed subscheme $X$ of $\prod_k \mathbb{P}^{\iota_k}$, flat over $\mathbb{Z}$, of dimension $d$. Here $\mathbb{P}^{\iota_k}$ is the projective space with coordinates $\iota_k$. A *metrized line bundle* (`HermLine`) is a multidegree $a \in \mathbb{Z}^K$ with a positive function $h$ on the cone of complex points, such that $h(c \cdot z) = \prod_k |c_k|^{a_k} h(z)$. A *family of heights* is a function $I(X; L_1, \dots, L_n) \in \mathbb{R}$ with the axioms `IsFamily`. The entry [the arithmetic intersection numbers exist](/entry/thm-iexists/) states these axioms.

Let $K \subseteq K'$, with $\iota_k = \iota'_k$ for $k \in K$ (`IsBlockMap`). Let $\pi$ forget the blocks outside $K$. Let $X' \subseteq \prod_{K'} \mathbb{P}^{\iota'_k}$ and $X = \pi(X')$ be arithmetic varieties of the same dimension $d$. Let $L_1, \dots, L_d$ be smooth metrized line bundles on $X$, with pullbacks $\pi^* L_j$ (`IsPullHerm`).

1. `GenDegStatement`: for each family $I$ of heights and each family $J$ of intersection numbers over fields (`IsFieldIntersection`),
$$I(X'; \pi^* L_1, \dots, \pi^* L_d) = J(X'_\eta) \cdot I(X; L_1, \dots, L_d),$$
where $X'_\eta$ is the generic fibre of $\pi$ (`fibreIdeal`), a variety of dimension $0$ over the function field of $X$, and $J(X'_\eta)$ is its degree.
2. `PFCoordStatement`: let $X'$ be the closure of a point $y$ with coordinates in a field $\Omega$ of characteristic $0$ (`mCoordIdeal y`). Then the factor is $[\mathbb{Q}(y) : \mathbb{Q}(\pi(y))]$, where $\mathbb{Q}(y)$ is the field generated by the ratios $y_p / y_q$ of coordinates in the same block.

**Theorem.** If `ResExistsStatement` holds, then (1) and (2) hold.

This is the projection formula of arithmetic intersection theory for a generically finite map. We have not matched it to a numbered result in the literature.

## Proof

Part (1) is [the projection formula from resolution](/entry/gen-deg-of-res-exists/), `genDeg_of_resExists`. Part (2) is `pfCoord_of_genDeg` applied to part (1). That lemma is `PFCoord.of_found`. It takes the point $y$, chooses a generic fibre (`genFibreExists`) and a field intersection family $J$ on it (`ExistsUnique.full`), and shows that the generic fibre has dimension $0$ (`fibreDim`). Then $J(X'_\eta)$ is the number of points of the fibre in one affine chart. The lemma `CoordFibreDeg` says that this number is $[\mathbb{Q}(y) : \mathbb{Q}(\pi(y))]$. Put this into (1).
