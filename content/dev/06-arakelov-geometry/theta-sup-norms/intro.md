title: Introduction
---
This chapter proves the analytic and geometric inputs of the volume branch of the proof. The first part bounds theta sections. In one complex frame, a theta function of level one is bounded by its $L^2$ norm times a factor $\exp(E)$, and it is small at many torsion points. Over all complex places of a number field, the mean of the logarithm of its sup norm exceeds the mean $\Phi$ of the logarithms of its $L^2$ norms by at most $C g \log(2 + g + \Phi)$. The second part proves three analytic facts on locally symmetric spaces and their compactifications: a bound for the mean cusp weight on a fundamental set, the plurisubharmonicity of log determinants of eigenframes, and the convergence of mixed Monge-Ampère integrals of singular potentials off a divisor. The third part builds flag models of a Hodge component with base metrics, analytic flag data on them, and for each adjacent swap of columns a square system. It computes the fibre integral of the log-sine function of a square system, and it uses this integral to prove the reflection sum, the swap relation and the antisymmetry of the height polynomial of a flag system.

We use [hermitian line bundles](/learn/arakelov/#hermitian-line-bundles), [arithmetic intersection numbers](/learn/arakelov/#arithmetic-varieties-and-intersection-numbers), [metrics, curvature and positivity](/learn/positivity-resolution/#metrics-curvature-and-positivity), [Chern-Weil forms](/learn/positivity-resolution/#chern-weil-forms), [resolution of singularities](/learn/positivity-resolution/#resolution-of-singularities-and-principalization), [theta functions on complex tori](/learn/theta-functions-cm/#theta-functions-on-complex-tori) and [theta functions with characteristics](/learn/theta-functions-cm/#theta-functions-with-characteristics) from Volume 1. The theta bound enters [the line bound of a key](/entry/line-of-key/). The cusp weight bound enters [the volume of a good tower](/entry/good-tower-and-volume/), and the convergence of mixed integrals enters [the volume theorem from its inputs](/entry/volume-theorem-x-of-inputs/). The antisymmetry gives [the swap relation from seven statements](/entry/good-tower-antisymmetry-of/), which serves the volume branch of [the Weil bound](/proof/weil-bound/).
