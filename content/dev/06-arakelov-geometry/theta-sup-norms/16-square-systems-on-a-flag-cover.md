## Square systems on a flag cover

Section 8 states that every adjacent swap of a flag cover carries a square system, and it builds the analytic square on the flag model below. This section gives the proof on the cover. The analytic square lives over the model $M$, but the forms $g$ and $h$ of the algebraic square live on the cover $M'$. So we lift the resolution of the swap variety of $M$ to a resolution of the swap variety of $M'$, and we count the degree of the lift. Then we pull the lines back along the two projections and compare the norms point by point.

### Covers and points over points

We keep the notation of Sections 6 to 8. Let $M$ be a flag model over $y$ and $M'$ a flag model over $y'$, both of type $(N, j)$ for the same CM type $\Psi$. A flag cover $M' \to M$ is a block map $v$ from the base coordinates of $M$ to those of $M'$ and a field map $\phi$ with $y'(v(q)) = \phi(y(q))$. Its degree is the degree of the field of the base of $M'$ over the field generated by the image of $v$, and it is positive. The transition matrices between frames of $M$ and frames of $M'$ are regular at all points.

Two cone points are in the same orbit if they give the same point of the product of projective spaces. A point $z$ of $M'$ lies over a point $w$ of $M$ if, for every point $z_1$ in the orbit of $z$ and all frames that are good there, the flag at $z_1$ moved by the transition matrix is the flag of a point $z_0$ in the orbit of $w$ whose base part is the base part of $z_1$ composed with $v$. The image of a point is unique up to orbit.

For an adjacent swap $(\tau, a, b)$ with block $k_0$, the swap variety $W$ of $M$ is the variety of pairs of points of $M$ that agree outside the block $k_0$. Write $w_0$ and $w_1$ for the two points of a pair. A pair $z$ of $M'$ lies over a pair $w$ of $M$ if $z_0$ lies over $w_0$ and $z_1$ lies over $w_1$.

A covering realization of analytic flag data $\mathcal{F}$ along the cover is a realization of the steps of $\mathcal{F}$ in the coordinate ideal of $y'$, with degree equal to the degree of the cover and with step lines on $M'$. It comes with a map $\varpi$ from the points of $M'$ to the base manifold of $\mathcal{F}$, constant on orbits, such that the weights at a point $z$ of $M'$ are the weights of $\mathcal{F}$ at $\varpi(z)$, and the flags and metrics of $z$ match those of the points over $\varpi(z)$.

### Lifting a resolution

**Lemma 10.16.1 (Lifting a resolution of the swap variety).** Assume resolution of singularities in the analytic form. Let $M' \to M$ be a flag cover of degree $\delta$, and let $(\tau, a, b)$ be an adjacent swap of both models with the same columns. Let $y$ and $y'$ be arithmetic varieties of dimension $d + 2$. Let $X$ be a compact complex manifold of dimension $d + 2$, let $R_W$ be a resolution of the swap variety $W$ of $M$, and let $e : R_W \to X$ be holomorphic of degree $1$ with no branch set. Then there are a resolution $R'$ of the swap variety $W'$ of $M'$, a holomorphic map $g : R' \to R_W$ and a set $B \subset X$ such that $e \circ g$ has degree $\delta$ off $B$, and for every $x \in R'$ the pair of $x$ lies over the pair of $g(x)$.

Lean: `PositivityTheory.Xgp.c_sqsys.mapsTo_twoFlagFramesOrthogonal_of_isometry`.

*Proof.* Step 1: the resolution. The swap variety $W'$ is an arithmetic variety, so resolution gives an ideal $J'$ not contained in it and a resolution $R'$ of $W'$ off $J'$. Since $e$ has degree $1$ and no branch set, every fibre of $e$ has one point, so $e$ is injective.

Step 2: the map. Let $x \in R'$. Near the pair $z$ of $x$ there are local analytic coordinates on the cover that give a pair $w$ of $W$ with $z$ over $w$. The resolution $R_W$ is onto the complex points of $W$, so there is $g(x)$ in $R_W$ whose pair is in the orbit of $w$. The image is unique up to orbit, and $R_W$ is injective on orbits. In the local coordinates $g$ is given by analytic functions, so $g$ is holomorphic, and $e \circ g$ is holomorphic.

Step 3: three forms on $W$. The cover gives a form $D_1$ on the base of $M$ such that off its zeros each point of $M$ has exactly $\delta$ orbits over it. It gives a form $D_2$ off whose zeros the cover has local analytic inverse branches. Pull both to $W$ along the first projection; this gives $G_1$ and $G_2$, which do not vanish on $W$. A third form $G_3$ on $W$, not in its ideal, vanishes at every pair under a pair of $W'$ that meets $V(J')$. Let $G = G_1 G_2 G_3$. Then $G$ is a form that does not vanish on $W$, and a form $G_4$ on $W'$, not in its ideal, vanishes at every pair over a zero of $G$.

Step 4: the degree. Let $B$ be the image in $X$ of the zeros of $G$. A general lemma on log resolutions gives the degree of $e \circ g$ off $B$ from four facts. First, two points of $R'$ over the same point of $R_W$ map to the same point, by Step 2. Second, the number of orbits of pairs of $W'$ over a pair $w$ with $G(w) \neq 0$ equals the number of orbits of points over $w_0$, which is $\delta$ since $D_1$ does not vanish. Third, pairs over $V(J')$ lie in the zeros of $G$, since $G_3$ vanishes there. Fourth, near a pair $w$ with $G(w) \neq 0$, the inverse branches of the cover on the two factors join to a local analytic inverse of the map from pairs of $W'$ to pairs of $W$. So $e \circ g$ has degree $\delta$ off $B$. $\square$

### The theorem

Let $\xi_{\tau,a}$ be the coefficients $1, -2, 1$ at the columns $a - 1$, $a$, $a + 1$ of row $\tau$, as in Section 10.

**Theorem 10.16.2 (Square systems on a flag cover).** Assume the inputs. Let $\mathcal{F}$ be analytic flag data of dimension $d$ for a base metric on $M$, let $M' \to M$ be a flag cover with a covering realization of $\mathcal{F}$, and let $y$ be an arithmetic variety of dimension $d + 2$. Then for every $\tau \in \Psi$ and $a$, $b = a + 1$ with $b < j$ or $j \leq a$, there is a square system for the covering realization at $((\tau, a), \xi_{\tau,a})$.

Lean: `PositivityTheory.Xgp.t_c_sqsys`.

*Proof.* Step 1: the swap. The covering realization makes $y'$ an arithmetic variety of dimension $d + 2$. The swap data of $M$ and of $M'$ at $(\tau, a, b)$ give the blocks $k_0$ and $k_0'$ and the columns $c_a$ and $c_b = c_a + 1$, the same for both models. On $M'$ the swap data give
$$
\sum_p \xi_{\tau,a}(p) \deg_{k_0'} L_p = -2 .
$$

Step 2: the analytic square. The theorem on the analytic square of Section 8 gives, for $\mathcal{F}$ on $M$, an analytic square $Q$ with maps $q_0, q_1$, a resolution $R_W$ of the swap variety of $M$, a map $e$ of degree $1$ with no branch set, and the norm formula: at a pair over a point of $Q$, the right side of the square system equation is a function of the two flags.

Step 3: the algebraic square. On the swap variety of $M'$ there are forms $g$ and $h$ with $\operatorname{div} g = \operatorname{div} h + \Delta$ and with the degree identity
$$
\deg g - \deg h = -\sum_p \xi_{\tau,a}(p) \deg L_p - [k_0'] ,
$$
where $[k_0']$ is $1$ on the block $k_0'$ and $0$ elsewhere, and the extra block of the pair has degree $1$. This gives the algebraic square.

Step 4: the lift. The lemma gives $R'$, $g$ over $R_W$, and the set $B$, with $e \circ g$ of degree $\delta$ off $B$. Since the covering realization has degree $\delta$, the realization of the square has the right degree.

Step 5: the realization. For each $x \in R'$ choose points $x_0$ and $x_1$ of the base manifold of $\mathcal{F}$ with $\pi(x_i) = q_i(e(g(x)))$, by the bijectivity of the analytic projection. The two points of the pair of $x$ lie over the lifts of $x_0$ and $x_1$. So $\varpi$ of the two points is $q_0(e(g(x)))$ and $q_1(e(g(x)))$. Define the lines of the realization on the steps as pullbacks along the first projection, and the line of the extra index as the pullback along the second projection of the line at $(\tau, a)$. Pullbacks of smooth lines are smooth. The potentials are local real parts of holomorphic functions, since the potentials on $M'$ and on the base manifold differ by such functions and the maps are holomorphic. The lines at $t$ match the weights of $\mathcal{F}$ through $\varpi$.

Step 6: the null set. The zero set of $g h$ on $R'$ is null, since $g h$ is a form that does not vanish on the swap variety and its zero set is analytic.

Step 7: the norm equation. Let $x$ be off the zeros of $g h$ and $0 < t \leq t_0$. The log of a product of lines is the sum of the logs with the exponents $\xi_{\tau,a}(p)$. Write the weights at the two points through $\varpi$. The norm formula of Step 2 then gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
\log \frac{\lvert g \rvert}{\lvert h \rvert} + \tfrac12 \bigl(\log \Xi_t \circ v_0 + \log \Xi_t \circ v_1\bigr) &= \log\lVert v\rVert + c \\
&\quad + \tfrac12 \sum_p \xi_{\tau,a}(p) (\varphi_{p,t} \circ q_0 + \varphi_{p,t} \circ q_1) ,
\end{aligned}
$$
at the point $e(g(x))$. The lines at $p \neq (\tau, a)$ do not depend on the block $k_0'$, since the step lines of $M'$ do not depend on blocks outside the step. $\square$

The degree count is the only place where the cover enters in an essential way. The forms of the algebraic square and the norm formula are on the two models separately, and the points over points glue them.
