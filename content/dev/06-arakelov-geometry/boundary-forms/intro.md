---
title: Introduction
---
This chapter proves the covolume bound for a family of unitary Shimura data: for every family that satisfies the standing family conditions, and for all large parameters, every point of the family satisfies the lattice inequality that the proof calls the covolume bound of $X$. The proof is a chain of estimates that hold at every level of the tower of a point and on every good cover of that level. A lower bound for the height of the cusp form sections on each component comes from the arithmetic Hilbert-Samuel theorem, from a lower bound for the Bergman kernel on the thick part of the frames, and from a section that carries cusp forms from one weight to another with controlled norms at the finite and infinite places. The reference exponents of the theta weights are bounded by sums over primes. At the last level, the degree of the Lie determinant is the negative of the degree of a flat section, and this gives a lower bound for the slope of the core lattice. Cusp forms of a large weight separate the orbits of a unipotent group of level automorphisms, because the Hodge line bundle has a rational linearization on an equivariant compactification and is nef and big. The second part of the chapter proves criteria for log canonical pairs and for simple log poles of canonical forms along the boundary, and the compatibility of equivariant linearizations with pullback that the orbit separation uses.

The chapter needs [hermitian line bundles](/learn/arakelov/#hermitian-line-bundles), [arithmetic intersection numbers](/learn/arakelov/#arithmetic-varieties-and-intersection-numbers), [heights](/learn/arakelov/#heights), [arithmetic volume](/learn/arakelov/#arithmetic-volume-and-bigness), [hermitian lattices and slopes](/learn/arakelov/#hermitian-lattices-and-slopes), [the Hodge bundle](/learn/arakelov/#the-hodge-bundle-and-zarhins-trick), [line bundles and divisors](/learn/geometry/#line-bundles-and-divisors), [vanishing theorems](/learn/geometry/#vanishing-theorems-and-resolution), [point separation](/learn/geometry/#point-separation) and [toroidal compactifications of Shimura varieties](/learn/geometry/#shimura-varieties-and-toroidal-compactifications). It also uses [Mumford proportionality](/entry/toroidal-flag-proportionality-of-good-chern-weil/) from the chapter on Hodge metrics. The covolume bound is the statement of [the covolume bound step](/step/covolume-bound-of-x-def/), and it enters [the literature step](/step/every-character-of-literature/) of the proof.
