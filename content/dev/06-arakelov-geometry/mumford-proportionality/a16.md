This theorem says that the higher Čech cohomology of the model $V$ vanishes in degrees $\beta + c\,e_{k_0}$ for large $c$. The theorem [`a17`](/entry/a17/) uses it to count sections along this ray with the Hilbert polynomial. We use the notation of [`a18`](/entry/a18/) and the groups $\check H^i(m)$ of `a17`.

## Statement

**Theorem.** Fix linear orders on $K$ and on each $\iota_k$. Suppose that $y$ is a model with base block $k_0$ (`IsModelWithBase`). Then for each $\beta \in \mathbb{Z}^K$ there is $c_0$ such that
$$\check H^i(\beta + c\,e_{k_0}) = 0 \quad \text{for all } c \geq c_0 \text{ and all } i \geq 1.$$

This is Serre vanishing on $V$ for the twists of a fixed sheaf by powers of $\mathcal{O}(e_{k_0})$. The proof moves the question to $X \subseteq \mathbb{P}^N$, where $\mathcal{O}(e_{k_0})$ becomes $\mathcal{O}_X(1)$.

## Proof

1. `BundleOfDeg.full`: there is a line bundle $Q$ on $X$ with $H^0(X, Q(c)) = \Gamma(\beta + c\,e_{k_0})$ for all $c \in \mathbb{Z}$.
2. `lineBundle_twist_vanish`: there is $c_0$ with $H^i(X, Q(c)) = 0$ for $i \geq 1$ and $c \geq c_0$. This is Serre vanishing on $X$. It comes from `TwistVanishAll.proof` with `Extension.full`, `SerreVanish.full` and `TwistSplit.proof`.
3. `ModelCechTwist`: $H^i(X, Q(c)) \cong \check H^i(\beta + c\,e_{k_0})$ for all $i$ and $c$. Its proof `ModelCechTwist.proof` uses `ChartSecMul.proof`, `BaseRealises.proof`, `CellLocEval.proof` (the cohomology of cells is the Čech cohomology of the graded module) and `a15` (`ModelCechRefine`: the chart cohomology of $X$ is the cohomology of cells).

Steps 2 and 3 give the vanishing. The rest is bookkeeping.

The theorem is in [A12.lean](lean:Development/WeilBound/CoreStatements/HodgeTatePoints/A12.lean#L120). Its proof is `RayVanish.proof_of_ties CellLocEval.proof a15`. `RayVanish.proof_of_ties`, `RayVanish.proof` and `lineBundle_twist_vanish` are in [LineBundleTwistVanish.lean](lean:GroundTheory/HeightsAndTheta/Heights/KleimanAmpleness/LineBundleTwistVanish.lean#L55); the statement is in [ImportsRestExtra.lean](lean:GroundTheory/HeightsAndTheta/Heights/KleimanAmpleness/ImportsRestExtra.lean#L11).

The cohomology $H^i(X, Q(c))$ is the Lean `SubSystem.H`, the Čech cohomology `Cech.H` of the modules of $Q(c)$ on the charts of $X$ ([Objects.lean](lean:GroundTheory/HeightsAndTheta/Heights/KleimanAmpleness/Objects.lean#L592)). The Lean form says "eventually in $c$", which is the existence of $c_0$ above.
