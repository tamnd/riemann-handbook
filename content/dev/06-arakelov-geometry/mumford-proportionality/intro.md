This chapter proves Mumford's proportionality theorem for a unitary Shimura variety, and it collects the integral and positivity facts that are used next to it. Its first part computes the Chern forms of the Hodge metric. At one point they are proportional to the Chern forms of the compact dual, a Grassmannian, so the Chern integrals over the open variety are the covolume times the flag degrees of the compact dual. Period charts at the boundary then show that the Hodge metric has the growth of a good metric in the sense of Mumford. With the good base of a toroidal compactification and Mumford's theorem on good metrics, taken as a hypothesis, this gives the proportionality theorem: on a toroidal flag space, the top self-intersection of the automorphic line bundle $L_\lambda$ is the covolume of the arithmetic group times the flag degree of $\lambda$. This part needs [Chern forms](/learn/positivity-resolution/#chern-weil-forms), [metrics and curvature](/learn/positivity-resolution/#metrics-curvature-and-positivity), [complex manifolds](/learn/geometry/#complex-manifolds) and [toroidal compactifications](/learn/abelian-varieties/#toroidal-and-minimal-compactifications), and it ends with [Mumford's proportionality theorem](/learn/abelian-varieties/#mumfords-proportionality-theorem) in the form the proof uses.

The second part works over local rings and in positive characteristic. It proves that Hodge modules of formal germs are free at every specialization, describes the finite part of the torsion of a degenerating abelian variety and its Hopf orders, and turns a tangent condition on a polarization into the injectivity of a map built from the $p$-th power cocycle. It also gives the compatibility of vertical divisors with blow-ups, an arithmetic intersection system, quotients by finite flat subgroups, Zarhin's trick, and Kleiman's positivity facts for intersection numbers on multiprojective models. These results feed [the classical theorems](/entry/to-classical-theorems/), [freeness at component stalks](/entry/hfree-at-component-stalks/), [the covolume bound](/entry/top-covol-slim2-use-alt-alt/), [the deep slim theorem](/entry/deep-slim-use/) and [the literature step](/step/every-character-of-literature/).
