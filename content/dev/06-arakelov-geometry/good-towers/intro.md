---
title: Introduction
---
This chapter builds good towers of integral models and proves the volume theorem for them. It starts with metrics on the Lie algebras of semi-abelian schemes and with the heights of the root lines of a flag, and it proves the fibre input and the bounds I5 and I6 for these heights. At the cusps it builds period charts, writes the Hodge potential and the cusp weight in these charts, and proves that the Hodge weight and the corrected weight are plurisubharmonic, locally bounded and of logarithmic growth. This gives tied regularisations of the cusp weight and the heights of points for the twisted cusp lines. It then constructs component data at a good origin and the base setting of a good tower. At a prime where the Hodge component is free it computes the local Chern numbers of the Hodge flag through the Frobenius action on the labels. The final theorem says that, from a list of inputs, every global integral model with split components has a good tower and every good tower satisfies the [volume theorem](/entry/good-tower-volume-theorem-bis/). It feeds [the fifth condition on global model towers](/entry/global-model-tower-fifth-condition/) and the volume branch of [the Weil bound](/proof/weil-bound/).

We use these results from Volume 1. [Hermitian lattices and slopes](/learn/arakelov/#hermitian-lattices-and-slopes) give the metrics on Lie algebras and the slopes in the volume theorem. [Heights](/learn/arakelov/#heights) give the heights of the root lines and of points for the twisted cusp lines. [Hermitian line bundles](/learn/arakelov/#hermitian-line-bundles) give the Hodge line and its regularised metrics near the cusps. [Arithmetic intersection numbers](/learn/arakelov/#arithmetic-varieties-and-intersection-numbers) give the local Chern numbers of the Hodge flag and the top intersections in the base setting. [Arithmetic volume and bigness](/learn/arakelov/#arithmetic-volume-and-bigness) give the volumes in the final theorem. [Semi-abelian degenerations](/learn/abelian-varieties/#semi-abelian-degenerations-and-mumfords-construction) give the period charts at the cusps and the metrics on semi-abelian Lie algebras. [Toroidal compactifications](/learn/abelian-varieties/#toroidal-and-minimal-compactifications) give the boundary charts on which the cusp weight is studied.
