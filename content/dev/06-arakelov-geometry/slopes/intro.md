title: Introduction
---
This chapter is about hermitian lattices over a totally complex number field and their slopes. It fixes the notation of Volume 6 for these objects, and it proves three results for the lattice of automorphic forms of level zero that the divisibility part of the proof needs. The first is the slope inequality: a linear map of bounded norm cannot send a lattice of large slope into a lattice of small maximal slope. The second is an exact formula for the slope of the ramified sublattice: for a level prime $T$ with $f$ places above it, the slope drops by $f \log(T-1)/(T-2)$. The third is an upper bound for the maximal slope of the level-zero lattice, with the constant $4$ in front of a logarithm of the root discriminant. The last section collects the statements for families that the proof uses, among them some that are only connected to slopes through the place where they are used.

We use the [hermitian lattices, degrees and slopes](/learn/arakelov/#hermitian-lattices-and-slopes) of Volume 1, the [hermitian forms](/learn/algebra/#hermitian-and-skew-hermitian-forms) of the algebra chapter, and the [rings of integers](/learn/number-fields/#number-fields-and-their-integers) and [fractional ideals](/learn/number-fields/#ideals-and-the-class-group) of the chapter on number fields. The slope of this chapter is twice the slope of Volume 1, and Section 1 explains why. The results feed [the remaining division bounds on the working class](/entry/b-rest-all-alt/) and [the connectedness statement from Green](/entry/shape-closed-link-n-of-green/), which serve the step [ZeroFreeAll from Green, VWin and HypsAlt](/step/zero-free-all-of-green-v-win/) of [the theta matching](/proof/theta-matching/).
