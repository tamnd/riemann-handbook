This chapter studies [[abelian schemes|JDDXZ89N33]] together with their polarizations and theta functions. Its first three sections prove three results that later volumes use as they stand: the real metaplectic representation on the Schwartz space, Zarhin's trick over a valuation ring in the form of a retract of a principally polarized abelian scheme, and the packing statement for small sections of a hermitian line bundle, with the grid separation behind it. The rest of the chapter builds the key invariant. For a principally polarized abelian variety with a theta section, a basis of tangent vectors and $g$ sections of a power of the line bundle, the Koszul complex of the sections is exact, and its torsion, corrected by the Jacobian at the common zeros, gives a unit, the key value. The chapter shows that this value is holomorphic in the period matrix, is a unit over a discrete valuation ring when the sections have finitely many zeros on the closed fibre, and does not depend on the sections. It then assembles the key invariant, and in the last section the key rule: one rule over all fields of characteristic $0$, integral over every discrete valuation ring and equal to $1$ at the theta functions with half characteristics.
