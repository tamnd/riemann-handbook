## Small sections, grid separation and packing

This section proves an upper bound for the number of small sections of a hermitian line bundle on an arithmetic variety in multiprojective coordinates: if the variety has dimension $D + 1$, the logarithm of the number of sections of the $\tau$-th power with sup norm at most $1$ grows at most like $(\tau + 1)^{D + 1}$. This is the upper half of an arithmetic Hilbert-Samuel estimate. It is the [packing bound](/entry/packing/) that the proof uses when it compares volumes of theta sup norms. The proof has three steps: a norm form that bounds a section on a fibre of a projection, a choice of finitely many points that separate sections, and a packing argument in a lattice. The background is in [hermitian line bundles](/learn/arakelov/#hermitian-line-bundles) and [arithmetic volume and bigness](/learn/arakelov/#arithmetic-volume-and-bigness).

**Definition 2.3.1 (Multiprojective coordinates).** Let $K$ be a finite set and, for each $k \in K$, let $\iota_k$ be a finite set. Let $\mathbb{Z}[X]$ be the polynomial ring over $\mathbb{Z}$ in the variables $X_{k,i}$ for $k \in K$ and $i \in \iota_k$. A polynomial $f$ is *multihomogeneous of multidegree* $d : K \to \mathbb{N}$ if every monomial of $f$ has degree $d_k$ in the block of variables $(X_{k,i})_{i}$, for each $k$. Let $\Omega$ be a field and $y = (y_{k,i})$ a family of elements of $\Omega$. The *coordinate ideal* $P_y$ is the kernel of the ring map $\mathbb{Z}[X] \to \Omega[T_k : k \in K]$ with $X_{k,i} \mapsto y_{k,i} T_k$.

The ideal $P_y$ is the multihomogeneous ideal of the point $y$ of $\prod_k \mathbb{P}^{\iota_k}$ over $\Omega$, so $\mathbb{Z}[X]/P_y$ is the multihomogeneous coordinate ring of the closure of this point over $\mathbb{Z}$.

**Definition 2.3.2 (Arithmetic variety).** A prime ideal $P$ of $\mathbb{Z}[X]$ is a *multiprojective prime* if it is generated by multihomogeneous elements and, for each $k$, some $X_{k,i}$ is not in $P$. It is *horizontal* if $P \cap \mathbb{Z} = 0$. We say that $P$ is an *arithmetic variety of dimension* $d$ if $K$ is nonempty, $P$ is a horizontal multiprojective prime, and the Krull dimension of $\mathbb{Z}[X]/P$ is $d + |K|$. Its *complex points* are
$$P(\mathbb{C}) = \{z \in \mathbb{C}^{\sqcup_k \iota_k} : \text{each block } (z_{k,i})_i \text{ is nonzero and } f(z) = 0 \text{ for all } f \in P\}.$$

Lean: `ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.Geo.IsArithVar`, `ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.Geo.mcPts`.

The term $|K|$ accounts for the cone directions: one for each projective factor. We say that $y$ is *birational* if every $s \in \Omega$ is a ratio $g(y)/h(y)$ of two multihomogeneous polynomials of the same multidegree with $h(y) \neq 0$. Then $\Omega$ is the function field of the variety.

**Definition 2.3.3 (Hermitian line and small sections).** A *hermitian line* $\bar{A}$ is a multidegree $a : K \to \mathbb{Z}$ and a function $h$ on the points of $\mathbb{C}^{\sqcup_k \iota_k}$ with nonzero blocks, such that $h > 0$ and $h(c \cdot z) = \prod_k |c_k|^{a_k} h(z)$ for $c \in (\mathbb{C}^{\times})^K$ acting blockwise. It is *smooth* if $h$ is $C^{\infty}$. For a chart $c$, that is a choice $c_k \in \iota_k$ with all $y_{k,c_k} \neq 0$, let $R_c \subset \Omega$ be the ring generated over $\mathbb{Z}$ by the ratios $y_{k,i}/y_{k,c_k}$. The *lattice of sections* of the $\tau$-th power is
$$\Gamma_\tau = \Bigl\{ s \in \Omega : s \prod_{k} y_{k,c_k}^{-\tau a_k} \text{ is integral over } R_c \text{ for every chart } c \Bigr\}.$$
A *representation* of $s \in \Omega$ in degree $\tau$ is a pair of multihomogeneous $g$, $h$ with $\deg g = \deg h + \tau a$ and $g(y) = s\, h(y)$. We say that $s$ is *small* in degree $\tau$ if $|g(z)| \leq |h(z)|\, h_{\bar A}(z)^{\tau}$ for every representation $(g, h)$ and every $z \in P_y(\mathbb{C})$.

Lean: `ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.Geo.HermLine`, `ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.Geo.chartLatNm`, `ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.Geo.IsSmallH`.

So $\Gamma_\tau$ is the group of sections of $\mathcal{O}(\tau a)$ over the normalization of the arithmetic variety, and a section is small when its pointwise norm $|g/h|\, h_{\bar A}^{-\tau}$ is at most $1$. For a representation with $h(z) \neq 0$ the value $g(z)/h(z)$ does not depend on the representation: if $(g', h')$ is a second one, then $g h' - g' h$ is multihomogeneous and vanishes at $y$, so it lies in $P_y$ and vanishes at $z$.

**Proposition 2.3.4 (Fibre norms).** Let $y$ be birational with $P_y$ an arithmetic variety of dimension $D + 1$. Let $e : K \to \mathbb{N}$ with all $e_k \geq 1$, let $L_0, \dots, L_D$ be multihomogeneous of multidegree $e$ with no common zero on $P_y(\mathbb{C})$, and let $q \in \mathbb{N}$. Then there are $n \geq 1$ and $M_0 \geq 1$ with the following property. For every $\tau$ and every nonzero $s \in \Gamma_\tau$, formed with the multidegree $q e$, there is a nonzero $F \in \mathbb{Z}[\zeta_0, \dots, \zeta_D]$, homogeneous of degree $n q \tau$, such that for every nonzero $\zeta \in \mathbb{C}^{D+1}$, every finite set $S$ and every $M \geq 0$ the following holds. Assume that every $z \in P_y(\mathbb{C})$ with $L(z) \in \mathbb{C}\zeta$ is a blockwise multiple of a point of $S$, and that every $z \in S \cap P_y(\mathbb{C})$ has a representation $(g, h)$ of $s$ with $h(z) \neq 0$ and
$$|g(z)| \leq M\, \|L(z)\|^{q\tau}\, |h(z)|,$$
where $\|\cdot\|$ is the sup norm. Then $|F(\zeta)| \leq M_0^{\tau}\, \|\zeta\|^{n q \tau}\, M^{n}$.

Lean: `MultiProjectiveCoordinates.fibre_pull_integral`.

*Proof.* The forms $L_j$ define a finite map from the variety to $\mathbb{P}^D$, since they have no common zero. Their values $L_j(y)$ satisfy no nonzero homogeneous relation over $\mathbb{Q}$, so they generate a purely transcendental subfield $\Omega_0 \subset \Omega$, and $\Omega$ is finite over $\Omega_0$, say of degree $N$. Put $n = N$. Fix $j$ with $L_j(y) \neq 0$ and let $\chi(W) = \sum_{i} c_i W^{N - i}$ be the characteristic polynomial of multiplication by $s / L_j(y)^{q\tau}$ on $\Omega$ over $\Omega_0$. Its coefficients are $c_i(L(y)/L_j(y))$ with $c_i \in \mathbb{Q}[\zeta]$ homogeneous of degree $i q \tau$, $c_0 = 1$, and $c_N \neq 0$ because $s \neq 0$. Since $s$ is integral on the charts, the $c_i$ are integral away from finitely many primes, and a bound on the bad denominators gives $M_0 \geq 1$ with $M_0^{\tau} c_N \in \mathbb{Z}[\zeta]$. Let $F = M_0^{\tau} c_N$. Now fix $\zeta \neq 0$. Each root $w$ of $\sum_i c_i(\zeta) W^{N - i}$ comes from a point $z \in P_y(\mathbb{C})$ with $L(z) = t \zeta$, $t \neq 0$, such that $g(z) = w\, t^{q\tau} h(z)$ for every representation with $h(z) \neq 0$. Replace $z$ by the point of $S$ of which it is a multiple. The assumption gives $|w| \leq M \|\zeta\|^{q\tau}$. Since the polynomial in $W$ is monic of degree $N$, $c_N(\zeta)$ is, up to sign, the product of its roots, so $|c_N(\zeta)| \leq M^N \|\zeta\|^{N q \tau}$, and the bound for $F$ follows. $\square$

**Theorem 2.3.5 (Grid separation).** Let $y$ be birational with $P_y$ an arithmetic variety of dimension $D + 1$, and let $\bar A$ be a smooth hermitian line. Then there is $C \geq 0$ such that for each $\tau$ there is a finite set $Z \subset P_y(\mathbb{C})$ with these two properties.

1. Every $s \in \Gamma_\tau$ and every $z \in Z$ admit a representation $(g, h)$ of $s$ in degree $\tau$ with $h(z) \neq 0$.
2. Every nonzero $u \in \Gamma_\tau$ has a point $z \in Z$ and a representation $(g, h)$ of $u$ in degree $\tau$ with $h(z) \neq 0$ and
$$e^{-C(\tau + 1)}\, h_{\bar A}(z)^{\tau}\, |h(z)| \leq |g(z)|.$$

Lean: `MultiProjectiveCoordinates.grid_separation`.

*Proof.* There is a multihomogeneous form $f \notin P_y$ such that every element of $\Gamma_\tau$ has a representation with $h(z) \neq 0$ at every point $z \in P_y(\mathbb{C})$ where $f(z) \neq 0$. A projection $L_0, \dots, L_D$ that avoids the bad locus, together with the [Proposition 2.3.4](/entry/fibre-pull-integral/) and a comparison of $\|L(z)\|$ with $h_{\bar A}(z)$, gives $n$, $c$, $B \geq 1$, a nonzero polynomial $G$, and for each $\zeta$ on the unit torus $|\zeta_i| = 1$ with $G(\zeta) \neq 0$ a finite fibre $\Phi(\zeta) \subset P_y(\mathbb{C})$ in the good locus, with this property: for $u \neq 0$ in $\Gamma_\tau$ there is a nonzero $F$ of degree at most $c(\tau + 1)$ in each variable such that $|F(\zeta)| \leq B^{\tau + 1} M^n$ whenever $|g(z)| \leq M h_{\bar A}(z)^{\tau}|h(z)|$ on $\Phi(\zeta)$. We also use a fact on roots of unity: if $Q$ has integer coefficients, $Q \neq 0$, and $Q$ has degree less than $m$ in each variable, then some $\zeta$ with all $\zeta_i^m = 1$ has $|Q(\zeta)| \geq 1$. Indeed, the mean of $|Q|^2$ over this grid is the sum of the squares of the coefficients, which is at least $1$.

Let $\Sigma_G$ be $1$ plus the sum of the absolute values of the coefficients of $G$, so $|G| \leq \Sigma_G$ on the torus. Put $C = \log(2B\Sigma_G)$, $m = \deg G + c(\tau + 1) + 1$, and let $Z$ be the union of the fibres $\Phi(\zeta)$ over the grid points $\zeta$ with $\zeta_i^m = 1$ and $G(\zeta) \neq 0$. Property 1 holds because $Z$ lies in the good locus. For property 2, let $u \neq 0$, take $F$ as above and apply the fact on roots of unity to $Q = GF$. We get a grid point $\zeta$ with $G(\zeta) \neq 0$ and $|F(\zeta)| \geq 1/\Sigma_G$. If every $z \in \Phi(\zeta)$ had a representation with $|g(z)| < e^{-C(\tau+1)} h_{\bar A}(z)^{\tau}|h(z)|$, then with $M = e^{-C(\tau+1)}$ we would get
$$\frac{1}{\Sigma_G} \leq B^{\tau + 1} e^{-nC(\tau + 1)} \leq (2\Sigma_G)^{-(\tau + 1)} < \frac{1}{\Sigma_G},$$
a contradiction. Since the value $g(z)/h(z)$ does not depend on the representation, some $z \in \Phi(\zeta) \subset Z$ satisfies the inequality of property 2. $\square$

**Theorem 2.3.6 (Packing bound).** Let $y$ be birational with $P_y$ an arithmetic variety of dimension $D + 1$, let $\bar A$ be a smooth hermitian line, and let $\Lambda_\tau \subset \Gamma_\tau$ be subgroups, one for each $\tau \in \mathbb{N}$. Then for each $\tau$ the set of small $s \in \Lambda_\tau$ is finite, and there is $C$ such that for all $\tau$
$$\log \#\{s \in \Lambda_\tau : s \text{ small in degree } \tau\} \leq C (\tau + 1)^{D + 1}.$$

Lean: `MultiProjectiveCoordinates.packing_statement`.

*Proof.* The group $\Gamma_\tau$ is finitely generated of rank at most $C_1(\tau + 1)^{D}$, by the Hilbert polynomial of the generic fibre: sections of $\mathcal{O}(\tau a)$ on a variety of dimension $D$ over $\mathbb{Q}$ form a space of dimension $O(\tau^D)$, and $\Gamma_\tau$ is a lattice in it. So $\Lambda_\tau$ is finitely generated of rank $r \leq C_1 (\tau + 1)^D$. Let $Z$ and $C$ be as in the [Theorem 2.3.5](/entry/grid-separation/). For $z \in Z$ let $\operatorname{ev}_z(s) = g(z)/h(z)$ for a representation with $h(z) \neq 0$, and put $v(s) = (\operatorname{ev}_z(s)/h_{\bar A}(z)^{\tau})_{z \in Z} \in \mathbb{C}^Z$. This map is additive. If $s$ is small, then $\|v(s)\| \leq 1$ in the sup norm. If $s \neq t$ are small, then property 2 for $u = s - t$ gives $\|v(s) - v(t)\| \geq \delta$ with $\delta = e^{-C(\tau + 1)}$. So the small sections map injectively to a $\delta$-separated set in the unit ball of the real span of $v(\Lambda_\tau)$, a real space of dimension $d \leq r$. Balls of radius $\delta/2$ around these points are disjoint and lie in the ball of radius $1 + \delta/2$, so a comparison of volumes gives at most $(1 + 2/\delta)^d$ points. Hence the set is finite, and
$$\log \# \leq d \log\bigl(1 + 2e^{C(\tau + 1)}\bigr) \leq C_1 (\tau + 1)^D \bigl(C(\tau + 1) + \log 3\bigr),$$
which is at most $C_2 (\tau + 1)^{D + 1}$. $\square$

**Remark 2.3.7.** The upper bound by packing is the easy half of the arithmetic Hilbert-Samuel theorem of Gillet and Soulé; the hard half is a lower bound for the same count. The statement here asks for no positivity of the metric, only for smoothness. The lattice is defined by integrality over the chart rings, so the count is over sections on the normalization of the arithmetic variety.
