This chapter proves the criterion of Néron, Ogg and Shafarevich for an abelian variety over the fraction field of a discrete valuation ring with perfect residue field: good reduction, an unramified Tate module and unramified torsion for infinitely many orders prime to the residue characteristic are equivalent. It also proves the theorem of Serre and Tate that an abelian variety with complex multiplication has potential good reduction. The hard implication passes to the inertia fixed field, where unramified torsion becomes rational and many rational torsion points give good reduction, and then descends the model along the unramified extension by compatible projective embeddings. The chapter needs from Volume 1 [Néron models and good reduction](/learn/abelian-varieties/#neron-models-and-good-reduction), [decomposition groups, inertia and Frobenius](/learn/galois-representations/#decomposition-groups-inertia-and-frobenius), [the cyclotomic character and Tate modules](/learn/galois-representations/#the-cyclotomic-character-and-tate-modules) and [henselian and strictly henselian rings](/learn/commutative-algebra/#henselian-and-strictly-henselian-rings).

The last three sections prove results of a different kind which do not concern good reduction: every self-dual Hermitian lattice has a majorant which is semistable of degree zero, every formal biextension with an action of an order has a canonical gauge over a $\mathbb{Q}$-algebra, and the formal biextensions read off from charts and frames on two semi-abelian schemes related by a map over the identity differ by a change of coordinates and a unit. For these the chapter uses [lattices in quadratic and Hermitian spaces](/learn/hermitian-lattices/#lattices-in-quadratic-and-hermitian-spaces), [formal group laws](/learn/formal-groups/#formal-group-laws) and [the theorem of the cube](/learn/abelian-varieties/#the-theorem-of-the-cube). The theorem of Serre and Tate is used in [the step on inputs on CM abelian varieties from the literature](/step/cm-abelian-inputs-from-literature/), and the last three results in [the step on characters from the literature](/step/every-character-of-literature/).
