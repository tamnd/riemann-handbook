This chapter has three parts. The first part builds arcs in the projective models of PEL moduli spaces. Let $Y$ be a normal projective model of a PEL moduli space over a valuation ring of its coefficient field. Let $a$ be degeneration data with an action of $\mathcal{O}_E$ over a ring $O'$ that is a field or a complete discrete valuation ring. We construct a point of the moduli space over the fraction field of a finite normal extension $R_1$ of $O'[[X]]$. At each complete trait of $R_1$ the point is abelian where $X$ is a unit, and it has the degeneration data $a$ where $X$ is not a unit. The point carries theta frames of level $1$ and of level $m^2$ for every odd $m$ prime to the residue characteristic. To get there, we build series of theta values in $X$ that are algebraic over $O'[[X]]$. We prove an identity principle that carries the algebraic relations of complex theta null values to these series. Then a criterion for theta null points turns the series into a moduli point. From the arcs we prove the fourth clause of the models: each arc extends to a unique map $\operatorname{Spec} O'[[X]] \to Y$ with the prescribed degeneration, and this map specializes correctly at the uniformizer. Together with the other clauses this gives Part I of the construction of the models. The first part ends with the recognition theorem: two sets of degeneration data with matching numerical invariants give isomorphic abelian varieties. The second part extends an abelian scheme with a theta structure of level $rN$ over a dense open set $U_0$ of a normal projective model $Z$ to a semi-abelian scheme over all of $Z$. This scheme has a cubical line bundle and theta sections that immerse it in a projective space. On the way we prove that theta series at $p$-adic points have integral coefficients controlled by the period valuation. We also turn uniformised tests into geometric tests, build a semi-abelian group scheme with a cubical extension of the line bundle on the closure of $A$ in a projective model, extend line bundles with theta sections, and split the generic point by weighted projective charts. The third part proves results on smooth group models. A weak Néron model with a birational group law gives a neutral group model (Weil's theorem on birational group laws). A polarized abelian variety over a $p$-adic field has potential semi-abelian reduction once its $\ell$-torsion is rational, and Frobenius counts the integral torsion on the special fibre. The torus of a semi-abelian scheme has a maximal quotient with no cocharacter. The chapter ends with the statement of the EGA inputs, derived from the laws.
