## Weighted charts split the generic point

Let $R$ be a noetherian domain with fraction field $K$, and let $G$ be a commutative group scheme over $\operatorname{Spec} R$. In this section we prove that if a system of weighted projective charts on $G$ splits the fibres of $G$ over a dense set of points, then it splits the generic fibre $G_K$. A splitting is a split torus inside the fibre for which the chart functions are eigenfunctions with prescribed weights. The result is used in the proof that the group scheme of a core is split everywhere and semi-abelian, which is an input of [the semi-abelian cubical extension](/entry/exists-law-split-all-cubical/), and in the description of the abelian locus. The charts of a core were introduced in [the section on the closure](/entry/base-facts-has-geo-exists-clos/).

### Weighted charts

**Definition 6.14.1 (Weighted charts).** A system of *weighted charts* on a scheme $X$ consists of finite sets $I$ and $\mathrm{Cl}$, a weight map $\mathrm{wt} : I \to \mathrm{Cl}$, a class $c_0 \in \mathrm{Cl}$, and for each $i \in I$ with $\mathrm{wt}(i) = c_0$ an affine open set $W_i \subset X$ and functions $r_i(j) \in \Gamma(W_i, \mathcal{O})$ for $j \in I$. These data satisfy:

1. the sets $W_i$ cover $X$;
2. $r_i(i) = 1$;
3. $W_i \cap W_{i'} = D(r_i(i'))$, the set where $r_i(i')$ does not vanish;
4. $r_i(j) = r_i(i') \, r_{i'}(j)$ on $W_i \cap W_{i'}$.

If $\varphi : Y \to X$ is an affine map, the *pullback* of the charts has the sets $\varphi^{-1} W_i$ and the functions $r_i(j) \circ \varphi$.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.ZJ.Charts` (also `GlobalTheory.ZJ.Charts.pull`, the pullback).

A typical example is weighted projective space: $W_i$ is the set where the coordinate $x_i$ does not vanish, and $r_i(j) = x_j / x_i$.

Let $H$ be a group scheme over a scheme $S$, let $\mathsf{X}$ be a free abelian group of finite rank, and let $i : D(\mathsf{X}) \to H$ be a map from the split torus with character group $\mathsf{X}$. An open set $V \subset H$ is *stable* if, for all points $t$ of the torus and $g$ of $H$ with values in any $S$-scheme, $g \in V$ implies $i(t) g \in V$. A function $u$ on $V$ has *weight* $\chi \in \mathsf{X}$ if $u(i(t) g) = \chi(t) \, u(g)$ for all such $t$ and $g$ with $g$ and $i(t) g$ in $V$.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.ZJ.Stable` (also `GlobalTheory.ZJ.HasWt`, weights).

**Definition 6.14.2 (Split charts).** Let $C$ be weighted charts on $H$, let $d \geq 0$, and let $c : \{1, \dots, d\} \to \mathrm{Cl}$. A map $i : \mathbb{G}_m^d \to H$ is a *torus for $(C, c)$* if:

1. $i$ is a homomorphism;
2. every chart $W_a$ is stable;
3. $r_a(j)$ has weight $0$ when $\mathrm{wt}(j) = c_0$;
4. $r_a(j)$ has weight $e_k$, the $k$-th basis character, when $\mathrm{wt}(j) = c(k)$;
5. for each $k$ and each $x \in H$ there are $a$ and $j$ with $\mathrm{wt}(j) = c(k)$ and $r_a(j)(x) \neq 0$;
6. every function of weight $0$ on $W_a$ lies in the subring generated by the functions from the base and the $r_a(j)$ with $\mathrm{wt}(j) = c_0$.

The charts *split* $H$ along $c$ if $H$ is geometrically connected over $S$ and there is a torus for $(C, c)$. For $G$ over $\operatorname{Spec} R$ and a set $S_g$ of points of $\operatorname{Spec} R$, the charts *split $G$ on $S_g$* if for each $x \in S_g$ there is a field point $t : \operatorname{Spec} k \to \operatorname{Spec} R$ with image $\{x\}$ such that the map $G_k \to G$ is affine and the pullback of $C$ splits $G_k$ along $c$.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.ZJ.Charts.Torus` (also `GlobalTheory.ZJ.Charts.Split`, split; `GlobalTheory.ZJ.Charts.SplitOn`, split on a set).

Condition 6 says that the chart $W_a$ is, up to the torus, described by the ratios of weight $0$. In a weighted projective space these are the coordinates of the quotient by the torus.

**Definition 6.14.3 (Based charts).** Let $C$ be weighted charts on $H$, let $a$ be a chart index, and let $j : \{1, \dots, d\} \to I$. The pair $(a, j)$ is a *base* for $(C, c)$ if the unit section of $H$ lies in $W_a$ and, for each $n$, $\mathrm{wt}(j_n) = c(n)$ and the unit section lies in $D(r_a(j_n))$. A point $k$ of $H$ lies *in the kernel* of $(a, j)$ if $k$ and the unit lie in $W_a$ and $r_a(j')(k) = r_a(j')(1)$ for every $j'$ with $\mathrm{wt}(j') = c_0$. A map $i : \mathbb{G}_m^d \to H$ *is the kernel* of $(a, j)$ if $i$ is a monomorphism, the points in the kernel are exactly the points that factor through $i$, and $r_a(j_n)(i(t)) = e_n(t) \, r_a(j_n)(1)$ for all $n$ and $t$.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.ZJ.Charts.Based` (also `GlobalTheory.ZJ.Charts.InK`, in the kernel; `GlobalTheory.ZJ.Charts.KerIs`, the kernel).

### Inputs

In the next five theorems, $R$ is a noetherian domain, $G$ is a commutative group scheme over $\operatorname{Spec} R$ that is smooth, separated and quasi-compact, $C$ are weighted charts on $G$ with a map $c : \{1, \dots, d\} \to \mathrm{Cl}$, the map $G_K \to G$ is affine, $S_g$ is a dense set of points of $\operatorname{Spec} R$, and $C$ splits $G$ on $S_g$ along $c$. In all but the third theorem, $(a, j)$ is a base for the charts.

**Theorem 6.14.4 (Kernel torus).** The kernel of $(a, j)$ on $G_K$ is given by a map $i : \mathbb{G}_m^d \to G_K$.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.T.exists_splitTorus_kernel_genericFibre`.

**Theorem 6.14.5 (Stability and weights).** If $i$ is the kernel of $(a, j)$ on $G_K$, then every chart of $G_K$ is stable under $i$ and the functions $r_a(j')$ have the weights of conditions 3 and 4.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.T.torusStable_weights_genericFibre`.

**Theorem 6.14.6 (Connectedness).** The generic fibre $G_K$ is geometrically connected, and condition 5 holds on $G_K$.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.T.geometricallyConnected_basePointFree_generic_fibre`.

**Theorem 6.14.7 (Homomorphism).** If $i$ is the kernel of $(a, j)$ on $G_K$, then $i$ is a homomorphism.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.T.isMonHom_generic_fibre_kernel_torus`.

**Theorem 6.14.8 (Invariants).** If $i$ is the kernel of $(a, j)$ on $G_K$ and every chart is stable under $i$, then condition 6 holds on $G_K$.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.T.generic_invariants_generated_by_ratios`.

**Theorem 6.14.9 (Field extensions).** Let $k \to k'$ be a field extension, $H$ a group scheme over $k$ and $H' = H_{k'}$. If weighted charts on $H$ split $H$ along $c$, then their pullback splits $H'$ along $c$, and the converse holds.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.SplitPull.pull_pull_and_split_pull`.

### Splitting from a base

**Theorem 6.14.10 (Splitting from a base).** Let $R$ be a noetherian domain with fraction field $K$, and let $G$ be a commutative group scheme over $\operatorname{Spec} R$ that is smooth, separated and quasi-compact. Let $C$ be weighted charts on $G$, let $c : \{1, \dots, d\} \to \mathrm{Cl}$, and let $S_g$ be a dense set of points of $\operatorname{Spec} R$. Assume that the map $G_K \to G$ is affine and that $C$ splits $G$ on $S_g$ along $c$. If $(a, j)$ is a base for $(C, c)$, then the pullback of $C$ splits $G_K$ along $c$.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.T.split_generic_of_based_weightedCharts` (also `GlobalTheory.ZJ.Charts.split_generic_of_based`, a second name).

*Proof.* The theorem on the kernel torus gives $i : \mathbb{G}_m^d \to G_K$ that is the kernel of $(a, j)$. By the theorem on homomorphisms, $i$ is a homomorphism. The theorem on stability and weights gives conditions 2, 3 and 4. The theorem on connectedness gives that $G_K$ is geometrically connected and gives condition 5. The theorem on invariants, which needs condition 2, gives condition 6. So $i$ is a torus for the pullback of $(C, c)$, and the charts split $G_K$. $\square$

### Splitting at the generic point

**Lemma 6.14.11 (Base open sets).** Let $C$ be weighted charts on $X$, let $a$ and $a'$ be chart indices and let $j \in I$. If $x \in W_a$ and $r_{a'}(j)(x) \neq 0$, then $r_a(j)(x) \neq 0$.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.ZJ.GenericSplit.mem_basicOpen_of_mem`.

*Proof.* The point $x$ lies in $D(r_{a'}(j)) \subset W_{a'}$, so $x \in W_a \cap W_{a'} = D(r_a(a'))$. On $W_a \cap W_{a'}$ we have $r_a(j) = r_a(a') \, r_{a'}(j)$, and both factors are nonzero at $x$. $\square$

**Lemma 6.14.12 (A base over an open set).** Let $R$ be a domain, let $S_g$ be dense, and let $C$ split $G$ on $S_g$ along $c$. Then there are a chart index $a$, a map $j$ with $\mathrm{wt}(j_n) = c(n)$ for all $n$, and $b \in R$ with $b \neq 0$, such that for every prime $x$ of $R$ with $b \notin x$ the unit section at $x$ lies in $W_a$ and in each $D(r_a(j_n))$.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.ZJ.GenericSplit.exists_open`.

*Proof.* The charts cover $G$, so the unit at the generic point lies in some $W_a$. The preimage of $W_a$ under the unit section is a nonempty open set of $\operatorname{Spec} R$, so it meets $S_g$ at a point $x$. Let $t$ be the field point at $x$ given by the splitting. By condition 5 at the unit of $G_k$, for each $n$ there are $a'$ and $j_n$ with $\mathrm{wt}(j_n) = c(n)$ and $r_{a'}(j_n)$ nonzero at the unit. The unit of $G_k$ maps to the unit of $G$ at $x$, and the lemma on base open sets gives $r_a(j_n)$ nonzero there. The set of primes where the unit lies in $W_a$ and in all $D(r_a(j_n))$ is open and contains $x$, so it contains a basic open set $D(b)$ with $x \in D(b)$. Since $D(b)$ is nonempty, $b \neq 0$. $\square$

**Lemma 6.14.13 (Transport).** Let $G$ be a group scheme over $S$, let $t : \operatorname{Spec} k \to S$ and $t' : \operatorname{Spec} k' \to S$ be field points with $t' = t \circ f$ for a map $f : \operatorname{Spec} k' \to \operatorname{Spec} k$, and let $H$ and $H'$ be group pullbacks of $G$ along $t$ and $t'$ by affine maps. If the pullback of $C$ splits $H$ along $c$, then the pullback of $C$ splits $H'$ along $c$.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.ZJ.GenericSplit.split_transport`.

*Proof.* The map $H' \to G$ factors through $H$, and the square of $H'$ over $H$ along $f$ is a group pullback. The pullback of the charts of $H$ to $H'$ is the pullback of $C$. The theorem on field extensions gives the splitting of $H'$. $\square$

**Theorem 6.14.14 (Splitting at the generic point).** Let $R$ be a noetherian domain with fraction field $K$, and let $G$ be a commutative group scheme over $\operatorname{Spec} R$ that is smooth, separated and quasi-compact. Let $C$ be weighted charts on $G$, let $c : \{1, \dots, d\} \to \mathrm{Cl}$, and let $S_g$ be a dense set of points of $\operatorname{Spec} R$. If the map $G_K \to G$ is affine and $C$ splits $G$ on $S_g$ along $c$, then the pullback of $C$ splits $G_K$ along $c$.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.T.weightedProjectiveCharts_split_generic_point` (also `GlobalTheory.ZJ.GenericSplit.split_generic`, a second name).

*Proof.* The lemma on a base over an open set gives $a$, $j$ and $b \neq 0$. Let $R' = R[1/b]$. It is a noetherian domain with fraction field $K$, and $\operatorname{Spec} R' \to \operatorname{Spec} R$ is an open immersion onto $D(b)$. Let $G'$ be the pullback of $G$ to $R'$. It is commutative, smooth, separated and quasi-compact, and the pullback of $C$ to $G'$ is a system of weighted charts.

The set $S_g \cap D(b)$ is dense in $D(b)$, since $D(b)$ is open. At each of its points the field point of the splitting factors through $\operatorname{Spec} R'$, and the lemma on transport moves the splitting to $G'$. By the choice of $b$, the pair $(a, j)$ is a base for the charts on $G'$. The theorem on splitting from a base gives a splitting of $G'_K$. The generic fibres of $G'$ and $G$ are the same, so the lemma on transport gives the splitting of $G_K$. $\square$

The charts are defined in [IsAffineSplitTorusLeft.lean](lean:Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/Degenerations/IsAffineSplitTorusLeft.lean#L15) and the bases in [OfTorusBased.lean](lean:Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/Degenerations/OfTorusBased.lean#L18). The proof of the splitting from a base only assembles five inputs, which take the hypotheses of the theorem as they are. The base enters only through these inputs, and the theorem on connectedness does not need it. The theorems on homomorphisms and invariants also take the kernel torus, and the theorem on invariants takes the stability. We did not unfold the five inputs.
