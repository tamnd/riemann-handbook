This chapter studies [[abelian schemes|JDDXZ89N33]] over a general base. Its first part proves that every abelian scheme $A$ over any [[scheme|5A35TYZD02]] $S$ has a dual: an abelian scheme $A^\vee$ over $S$ with a Poincaré bundle on $A \times_S A^\vee$ that represents the additive line bundles on $A$, together with biduality. The proof reduces to an affine base, then by a limit argument to a ring of finite type over $\mathbb{Z}$, and then by noetherian induction to a reduced ring with an affine open set that meets every fibre. There the base is pinched from a finite product of normal domains, and over a normal domain the abelian scheme has a relatively ample line bundle $L$. For such $L$ the dual is the quotient of $A$ by the finite flat kernel $K(L)$, and the Poincaré bundle is the descent of the Mumford bundle $\Lambda(L)$. The first section proves a result of the same kind that the proof needs separately: the identity component of a [[smooth|EXKGG9X6J0]] group scheme is an open subgroup scheme. The later parts of the chapter prove biduality, the cohomology of the structure sheaf of an abelian scheme, and [the lifting of homomorphisms along nilpotent divided power thickenings](/entry/abelian-scheme-hom-lifting-nilpotent-divided-powers/). The last parts compute cohomology by ordered Čech complexes of affine covers, prove the Hodge and de Rham structure and the de Rham structure of an abelian scheme, lift sections of smooth group schemes and line bundles along thickenings, prove that abelian schemes are unobstructed, construct ample line bundles over normal domains and ample roots over algebraically closed fields, algebraize morphisms from formal completions, and study the periods of abelian schemes over $\mathbb{C}$ and their variation in families.
