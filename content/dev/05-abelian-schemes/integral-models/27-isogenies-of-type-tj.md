## Isogenies of type T_j

This section states that the isogenies of $p$-power degree and of type $T_j$ out of an object of level $N$ are classified by a [[finite|N9C4X7XKE4]] [[étale|H84VHC8PX2]] scheme over the base. Such isogenies give the Hecke correspondences at the prime $p$ that are used in [the Kodaira-Spencer map of weight one](/entry/kodaira-spencer-of-weight-one/). The type is a condition on the kernel of the isogeny at the geometric points of characteristic $0$: the part of the kernel above a prime $w$ of $\mathcal{O}_F$ over $p$ has a prescribed size and is killed by $w$. We have not checked this condition against the type $T_j$ of Harris and Taylor (*The geometry and cohomology of some simple Shimura varieties*, Annals of Math. Studies 151, 2001). The theorem is the result of [a later section](/entry/isog-rep-exists-rep/) under a second name, and the proof is given there.

### Isogenies and their kernels

Let $D$ be a datum for $p$ (Definition 8.1.1), with CM field $F$, element $\omega \in \mathcal{O}_F$ and integers $m$ and $n$. Let $b$ be a base over $R$ (Definition 8.1.2), let $s : S \to \operatorname{Spec} R$ and let $N \geq 0$. Objects of level $N$ over $s$ and maps of objects are as in Definition 8.1.3. We write an object as $o = (A, \iota, P, \eta)$, where $\iota$ is the action of $O$, and we write the group law of $A$ additively.

**Definition 8.27.1 (The ideal $u_r$).** For $r \in \mathbb{Z}$ let $u_r = (p, \omega - r)$, the ideal of $\mathcal{O}_F$ generated by $p$ and $\omega - r$.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.UPel.HT.ES.uIdeal`.

By part 1 of Definition 8.1.1 there is $r \in \mathbb{Z}$ with $p \mid r^2 - t_0 r + n_0$. For such $r$ the ideal $u_r$ is proper, so it lies in some maximal ideal $w$.

**Definition 8.27.2 ($c$-isogeny).** Let $o = (A, \iota, P, \eta)$ and $o' = (A', \iota', P', \eta')$ be objects of level $N$ over $s$ and let $c \geq 0$. A map of schemes $\psi : A \to A'$ over $S$ is a *$c$-isogeny* from $o$ to $o'$ if the following hold.

1. $c = p^k$ for some $k \geq 0$.
2. $\psi$ is a homomorphism of group schemes, and $\psi \circ \iota(a) = \iota'(a) \circ \psi$ for all $a \in O$.
3. $\eta'(x) = \psi \circ \eta(x)$ for all $x \in \Lambda$.
4. There is an isomorphism of line bundles $P^{\otimes c} \cong (\psi \times \psi)^* P'$ on $A \times_S A$.
5. There is a homomorphism $\psi' : A' \to A$ with $\psi' \circ \psi = [c]$ on $A$ and $\psi \circ \psi' = [c]$ on $A'$, where $[c]$ is multiplication by $c$.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.UPel.HT.ES.IsPIsog`.

**Definition 8.27.3 (Killed by an ideal).** Let $o$ be an object over $s$, let $v$ be an ideal of $\mathcal{O}_F$, and let $g : T \to A$ be a point of $A$ over a scheme $T$ over $S$. Then $g$ is *killed by $v$* if $\iota(a) \circ g = 0$ for all $a \in v$, where $a$ acts through its image in $O$.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.UPel.HT.ES.KilledBy`.

**Definition 8.27.4 (Type $T_j$).** Let $r \in \mathbb{Z}$, let $w$ be an ideal of $\mathcal{O}_F$, let $j \geq 0$ and let $\psi$ be a map from $A$ to $A'$ as above. Then $\psi$ has *type $T_j$* for $(r, w)$ if, for every algebraically closed field $k$ of characteristic $0$ and every $x : \operatorname{Spec} k \to S$, the following two conditions hold. Here a point of $A$ over $x$ is a map $g : \operatorname{Spec} k \to A$ over $S$, and it lies in the kernel of $\psi$ if $\psi \circ g = 0$.

1. Every point $g$ of $A$ over $x$ in the kernel of $\psi$ that is killed by $u_r^e$ for some $e \geq 0$ is killed by $w$.
2. The number of points $g$ of $A$ over $x$ in the kernel of $\psi$ that are killed by $w$ is $N(w)^{mj}$, where $N(w)$ is the absolute norm of $w$.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.UPel.HT.ES.IsTj`.

So at each geometric point of characteristic $0$, the $u_r$-primary part of $\ker \psi$ equals its $w$-torsion, and this group has $N(w)^{mj}$ points.

### Representability

**Theorem 8.27.5 (Isogenies of type $T_j$ are representable).** Let $R$ be a [[noetherian|0670T59HGE]] ring, $b$ a base for $D$ over $R$, and $N \geq 3$ an integer prime to $p$ such that $p$ is a unit in $R$. Let $r \in \mathbb{Z}$ and let $w$ be a maximal ideal of $\mathcal{O}_F$ with $u_r \subseteq w$. Let $j \leq n$ and $c = p^e$, where $e > 0$ if $j > 0$. Assume $m = 1$. Let $S$ be a locally noetherian scheme, $s : S \to \operatorname{Spec} R$, and $o$ an object of level $N$ over $s$. Then there are the following data.

1. A map $\pi : Y \to S$ that is finite and étale.
2. An object $o_0$ of level $N$ over $s \pi$, with a map of objects $o_0 \to o$ over $\pi$.
3. An object $o'$ of level $N$ over $s \pi$ and a $c$-isogeny $\psi : A_0 \to A'$ from $o_0$ to $o'$ of type $T_j$ for $(r, w)$.

They have the following property. Let $T$ be a scheme, $x : T \to S$ a map, $o_1$ and $o_1'$ objects of level $N$ over $s x$ with a map of objects $o_1 \to o$ over $x$, and $\psi_1 : A_1 \to A_1'$ a $c$-isogeny from $o_1$ to $o_1'$ of type $T_j$ for $(r, w)$. Then there is a unique $y : T \to Y$ with $\pi y = x$ such that there exist maps of objects $\varphi_0 : o_1 \to o_0$ and $\varphi_1 : o_1' \to o'$ over $y$ with $\psi \circ \varphi_0 = \varphi_1 \circ \psi_1$.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.T.harrisTaylor_typeTj_isogenies_representable`.

In words, $(Y, \psi)$ is a universal $c$-isogeny of type $T_j$ out of $o$: every such isogeny out of a pullback of $o$ is the pullback of $\psi$ along a unique map to $Y$ over $S$.
