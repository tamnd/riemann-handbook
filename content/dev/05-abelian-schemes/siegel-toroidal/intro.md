This chapter proves the existence of an integral toroidal compactification of the Siegel moduli schemes, in the form of Faltings and Chai. For each $g \ge 1$ there is a polarization function $\varphi$ of rank $g$ such that, for each odd $n \ge 3$, there are a Siegel family of genus $g$ and level $n$ over $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_n, 1/n]$ and a smooth projective compactification $\bar S$ of its base, with a semi-abelian scheme that extends the universal abelian scheme, a boundary divisor, and charts at every point in which the boundary is cut out by a product of parameters and the semi-abelian scheme comes from Mumford's construction ([the integral toroidal compactification](/entry/siegel-toroidal-integral-holds/)). The compactification is a normalized blowup of a minimal compactification. Its charts are built at each point from a trait through the point: the trait gives degeneration data, the degeneration data give a lower base and a tautological chart, and Mumford's construction over the chart gives the semi-abelian scheme. On the way the chapter proves formal algebraization of ample bundles along a tower of thickenings, the existence of roots of the Mumford bundle over complete and étale local bases, and the compatibility of uniformizations with the image of an idempotent.
