This theorem proves $\mathrm{SiegelToroidalIntegral}$, the existence of a projective smooth toroidal compactification of the Siegel moduli space of odd level $n \ge 3$ over $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_n, 1/n]$, with charts at each point and an ample twist of the Hodge bundle. Its one caller is the volume 8 entry [the toroidal compactification as a literature input](/entry/siegel-toroidal-integral-of-literature/). The proof is a short reduction to the statement $\mathrm{SiegelToroidalPolCharts}$, which [`siegelToroidalCompactification_polarisedCharts`](/entry/siegel-toroidal-compactification-polarised-charts/) supplies. That branch holds 3,537 of the 3,655 declarations below this theorem.

## Statement

**Theorem.** $\mathrm{SiegelToroidalIntegral}$ holds.

The [volume 8 entry](/entry/siegel-toroidal-integral-of-literature/) states it in full. In short: for each $g \ge 1$ there is a polarization function $\varphi$ (a function on positive semidefinite forms of rank at most $g$, with a cone decomposition) such that for each odd $n \ge 3$ there are a Siegel family $\mathcal{A}$ of genus $g$ and level $n$, a projective smooth scheme $\overline{S}$ over $\Lambda_n = \mathbb{Z}[\zeta_n, 1/n]$ of relative dimension $g(g+1)/2$ with a dense open immersion $j$ of the base $X$ of $\mathcal{A}$ into $\overline{S}$, a semi-abelian scheme $G$ over $\overline{S}$ that extends the universal abelian scheme and is abelian exactly on $j(X)$, a boundary divisor $B$ and $d \ge 1$ with a chart at each point, and $N \ge 1$ such that $\omega^N$ is generated by global sections and $\omega^{Nk} \otimes \mathcal{O}(-B)$ is ample for all large $k$. Here $\omega$ is the Hodge line bundle of $G$.

## Proof

The statement $\mathrm{SiegelToroidalPolCharts}$ (stated in full in the entry [`siegelToroidalPolCharts`](/entry/siegel-toroidal-pol-charts/)) gives the same objects for each $\varphi$ that is a polarization function with no self-intersections, with two differences: the chart at each point is a *polarized* chart, and the ample twist is asked for one $k$ only.

1. Fix $g \ge 1$. Choose $\varphi$ with both properties by `exists_invariant_smoothProjFan_noSelfIntersections`.
2. For each odd $n \ge 3$, apply $\mathrm{SiegelToroidalPolCharts}$ to $\varphi$ and $n$, and keep all its data.
3. Turn each polarized chart into a chart by `hasSiegelChart_of_polarisedMonomialPeriodChart`. This uses $n \neq 0$.
4. From ampleness of $\omega^{Nk} \otimes \mathcal{O}(-B)$ for one $k$ and global generation of $\omega^N$, get ampleness for all $k' \ge k$ by `isAmple_tensor_lbPow_mono_of_generated`.

The rest is bookkeeping.
