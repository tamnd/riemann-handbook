This theorem states $\mathrm{SiegelToroidalPolCharts}$ under a second name. Its one caller is [`siegelToroidalIntegral_holds`](/entry/siegel-toroidal-integral-holds/), which turns the polarized charts into the charts of $\mathrm{SiegelToroidalIntegral}$. The real content is in [`siegelToroidalPolCharts`](/entry/siegel-toroidal-pol-charts/). Its 3,537 declarations are itself and the 3,536 below that theorem.

## Statement

**Theorem.** $\mathrm{SiegelToroidalPolCharts}$ holds.

In short: for each $g \ge 1$, each polarization function $\varphi$ for $g$ with no self-intersections and each odd $n \ge 3$, there are a Siegel family of genus $g$ and level $n$, a smooth projective compactification $\overline{S}$ of its base over $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_n, 1/n]$, a semi-abelian scheme over $\overline{S}$ that extends the universal abelian scheme, a boundary divisor $B$ with a polarized chart at each point, and a power of the Hodge bundle that is generated by global sections and whose twist by $\mathcal{O}(-B)$ is ample. The entry [`siegelToroidalPolCharts`](/entry/siegel-toroidal-pol-charts/) states it in full.

## Proof

Apply `SiegJoin.siegelToroidalPolCharts`. The two statements are the same.
