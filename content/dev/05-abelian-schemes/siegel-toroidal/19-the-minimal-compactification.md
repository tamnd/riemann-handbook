## The minimal compactification

This section constructs the minimal compactification data used in the [theorem with polarized charts](/entry/siegel-toroidal-pol-charts/): a normal projective scheme $Z$ over $\Lambda_n$ that contains $X$ as a dense open set, an ample line bundle $\mathcal{O}_Z(1)$, and a semi-abelian scheme over a finite cover of $Z$ whose Hodge bundle is a root of the pullback of $\mathcal{O}_Z(1)$. The scheme $Z$ is the normalization of $\mathbb{P}^N$ in $X$ along a theta map: the theta constants of the eighth power of $A$, twisted by an $E_8$ matrix, at a level that is a power of a prime dividing $n$. Because $Z$ is built from theta constants, it is a theta compactification in the sense of the section on [traits and classical points](/entry/trait-uniq/), and so the [fan order ideal](/entry/ideal-st-holds/) and its normalized blowup exist on it.

We keep the notation of the previous sections: $F$ is a Siegel family of genus $g$ and level $n$ over $\Lambda_n$ with base $s : X \to \operatorname{Spec}\Lambda_n$ and universal datum $(A, P, \ell)$. Minimal compactification data are as in the section on [charts on the normalized blowup](/entry/chart-st5-holds/), with conditions 1 to 4. For a commutative group scheme $G$ smooth of relative dimension $g$ we write $\omega_G$ for its Hodge bundle, the determinant of the dual of its Lie algebra.

**Theorem 9.19.1 (The minimal compactification).** Let $g \ge 1$, let $n \ge 3$ be odd, and let $F$ be a Siegel family of genus $g$ and level $n$ over $\Lambda_n$. Then there are $z : Z \to \operatorname{Spec}\Lambda_n$, $i : X \to Z$ and a line bundle $\mathcal{O}_Z(1)$ on $Z$ that are minimal compactification data for $F$.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.SiegMinTop.minSt5` (also `GlobalTheory.T.minimalSiegelCompactification_exists`, a wrapper, and `GlobalTheory.SiegJoin.MinSt5`, the statement).

The proof uses two general lemmas.

**Lemma 9.19.2 (Properness of the cover).** Let $R$ be a noetherian ring and $\beta : R \to o'$ a finite ring map. Let $s' : S' \to \operatorname{Spec} o'$ and $z : Z \to \operatorname{Spec} R$ be projective, let $i : X \to Z$ have dense image, let $U \subseteq S'$ be open, and let $c : U \to X$ be surjective. Let $\pi : S' \to Z$ with $\pi|_U = i \circ c$ and $z \circ \pi = \operatorname{Spec}\beta \circ s'$. Then $\pi$ is surjective and proper, and $Z$ is noetherian.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.SiegMinTop.lemProper`.

*Proof.* Projective maps are proper, and $\operatorname{Spec}\beta$ is finite, hence proper. So $z \circ \pi = \operatorname{Spec}\beta \circ s'$ is proper. Since $z$ is separated, $\pi$ is proper. In particular $\pi(S')$ is closed. Each point $i(x)$ is $\pi(u)$ for some $u \in U$ with $c(u) = x$, since $c$ is surjective; so $i(X) \subseteq \pi(S')$. As $i(X)$ is dense, $\pi(S') = Z$. Finally $Z$ is of finite type over the noetherian ring $R$, so it is locally noetherian and quasi-compact, hence noetherian. $\square$

**Lemma 9.19.3 (The generic point of an open set).** Let $M$ be a rational model of a field $\Omega$ and $U \subseteq Z_M$ a nonempty open set. Then $\mathrm{pt} : \operatorname{Spec}\Omega \to Z_M$ factors as $\iota_U \circ x$ for a map $x : \operatorname{Spec}\Omega \to U$ whose image is the generic point of $U$.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.SiegMinTop.lemGen`.

*Proof.* The image of $\mathrm{pt}$ is the generic point of $Z_M$. It specializes to every point, so it lies in every nonempty open set, and in particular in $U$. So $\mathrm{pt}$ lifts through the open immersion $U \to Z_M$. The generic point of $U$ maps to a point whose closure is the closure of $U$, which is $Z_M$ since $U$ is open in the irreducible space $Z_M$. So it maps to the generic point of $Z_M$, and the lift sends $\operatorname{Spec}\Omega$ to it. $\square$

*Proof of the theorem.* The construction has five steps. In each we use only the statements of the theorems named.

*Step 1: the theta map.* Since $n \ge 3$ there is a prime $\ell$ with $\ell \mid n$, and $\ell$ is odd since $n$ is odd. A lemma on $E_8$ matrices gives an $E_8$ matrix $W$ with a sharp basis. The base $X$ is integral and normal by a theorem on the Siegel moduli scheme. A theorem on theta powers gives a polarized abelian scheme $P_X$ over $X$, the eighth fibre power of $A$ with the polarization twisted by $W$, with a rigidification. A theorem on theta maps at deep level gives $j_0$ such that theta maps of level $\ell^{j}$ with $j > j_0$ have finite fibres. Put $m = \ell^{j_0 + 1}$. Then $m$ is odd, $m \ge 3$, and $m$ is a unit in $\Lambda_n$, since $\ell \mid n$ and $n$ is a unit. A theorem on theta maps of the eighth power gives $N$ and a map $f : X \to \mathbb{P}^N_{\Lambda_n}$ over $\Lambda_n$ given by the theta constants of $P_X$ of level $m$. By the choice of $j_0$, $f$ is quasi-finite.

*Step 2: the normalization.* The ring $\Lambda_n$ is a Dedekind domain of finite type over $\mathbb{Z}$, and $s$ is locally of finite type, quasi-compact and separated. A theorem on finite normalizations of quasi-finite maps gives a normal integral scheme $Z$, a finite map $p : Z \to \mathbb{P}^N_{\Lambda_n}$ and an open immersion $i : X \to Z$ with dense image and $p \circ i = f$. This is the form of Zariski's main theorem used here. Let $z$ be $p$ followed by the structure map of $\mathbb{P}^N$, and $\mathcal{O}_Z(1) = p^*\mathcal{O}(1)^{\otimes 8}$. A theorem on schemes finite over projective space shows that $z$ is projective and that $\mathcal{O}_Z(1)$ is an ample line bundle. Then $z \circ i = s$.

*Step 3: a normal model with a semi-abelian scheme.* A theorem on realized theta systems gives an arithmetic base $(o, \Omega)$ with a finite injective map $\beta : \Lambda_n \to o$, a theta system $S$ over it with a realization, and a normal level model. A theorem on toric models then gives a normal rational model $M$ of $\Omega$, projective over $o$, on which the theta values of $S$ have divisors, together with a semi-abelian scheme $G_8$ over $Z_M$. Two theorems on level covers give a dense open $U \subseteq Z_M$ and a finite surjective map $c : U \to X$ compatible with the structure maps. A theorem on the first factor of the eighth power gives a commutative group scheme $G$ over $Z_M$ that is semi-abelian and smooth of relative dimension $g$, whose restriction to $U$ is the group pullback of $A$ along $c$, and whose Hodge bundle satisfies $\omega_{G_8} \cong \omega_G^{\otimes 8}$, for the semi-abelian scheme $G_8$ of the toric model.

*Step 4: the map to $Z$.* The theta constants of the realization are the coordinates of the theta map, up to the identification of the theta system with $P_X$, by a theorem on symmetric theta constants. A theorem on powers of theta constants writes a power of each constant as a unit times a symmetric function of the theta values. Since the theta values have divisors on $M$, so does the family of theta constants. So it defines $h : Z_M \to \mathbb{P}^N$ over $\Lambda_n$. By the lemma on the generic point, $\mathrm{pt}$ factors through $U$ at the generic point of $U$. At that point $h$ and $f \circ c$ have the same projective coordinates, so $h|_U = f \circ c$ by a theorem on maps with the same generic coordinates. Since $Z$ is the normalization of $\mathbb{P}^N$ along $f$ and $Z_M$ is normal, a theorem on extension through finite covers gives $\pi : Z_M \to Z$ with $\pi|_U = i \circ c$ and $p \circ \pi = h$. The lemma on properness of the cover, with $R = \Lambda_n$, $o' = o$ and $S' = Z_M$, shows that $\pi$ is proper and surjective and that $Z$ is noetherian.

*Step 5: the conditions.* Theta integrality for $W$ follows from a theorem on projective integrality of Siegel theta constants over any base: a rational model with a map $\pi'$ to $Z$ compatible with a sharp theta system gives, after composing with $p$, a model over $\mathbb{P}^N$ on which the theta values have divisors. With Step 2 this shows that $(Z, z, i)$ is a theta compactification, which is condition 1 together with the ampleness of $\mathcal{O}_Z(1)$. Condition 2 holds with $o' = o$, $S' = Z_M$, the dense open $U$, the finite surjective $c$ and the proper surjective $\pi$; here $z \circ \pi = \operatorname{Spec}\beta \circ s_M$ because $p \circ \pi = h$ and $h$ lies over $\beta$. Condition 3 is Step 3. For condition 4, let $\delta$ be the line bundle of the theta constants on $Z_M$. Then
$$\begin{aligned} \pi^*\mathcal{O}_Z(1) &= h^*\mathcal{O}(1)^{\otimes 8} \cong \delta^{\otimes 8 (N+1)!}, \\ \delta^{\otimes 8} &\cong \omega_{G_8}^{\otimes 4}, \qquad \omega_{G_8} \cong \omega_G^{\otimes 8}, \end{aligned}$$
by a theorem on pullbacks of $\mathcal{O}(1)$ along coordinate sections, a theorem comparing the theta constant bundle with the Hodge bundle, and Step 3. Together they give
$$\pi^*\mathcal{O}_Z(1) \cong \omega_G^{\otimes 32 (N+1)!},$$
which is condition 4 with exponent $32 (N+1)! \ge 1$. $\square$

The scheme $Z$ is a minimal compactification in the sense that $\mathcal{O}_Z(1)$ pulls back to a power of the Hodge bundle; the data do not say that $Z$ is the Proj of the ring of Siegel modular forms. The exponent $32 (N+1)!$ comes from the factor $8$ in $\mathcal{O}_Z(1)$, the comparison $\delta^{8} \cong \omega_{G_8}^{4}$ and the factor $(N+1)!$ that makes $h^*\mathcal{O}(1)$ a power of $\delta$.

**Remark 9.19.4 (Formalization).** The lemmas are `lemProper` and `lemGen` ([MinimalSiegelCompactificationExists.lean](lean:Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/DivisorProofs/MinimalSiegelCompactificationExists.lean#L40), line 59). The data `MinAt5` list projectivity of $z$, normality of $Z$, $z \circ i = s$ and density of $i(X)$ twice, once inside the theta compactification and once on their own, and they contain no condition that makes $Z$ minimal ([MinSt5OfMinSt4.lean](lean:Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/Divisors/MinSt5OfMinSt4.lean#L19)). In the proof the letter $N$ is the dimension of projective space, and the exponent of condition 4 is the number $32 (N+1)!$ (line 164). The theorems on the Siegel moduli scheme, theta powers, theta maps, normalizations, realized theta systems, toric models, level covers, theta constants and line bundles have no entries in this part; we use only their statements.

## Notes and references

The chapter proves the existence of an integral toroidal compactification of the Siegel moduli scheme over $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_n, 1/n]$ for odd $n \ge 3$, with charts given by Mumford's construction and an ample twist of a power of the Hodge bundle by the boundary ([the integral toroidal compactification](/entry/siegel-toroidal-integral-holds/)). It is used through [its statement among the results from the literature](/entry/siegel-toroidal-integral-of-literature/), at [EveryCharacter from the literature](/step/every-character-of-literature/).

The toroidal and minimal compactifications are those of Faltings and Chai, who prove the result for all $n \ge 3$; the statement here asks only for odd $n$. Faltings and Chai build the minimal compactification as the Proj of a ring of Siegel modular forms, and the toroidal compactification by gluing charts from degeneration data, then show that for a polarization function the toroidal compactification is a normalized blowup of the minimal one. Here the order is reversed. The minimal compactification is the normalization of projective space along a theta map of the eighth power of the universal abelian scheme, twisted by an $E_8$ matrix. The toroidal compactification is the normalized blowup of an ideal whose orders along traits are prescribed by the polarization function, and the ideal is built from $p$-adic Fourier expansions of theta functions at classical points. The charts are then constructed at each point from a trait through it. Degeneration data, Mumford's construction and the uniformization of degenerating abelian varieties follow Mumford and Faltings and Chai. Formal algebraization of ample bundles rests on Grothendieck's existence theorem. Theta constants and the projective maps they define follow Mumford. The $E_8$ lattice enters through the classification of even unimodular lattices, and finiteness of integral closures through the standard result for separable extensions.
