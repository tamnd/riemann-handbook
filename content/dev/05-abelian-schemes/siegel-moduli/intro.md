This chapter builds the moduli spaces of PEL type for a CM field and their models over the integers, and proves Part I: Siegel families of large level exist at every prime, and the PEL moduli spaces have systems of local models over the valuation rings above every prime. These models are normal, projective and flat, with semi-abelian schemes whose fibres split, they are compatible under change of level, finite over Siegel families, uniformized at complete local rings in the sense of Mumford's construction, and they contain the arcs that realize given degeneration data. The first part of the chapter goes from tangent spaces to Part I. It bounds the tangent space of a family of theta level objects by the Kodaira-Spencer map, constructs theta families with smooth generic fibre as schemes of theta values, compactifies them to normal projective models with ample frames by gluing over generic points, builds from these one projective model of full level that receives every moduli space of that level, and deduces the local model systems and Part I from the semistable reduction theorem and the existence of arc families with frames. The second part proves the inputs used along the way: systems of full level models with base models, base models of semi-abelian schemes and their quotients by tame finite groups, the existence of the moduli schemes at every level and at deeper CM levels, the key formula, the geometric statement on theta systems from semistable reduction, the count of sections whose square is the determinant of the form on torsion, theta families over the closure of an open set and theta objects near good primes, the sharp $E_8$ class among good theta classes and the principal Siegel families of eighth powers, and the gluing of theta level abelian schemes over affine covers.
