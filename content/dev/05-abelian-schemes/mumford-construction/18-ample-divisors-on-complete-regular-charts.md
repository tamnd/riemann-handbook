## Ample divisors on complete regular charts

This section proves that a symmetric line bundle on a semi-abelian scheme over a complete regular local ring is ample if its restriction to the generic fibre is ample. Then it applies this to the closure of an ample divisor on a chart. The second result is Step 5 of [the theorem on extension away from a divisor](/entry/has-ext-away-map-of-charts/). There it supplies the ample divisors $D_1(x)$ on the charts that the gluing theorem of the next section needs. For semi-abelian schemes see [semi-abelian degenerations and Mumford's construction](/learn/abelian-varieties/#semi-abelian-degenerations-and-mumfords-construction) in Volume 1.

### Ample line bundles and ample divisors

**Definition 4.18.1 (Ample line bundle).** Let $X$ be a scheme and $L$ a line bundle on $X$. We say that $L$ is *ample* if $X$ is quasi-compact and for every point $x$ of $X$ there are an integer $n > 0$ and a global section $s$ of $L^{\otimes n}$ such that $x$ lies in the open set $X_s$ where $s$ does not vanish and $X_s$ is affine.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.IndefiniteRowSideFullHecke.PolarizedAbelianVarieties.IsAmpleLB`.

**Definition 4.18.2 (Ample divisor).** Let $X$ be an integral scheme with function field $K(X)$ and let $D$ be a Cartier divisor on $X$. For $k \geq 0$ let $\Gamma(kD)$ be the set of $u \in K(X)$ that are regular for $kD$ at every point, that is, the global sections of $\mathcal{O}(kD)$. For $u \in \Gamma(kD)$ let $X_u$ be the set of points where $u$ generates $\mathcal{O}(kD)$. We say that $D$ is *ample* if for every point $x$ there are $k \geq 1$, $u \in \Gamma(kD)$ and an affine open $U$ with $U = X_u$ and $x \in U$.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.Desc.AmpleDiv`.

On a quasi-compact integral scheme, $D$ is ample in this sense if and only if $\mathcal{O}(D)$ is an ample line bundle. A line bundle $L$ on a group scheme $G$ is *symmetric* if $[-1]^*L \cong L$.

### Ampleness from the generic fibre

The following theorem is a result of Raynaud from his work on ample sheaves on group schemes. We state it in the form that the chapter uses. Here $K = \operatorname{Frac} R$ and $G_K$ is the generic fibre.

**Theorem 4.18.3 (Ampleness from the generic fibre).** Let $R$ be a regular local domain of characteristic $0$ that is complete for its maximal ideal. Let $G$ be a semi-abelian group scheme over $R$ whose generic fibre $G_K$ is an abelian scheme over $K$. Let $L$ be a line bundle on $G$ such that the restriction $L_K$ is relatively ample on $G_K$ over $K$ and $[-1]^*L \cong L$. Then $L$ is ample.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.AmpleGenericSemiAb.isAmpleLB_of_generic_of_symm` (also `GlobalTheory.AmpleGenericSemiAb.isAmpleLB_of_generic` and `GlobalTheory.T.raynaud_lineBundle_relAmple_of_generic_semiAbelian`, second names).

*Proof.* *Step 1: a reference bundle.* By an input, $G$ carries an ample symmetric line bundle $M$. The input needs only that $R$ is a regular local domain, that $G$ is semi-abelian and that $G_K$ is abelian.

*Step 2: $M_K$ is relatively ample.* The map $\operatorname{Spec} K \to \operatorname{Spec} R$ is affine, so its base change $G_K \to G$ is affine. The pullback of an ample bundle along an affine morphism is ample, so $M_K$ is ample. Since $G_K$ is proper over the affine scheme $\operatorname{Spec} K$, $M_K$ is relatively ample.

*Step 3: a finite normal cover.* By an input applied to the two symmetric bundles $L$ and $M$, both relatively ample on the generic fibre, there are the following data.

1. A noetherian, integrally closed, local domain $R'$ and an injective finite ring map $\rho : R \to R'$.
2. The group pullback $G'$ of $G$ to $R'$, with projection $\pi : G' \to G$.
3. Integers $N \geq 1$, $a_0 \geq 1$, $a_1, \dots, a_r \geq 0$ and homomorphisms $\beta_1, \dots, \beta_r : G' \to G'$ with

$$
(\pi^*L)^{\otimes N} \cong (\pi^*M)^{\otimes a_0} \otimes \bigotimes_{i=1}^{r} \beta_i^*(\pi^*M)^{\otimes a_i}.
$$

*Step 4: $\pi^*L$ is ample.* The morphism $\pi$ is the base change of the finite morphism $\operatorname{Spec} \rho$, so it is finite. Thus $\pi^*M$ is ample. Choose $k \geq 1$ such that $(\pi^*M)^{\otimes k}$ is generated by global sections. Then each $\beta_i^*(\pi^*M)^{\otimes k a_i}$ is generated by global sections. The $kN$-th power of $\pi^*L$ is an ample bundle tensored with a bundle that is generated by global sections, so it is ample. Hence $\pi^*L$ is ample.

*Step 5: descent along $\pi$.* A regular local ring is a normal domain, so $R$ is noetherian, integrally closed and local. By an input on finite covers, if $R$ and $R'$ are noetherian, integrally closed local domains, $\rho$ is injective and finite, $G$ is semi-abelian and $G'$ is its group pullback, then ampleness of $\pi^*L$ implies ampleness of $L$. This gives the claim. $\square$

Over a field, the last step is the familiar fact that ampleness descends along finite surjective morphisms of proper schemes (Hartshorne, *Algebraic Geometry*, Springer 1977, Chapter III, Exercise 5.7(d)). Here $G$ is not proper over $R$, so the descent needs the group structure.

### Ampleness on a chart

We need two conditions on morphisms of integral schemes. For an integral scheme $Y$, an open $V$ of $Y$, a morphism $\varphi : V \to X$ to an integral scheme $X$ and a field map $\theta : K(X) \to K(Y)$, we say that $\theta$ is *induced by* $\varphi$ if there is a morphism $l : \operatorname{Spec} K(Y) \to V$ that maps to the generic point of $Y$ and satisfies $\varphi \circ l = \eta_X \circ \operatorname{Spec}\theta$, with $\eta_X$ the generic point of $X$. For Cartier divisors $D$ on $X$ and $D_1$ on $Y$, we say that $D_1$ *restricts to* $\varphi^*D$ if for every $v \in V$ and every local equation $g \in K(X)^\times$ of $D$ at $\varphi(v)$, the element $\theta(g)$ is a local equation of $D_1$ at $v$.

**Theorem 4.18.4 (Ampleness on a chart).** Let $R_0$ be a domain and $f \in R_0$. Let $A$ be an abelian scheme over $R_0[1/f]$ whose total space is integral, and let $D$ be an ample Cartier divisor on $A$ with $[-1]^*\mathcal{O}(D) \cong \mathcal{O}(D)$. Let $R$ be a regular local domain of characteristic $0$ that is complete for its maximal ideal, with $K = \operatorname{Frac} R$, and let $\rho : R_0 \to R$ be a flat ring map with $\rho(f) \neq 0$. Let $G$ be a semi-abelian group scheme over $R$ whose total space is integral. Let the following data be given.

1. The open $V$ of $G$ of points $z$ such that $\rho(f)$ is not in the prime ideal of the image of $z$ in $\operatorname{Spec} R$.
2. A morphism $\varphi : V \to A$ such that the square with sides $\varphi$, $V \to \operatorname{Spec} R$, $A \to \operatorname{Spec} R_0$ and $\operatorname{Spec}\rho$ is cartesian.
3. A morphism $y : \operatorname{Spec} K \to \operatorname{Spec} R_0[1/f]$ and a morphism $\alpha : G_K \to A$ that make $G_K$ the group pullback of $A$ along $y$.
4. The compatibility: for every scheme $T$ and morphisms $t : T \to G_K$ and $t' : T \to V$ that agree in $G$, we have $\alpha \circ t = \varphi \circ t'$.
5. A field map $\theta : K(A) \to K(G)$ induced by $\varphi$.
6. A Cartier divisor $D_1$ on $G$ that restricts to $\varphi^*D$.

Then $D_1$ is ample.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.Desc.ampleDiv_closure_on_chart`.

*Proof.* *Step 1: the generic fibre in $V$.* The element $\rho(f)$ is a unit in $K$, so $G_K \to G$ factors through $V$. By an input, this gives $l : G_K \to V$ such that the square with sides $\varphi \circ l$, $G_K \to \operatorname{Spec} K$, $A \to \operatorname{Spec} R_0[1/f]$ and a map $\operatorname{Spec} K \to \operatorname{Spec} R_0[1/f]$ is cartesian.

*Step 2: $G_K$ is abelian.* By this square, $G_K$ is a base change of $A$, so it is proper and has geometrically connected fibres. It is smooth because $G$ is smooth. Hence $G_K$ is an abelian scheme.

*Step 3: the pullback of $D$.* Since $A$ is quasi-compact, $\mathcal{O}(D)$ is an ample line bundle. The pullback of an ample bundle along the cartesian square of Step 1 is ample, so $(\varphi \circ l)^*\mathcal{O}(D)$ is ample.

*Step 4: $D_1$ on the generic fibre.* Since $D_1$ restricts to $\varphi^*D$, we have $\mathcal{O}(D_1)|_V \cong \varphi^*\mathcal{O}(D)$. Hence $\mathcal{O}(D_1)|_{G_K} \cong (\varphi \circ l)^*\mathcal{O}(D)$ is ample. As $G_K$ is proper over $\operatorname{Spec} K$, it is relatively ample.

*Step 5: symmetry on the generic fibre.* Condition 4 with $t$ the identity and $t' = l$ gives $\alpha = \varphi \circ l$. The pullback of a symmetric bundle along a group pullback is symmetric. So $\alpha^*\mathcal{O}(D)$ is symmetric, and by Step 4 the restriction of $\mathcal{O}(D_1)$ to $G_K$ is symmetric.

*Step 6: symmetry on $G$.* The ring $R$ is a normal domain. By an input, a line bundle on a semi-abelian scheme over such a base that is symmetric on the generic fibre is symmetric. So $[-1]^*\mathcal{O}(D_1) \cong \mathcal{O}(D_1)$.

*Step 7: conclusion.* By Steps 2, 4 and 6, the theorem on ampleness from the generic fibre applies to $\mathcal{O}(D_1)$, so this bundle is ample. Since $G$ is quasi-compact over $R$, the divisor $D_1$ is ample. $\square$

The flatness of $\rho$ is not used in Steps 1 to 7. It is part of the definition of a chart, so the hypothesis costs nothing where the theorem is applied.
