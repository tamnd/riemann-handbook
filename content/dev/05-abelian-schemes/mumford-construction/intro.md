This chapter proves Mumford's construction: over a complete normal noetherian local domain of characteristic $0$, degeneration data of an abelian scheme, a torus extension, periods and a polarisation are realised, after a finite extension of the base, by a commutative semi-abelian scheme with a uniformisation and a compatible line bundle. The proof first reduces to data of unitary PEL type, by a tensor product with a unimodular Hermitian matrix and the choice of an algebraic factor. For such data it builds a labelled cover, a semi-abelian scheme over a proper modification of the base that is uniformised at every complete trait. From theta expansions at the traits it gets sections with prescribed reduction and a projective embedding near the closed fibre, and with this embedding it descends the group and its line bundle from the modification to the base. Galois descent and the descent of line bundles then remove the finite extensions where this is possible. On the way the chapter proves results on idempotent factors and polarised data with the same invariants, on sections of order two and even cubical structures, on an ample line bundle in the style of Raynaud, and on the algebraicity of endomorphisms of the formal completion. It also proves a converse: a semi-abelian scheme with a uniformisation comes from degeneration data, which the chapter constructs from a count of torsion points and a functional equation of degree one. It also proves an extension criterion: an abelian scheme with a relatively ample line bundle over a dense open subscheme of a normal noetherian scheme extends to a semi-abelian scheme if it extends over a complete regular local ring at every point. A separate result shows that the archimedean condition on toroidal data is closed.
