## Smoothness and normal crossings

This section shows that the separating blowup $S$ of Section 2 is smooth over $F$ of relative dimension $n_D$, and that its boundary $S \setminus j(X_F)$ is a strict normal crossings divisor. Section 12 uses this result in the proof of [the theorem on rigid charts from a separating blowup](/entry/rigid-boundary-charts-for-fan-of-unitary-separation/), where it gives condition 1 of Section 1. The proof is local. On the image of $X_F$ the stalks are regular because $X_F$ is smooth. At a boundary point the prechart of Section 12 gives a flat local map from the stalk to a power series ring, and regularity and the equation of the boundary descend along this map. We use the notation of Sections 1, 2 and 12. In particular, $(Z, i, \mathcal{O}_Z(1), N, d, J, \pi : S \to Z, j, B)$ are separating blowup data with $j$ separating for $\varphi$, $z_S : Z \to \operatorname{Spec} F$ is the structure map of $Z$, and $R = k[[u_1, \dots, u_m, v_1, \dots, v_e]]$ is a chart ring.

We first make the condition on the boundary precise. For a local ring $A$, a family $r_1, \dots, r_n$ in $A$ is a *regular system of parameters* if the $r_i$ generate the maximal ideal of $A$ and $n = \dim A$.

**Definition 7.15.1 (Strict normal crossings).** Let $Y$ be a scheme, $I$ a sheaf of ideals on $Y$ and $y \in Y$. Then $I$ has *strict normal crossings at $y$* if the stalk $\mathcal{O}_{Y,y}$ is a regular local ring and there are an affine open $U \ni y$, sections $f_1, \dots, f_n \in \Gamma(U, \mathcal{O}_Y)$ and a subset $T \subseteq \{1, \dots, n\}$ such that the germs of the $f_i$ at $y$ are a regular system of parameters of $\mathcal{O}_{Y,y}$ and $I(U) = (\prod_{i \in T} f_i)$. The ideal $I$ has *strict normal crossings* if it has strict normal crossings at each point of $Y$. A closed subset $B$ of $Y$ is a *strict normal crossings divisor* if its vanishing ideal has strict normal crossings.

If $I$ has strict normal crossings, then each stalk of $Y$ is regular. At a point $y$ outside the support of $I$ we can take $T$ empty, so that $I(U)$ is the unit ideal.

**Theorem 7.15.2 (Smoothness and normal crossings).** Let $E$, $H$ and $D$ be as in Section 1 with $p + q \ge 1$, and let $\varphi$ be a polarized fan with no self-intersections in rank at most $\min(p, q)$. Let $(Z, i, \mathcal{O}_Z(1), N, d, J, \pi : S \to Z, j, B)$ be separating blowup data with $j$ separating for $\varphi$, and suppose that each $x \in S$ with $x \notin j(X_F)$ has a prechart. Then:

1. $z_S \circ \pi : S \to \operatorname{Spec} F$ is smooth of relative dimension $n_D$;
2. the closed subset $S \setminus j(X_F)$ is a strict normal crossings divisor.

*Proof.* The first four steps prove item 1, and the last three steps prove item 2.

1. By item 5 of the separating blowup data, $S$ is projective over $F$. So $z_S \circ \pi$ is locally of finite type, and $S$ is locally Noetherian.
2. Let $x$ be a closed point of $S$. The residue field $\kappa(x)$ is finite over $F$, because $S$ is locally of finite type over $F$. So $\operatorname{trdeg}_F \kappa(x) = 0$.
3. We show that each stalk $\mathcal{O}_{S,x}$ is a regular local ring. Suppose first that $x = j(w)$ for a point $w$ of $X_F$. Since $\pi \circ j = i$ and $i$ lies over $F$, the composite $z_S \circ \pi \circ j$ is the structure map of $X_F$. This map is smooth of relative dimension $n_D$. So the stalk $\mathcal{O}_{X_F, w}$ is regular, because a smooth scheme over a field has regular stalks. The map $j$ is an open immersion, so $\mathcal{O}_{S,x}$ is isomorphic to $\mathcal{O}_{X_F, w}$ and is regular. Suppose now that $x \notin j(X_F)$, and take a prechart at $x$ with $\xi : \operatorname{Spec} R \to S$. The chart ring $R$ is a regular local ring. By item 2 of the definition of a prechart, the stalk map $\mathcal{O}_{S,x} \to R$ of $\xi$ is flat and local, it carries the maximal ideal of $\mathcal{O}_{S,x}$ onto a generating set of the maximal ideal of $R$, and the residue map is finite. A lemma on flat local maps of this type gives that $\mathcal{O}_{S,x}$ is regular and that $\dim \mathcal{O}_{S,x} = \dim R$.
4. A lemma on the separating blowup data gives $\dim \mathcal{O}_{S,x} + \operatorname{trdeg}_F \kappa(x) = n_D$ for each $x \in S$. At a closed point, step 2 gives $\dim \mathcal{O}_{S,x} = n_D$. The field $F$ has characteristic $0$, so it is perfect. A lemma on schemes locally of finite type over a perfect field gives that $z_S \circ \pi$ is smooth of relative dimension $n_D$ if each closed point has a regular stalk of dimension $n_D$. Steps 1, 3 and this formula give item 1.
5. Let $I$ be the vanishing ideal of $S \setminus j(X_F)$, and let $x \in S$. If $x \in j(X_F)$, then $x$ is outside the support of $I$. Choose a finite generating set $r_1, \dots, r_n$ of the maximal ideal of $\mathcal{O}_{S,x}$ of minimal size. Since $\mathcal{O}_{S,x}$ is regular by step 3, this is a regular system of parameters. With $T$ empty, $I$ has strict normal crossings at $x$.
6. Suppose that $x \notin j(X_F)$, and take a prechart $(k, m, e, C_1, \xi, a)$ at $x$, with stalk map $\psi : \mathcal{O}_{S,x} \to R$. Item 3 of the definition of a prechart gives $\psi(I_x) R = (\prod_i s^1_i)$, and $\xi(p) \in j(X_F)$ if and only if $\prod_i s^1_i \notin p$, for each point $p$ of $\operatorname{Spec} R$. The family $C_1$ has $m$ parameters and $s^1_i = u_i$, so $\prod_i s^1_i = u_1 \cdots u_m$. A lemma on boundary branches of toric charts gives that the primes $\psi^{-1}(u_i R)$ for $i = 1, \dots, m$ are distinct. This is the only step that uses $p + q \ge 1$, the two hypotheses on $\varphi$ and the separation of $j$.
7. A lemma on descent of normal crossings along $\psi$ now applies. Its inputs are the flat local map $\psi$ of step 3, the equation $\psi(I_x) R = (u_1 \cdots u_m)$ and the distinct primes of step 6. It gives a regular system of parameters $r_1, \dots, r_n$ of $\mathcal{O}_{S,x}$ and a subset $T$ with $I_x = (\prod_{i \in T} r_i)$. A last lemma takes this equation of stalks, together with the regularity of step 3, to an affine open $U \ni x$ and sections as in the definition. So $I$ has strict normal crossings at $x$.

Steps 5 to 7 cover all points of $S$, and this gives item 2. $\square$

Item 1 does not use the separation of $j$, the hypothesis $p + q \ge 1$ or the hypotheses on $\varphi$. Only the stalk maps of the precharts enter it.
