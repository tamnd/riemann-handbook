## The toroidal compactification and rigid charts

This section states the main theorem of the chapter: a unitary moduli scheme has a smooth projective toroidal model over each coefficient field, with fan charts at the boundary, an ample twist of the Hodge bundle and the logarithmic Kodaira-Spencer isomorphism. We define the data of the statement, and we prove the theorem from two inputs. The first input replaces the Kodaira-Spencer clause by *rigid* charts at the closed boundary points. The second input, the existence of a normalized blowup that separates points over a base field, is the subject of Sections 2 to 5. The theorem is an input to the covolume bound at [EveryCharacter from the literature](/step/every-character-of-literature/).

**Definition 7.1.1 (Host).** Let $E$ be a CM field with ring of integers $\mathcal{O}_E$. A *host* for $E$ consists of a prime $d$ with $d \equiv 7 \pmod{16}$, an element $\sqrt{-d} \in \mathcal{O}_E$ whose square is $-d$, an element $w_0 \in \mathcal{O}_E$ with $2 w_0 = 1 + \sqrt{-d}$, the ring automorphism $a \mapsto \bar a$ of $\mathcal{O}_E$ that is complex conjugation, and a complex number $\sqrt{-d}_0$ with square $-d$. In addition, $\mathcal{O}_E$ must be unramified over the ring of integers of the maximal real subfield $E^+$.

Lean: `ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.Geo.Tor.Host`.

**Definition 7.1.2 (Hermitian datum).** Let $H$ be a host for $E$. A *Hermitian datum* $D$ of signature $(p, q)$ consists of a finitely generated projective $\mathcal{O}_E$-module $\Lambda$ of rank $p + q$ and an alternating bilinear form $\langle \cdot, \cdot \rangle : \Lambda \times \Lambda \to \mathbb{Z}$ that is unimodular and satisfies $\langle a x, y \rangle = \langle x, \bar a y \rangle$ for $a \in \mathcal{O}_E$. We also ask for an $\mathbb{R}$-linear map $J$ on $\Lambda_{\mathbb{R}}$ with $J^2 = -1$, $\langle Jx, Jy \rangle = \langle x, y \rangle$ and $\langle Jx, x \rangle > 0$ for $x \neq 0$. For each $a \in \mathcal{O}_E$, with $A$ the action of $a$ on $\Lambda_{\mathbb{R}}$, the map $J$ commutes with $A$, $\operatorname{tr} A = 2 \operatorname{Re} t$ and $\operatorname{tr}(JA) = -2 \operatorname{Im} t$, where $t = \sum_\tau (p\, \tau(a) + q\, \overline{\tau(a)})$ and $\tau$ runs over the embeddings $E \to \mathbb{C}$ with $\tau(\sqrt{-d}) = \sqrt{-d}_0$. The *dimension* of $D$ is $n_D = \tfrac{[E:\mathbb{Q}]}{2}\, p q$.

Lean: `ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.Geo.Tor.Datum` (also `ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.Geo.Tor.Datum.dim`).

So $\Lambda$ is a unimodular Hermitian lattice ([lattices in Hermitian spaces](/learn/hermitian-lattices/#lattices-in-quadratic-and-hermitian-spaces)), and the trace conditions are the signature condition of PEL type.

**Definition 7.1.3 (Level).** A *level* $l$ for $H$ consists of an odd integer $M \ge 3$, an ideal $\mathfrak{M} \subseteq \mathcal{O}_E$ that is generated by its elements in $\mathbb{Z}[w_0]$, with $\mathfrak{M} \bar{\mathfrak{M}} = (M)$ and $\mathfrak{M} + \bar{\mathfrak{M}} = \mathcal{O}_E$, and an integer $A \ge 1$. We write $M' = \operatorname{lcm}(M, A)$ and $K_A = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-d}_0, \zeta_A) \subseteq \mathbb{C}$.

Lean: `ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.Geo.Tor.Level` (also `ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.Geo.Tor.Level.M'`, `ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.Geo.Tor.KA`).

**Definition 7.1.4 (Semi-abelian datum).** Let $S$ be a scheme. A *semi-abelian datum* $t$ on $S$ is a commutative semi-abelian group scheme $G \to S$ with an action of $\mathcal{O}_E$, a rigidified line bundle $L$ on $G$ with a cubical structure, such that $L$ is symmetric, $L$ is compatible with the action through $a \mapsto \bar a$, and $L$ is isomorphic to the cube combination of $1$ and $w_0$. A map of semi-abelian data over $f : S' \to S$ is a cartesian map of group schemes over $f$ that respects the action and the cubical structures.

Lean: `ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.Geo.Tor.SAB` (also `ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.Geo.Tor.SABMap`).

Over a field, the cubical structure makes $L$ a line bundle of the kind that [the theorem of the cube](/learn/abelian-varieties/#the-theorem-of-the-cube) controls. We write $\operatorname{ab}(G)$ for the set of points of $S$ where the fibre of $G$ is an abelian variety.

**Definition 7.1.5 (Moduli scheme, coefficient field, model).** A *moduli scheme* $X$ for $D$ and $l$ is a quasi-projective scheme over $K_A$, smooth of relative dimension $n_D$, with a universal object that represents the moduli problem of $D$ and $l$ on reduced schemes, and with no nonzero tangent vectors along which the object is constant. A *coefficient field* is a subfield $F \subseteq \mathbb{C}$ of finite degree that contains $\tau(E)$ for each $\tau$, the root of unity $\zeta_{M'}$ and $K_A$. We write $X_F = X \times_{K_A} F$. Let $\beta : O \to F$ be a ring map. A *model* of $X_F$ over $\beta$ is a normal scheme $Y$, projective and flat over $O$, with an open immersion $j : X_F \to Y \times_O F$ over $F$ with dense image, and a semi-abelian datum $t$ on $Y$ that restricts to the universal object on $X_F$. The preimage of $\operatorname{ab}(G)$ in $Y \times_O F$ must be the image of $j$.

Lean: `ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.Geo.Tor.Moduli`, `ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.Geo.Tor.Coeff`, `ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.Geo.Tor.Model`.

For a model $Y$ we write $Y_F = Y \times_O F$ and $\partial Y_F$ for the closed set $Y_F \setminus j(X_F)$, the *boundary*. In this chapter $\beta = \mathrm{id}_F$ in the main theorem, so $Y_F \cong Y$.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.Toroidal.gen` (also `GlobalTheory.Toroidal.genBd`, `GlobalTheory.Toroidal.genBdIdeal`).

**Definition 7.1.6 (Invariant function).** Let $O$ be a ring. An *invariant function* $\varphi$ on $O$ assigns a real number $\varphi(B)$ to each $n$ and each function $B : O^n \times O^n \to \mathbb{R}$, with $\varphi(B) = 0$ for $n = 0$ and $\varphi(B \circ (r \times r)) = \varphi(B)$ for each surjective $O$-linear map $r : O^n \to O^m$. So $\varphi$ gives a value $\varphi(\beta)$ for each form $\beta$ on a finitely generated projective $O$-module $X$, through any surjection $O^n \to X$.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.LocM.InvFun`.

Let $X$ be a finitely generated projective $\mathcal{O}_E$-module. A bilinear form $\beta : X \times X \to \mathbb{Z}$ is *balanced* if it is symmetric and $\beta(a y, z) = \beta(y, \bar a z)$. For $N \ge 1$, a *level form* of level $N$ is a balanced form with all values in $N\mathbb{Z}$.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.Toroidal.levelForms`.

**Definition 7.1.7 (Cone, polarized fan).** Let $\varphi$ be an invariant function on $\mathcal{O}_E$. A family $\gamma_1, \dots, \gamma_m$ of forms on $X$ is a *cone* of $\varphi$ if each $\gamma_i$ is balanced and positive semidefinite, the $\gamma_i$ are part of a basis of the balanced forms, $\varphi$ is linear on the cone they span, and a strictness condition holds. The function $\varphi$ is a *polarized fan* on $X$ if: $\varphi$ is homogeneous of degree one and superadditive on positive semidefinite balanced real forms; $\varphi(\beta)$ is an integer for each positive semidefinite balanced $\beta$, and it is positive when $\beta \neq 0$; each subfamily of a cone is a cone; each positive semidefinite form is a nonnegative real combination of the forms of one cone; and up to the action of $\operatorname{GL}_{\mathcal{O}_E}(X)$ there are finitely many forms in cones. We say $\varphi$ is a polarized fan *in rank at most $q$* if it is a polarized fan on each $X$ of rank at most $q$. It has *no self-intersections* in rank at most $q$ if, for each such $X$ and each cone $\gamma$, no automorphism of $X$ takes $\gamma_i$ to $\gamma_j$ with $i \neq j$.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.PolFan.IsCone`, `GlobalTheory.PolFan.IsPolFan` (also `GlobalTheory.PolFan.IsPolFanOn`, `GlobalTheory.PolFan.NoSelfInt`).

This is the data of a smooth admissible cone decomposition in [toroidal compactifications](/learn/abelian-varieties/#toroidal-and-minimal-compactifications), with $\varphi$ the polarization function that is linear on each cone.

**Definition 7.1.8 (Fan chart).** Let $Y$ be a model of $X_F$, $B$ an effective Cartier divisor on $Y$, $d \ge 1$ and $x \in \partial Y_F$. Let $k$ be a field and let $R = k[[u_1, \dots, u_m, v_1, \dots, v_e]]$ be the *chart ring*. A *fan chart* for $\varphi$, $B$ and $d$ at $x$ consists of a map $\xi : \operatorname{Spec} R \to Y_F$ that sends the closed point to $x$, a semi-abelian datum $t$ on $\operatorname{Spec} R$ with a map to the pullback of the datum of $Y$, a uniformization $P$ of $t$ by degeneration data with character lattice $X_P$, level forms $\gamma_1, \dots, \gamma_m$ of level $M'$ on $X_P$, forms $\delta_i$ and integers $a_i$, such that:

1. the stalk map of $\xi$ at $x$ is an étale completion, and it sends the ideal of $\partial Y_F$ to $(u_1 \cdots u_m)$;
2. a point of $\operatorname{Spec} R$ maps into $\partial Y_F$ if and only if it contains $u_1 \cdots u_m$;
3. the periods of $P$ are monomial in the $u_i$ with exponents $\gamma_i$;
4. $X_P$ has rank at most $\min(p, q)$, the $\delta_i$ form a cone of $\varphi$, and $\gamma_i = M' \delta_i$;
5. $a_i = d\, \varphi(\gamma_i)$, and the ideal of $B$ pulls back to $(\prod_i u_i^{a_i})$.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.MumfordChart.HasChartOf` (also `GlobalTheory.Toroidal.ChartRing`, `GlobalTheory.Toroidal.bdParam`).

So near $x$ the boundary is the union of the coordinate hyperplanes $u_i = 0$, the semi-abelian scheme comes from [Mumford's construction](/learn/abelian-varieties/#semi-abelian-degenerations-and-mumfords-construction) with periods $\prod u_i^{\gamma_i}$, and $B$ is the divisor of $d \varphi$ on the cone.

**Definition 7.1.9 (Rigid chart).** A *rigid chart* at $x$ is a chart with data $\xi, t, P, \gamma$ as in conditions 1 to 3, where the $\gamma_i$ are part of a basis, and with the following *rigidity*. Let $k'$ be a field, $\rho : R \to k'[\varepsilon]$ a ring map into the dual numbers with $\rho(\mathfrak{m}_R) \subseteq \varepsilon k'$ and $\rho(u_i) = 0$, and $a \in k'^m$. Suppose that the pullback of $(t, P)$ along $\rho$ is isomorphic to its pullback along the constant map $R \to k' \to k'[\varepsilon]$ that $\rho$ gives modulo $\varepsilon$, by an isomorphism that multiplies each period $\tau(y, z)$ by $1 + \varepsilon \sum_i a_i \gamma_i(y, z)$. Then $\rho$ is constant: the $\varepsilon$-part of $\rho(r)$ is zero for each $r \in R$.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.RigChart.HasRigidChart` (also `GlobalTheory.RigChart.IsRigid`, `GlobalTheory.RigChart.IsConstAlong`).

A rigid chart says that the only first-order deformations that fix the boundary and the abelian part are the log deformations of the torus. This is the injectivity half of the Kodaira-Spencer isomorphism along the boundary.

**Definition 7.1.10 (Logarithmic Kodaira-Spencer isomorphism).** Let $Y$ be a scheme over $F$ with a semi-abelian datum $t$ and an open set $V$. For an affine open $U$, let $\operatorname{Der}^{\log}(U)$ be the module of derivations of $\Gamma(U, \mathcal{O})$ that kill $F$ and preserve the ideal of $Y \setminus V$. The datum has the *logarithmic Kodaira-Spencer isomorphism* if for each $U$, each basis $v_1, \dots, v_n$ of the tangent vectors of $G$ along $U$, and the matrices $A(a)$ of the $\mathcal{O}_E$-action in this basis, there is a linear isomorphism $e$ from $\operatorname{Der}^{\log}(U)$ onto the *balanced matrices*, the symmetric $\Theta$ with $A(a)^{\mathsf T} \Theta = \Theta A(\bar a)$, such that $\Theta$ is the Kodaira-Spencer matrix of $D$ if and only if $\Theta = e(D)$.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.LogKS.HasLogKS` (also `GlobalTheory.LogKS.logDer`, `GlobalTheory.LogKS.balanced`).

This is the logarithmic form of [the Kodaira-Spencer map](/learn/abelian-varieties/#the-kodaira-spencer-map) for PEL type: the log tangent space is the space of balanced symmetric matrices, of dimension $\tfrac{[E:\mathbb{Q}]}{2} pq$. It is proved from rigid charts in [the log Kodaira-Spencer isomorphism from rigid charts](/entry/toroidal-fan-kodaira-spencer-of-rigid-boundary-charts/).

For a model $Y$, an effective Cartier divisor $B$ and $k \ge 1$, let $\omega_Y$ be the determinant of the dual of the Lie algebra of $G$, and let $\omega_Y^k(-B) = \omega_Y^{\otimes k} \otimes \mathcal{O}(B)^{\vee}$.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.TorRef.polOf` (also `GlobalTheory.FlagMod.detOm`).

**Theorem 7.1.11 (Rigid charts for a fan).** Let $E$, $H$ and $D$ be as above with $p + q \ge 1$. Let $\varphi$ be a polarized fan with no self-intersections in rank at most $\min(p, q)$. Then for each level $l$, moduli scheme $X$ and coefficient field $F$, there are a model $Y$ of $X_F$ over $\mathrm{id}_F$, an effective Cartier divisor $B$ on $Y$ and integers $d, k \ge 1$ with:

1. $Y_F$ is smooth over $F$ of relative dimension $n_D$, and $\partial Y_F$ is a strict normal crossings divisor;
2. the support of $B$ does not meet $\operatorname{ab}(G)$;
3. each point of $\partial Y_F$ has a fan chart for $\varphi$, $B$ and $d$;
4. if $\omega_Y$ is a line bundle, then $\omega_Y^k(-B)$ is ample;
5. each closed point of $\partial Y_F$ has a rigid chart.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.RigChart.rigidForFan_holds` (also `GlobalTheory.T.rigidBoundaryChartsForFan_unitaryDatum`, a second name; `GlobalTheory.RigChart.RigidForFan`, the statement).

*Proof.* This is [the theorem on rigid charts from a separating blowup](/entry/rigid-boundary-charts-for-fan-of-unitary-separation/) applied to [the separating normalized blowup](/entry/u-poin-st-x-sep/) of Section 2. $\square$

**Theorem 7.1.12 (Toroidal compactification for a fan).** Let $E$, $H$, $D$, $\varphi$, $l$, $X$ and $F$ be as in the theorem above. Then there are a model $Y$ of $X_F$ over $\mathrm{id}_F$, an effective Cartier divisor $B$ on $Y$ and integers $d, k \ge 1$ with conditions 1 to 4 of that theorem, and such that the datum of $Y$ has the logarithmic Kodaira-Spencer isomorphism along $j(X_F)$.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.TorKSField.toroidalForFanKS` (also `GlobalTheory.Published.ToroidalForFanKS`, the statement).

*Proof.* This is [the log Kodaira-Spencer isomorphism from rigid charts](/entry/toroidal-fan-kodaira-spencer-of-rigid-boundary-charts/) applied to the theorem on rigid charts for a fan. $\square$

The statement has the form of the existence theorem for [toroidal compactifications](/learn/abelian-varieties/#toroidal-and-minimal-compactifications) of Faltings and Chai (Theorem IV.5.7 of *Degeneration of abelian varieties*), and of its unitary form due to Lan (*Arithmetic compactifications of PEL-type Shimura varieties*, 2013). Here the base is the coefficient field $F$, of characteristic zero, so the statement is weaker than Lan's integral result.
