## Fourier systems and class sums

This section proves that the theta constants of a theta setup have integral class sums at all deeper levels at a point $z_1$ of its model. We collect a family of uniformizations at $z_1$ in a *raw Fourier system*, whose terms are the theta terms of the uniformizations. A translate of a theta constant by a label orthogonal to the dividing labels is a sum of twisted terms, and at a deeper level of a tower the twists cancel. The Galois group of the tower then acts on the class sums through the lattice. [The raw Fourier system on a chart](/entry/exists-v-pre-traits-on-chart/) of Section 6 uses both results. The setting is close to that of Section 3, but here the evaluations take values in complete local domains and not in $\bar{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell$, and the theta setup need not map to $X_F$. We use the notation of Sections 2 and 3.

**Definition 7.5.1 (Raw Fourier system).** Let $A$ be a local ring. A *raw Fourier system* $\Phi$ over $A$ consists of a set $Y$, a set $Q$ of labels and, for each $y \in Y$:

1. a complete noetherian normal local domain $V_y$ of characteristic $0$ with a local map $\mathrm{ev}_y : A \to V_y$;
2. a finitely generated projective $\mathcal{O}_E$-module $X_y$ of rank at most $\min(p, q)$ that is free of finite rank over $\mathbb{Z}$;
3. nonzero elements $t_{y,1}, \dots, t_{y,m_y}$ of the maximal ideal, and balanced forms $\delta_{y,k}$ on $X_y$ with $\delta_{y,k}(x, x) \ge 0$, such that for each $x \ne 0$ some $\delta_{y,k}(x, x) > 0$;
4. coefficients $u_y(\mu, c) \in V_y$ for $\mu \in X_y$ and $c \in Q$, such that for each $\mu$ some $u_y(\mu, c)$ is a unit.

The *term* is $\mathrm{term}_y(\mu, c) = u_y(\mu, c) \prod_k t_{y,k}^{\delta_{y,k}(\mu, \mu)}$. Let $\iota : V_y \to V'$ be a map to a local ring. An element $x \in V'$ is a *class sum of level $N$* at $(\kappa, c)$ if for each $j$ there is a finite set $S$ of $\mu \equiv \kappa \pmod N$ with $\sum_k \delta_{y,k}(\mu, \mu) \ge j$ for each other $\mu \equiv \kappa$, and $x - \iota(\sum_{\mu \in S} \mathrm{term}_y(\mu, c)) \in \mathfrak{m}_{V'}^j$. $\Phi$ is *exact at level $N_1$* if each class sum of level $N_1$ in $V_y$ is $\mathrm{ev}_y(h)$ for some $h \in A$. Let $A$ be a domain. $\Phi$ has *class sums at level $N_1$* if for each $y_0$ and $N_0$ there are:

1. $N \ge N_0$ with $N_1 \mid N$, and maps $k_y : X_{y_0} \to X_y$, unimodular modulo $N$ as in Section 3, with $k_{y_0} = \mathrm{id}$;
2. a finite Galois extension $L$ of $\operatorname{Frac} A$, a maximal ideal $\mathfrak{z}$ of the integral closure $B$ of $A$ in $L$, and $f(\kappa, c) \in B$ that depends only on $\kappa$ modulo $N$;
3. for each $y$, a finite local map $\iota_y : V_y \to V'_y$ with $\iota_y^{-1}(I V'_y) = I$ for each ideal $I$, and a map $\mathrm{ev}'_y : B \to V'_y$ that extends $\iota_y \circ \mathrm{ev}_y$ with $\mathrm{ev}_y'^{-1}(\mathfrak{m}) = \mathfrak{z}$, such that $\mathrm{ev}'_y(f(\kappa, c))$ is a class sum of level $N$ at $(k_y \kappa, c)$;
4. for each $\sigma \in \operatorname{Gal}(L / \operatorname{Frac} A)$ that preserves $\mathfrak{z}$, a $\mathbb{Z}$-linear $\gamma$ on $X_{y_0}$ that is $\mathcal{O}_E$-linear modulo $N$, with $N \mid \det \gamma - 1$, $\gamma \equiv 1 \pmod {N_1}$ and $\sigma f(\kappa, c) = f(\gamma \kappa, c)$.

**Definition 7.5.2 (General theta setup, tower).** A *general theta setup* $X_1$ has the data 1, 2 and 4 of a theta setup in Section 3, without the map to $X_F$ and the map to $Z_C$. Its labels are $\Gamma = (\mathbb{Z}/L)^{2g}$ with the standard pairing $e$, and $\zeta_S$ is its root of unity. A label $x_0$ is *dividing* at $z_1 \in Z_M$ if $\theta_{x_0} \ne 0$ and $\theta_a / \theta_{x_0} \in \mathcal{O}_{Z_M, z_1}$ for all $a$. We let $H(z_1)$ be the group generated by the dividing labels and $H(z_1)^\perp$ its orthogonal. A *member* of $X_1$ at $z_1$ over a local domain $V$ is a uniformization of the abelian variety of $X_1$ over $V$ by degeneration data, with a lattice $X$ and a relation "$a$ corresponds to $\mu$" between labels and points of $X$, and with *theta terms* $T(\mu, c)$ for $x_0$ and labels $\mathrm{lab}(c)$. A *tower* over $X_1$ is a general theta setup $X_2$ of level $L_2 = d L_1$ with $\Omega_2$ finite Galois over $\Omega_1$, a finite map $Z_{M_2} \to Z_{M_1}$ over $\Omega_1 \to \Omega_2$, a map $\lambda$ of labels and an action of $\operatorname{Gal}(\Omega_2 / \Omega_1)$ on labels that fixes the image of $\lambda$. $X_1$ has *deep towers* if it has towers of each level $L_2 \ge N_0$.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.GSep.GSet` (also `GlobalTheory.GSep.GMem`, `GlobalTheory.GSep.GMem.Terms`, `GlobalTheory.GSep.Tower`, `GlobalTheory.GSep.Deep`, `GlobalTheory.GSep.IsGeo`).

We say that $\Phi$ is *geometric* for $x_0$ and $\mathrm{lab} : Q \to \Gamma$ if $x_0$ is dividing at $z_1$, $\mathrm{lab}$ has image $H(z_1)$, and each $y$ carries a member over $V_y$ with lattice $X_y$ whose theta terms are the terms of $\Phi$.

**Theorem 7.5.3 (Twisted theta sums).** Let $X_1$ be a general theta setup and $x_0$ a dividing label at $z_1$. Then there are $s = \pm 1$ and $\varepsilon(a, \tau) \in \mathbb{N}$ with the following property. Let $T$ be theta terms of a member at $z_1$ over $V$ via $\mathrm{ev}$, with labels in $H(z_1)$. Let $c$ be a label, $\tau \in H(z_1)^\perp$, and $z, h \in \mathcal{O}_{Z_M, z_1}$ with $z = \zeta_S^{L}$ and $h = \zeta_S^{\varepsilon(\mathrm{lab}(c), \tau)} \theta_{\mathrm{lab}(c) + \tau} / \theta_{x_0}$. Let $w(\mu) = s \cdot e(\tau, a)$ whenever $a$ corresponds to $\mu$. Then $\sum_\mu \mathrm{ev}(z)^{w(\mu)} T(\mu, c)$ converges to $\mathrm{ev}(h)$: the terms and $\mathrm{ev}(h)$ lie in a finitely generated submodule $N$, and for each $n$ all large finite partial sums lie in $\mathrm{ev}(h) + \mathfrak{m}_V^n N$.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.GSep.twistSum_translate` (also `GlobalTheory.T.thetaConstants_translate_eq_twisted_sums`, a second name; `GlobalTheory.GSep.TwistSum` and `GlobalTheory.UT.SumI`, the conditions).

*Proof.* A lemma gives one sign $s$ such that for each member, $c$ and $\tau$ the statement holds with some exponent $e$ in place of $\varepsilon$. Call $(a, \tau, e)$ *witnessed* if some member and $c$ with $\mathrm{lab}(c) = a$ satisfy the statement with $e$, and $\theta_{a + \tau} / \theta_{x_0}$ is a germ with nonzero value. Let $\varepsilon(a, \tau)$ be such an $e$ if one exists, and $0$ if not. Take a member, $c$ and $\tau$, and the exponent $e$ of the lemma. If a witness exists, a second lemma proves that its exponent works for every member, because the witness has a nonzero value. If not, then $\mathrm{ev}(h) = 0$, since otherwise our member would be a witness with $e$. Take $s' \in \mathcal{O}_{Z_M, z_1}$ with image $\zeta_S^{e}$. The statement for $e$ with $s' h$ in place of $h$ gives convergence to $\mathrm{ev}(s' h) = 0 = \mathrm{ev}(h)$. $\square$

**Theorem 7.5.4 (Class sums at deeper levels).** Let $X_1$ be a general theta setup of level $L_1$ with an action of a ring by endomorphisms and deep towers, and $z_1 \in Z_M$ such that $A = \mathcal{O}_{Z_M, z_1}$ is a domain in which nonzero integers are units. Let $\Phi$ be a raw Fourier system over $A$ that is geometric for $x_0$ and $\mathrm{lab}$. Then $\Phi$ has class sums at level $L_1$.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.GSep.DeepSums.classSums_deeper` (also `GlobalTheory.T.fourierTermSystem_class_sums_unbounded_levels_one_index`, a second name).

*Proof.* Fix $y_0$ and $N_0$, and take a tower $X_2$ with $L_2 \ge N_0$. Let $N = L_2$, so $L_1 \mid N$, and let $L = \Omega_2$, a finite Galois extension of $\operatorname{Frac} A$. A lemma gives $z_2$ over $z_1$, an injective map $j_2 : B \to \mathcal{O}_{z_2}$, the maximal ideal $\mathfrak{z} = j_2^{-1}(\mathfrak{m})$, lifts $\sigma_2$ to $\mathcal{O}_{z_2}$ of the $\sigma$ that preserve $\mathfrak{z}$, and the fact that germs integral at all points over $z_1$ lie in $B$. The label $\lambda(x_0)$ is dividing over $z_1$ and $\lambda(\mathrm{lab}(c)) \in H(z_2)$. Then a lemma gives functions $f_2(a, b)$, integral over $z_1$ and periodic in $a$ modulo the labels of class zero, whose values are the twisted sums of the previous theorem for each member at $z_2$. A base change lemma gives exponents $\varepsilon$ and members of $X_2$ with terms $\zeta_2^{\varepsilon} \cdot \mathrm{term}$. Let $M = L_2^2$ and $F(a, c) = \zeta_2^{(M - 1)\varepsilon(\mathrm{lab}(c))} f_2(a, \lambda(\mathrm{lab}(c)))$, which lies in $B$.

For each $y$, a lemma gives $\iota_y : V_y \to V'_y$ and $\mathrm{ev}_2 : \mathcal{O}_{z_2} \to V'_y$ compatible with $\mathrm{ev}_y$, and the base change of the member. A lemma gives isomorphisms $k_y : X_{y_0} \to X_y$ that respect the correspondence with labels, with $k_{y_0} = \mathrm{id}$. Each $k_y$ is unimodular, with $\varepsilon = k_y^{-1}$. Since labels map onto $X_{y_0}$ modulo $L_2$, each $\kappa$ has a label $a_\kappa$, and $f(\kappa, c) = F(a_\kappa, c)$ depends only on $\kappa$ modulo $L_2$. The value of $F$ is a class sum, because $\zeta_2^{(M-1)\varepsilon} \zeta_2^{\varepsilon} = 1$ cancels the twist.

Let $\sigma$ preserve $\mathfrak{z}$. The member at $y_0$ and its twist by $\sigma_2$ give an isomorphism $\gamma$ of $X_{y_0}$ that moves labels by the action of $\sigma$. If $a'$ corresponds to $\kappa$, then $\lambda(a')$ corresponds to $d\kappa$ and is fixed by $\sigma$, so $\gamma \equiv 1 \pmod {L_1}$. $L_1$ is odd and at least $2$, so $L_1 \ge 3$, and then $\det \gamma = 1$. Finally, $\sigma F(a, c) = F(\sigma a, c)$: we evaluate both sides at a member with injective evaluation, and the two sums have the same limit. $\square$
