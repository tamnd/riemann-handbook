This chapter proves the existence of toroidal compactifications of unitary moduli schemes, with the logarithmic Kodaira-Spencer isomorphism, in the form of Faltings, Chai and Lan. Take a CM field $E$, a Hermitian datum of signature $(p, q)$ with $p + q \ge 1$, a level, and a smooth polarized fan $\varphi$ with no self-intersections in rank at most $\min(p, q)$. Then over each coefficient field $F$ there is a normal projective model $Y$ of the moduli scheme $X_F$ with a boundary divisor $B$, such that $Y$ is smooth with a strict normal crossings boundary, every boundary point has a chart cut out by the fan, a twist of a power of the Hodge bundle by $-B$ is ample, and the Kodaira-Spencer map identifies the logarithmic derivations with the balanced matrices ([toroidal compactification for a fan](/entry/toroidal-for-fan-ks/)). The proof first reduces the theorem to charts that are *rigid* under first-order deformations. Then it builds the compactification as a normalized blowup of a minimal compactification along a boundary ideal, and it proves that the blowup separates points over a base field. The boundary ideal comes from approximations of Poincaré series by theta functions at $p$-adic points, and the separation comes from Fourier expansions of theta constants at boundary points of deeper level.
