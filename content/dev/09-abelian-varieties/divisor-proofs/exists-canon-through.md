From a degenerating monomial family $C$ over a complete local domain $V$, this theorem builds a family $C_1$ over a power series ring $R$ out of data for a smaller datum, and a local map $\theta \colon R \to V$ along which $C_1$ pulls back to $C$. It proves [the statement form](/entry/exists-canonical-family-through-degenerating-family/), which is step 1 of [`exists_taut_through`](/entry/exists-taut-through/).

## Statement

Use monomial families and admissible bases as in [`exists_taut_through`](/entry/exists-taut-through/). A monomial family has a degeneration datum with character group $X_C$, parameters $s_1, \dots, s_m$ and balanced forms $\delta_1, \dots, \delta_m$ on $X_C$.

**Lower bases.** A `LowBase` $L$ for a datum $D'$ is a regular local ring $W$, essentially of finite type over $F$, with an object of the moduli problem of $D'$ over $W$ and a degeneration datum $L.D$ over $W$ with that abelian part. Other fields fix a dimension and ask that maps out of $W$ be fixed by the data.

**`DivFrom`.** `DivFrom L Q ι t ε e` says: after a rescaling by units $g(y)$ of $\operatorname{Frac} V$ with $g(y)^{-1} = \prod_k t_k^{M' \varepsilon_k(e y,\, w_0 e y)}$ (`PolDeg.rescale`, not unfolded), the degeneration datum $Q$ over $V$ is the pullback along $\iota \colon W \to V$ of $L.D$ with $c(y), \psi(y)$ replaced by $c(M'y), \psi(M'y)$, compatibly with $e$ and the $\mathcal{O}_E$-actions.

**`Canon C₁`** is a datum $D'$ with $p' + r = p$, $q' + r = q$ ($r$ the rank of $X_{C_1}$); a lower base $L$ for $D'$; a $\mathbb{Z}$-basis $(\varepsilon_k)$ of the balanced forms on $X_L$, indexed by $m + N$ letters, with $e^*\varepsilon_i = \delta_i$ for $i \leq m$; and a map $\psi \colon W[x_1, \dots, x_m, z_1, \dots, z_N] \to R$ with $\psi(x_i) = s_i$, each $\psi(z_k)$ a unit, `IsComplAt ψ`, $\psi|_W$ local and compatible with $F$, and `DivFrom` for the values $\psi(x_i), \psi(z_k)$. `IsComplAt ψ` asks that $R$ be Noetherian and complete, $\psi$ flat, $\mathfrak{m}_R$ generated by the image of its contraction, and each residue class a fraction of images.

**`Through C C₁ θ`**: with $R_s = R[1/\prod_i s_i]$, there are $y_o \colon \operatorname{Spec} R_s \to X^\circ_F$ that restricts to $y_{C_1}$ at the generic point, and $\kappa \colon \operatorname{Spec}\operatorname{Frac} V \to \operatorname{Spec} R_s$ over $\operatorname{Spec}\theta$ with $y_o \circ \kappa = y_C$.

**Theorem.** Let $V$ be an admissible base with algebraically closed residue field, and $C$ a monomial family over $V$ with $X_C \neq 0$. Then there are $k, m, e$, a family $C_1$ over $R = k[[s_1, \dots, s_m, u_1, \dots, u_e]]$ with parameters $s_1, \dots, s_m$ and a nonempty `Canon C₁`, and a local $\theta \colon R \to V$ with `Through C C₁ θ`.

## Proof

1. [`degeneratingFamily_data_from_base_algClosed_residue`](/entry/degenerating-family-data-from-base-alg-closed-residue/) gives $D'$, $L$, a local $\theta_0 \colon W \to V$, a basis $\varepsilon$, $e$ and elements $t_k$ of $V$ (the first $m$ associate to the parameters of $C$, the rest units) with `DivFrom` over $V$.
2. `exists_toricChartRing_factorisation_localHom` factors $x_k, z_k \mapsto t_k$ as $\theta \circ \psi$, with $\psi$ as in `Canon` and $\theta$ local.
3. $R$ is an admissible base, and $W \to R$ is injective as $\psi$ is flat. For $i \leq m$, $\varepsilon_i(y, y) \geq 0$ (cone condition) and $\sum_i \varepsilon_i(y, y) > 0$ for $y \neq 0$ (monomial property of $C$).
4. `mumford_family_from_regular_base_coordinates` gives a semi-abelian scheme over $R$ with uniformization, monomial in the $\psi(x_i)$, with `DivFrom` over $R$.
5. `classified_on_open_of_specialises_to_classified` gives its generic point $y_{C_1}$ and the data $y_o, \kappa$ of `Through`.
