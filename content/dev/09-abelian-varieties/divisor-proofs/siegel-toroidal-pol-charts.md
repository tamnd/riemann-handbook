This theorem builds a smooth projective toroidal compactification of the Siegel moduli space of odd level, with polarized charts, for each polarization function $\varphi$ with no self-intersections. It is the real content below the wrapper [`siegelToroidalCompactification_polarisedCharts`](/entry/siegel-toroidal-compactification-polarised-charts/), and so below [`siegelToroidalIntegral_holds`](/entry/siegel-toroidal-integral-holds/).

## Statement

Let $\Lambda_n = \mathbb{Z}[\zeta_n, 1/n]$. Siegel families (`SFam`) and polarization functions (`IsPolFan`) are as in the [volume 8 entry](/entry/siegel-toroidal-integral-of-literature/). The predicate `NoSelfInt` says: no automorphism of the lattice takes one ray of a cone of $\varphi$ to another ray of the same cone.

**Theorem.** Let $g \ge 1$, let $\varphi$ be a polarization function for $g$ with no self-intersections, and let $n \ge 3$ be odd. Then there are a Siegel family $\mathcal{A}$ over a base $X$, a scheme $s \colon \overline{S} \to \operatorname{Spec} \Lambda_n$, an open immersion $j \colon X \to \overline{S}$ over $\Lambda_n$, a group scheme $G$ over $\overline{S}$, an effective Cartier divisor $B$ and integers $d, N \ge 1$ with:

1. $s$ is projective and smooth of relative dimension $g(g+1)/2$, and each point $x$ is in the closure of $j(X) \cap s^{-1}(s(x))$;
2. $G$ is semi-abelian and smooth of relative dimension $g$, it restricts to the universal abelian scheme on $X$, and its abelian locus is $j(X)$;
3. each point $x$ has a polarized chart (`HasPolChart`);
4. $\omega^N$ is generated by global sections and $\omega^{Nk} \otimes \mathcal{O}(-B)$ is ample for some $k$, where $\omega$ is the Hodge line bundle of $G$.

A *polarized chart* at $x$ is a complete regular local domain $R$ of characteristic zero with an étale completion $\xi \colon \operatorname{Spec} R \to \overline{S}$ at $x$ (`IsEtaleCompletion`) and relative parameters $t_1, \dots, t_m$, such that:

- the boundary pulls back to $(t_1 \cdots t_m)$;
- degeneration data $Q$ over $R$ uniformize $G$ (`IsMumfordOf`), with period the monomial $\prod_i t_i^{n\delta_i}$ for a cone $\delta$ of $\varphi$ (`IsMonomialTau`);
- $B$ pulls back to $\bigl(\prod_i t_i^{a_i}\bigr)$ with $a_i = d\,\varphi(n\delta_i)$.

## Proof

The theorem applies `siegelToroidalPolCharts_of_steps5` to five statements.

1. **A Siegel family**, from `exists_siegelFamily_cyclotomicAway_odd_level`.
2. **The minimal compactification** ($\mathrm{MinSt5}$, from `minimalSiegelCompactification_exists`): a normal projective $Z$ with a dense open immersion $i \colon X \to Z$ (`IsThetaCompact`), an ample $\mathcal{O}_Z(1)$, and a cover of $Z$ on which a power of the Hodge bundle is the pullback of $\mathcal{O}_Z(1)$.
3. **The blowup** ($\mathrm{PoinSt}$, from `normalisedBlowup_ampleDivisor_compactification`): $d \ge 1$ and an ideal $J$ on $Z$ off $i(X)$ with valuations $d\,\varphi(\beta)$ along degenerations of period $\varpi^\beta$ (`ValOn`). A normalized blowup $\pi \colon \overline{S} \to Z$ of $J$ is projective, with $B$ the divisor of $\pi^{-1}J$ and $\pi^*\mathcal{O}_Z(k) \otimes \mathcal{O}(-B)$ ample for large $k$.
4. **Smoothness and pre-charts** ($\mathrm{ChartSt5}$, from [`minimalBlowup_smooth_with_semiAbelianCharts`](/entry/minimal-blowup-smooth-with-semi-abelian-charts/)). A pre-chart is a chart with a local semi-abelian scheme over $R$ in place of $G$.
5. **Descent** ($\mathrm{DescSt5}$, from `SiegDescent.descSt5`): the pre-charts give $G$ by `semiAbelian_descent_from_complete_regular_charts`.

Then `hodgeBundle_power_iso_minimalSiegelCompactification` gives $\omega^N \cong \pi^*\mathcal{O}_Z(a)$, and `exists_globallyGenerated_ample_power_toroidalPullback` gives item 4. From each pre-chart, `mem_closure_moduliFibre_of_monomialPeriodPreChart` gives the density in fibres and `hasPolarisedChart_of_monomialPreChart_semiAbelianExt` gives a polarized chart.

This route has the shape of the construction in Faltings and Chai, *Degeneration of Abelian Varieties* (1990). We have not compared the Lean statements with the book.
