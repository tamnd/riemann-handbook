This theorem compares two cohomology groups of the divided power de Rham complex in degrees 1 and 2: one over all nilpotent divided power thickenings of $X_0$, and one over the affine opens of a smooth lift $X$. Its statement form is [`lift_restriction_bijective_low_degrees`](/entry/lift-restriction-bijective-low-degrees/), and [`rel_total_zero`](/entry/rel-total-zero/) uses it to relate crystalline and Čech-de Rham classes. It is a step towards the comparison of crystalline and de Rham cohomology of a smooth lift (Berthelot and Ogus, *Notes on crystalline cohomology*, 1978, §7).

## Statement

Let $D$ be the data `Data`: a ring $R$, an ideal $I$ with nilpotent divided powers $\kappa$, a smooth, separated, quasi-compact scheme $X$ over $R$, and $X_0 = X \times_R R/I$ with its closed immersion $i \colon X_0 \to X$.

Let $\mathrm{Thick}$ be the category of nilpotent divided power thickenings of $X_0$ over $(R, I, \kappa)$. An object is an $R$-algebra $B$, an ideal $J$ with nilpotent divided powers $\delta$ compatible with $\kappa$, and a map $\operatorname{Spec}(B/J) \to X_0$ over $R$. Morphisms are $R$-algebra maps $B \to B'$ that respect this data.

Let $K$ be the complex of functors (`KK`) $B \mapsto \bigl(B \to \Omega^1_{B/R}/N^1 \to \Omega^2_{B/R}/N^2 \to \Omega^3_{B/R}/N^3\bigr)$, where $N$ is the ideal of the de Rham algebra generated by $d\gamma_{n+1}(a) - \gamma_n(a)\, da$ for $a \in J$. For a category $C$ and such a complex, $H^1(C, K)$ and $H^2(C, K)$ are the cohomology of the total complex of nerve cochains: degree 1 is $C^1(K^0) \oplus C^0(K^1)$ and degree 2 is $C^2(K^0) \oplus C^1(K^1) \oplus C^0(K^2)$.

A lift $\Lambda$ of $D$ (`Lift D`) is a functor $L$ from the affine opens of $X$ to $\mathrm{Thick}$, with natural isomorphisms $\mathcal{O}_X(U) \cong B_{L(U)}$ of $R$-algebras, ideal $I \cdot B_{L(U)}$, and the evident point in $U$.

**Theorem.** For all $D$ and $\Lambda$, restriction along $L$ gives bijections $H^j(\mathrm{Thick}, K) \to H^j(\mathrm{AffOp}(X), K \circ L)$ for $j = 1, 2$.

## Proof

Let $E$ be the category of pairs $(T, U)$ with $T$ a thickening and $U$ an affine open of $X$ that contains the image of $T$. Then $L$ factors as $L = p \circ \ell$, with $p(T, U) = T$ and $\ell(U) = (L(U), U)$. By `resMap_comp_bij12` it is enough to treat $p$ and $\ell$.

1. **Along $p$.** `fnMap_pullbackBijective12_kk_and_res` says that forgetting the forms, $K \to \mathcal{O}$, is bijective on $H^1$ and $H^2$, both on $\mathrm{Thick}$ and on $E$. `pullbackBijective12_resMap_of_affine_inter` says that restriction along $p$ is bijective on $H^1$ and $H^2$ with coefficients $\mathcal{O}$. It needs that intersections of affine opens of $X$ are affine (`Data.smooth_mono_inf`). The two maps commute, so restriction along $p$ is bijective for $K$ (`resMap_bij12_of_fnMap`).
2. **Along $\ell$** (`Lift.resMap_toE_pullbackBijectiveDegreesOneTwo`). Filter $E$ by the full subcategories $E_N$ of thickenings whose divided power filtration vanishes at step $N$. These increase and exhaust $E$, since each $\delta$ is nilpotent. By `hom_spec_points_levels` there is $N_0$ such that every $L(U)$ lies in $E_N$ for $N \geq N_0$. For such $N$, `crystallineForms_restriction_affineLift_bijective` gives bijections in degrees 0, 1 and 2 for the restriction from $E_N$. The lemmas `h1_resMap_bijective_of_exhausting_properties` and `bijective_restriction_fourTermComplex_of_exhaustion` pass from the $E_N$ to $E$ in degrees 1 and 2.

The rest is bookkeeping.
