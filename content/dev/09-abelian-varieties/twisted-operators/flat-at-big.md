This theorem proves the fact `FlatAt` for all large admissible choices. [The unfolding of the iterated theta lift](/entry/twisted-theta-integral-unfolding/) uses it to get the statements `SuppOn` and `ValAt` of the row test. The theorem joins three facts, and the work is in the first: [the flat dictionary relative to base vacua](/entry/flat-dictionary-base-vacua-large-depth/).

## Statement

**Theorem.** For all large admissible choices $\mathrm{ch}$ (in the sense of `Big`, as in [the parent entry](/entry/twisted-theta-integral-unfolding/)) there is a sign $s \in \{\pm 1\}$ with $\mathrm{FlatAt}(\mathrm{ch}, s)$.

$\mathrm{FlatAt}(\mathrm{ch}, s)$ says this. There are units $z_t$ in the subgroup of $\mathbb{C}^\times$ generated by the local values of the character $\xi_t$ with this property. For each polynomial datum $P$ on the descending path and each real point $p$, there is a real family $C$ (for each even $j < m$, $C_j$ is a smooth bi-vacuum for the lattices $\Lambda_j, \Lambda_{j+1}, \Lambda_{j+2}$ of the top row). For each finite flip expansion $\tau$, volume $M$, coset family $\beta$ with representative $b$ for the sign $s$, and expansion $\tau'$ of the term at $\beta$ with combination data $c$, there is $\zeta \in \mathbb{C}$ with

$$\sum_d \tau_d\, \mathrm{ev}\bigl(\mathrm{con}_M(\mathrm{fock}(d_2, P), p\,b)\bigr)(d_1) = \zeta \sum_d \tau'_d\, \mathrm{ev}\bigl(\mathrm{comb}_{c(d)}(C)\bigr)\bigl(\mathrm{comb}_{c(d)}\bigr),$$

as functions of $(u, y)$, and $\zeta = z_t^{-1}$ when $\beta$ is a staircase coset at the places of $W_0$. So the unfolded integrand at $b$ is a constant times a combination of one real family $C$.

## Proof

Take the sign $s$ and $(\kappa, z^{(1)})$ from `LocAt` ([`flat_dictionary_base_vacua_large_depth`](/entry/flat-dictionary-base-vacua-large-depth/)). `LocAt` gives the same identity, but with the family $\mathrm{con}_M(\mathrm{fock}(v, P), p\,b_0)$ at a base point $b_0$ in place of $C$, and with $\zeta = \kappa (z^{(1)}_t)^{-1}$ on staircase cosets. `VacAt` (`unfolded_vacua_real_up_to_twist_large_depth`) gives a base point $b_0$ for each $t$ and says that the family at $b_0$ is $z^{(2)}_{t,j} C^0_j$ for even $j$, with $C^0$ real. Put $C_j = \kappa C^0_j$ for $j = 0$ and $C_j = C^0_j$ otherwise, and $z_t = z^{(1)}_t \bigl(\prod_{k < m/2} z^{(2)}_{t,2k}\bigr)^{-1}$. The scalars come out of the combination by `evT_combC_smul`.

If $\beta$ is not a staircase coset, `OneAt` (`rowTest_support_families_staircase_cosets`) says that $\beta$ is outside the support. Then `isExpansion_zero` says that $\tau' = 0$ is also an expansion at $\beta$, and `LocAt` with $\tau' = 0$ makes the left side zero. Take $\zeta = 0$.

## In the Lean code

The theorem is in [IsExpansionZero.lean](lean:Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/TwistedOperators/IsExpansionZero.lean#L87). It is `big_mono` applied to `flatAt_of` ([L64](lean:Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/TwistedOperators/IsExpansionZero.lean#L64)) and to the `Big.and` of the three facts. The definitions are in [IsRealFam.lean](lean:Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/TwistedInnerProducts/IsRealFam.lean#L267).

- We have not unfolded `evT`, `conAt`, `fockD` or `biComb`.
- The file is named after the helper `isExpansion_zero`, not after the theorem.
- `FlatAt` fixes $\zeta$ only on staircase cosets. `OneAt` is used only for the other cosets, where the left side vanishes.
- The sign comes from `LocAt`; its proof always gives $s = -1$ (see [`exists_locAt_big`](/entry/exists-loc-at-big/)).
