This theorem bounds the sum of the weights of a finite family of lattices ("terms") that share their definite part and are pairwise inequivalent under a unipotent group $R$. Its statement form is [`sum_weights_inequivalent_hermFrameTerms_le`](/entry/sum-weights-inequivalent-herm-frame-terms-le/), which goes to [`PerDat.one_class`](/entry/one-class/). The entries below use its notation.

## Statement

Let $K$ satisfy `IsCMUnram`. Let $(V, H)$ be a nondegenerate Hermitian space over $K$ of finite dimension, also an $\mathcal{O}_K$-module. Fix $r$ and a *normal form* $N$ (`NF H r`). It consists of two flags $F, F'$, each a list $e_0, \dots, e_{r-1}$ of independent isotropic vectors that are pairwise orthogonal, and a vector $z = e_r$ orthogonal to them with $H(z, z) = -T$, $T \in \mathbb{N}$. It also has an index $j \leq r$ from which the two flags agree up to a scalar of norm 1, conditions on the matrix $H(e_a, e'_b)$ for $a, b < j$, and a nonzero ideal $\mathfrak{d}$ stable under the involution.

The objects attached to $N$:

- $R$, the group generated by the isometries that are unipotent for both flags (`FlagD.InN`).
- A *term of level* $g$ (`IsTerm`): a unimodular $\mathcal{O}_K$-lattice $\Lambda$ with fixed leading ideals along both flags and a congruence condition modulo $g\Lambda$.
- Lattices made from $\Lambda$: $L(\Lambda)$ (`latL F F'`), $L'(\Lambda)$ (`latL F' F`), the definite parts $L_W(\Lambda)$ and $L'_W(\Lambda)$ (`flagPairDefinitePartLattice`), a graph $\Gamma_g(\Lambda)$ (`iotaGraph`) and its fixed lattice $L_g(\Lambda)$ (`latLa`). The index $g_I(\Lambda) = [L_W(\Lambda) : L_g(\Lambda)]$.
- The weight $\omega(\Lambda)$ (`NF.om`), a ratio of indices of the stabilizer of $\Lambda$ in $R$ and the stabilizer of a reference lattice.
- Real numbers: $\Delta$ (`Del`), a normalized norm of the $j \times j$ minor of $H(e_a, e'_b)$; $\Delta_b$ (`Db`), the product of the nonzero normalized minors of sizes $1$ to $j$; $N_T$ (`NT`), a power of $T$; $c_K$ (`cf K`); and $k = \dim W_t$ for a subspace $W_t$ (`Wt`).

**Theorem.** Let $g \geq 1$ and let $\Lambda_0$ be a term of level $g$. Let $(\Lambda_i)_{i \in s}$ be a finite family of terms of level $g$ with $L(\Lambda_i) = L(\Lambda_0)$, $L'_W(\Lambda_i) = L'_W(\Lambda_0)$ and $\Gamma_g(\Lambda_i) = \Gamma_g(\Lambda_0)$, no two of which are in one $R$-orbit. Then
$$\sum_{i \in s} \omega(\Lambda_i) \leq N_T^2 \Bigl(\frac{\Delta}{g_I(\Lambda_0)}\Bigr)^{k/2} \Delta^{c_K + 2} (\Delta_b \Delta N_T)^{10000 (r+1)^3}.$$

## Proof

Call $B$ the factor after $N_T^2$.

1. Sort the $i$ by $L'(\Lambda_i)$. By `card_innerLattices_with_definite_trace_le_norm`, the terms with the definite part $L'_W(\Lambda_0)$ have at most $N_T^2$ lattices $L'$. So there are at most $N_T^2$ classes.
2. In one class, all $\Lambda_i$ have the same *datum* as one of them, $\Lambda_{i_0}$: equal $L$, $L'$ and $\Gamma_g$ (`SameDat`). Also $g_I(\Lambda_{i_0}) = g_I(\Lambda_0)$, since $g_I$ depends only on $L$ and $\Gamma_g$.
3. By `StabInv.main`, $R$ takes terms to terms with the same datum, and by `WeightInf.main` the weight is positive and $R$-invariant. So the sum over the class is at most the sum of $\omega$ over the $R$-orbits of terms with the datum of $\Lambda_{i_0}$ (`sum_le_orbit`). By [`hermFrame_weightSum_sameDatum_le`](/entry/herm-frame-weight-sum-same-datum-le/), this is at most $B$.
4. Sum over the classes. Since $\Delta, \Delta_b, N_T \geq 0$ (`nD_nonneg`), $B \geq 0$.
