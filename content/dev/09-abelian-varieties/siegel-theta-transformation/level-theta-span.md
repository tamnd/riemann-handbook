This theorem says that each bounded holomorphic form of a given level on a unitary group is a finite sum of hermitian theta series. It is the special case of [the spanning theorem](/entry/hermitian-theta-series-span-bounded-forms/) for the family of theta functions that a level structure gives. It lies below [the step from the literature](/step/every-character-of-literature/). Its two callers write stable forms and bounded holomorphic forms as sums of theta series. The real content is in [`theta_span`](/entry/theta-span/).

## Statement

Let $E$ be a CM field of degree $d$ over $\mathbb{Q}$. Let `X : Data E r` be a [[Hermitian form|48P4A1CX85]] $G$ of signature $(p_0, q_0)$ on $E^r$, with a CM type $\Psi$, a lattice $\Lambda$ and an arithmetic group. Fix a symplectic basis `bas` of $\Lambda$ as a $\mathbb{Z}$-module of rank $2g$, and let $n$ be even with $4dr + 4 \le n$.

A level `D : Level X` is a proper nonzero ideal $\mathfrak{m}$ of $O_E$, prime to its conjugate and to $2$, together with vectors $\beta$ that span $\Lambda$ modulo $\mathfrak{m}$, a subgroup $J$ of $\mathrm{GL}_r(O_E/\mathfrak{m})$, a character $\lambda$ of $J$, and an integral $w$ with $w + \bar w = 1$. From $D$ the Lean code builds:

- the group $\Delta$ generated by the unitary $\gamma$ whose reduction lies in $J$;
- the set $\mathcal{L}$ of self-dual lattices equal to $\Lambda$ away from $\mathfrak{m}\bar{\mathfrak{m}}$;
- a family $\Phi(L, \Lambda')$ of test functions, built from vectors $v_0(L)$ by projection, Heisenberg translation and the values of $\lambda$.

Let $M > 0$ with $M \in \mathfrak{m}$. Suppose each $v_0(L)$ is a unit vector fixed by a Heisenberg group (`IsVac`), and that $\gamma \in \Delta$ moves $v_0(L)$ to $\mu(\gamma, L)\lambda(k)\, v_0(\gamma L)$. Then each form $F$ of weight `wtOf c c' n` for $(\Delta, \mathcal{L}, \mu)$ is in the span of the theta series $\theta_P(\Phi(L, \Lambda_i))$ with $P$ in the row-highest harmonic polynomials of type $(c, c')$.

## Proof

From $d \ge 1$ and $4dr + 4 \le n$ we get $4r < n$. The theorem `hermitianThetaLevelData_forms_system` shows that $(\Delta, \mathcal{L}, \Phi, \mu)$ is a theta system (`IsSystem`). Then the [spanning theorem](/entry/hermitian-theta-series-span-bounded-forms/) gives the result.
