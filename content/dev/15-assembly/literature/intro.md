This chapter collects the results that the proof takes from the published literature and the short derivations that the development adds to them. It covers the representation theory of real unitary groups and the Weil representation, the theta lift for $\mathrm{GL}$, strong approximation and Flath's theorem for adelic unitary groups, the arithmetic Hilbert-Samuel theorem and arithmetic volumes, good metrics and toroidal compactifications, complex multiplication, and the theorem of Bhatt and Scholze on crystalline lattices. Each statement is given in the form that the development uses, and each proof says whether the result is taken from a source or derived in the development from smaller published inputs. The last section shows how the results are grouped into four bundles, all under one record of statements that the development does not prove.

The bundles feed the step [EveryCharacter from the literature](/step/every-character-of-literature/) in [the proof](/proof/literature/). Background is in the path on [the literature](/learn/literature/), and for the separate topics in the paths on [real groups](/learn/real-groups/), [theta correspondence](/learn/theta/), [Arakelov geometry](/learn/arakelov/), [elliptic curves](/learn/elliptic-curves/) and [abelian varieties](/learn/abelian-varieties/). For the [[local Langlands correspondence|9E45NBX1G0]], which enters through the record of open statements, see Volume 1.
