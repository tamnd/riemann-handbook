This chapter collects three groups of results of the assembly. The first group proves the statement VWin of [the proof chapter on VWin](/proof/vwin/) from counts. Section 1 bounds the plus part of the Tate-Shafarevich lengths of a CM field at one point from six counts, each attached to a [[Dirichlet character|JR4QSEFXWF]] and a prime $l$. Section 2 moves this bound to a window of points and joins it with a bound at the prime $2$ and on cyclotomic layers and with a bound on the minus part. The second group proves the closed form of [the Selmer bound at a point](/entry/selmer-bound-at-point-main/). Section 3 changes the family at the point $\pi$ so that it becomes block diagonal and builds a finite extension of the Hecke algebra on which the eigenvalues of one matrix become functions. Section 4 proves the local clauses for the changed family, and Section 5 bounds the congruence length $\ell(\mathcal{O}/\eta_\pi)$ by the Selmer, Tate-Shafarevich, class number and local terms. The third group gives three local facts on theta lifts and smooth vectors. Section 6 proves the spherical part of Howe duality at the places that are inert in a CM extension. Section 7 proves that smooth vectors of a unitary representation of a real [[unitary group|0R9R0Y1HDK]] are limits of $K$-finite vectors in Sobolev seminorms. Section 8 finds a generating $K$-type of the real theta quotient of the Fock model and a Casimir gap above it. The chapter needs from Volume 1 the [Selmer groups](/learn/galois-cohomology/#selmer-and-tate-shafarevich-groups), the [Weil representation](/learn/theta/#the-weil-representation) and the [$(\mathfrak g, K)$-modules](/learn/real-groups/#admissible-representations-and-mathfrakg-k-modules).

The window bound is used by [the theorem with four counts](/entry/of-four-alt/), which reduces VWin to Lemma K and four counts. The closed form of the Selmer bound is the main input of [the Selmer bound at a point](/entry/selmer-bound-at-point-main/) in Volume 4. The local facts of Sections 6 and 8 are inputs of [Theorem 2.5 of Volume 12](/entry/theorem-k-head2-of-mod/) on theta integrals, and all three lie below the step [EveryCharacter from the literature and VWin](/step/every-character-of-literature/).
