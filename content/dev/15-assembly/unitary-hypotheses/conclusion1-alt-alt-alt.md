This theorem proves `SphericalInertData.Conclusion1 D`: for a datum $D$, automorphic representations of the definite unitary groups of $D$ that are spherical at the inert places have a base change. It passes its inputs to `aux_of_cgTrue_of_auTrue` and adds one proved fact. It has four users, all below [the proof that HypsAlt is nonempty](/proof/unitary-hypotheses/) in branch 1 of the [unitary hypotheses chapter](/proof/unitary-hypotheses/). One of them fills the field `bc` of the inputs of [`locComp_of_isRc_classMapD_cenLoc`](/entry/rows-all-loc-comp-of-is-rc-class-map-d-cen-loc/).

## Statement

Assume four named inputs: `DefiniteN`, `BernsteinUnitaryProductIrreducible`, `GenericUnitaryDualGL` and `StandardCosocleGL`. `DefiniteN` says: let $E$ be a CM field with $E/E^+$ unramified at all finite places, and $n \ge 1$. Then each automorphic representation $\pi$ of the unitary group in $n$ variables has a base change in the sense of `IsBaseChangeU`, given by a partition $n = \sum_i N_i$, spaces of automorphic forms on $\mathrm{GL}_{N_i}$, local components and infinitesimal characters. Let $h_L$ witness [RecTExists](/step/rect-exists-def/), $D$ a datum, $T$ a tie of Weil-Deligne data and $A$ an automorphic side. Assume `CGTrueT hL D T` and `AutomorphicSideRealised hL D T A`.

Then `Conclusion1 D` holds. It says: if $E$ is generated by $F$ and $K$ (`HypEFK`), then under the CM hypothesis `HypCM` of $D$, for each $j$ with $1 \le j \le m$ such that $V_j$ is definite, each $\pi$ in the set `AU j` of automorphic data of $D$ that is spherical at the inert places (`SphInert`) has a base change parameter (`IsBC`). We have not unfolded `AU`, `SphInert` or `IsBC`.

## Proof

The proof is `fun h _ => aux_of_cgTrue_of_auTrue ascComp h`. It gives `aux_of_cgTrue_of_auTrue` the theorem `ascComp`, which proves `AscComp` (a statement on discrete automorphic spectra of $\mathrm{GL}_N$ over totally complex fields). It drops `BernsteinUnitaryProductIrreducible`. `aux_of_cgTrue_of_auTrue` has the content. It gets the laws `RecProd`, `RecSpeh` and `RecSatake` of the local map from `recProd_recSpeh_recSatake`, and then applies `baseChangeDefinite_of_cmDatum_recLaws` with `DefiniteN`.
