This chapter proves that the record of hypotheses of [the unitary hypotheses](/proof/unitary-hypotheses/) has an element. This is branch 1 of that node, and [the step on nonempty hypotheses](/step/nonempty-hyps/) uses it at one constant. The proof assembles rows. A row is a statement about the bundles at the good points of a scale, and the record of hypotheses is read off a record of twenty six rows. Most rows come from the open parameter statements of the open orbit chapter, in one step or with no step. The others come from a short list of published results, and each of them enters at one place. The proof also gives the link bound, a closed bound for the congruence valuation of a family, from the local Galois facts at the type primes, the inert places and the places over two and three.

The chapter uses these results of Volume 1. The [Siegel-Weil formula](/learn/theta/#the-siegel-weil-formula) in the convergent range gives the count row and the three rows of loop, off-diagonal and frame bounds. The [discrete series](/learn/real-groups/#discrete-series-and-formal-degrees) and their classification give the real bound row and the placement of the real realisation in the definite rows. The structure of [unitary groups](/learn/groups/#unitary-groups) of hermitian forms over a CM extension is the setting of every row. [Global class field theory](/learn/class-field-theory/#global-class-field-theory), through Artin reciprocity, gives the service row and the last step of the passes without a cut. [Local Bloch-Kato theory](/learn/padic-hodge/#local-bloch-kato-theory) gives the crystalline period bounds of the closing local inputs, and the lengths of [Selmer and Tate-Shafarevich groups](/learn/galois-cohomology/#selmer-and-tate-shafarevich-groups) are the error terms of the congruence bound.
