This chapter proves branch 3 of [the unitary hypotheses](/proof/unitary-hypotheses/): from the published statements of the unitary group hypotheses, the parameter set of dependence satisfies twelve open parameter statements. Eleven of the twelve come from results of other chapters, and Section 1 collects them. The twelfth, the multiplicity row, is the subject of the rest of the chapter. It asks that at every good linked bundle a period identity holds: each period of the package equals, up to a nonzero scalar and a unitary change of coordinates, an inner product of a theta vector of a [[Weil representation|CD20HVR9V8]] with a vector of the row side. Section 2 reduces the multiplicity row to the period identity for one real package, and Section 3 reduces this to a list of family conditions. Section 4 proves the family conditions from a geometric family, a row choice and a column choice. Section 5 finds one central element of the adelic [[unitary group|0R9R0Y1HDK]] that pins a bottom datum on every placed framed Bessel datum. Sections 6 to 8 build the local data at the places of the twist set: quadratic extension data with their readings, a rescaling by roots of unity that makes the local values rational, and the constancy of the volumes of the level subgroups times the local weights. Section 9 builds the column side of a geometric family and proves the place laws of the rescaled tuples, and Section 10 assembles the column data and the link conditions.

The chapter uses from Volume 1 [the Weil representation](/learn/theta/#the-weil-representation), [the Fock model](/learn/theta/#the-fock-model), [seesaw pairs](/learn/theta/#seesaw-pairs), [the Siegel-Weil formula](/learn/theta/#the-siegel-weil-formula) and [the doubling method](/learn/theta/#the-doubling-method), together with [unitary groups](/learn/groups/#unitary-groups), [adelic points and compact open subgroups](/learn/groups/#adelic-points-and-compact-open-subgroups), [strong approximation](/learn/groups/#strong-approximation) and [discrete series](/learn/real-groups/#discrete-series-and-formal-degrees). The main theorem is used in item 7 of the step [nonempty hypotheses](/step/nonempty-hyps/), which gives the twelve statements at the parameters of dependence for the constant $c_{\mathrm{Dep}} = \max(C_{F0}, C_{F1})$.
