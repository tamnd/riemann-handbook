## The column side

This section builds, for a geometric family with a good row choice, a column choice together with the tuples at each twist index, and proves the conditions that [the Theorem on column data](/entry/true-geometry-family-column-data-alt/) needs. It is step 4 of [the Theorem on family conditions](/entry/fam-all-q-of-all-true-alt/). The construction puts together the three previous sections: the quadratic extension data that read, the rescaling by roots of unity with the constants $C_j$, and the volume constants $V_j$ and $V_T$. We first prove the place laws for a rescaled tuple, which is the one input of the construction that is not an explicit formula.

We keep the notation of [the section on family conditions](/entry/fam-all-q-of-all-true-alt/). Let $a$ be a geometric family for $W$ and $u$, with the sequence $c_0(j)$ and the type data $o$ over the local field $k$ of $a$. Let $\mathrm{row}$ be a row choice and $\mathrm{col}$ a column choice, and $f = a(\mathrm{row}, \mathrm{col})$.

The family character $f$ has *good rows* if the measures, the last level and the lattices of $a$ are good, every row is good, and all rows have the same shape. It has *good columns* if the kernel data of the columns are given at each level, the kernel at each level is a vacuum kernel for the lattices $L_j$ and $L_{j+1}$ with a finite combination, the elements $e_j$ and $e_T$ of the columns are supported on the right cosets, and the kernels of the chain and of the see-saw preserve the ramified forms.

A *tuple* $w$ for $f$ at $t$ carries a set of primes $w.Q$, combinations $w.eQ_j$, $w.eQT$, $w.dQ$, elements $w.\varepsilon_j$, $w.\varepsilon_T$, and constants $w.\mathrm{sc}$, $w.cT_j$, $w.cQ_j$. For functions $s_j$ on the character sets of the column, the *rescaled tuple* $\mathrm{resc}(w, s)$ multiplies $w.dQ(j, \alpha, \gamma)$ by $s_j(\alpha) s_{j+1}(\gamma)^{-1}$, multiplies $w.\varepsilon_j(\alpha)$ by $s_j(\alpha)$ and $w.\varepsilon_T(\beta)$ by $s_m(\beta)$, and leaves the rest. A *place family* $F$ for the tuples of $f$ at $t$ is a family of local data at the places of the commuting vector data $v$ of $a$, and $F$ *pins* a tuple $w$ if the places of $w$ are those of $F$, the kernel data and the middle and last columns agree with the rows, and the combinations and elements of $w$ are those of $F$.

The *place laws* of a tuple $w$ are three conditions on $w$ relative to $v$: the law $\mathrm{QLaw}$, the last value law $\mathrm{QLast}$ and the finiteness law $\mathrm{QFin}$. We do not unfold them; they are the conditions that the next section passes on to the place laws of [the family conditions](/entry/fam-all-q-of-all-true-alt/).

**Theorem 4.9.1 (Place laws of a rescaled tuple).** Assume the discrete series cover and the Harish-Chandra modules. Let $W$ satisfy the family conditions on a package, and let $a$ be good and true. Let $\mathrm{row}$ and $\mathrm{col}$ be choices such that $f = a(\mathrm{row}, \mathrm{col})$ has good rows and good columns. Let $t$ be a twist index, $F$ a place family at $t$, $w_0$ a tuple pinned by $F$, and $s_j$ functions whose values are roots of unity. Assume that the primes of $w_0$ are exactly the rational primes under the places of $Q(t)$. Then $\mathrm{resc}(w_0, s)$ satisfies the three place laws.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.QColLaws.laws_resc_alt` (also `GlobalTheory.T.true_geometry_family_final_alt`, a second name).

*Proof.* We take three steps.

1. From the discrete series cover, the Harish-Chandra modules, the package hypotheses and the good rows, a result of another chapter gives that the commutation datum of $f$ is good, and that each tuple of $f$ is good once the commutation datum is.
2. From the package hypotheses, the good rows and columns, the tie of $a$ for $(\mathrm{row}, \mathrm{col})$ and the shape of $a$ (its line and its type condition at each level), a result on Gram matrices of rank one gives the Weil condition for $w_0$ at the primes of $Q(t)$.
3. [The Theorem on place laws under rescaling by roots of unity](/entry/see-saw-twist-place-laws-of-root-of-unity-rescaling/) says that a tuple of the form $\mathrm{resc}(w_0, s)$, with $s$ of roots of unity and $w_0$ pinned by a place family, satisfies the place laws, given the goodness of step 1, the visibility of $a$ and the Weil condition of step 2. $\square$

The next definitions describe the output of the construction.

**Definition 4.9.2 (Family constants).** *Family constants* $\kappa$ for $a$ are units $\varepsilon_j$ of the residue field of $k$ indexed by the type index set of $a$, bilinear operations $\mathrm{op}_j$ on $X_j \times X_{j+1}$ with values in $X_{j+2}$, real numbers $r_j$ and integers $e_j$ on the type index set, numbers $c_G(j) \in \mathbb{C}$, and scalars $\mathrm{sc}(t, j) \in \mathbb{C}$ for each twist index $t$. They change the commuting vector data $v$ of $a$ to $v_\kappa$, with the type operator of $o$ at $\varepsilon$, the operations $\mathrm{op}_j$ and the constants $c_G$.

**Definition 4.9.3 (Column conditions).** Let $\mathrm{row}$ and $\mathrm{col}$ be choices and $f = a(\mathrm{row}, \mathrm{col})$.

1. The *family column condition* on $f$ says that there is a sequence $c_0$ such that $a$ is pinned at $c_0$ (its lattices, vacuum vectors and levels have the standard form at $c_0$), and for each $t$: there are tuple data $A$ with a map $\iota$ from the character sets of the column to the index sets of $A$ and weights $w$, such that the column of $t$ is pinned to $A$ along $\iota$ with weights $w$, and the column of $t$ has a top pin with a subgroup $U_T$ and a constant $\varepsilon$.
2. For tuples $w_0(t)$ and $w(t)$ and place families $F(t)$, the *column output condition* for $\kappa$ says: $f$ has good columns; $F(t)$ pins $w_0(t)$; $w(t)$ and $w_0(t)$ have the same primes and the same combinations $eQ$, $eQT$, $cQ$ and typed sets; $w(t)$ satisfies the place laws; the levels and the rational vector condition hold for $F(t)$ and $w_0(t)$; the combinations of $w_0(t)$ are supported at its primes; the elements $e_j$, $e_T$ and the level combinations of the column are those of $w(t)$; the column has a reading for $\varepsilon$; $w(t).\mathrm{sc} = \mathrm{sc}(t, \cdot)$, the top constants $w(t).cT_j$ are the values of $o$ at $(r_j, e_j)$ on the character of the row, and the vector constants of $w(t)$ match $v_\kappa$; the chart condition at zero holds; and the column constants $c_K(t, j)$ and $c_P(t)$ satisfy, for $j < m$,
$$\frac{c_K(t, j)}{c_K(\mu_0, j)} \ \text{ and } \ \frac{c_P(t)}{c_P(\mu_0)} \quad \text{are } S_t\text{-unit ratios}.$$

**Theorem 4.9.4 (The column side).** Assume the discrete series cover and the Harish-Chandra modules. Let $W$ satisfy the family conditions on a package, and let $a$ be good and true with top matrix $i_A = 1$. Let $\kappa$ be family constants and $\mathrm{row}$ a row choice in which every row is good and all rows have the same shape. Then there are a column choice $\mathrm{col}$, tuples $w_0(t)$ and $w(t)$ and place families $F(t)$ such that $a(\mathrm{row}, \mathrm{col})$ satisfies the family column condition and the column output condition for $\kappa$ holds.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.Fam.colSide_alt` (also `GlobalTheory.T.true_geometry_family_invariance_alt`, a second name).

*Proof.* The column choice is given by explicit constants. We take the steps in order.

1. For every column choice, $a(\mathrm{row}, \mathrm{col})$ has good rows: this needs only the goodness of $a$ and of the rows. By step 1 of the previous proof, the commutation datum of $a$ is good.
2. By [the Theorem on readings](/entry/reads-exists-alt/), choose for each $t$ a quadratic extension datum $J_t$ for $(a, t)$ that reads.
3. By [the Theorem on rescaling](/entry/dat-exists/), there are constants $C_j \neq 0$ for $j < m$ and, for each $t$, a rescaling datum $(s_t, \zeta_t, \rho_t)$ for $C$ on $J_t$.
4. By [the Theorem on volume constants](/entry/vol-level-wt-const-alt/), there are $V_j \neq 0$ for $j < m$ and $V_T \neq 0$ such that every $J_t$ has level volumes $V$ and top volume $V_T$.
5. Results of another chapter give kernel data for the columns, and, for $j < m$, finite combinations $d_t(j, \alpha, \gamma)$ with $\mathrm{comb}(d_t(j, \alpha, \gamma)) = \mathrm{comb}(dQ_{s_t}(j, \alpha, \gamma)) \circ \mathrm{comb}(d_G(j)) \circ \mathrm{comb}(d_T(j))$, and a joint twist relation $S'$ for the type operators.
6. The set $\mathrm{Ch}(m)$ of $J_t$ has one element $\beta_0(t)$: it is the product over $i$ of the sets $E_i.\mathrm{Ch}(m)$, and $m = s_i + 2$ is the top level of $E_i$, where $E_i$ has one character.
7. Define, for each $t$ and $j$,
$$\begin{aligned} c_Q(t, j) &= \prod_i \bigl( E_i.\mathrm{idx}(j + 1)\, E_i.\mathrm{idx}(j + 2) \bigr)^{-1}, \\ c_T(t, j) &= o.\mathrm{mono}\bigl(r_j, e_j, \mu(t, j + 1)\bigr), \\ c_K(t, j) &= V_j\, C_j\, c_0(j)\, \rho_t(j), \\ c_P(t) &= V_T \sum_{\beta \in \mathrm{Ch}(m)} s_t(m, \beta), \end{aligned}$$
where the $E_i$ and $s_t$ are those of $J_t$, and $\mu(t, j + 1)$ is the level character of $t$. Let $c_V(t, j) = \mathrm{sc}(t, j)\, \mathrm{sc}(t, j + 1)\, c_Q(t, j)\, c_G(j)\, c_T(t, j)$, multiplied by the constant $\kappa_j(\sigma)$ of $v_\kappa$ when $j + 2(\sigma + 1) + 2 = m$ for some $\sigma$.
8. Let $\mathrm{col}$ be the column choice with these coefficients, the kernel data, the combinations $d_t$, the type operators $S'(t, j)$ at $\varepsilon_j$ and the functions $s_t$. Let $F(t)$ be the place families of $a$, $J$, $\mathrm{row}$ and $\mathrm{col}$, let $w_0(t)$ be the tuple pinned by $F(t)$ with scalars $\mathrm{sc}(t, \cdot)$ and top constants $c_T(t, \cdot)$, and let $w(t) = \mathrm{resc}(w_0(t), s_t)$.
9. Family column condition. Take $c_0$ of $a$, which pins $a$ since $a$ is true. For each $t$, the column is pinned to the tuple data $A$ of $J_t$ along $\iota$, with weights $C_j\, \mathrm{wt}_j(\alpha)\, \zeta_t(j, \alpha, \gamma)$. The rational data are those of step 3, through the combinations of step 5. The constant condition is
$$c_K(t, j) = V_j C_j c_0(j) \rho_t(j) = \mathrm{vol}_j(U_j(\iota_j \alpha))\, \mathrm{wt}_j(\alpha)\, C_j\, c_0(j)\, \zeta_t(j, \alpha, \gamma)\, \chi_\alpha (\chi'_\gamma)^{-1},$$
by the level volumes and by condition 3 of the rescaling datum. The top pin takes $U_T$ of the top volume and $\varepsilon = s_t(m, \beta_0)\, \mathrm{wt}_m(\beta_0)$; then $c_P(t) = V_T\, s_t(m, \beta_0) = \mathrm{vol}_T(U_T)\, \varepsilon$.
10. The good columns follow from step 5, from the pins of $a$ (the lattices are the integral vectors and the vacuum vectors are vacuum kernels), from the support of the joint twist data, and from the preservation of ramified forms by the chain kernels on live charts.
11. The place laws of $w(t)$ are the previous theorem, with $w_0(t)$, $F(t)$ and $s_t$; the primes of $w_0(t)$ are those of the groups $G_i$ of $J_t$, that is, the primes under the places $P_i$, and these are all places of $Q(t)$ by the readings.
12. Unit ratios. If $x$ and $y$ are roots of unity and $c \neq 0$, then $cx / cy$ is an $S$-unit ratio for every $S$: if $x^{n_1} = 1$ and $y^{n_2} = 1$, then $(cx / cy)^{n_1 n_2} = 1$. For $c_K$ take $c = V_j C_j c_0(j)$, nonzero by steps 3 and 4 and the pin of $a$, and $x = \rho_t(j)$, $y = \rho_{\mu_0}(j)$. For $c_P$, the sum has one term $s_t(m, \beta_0(t))$, a root of unity; take $c = V_T$.
13. The other conditions of the column output hold by construction: the levels, the rational vector condition, the support of the combinations at the primes, the elements $e_j$ and $e_T$, the readings from $S'$, the top constants from the shape of the rows, the vector constants by the choice of $c_V$, and the chart condition at zero by a bound on the joint twist, which uses $s_t(0) = 1$. $\square$

So the column constants are the products $V_j C_j c_0(j)$ of the uniform constants of the previous three sections, times the roots of unity $\rho_t(j)$; this is why their ratios at $t$ and at $\mu_0$ are unit ratios.
