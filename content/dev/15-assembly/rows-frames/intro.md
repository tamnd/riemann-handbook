This chapter collects inputs of the third place series and of the surjectivity branch of [the unitary hypotheses](/proof/unitary-hypotheses/). The first group is finite group theory and counting. Section 1 proves that Harish-Chandra induction of cuspidal characters with distinct labels is irreducible for $\mathrm{GL}_r(\mathbb{F}_q)$, which feeds [the nonempty hypotheses from unramified exponents](/entry/hypotheses-nonempty-of-unramified-exponents-alt/). Section 2 bounds multiplicities of unipotent characters from rows and gives the pass condition of [the third place at real $t$](/entry/pass-in3-at-real-t-alt/), below the step [the definite side condition](/step/definite-side-condition-def/). Section 3 proves the second complement inputs along a family and the [Closed](/step/closed-def/) property for [the second complement at the constructed points](/entry/division-second-complement-constructed-points/). Section 4 bounds the volumes of height shells in a quotient of an adelic [unitary group](/learn/groups/#unitary-groups) by a [Haar measure](/learn/analysis/#haar-measure) estimate, for [the frame bound for the spherical pole](/entry/sph-pole-frame-bound/).

The second group serves the surjectivity chapter. Section 5 reads the depths and lattices of a leaf off a record, for [the geometric statement](/entry/geo-st-of-page-st-alt/). Sections 6 and 7 build Wach lattices of a [crystalline](/learn/padic-hodge/#crystalline-and-semistable-representations) block and the lattice links of [highest weights](/learn/representations/#highest-weights-and-branching) of simple subquotients, which give [the model statement for a small frame](/entry/model-st-of-small-frame-gal/). Section 8 bounds the exponents at the twisted places for [the exponent statement](/entry/exp-st-of-alt/). Section 9 gives readings at small places, runs pinned at a special prime and the bound at the twisted places, for [the readings](/entry/reads-of-small-alt/), [the runs](/entry/exists-runs-at-alt/) and [the onto path](/entry/onto-path-alt/). Section 10 compares trace lattices with lattices of [theta series](/learn/theta-functions-cm/#theta-series-of-lattices) away from the own prime, for [the comparison away from the own prime](/entry/compare-off-own-at-runs-alt/), and records the cusp forms with independent translates used by [the surjectivity from the hypotheses](/entry/onto-x-of-hypotheses-alt-alt/).
