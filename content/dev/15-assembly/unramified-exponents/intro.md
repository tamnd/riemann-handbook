This chapter proves the first step of branch 1 of [the unitary hypotheses](/proof/unitary-hypotheses/): from the literature hypotheses of the unitary groups, it gets the Siegel mass ratio for CM fields, the membership statement for the stronger parameters, and the existence of linked bundles for these parameters. Section 1 sets up the language of parameter sets, slots and eventual statements, and states the two main theorems. Section 2 proves the membership statement for the parameter set of a norm choice extension from a list of step conditions. Section 3 proves the step from level three to level four, through the spectral setting of a datum with large gaps of its Harish-Chandra parameters. Sections 4 and 5 prove that built cores export: they reduce the export statement to the realization of theta series at a datum, and the realization to Galois inputs at the places of the datum. Section 6 states the stage statements at the origin extension and proves the settings and the weight data at the first level. Section 7 proves finiteness at level three and the automorphic side at a strict henselian acyclic level. Section 8 proves the eta statement: the eta values of the localized data agree with those of the column datum. Section 9 builds the series data at the second level. The later sections prove the bundle statement, the finiteness and nonvanishing statements and the multiplicity statements that Sections 1 and 2 use.

The chapter needs from Volume 1 [the local Langlands correspondence](/learn/galois-representations/#the-local-langlands-correspondence), [discrete series and formal degrees](/learn/real-groups/#discrete-series-and-formal-degrees), [the Weil representation](/learn/theta/#the-weil-representation), [cusp forms](/learn/automorphic-forms/#constant-terms-and-cusp-forms), [global class field theory](/learn/class-field-theory/#global-class-field-theory), [the Galois representation of an automorphic representation](/learn/galois-representations/#the-galois-representation-of-an-automorphic-representation) and [crystalline and de Rham representations](/learn/galois-representations/#crystalline-de-rham-and-potentially-semistable-representations). Its main theorem is used by [the theorem that the hypotheses are nonempty](/entry/hypotheses-nonempty-of-unramified-exponents-alt/), which is step 1 of branch 1 of [the unitary hypotheses](/proof/unitary-hypotheses/) and lies below the step [nonempty hypotheses](/step/nonempty-hyps/).
