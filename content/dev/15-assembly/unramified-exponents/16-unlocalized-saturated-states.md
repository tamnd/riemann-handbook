## Unlocalized saturated states

This section proves the unlocalized saturated state statement of Section 1 from the first record $\mathcal{H}_A$ and the multiplicity cover record $\mathcal{H}_M$. It is the first hypothesis of [the Theorem on the origin extension](/entry/exists-base-lattice-of-prime-alt/), where it gives, with the stage statements and spectral finiteness, the column step to $\mathrm{Has}_3$. The statement asks for a datum over each column datum whose weight set is saturated, together with a state with no localized places and all values $\eta_j(v)$ equal to $0$. The saturation is the deep part: at an untwisted base, the weight set must be closed under the relation of lying over the same place of the fixed field. It comes from the lower unitary case condition and Green's classification. The state comes from a construction in which the localization set is empty and the values $\eta$ are chosen to be $0$.

We use the records of Section 1. The record $\mathcal{H}_A$ contains, among others, the existence of ray class fields, the theory of Harish-Chandra modules (field 5), rapid decay of cusp forms (field 6), the Casselman-Wallach theorem (field 7), the Weil representation of a unitary pair (field 8), the existence of discrete series of $U(p, q)$ (field 9) and Harish-Chandra's finiteness theorem (field 10). The record $\mathcal{H}_M$ contains the discrete series cover, the theta condition theorems of Section 15, the continuation of partial Hecke $L$-functions and two multiplicity statements: the *lower statement*, that along $\mathrm{Step}_w$ from the pinned condition every datum with a true group, the group condition, the extension property and the geometric condition satisfies the multiplicity equation, and the *weight statement*, that along $\mathrm{Step}_w$ from the start condition every datum in the weight case of Section 15 satisfies the multiplicity equation and the row test.

**Definition 6.16.1 (Saturated data).** Let $K$ be a context and $w$ a column datum over a base $B$ with twist datum $T$, and let $P$ be a datum of $w$ with base stage $Z_0$ and weight set $W_t(P)$. Two places $v$ and $v'$ of $B$ lie *over one place* if they lie over the same place of the fixed field of the datum of $w$ at $Z_0$. The datum $P$ is *unlocalized saturated* if, when $T$ is the untwisted datum of $B$, the weight set $W_t(P)$ is closed under this relation: for all $v$ and $v'$ over one place, $v \in W_t(P)$ implies $v' \in W_t(P)$. When $T$ is twisted, the condition is empty.

The saturation says that the weight set does not separate places that the fixed field cannot tell apart. This is what the origin extension of Section 1 needs to read the weights of a point from the datum $P$ alone.

**Definition 6.16.2 (Extension of fine adelic data).** The *extension condition* for fine adelic data says that along $\mathrm{Step}_w$ from the pinned condition, for each enlargement $B \to B'$ of the first kind with matching frames and positions, fine adelic data over $B$ with the lower unitary case condition extend to fine adelic data over $B'$. Section 1 derives it from the discrete series cover and fields 5 to 10 of $\mathcal{H}_A$, by the extension of adelic data to larger fields.

**Theorem 6.16.3 (Saturated bare states).** Assume $\mathcal{H}_A$, $\mathcal{H}_M$ and the extension condition for fine adelic data. Then along $\mathrm{Step}_w$ from the start condition, for each column datum $w$ there are a datum $P$ of $w$ and a stage $x$ of $P$ such that $P$ is unlocalized saturated, the set of localized places of $x$ is empty, and $\eta_j(v) = 0$ for all $j$ and $v$.

*Proof.* First the two case conditions. The theta condition theorems, the continuation of partial $L$-functions and the weight statement give [the weight case condition](/entry/w-of-weight-residue-char/) of Section 15. The same inputs with the lower statement give the lower unitary case condition. The discrete series cover and fields 5 to 10 of $\mathcal{H}_A$ give fine adelic data at every stage.

Next the saturation. Green's classification, the fine adelic data, the lower unitary case condition and the extension condition show that, along $\mathrm{Step}_w$ from the pinned condition, untwisted adelic data are saturated over the fixed field. The Weil representation, the existence of discrete series, Harish-Chandra's finiteness theorem, rapid decay and the weight case condition give the construction starting choices of fine adelic data: at each datum in the weight case they give a datum $P$ whose stage and weight set are those of the datum. Now fix $w$ with the start condition. If $T$ is untwisted, the saturation gives a datum $z$ with a saturated weight set, the starting choices give $P$ with the stage and the weight set of $z$, and so $P$ is unlocalized saturated. If $T$ is twisted, the support condition gives a datum in the weight case, the starting choices give $P$, and the saturation condition is empty.

Last the state. The datum $P$ has a pack, and the step of localization free stages with true groups gives a twist $N$ with no localized places and a column choice. We take a division stage with values $\eta_j(v) = 0$ for all $j$ and $v$, an $S$-stage at the scale of $K$, and the group of the base stage. The fitting of later stages to the row, which uses the Weil representation, the existence of discrete series, Harish-Chandra's finiteness theorem and rapid decay, gives a construction state $x$ whose object is this object. So $x$ has no localized places, and its values $\eta$ are $0$ by the choice of the division stage. $\square$

**Theorem 6.16.4 (Unlocalized saturated state statement).** Assume $\mathcal{H}_A$ and $\mathcal{H}_M$. Then the unlocalized saturated state statement holds.

*Proof.* This is the theorem on saturated bare states applied to $\mathcal{H}_A$, $\mathcal{H}_M$ and the extension condition, which follows from the discrete series cover of $\mathcal{H}_M$ and fields 5 to 10 of $\mathcal{H}_A$. $\square$
