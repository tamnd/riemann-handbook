This chapter proves that no [[Dirichlet L-function|24GAZHVTGB]] vanishes in the half-plane $\operatorname{Re} s > \tfrac12$, from three inputs: the statement Green on the cuspidal characters of $\mathrm{GL}_d$ over a finite field, the window bound VWin, and a record of hypotheses on families of theta data. The analytic part turns a Chebyshev bound on a coset into a bound on windows $[Y, Y^\lambda]$, and a criterion of Landau type turns such bounds into zero-free half-planes. The arithmetic part builds hosts on windows from VWin, proves the local bound $\mathrm{Link}_G$ and the division bounds, and joins them in the shape theorem. The last section also proves the standard Siegel-Weil formula and the theta inputs that follow from it.

From Volume 1 the chapter uses [the explicit formula](/learn/analytic-number-theory/#the-explicit-formula), [Dirichlet characters](/learn/l-functions/#dirichlet-characters) and [Dirichlet $L$-functions](/learn/l-functions/#dirichlet-l-functions), [primes in progressions](/learn/analytic-number-theory/#primes-in-progressions-and-in-short-intervals) with Linnik's theorem, [primes represented by norm forms](/learn/analytic-number-theory/#primes-represented-by-norm-forms), and [the Siegel-Weil formula](/learn/theta/#the-siegel-weil-formula). Its results are the steps [ZeroFreeAll from Green, VWin and HypsAlt](/step/zero-free-all-of-green-v-win/) and [EveryCharacter from Green, VWin and HypsAlt](/step/green-vwin/), which the step [EveryCharacter from theta integrals and local matching](/step/theta-local-matching/) applies.
