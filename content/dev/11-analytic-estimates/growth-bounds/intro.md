title: Introduction
---
This chapter proves the growth bounds that the local estimates of the proof need: upper bounds on valuations of pairings and lattice functions at an unramified place, and counting statements for Hecke operators, paths of weights and orbits, with explicit constants. Section 1 gives the plane Gram theorem, a lift bound for polynomials in several groups of variables over a $p$-adic field, and Section 2 states it over a discrete valuation ring with fixed constants. Section 3 defines Statement E, a local lattice estimate at an unramified place, and proves the closed form of the second theorem from one remaining input, the test bound. Section 4 proves Proposition 5, a valuation bound for the pairing of a test polynomial with one-sided elements, and Section 5 uses it to remove the test bound and prove Statement E. Section 6 proves a counting statement for Hecke operators on functions on tuples of lattices. Section 7 proves the packet statements, valuation bounds for a chain of kernel maps of a formal group system. Section 8 proves the second part of the selection, a large free submodule with a degree bound, by a count of paths of weights. Section 9 makes the estimates uniform along a family of data and gives one constant for Statement E at all data. From Volume 1 the chapter uses [discrete valuation rings and lattices](/learn/commutative-algebra/#discrete-valuation-rings-and-lattices), [partitions and Young tableaux](/learn/combinatorics/#partitions-and-young-tableaux), [the Pieri rule](/learn/combinatorics/#the-pieri-and-littlewood-richardson-rules), [highest weights and branching](/learn/representations/#highest-weights-and-branching) and [finite fields](/learn/algebra/#finite-fields).

Sections 3 to 5 lead to [Statement E](/entry/stmt-e/), which feeds [the proof of the local bound](/entry/loc-proved/). Section 6 feeds [the admissible family without kernels](/entry/admissible-family-no-kernel/), and Section 7 feeds [the service bound for sums over primes](/entry/sum-over-primes-service/). Section 8 feeds [the local model results](/entry/local-model-results/). The family statement of Section 9 feeds [the link without kernels](/entry/link-g-without-kernels/), and the absolute constant of Section 9 feeds [the closed local link](/entry/link-of-local-closed/).
