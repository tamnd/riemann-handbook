This chapter puts the hermitian curvature operators of a place in normal form and proves the analytic estimates that use this normal form. The first sections define a model algebra of shift operators with a Yangian relation, compute its quantum determinant, and get the curvature relations of a normal form datum from the Yangian relation. The next section builds gauge frames on the domain $|\zeta|^2 > T_2$ in which the actual operators take the model form. Then a holomorphic stationary phase lemma gives the germ expansion of the plane wave integrals at the critical points. A change of variables in the orbital integral gives the head formula at the top of a level tower. The last section of this part proves the compact form of Theorem P by a chain of reductions to two facts about radial operators. The chapter needs from Volume 1 the [unitary groups](/learn/groups/#unitary-groups), [highest weights and branching](/learn/representations/#highest-weights-and-branching), [Haar measure](/learn/analysis/#haar-measure), [the Fourier transform](/learn/analysis/#the-fourier-transform), [Lie groups and Lie algebras](/learn/analysis/#lie-groups-and-lie-algebras), and the [asymptotics of matrix coefficients](/learn/real-groups/#asymptotics-of-matrix-coefficients).

The [extra relations from a start](/entry/shift-extra/) and the [curvature of a normal form datum](/entry/curv/) feed the gauge frames and the [second shift gauge](/entry/second-shift-gauge-holds/). The [germ rate](/entry/germ-rate/), the [head formula from the top truncation statement](/entry/pb-head-top-of-mk-top/) and [the compact form of Theorem P](/entry/actual-compact-theorem/) are used in the step [HypsAlt is nonempty](/step/nonempty-hyps/) of [the unitary hypotheses](/proof/unitary-hypotheses/), where they give the analytic inputs at the real places.
