---
title: Introduction
---
This chapter proves the integrality and lattice estimates on polynomials in several vector and covector letters for $\mathfrak{gl}_r$ that the analytic estimates need. It proves that a lattice over $\mathbb{Z}_p$ with a symmetric pairing, for odd $p$, has an orthogonal family that misses its rank only by the number of bad vectors; it computes the leading term of the Fock pairing on the tie operators and splits the standard lattice of a block into Fock blocks with good dual pairs; and from these it proves the block valuation bound over $\mathbb{Z}_p$, an operator at one tie that moves a tie operator across the Fock pairing, the box bound, and the reduction of the closed form of Theorem A to a window condition and two facts on lattices. In the second part it shows that the path polynomials with weights in a dominant lower set are described by the weights of the module they generate, proves the conditions A3 over a discrete valuation ring and its residue field, describes the integral span of the bracket polynomials, and builds the column systems and pre-systems of path polynomials. It needs [lattices over Dedekind domains](/learn/hermitian-lattices/#lattices-over-dedekind-domains), [lattices in quadratic and hermitian spaces](/learn/hermitian-lattices/#lattices-in-quadratic-and-hermitian-spaces), [discrete valuation rings](/learn/commutative-algebra/#regular-local-rings-and-discrete-valuation-rings), [the Fock model](/learn/theta/#the-fock-model), [highest weights](/learn/representations/#highest-weights-and-branching), [partitions and Young tableaux](/learn/combinatorics/#partitions-and-young-tableaux), [the Pieri rule](/learn/combinatorics/#the-pieri-and-littlewood-richardson-rules) and [minors](/learn/combinatorics/#minors-and-determinant-identities) from Volume 1.

The results are used by [the closed form of Theorem A](/entry/theorem-a-closed-form-holds/), [the theorem on the tie operators](/entry/tie-ops-x-main/), [the theorem on the mean of the ratios](/entry/mean-gam-x/), [the tool statements](/entry/tool-statements-hold/) and [the bracket lemma](/entry/b10/). The step of the proof that uses the chapter is [theta local matching](/step/theta-local-matching/), which takes from it the admissible words, the column systems and the pre-systems.
