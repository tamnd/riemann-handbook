## The weight theorem at the top

This section moves the local bounds of [Section 4.2.10](/entry/formalism-cohomological-descent-ld-last-graded-of-graded-alone/) to automorphic forms. Let $V$ be a tensor product of dual Weyl modules over a ring $O$, and let $V^\circ \subseteq V$ be the tensor product of the Weyl lattices. Consider forms on a group with values in $V$, localized at a multiplicative set of Hecke operators. We prove that $l^J$ moves every such form into the forms with values in $V^\circ$, with an explicit $J$. The first step is a bound for one factor in a window of rows (the application window). The second step is a dévissage over the simple subquotients of $V / \varpi V$. The explicit bound is the input of the lattice inclusions of [Section 4.4.6](/entry/short-weight-bounds-inclusion-w-real-holds/), and through them of the [inclusion of models](/entry/l-incl-of-models/).

We use the exponents $\mathrm{Exp}_l(\nu, \mu)$, the weighted depth $d_\xi$ and the [last block form of Lemma D](/entry/formalism-cohomological-descent-ld-last-graded-of-graded-alone/) of Section 2, and [Noetherian and Artinian modules](/learn/algebra/#rings-ideals-and-modules).

**Definition 4.3.1 (Shifted entries and the window constants).** Let $N \in \mathbb{N}$ and $\lambda : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$. Write $\lambda|_N = (\lambda(0), \dots, \lambda(N-1))$ and put $U_N(\lambda, a) = \lambda(a) + N - 1 - a \in \mathbb{Z}$. For $c \in \mathbb{N}$ let $\varpi_c(a) = 1$ for $a < c$ and $\varpi_c(a) = 0$ otherwise. Let $l \geq 2$ and $x_-, x_+, m \in \mathbb{Z}$. Put $\iota_l(x_-, x_+, m) = 1$ if some $y$ with $x_- \leq y \leq x_+$ has $l \mid y - m$, and $0$ otherwise. For $D \in \mathbb{Z}$ let $d_0(D)$ be the least $d \geq 1$ with $l \mid D - d$. For $h \in \mathbb{N}$ and real $I$, let $H_l(h, I) = 0$ if $h < l$ and $H_l(h, I) = (I/l)(1 + \log(h/l))$ otherwise. For $k, t \in \mathbb{N}$ put
$$\begin{aligned} \Lambda_l(k, t, h, I; x_-, x_+, m) ={}& k\Bigl(\frac{x_+ - x_-}{l} + \iota_l(x_-, x_+, m)\Bigr) + H_l(h, I) \\ &+ \begin{cases} \min\bigl(k, t, I / d_0(x_- - m)\bigr) & \text{if } d_0(x_- - m) < x_- - m, \\ 0 & \text{otherwise.} \end{cases} \end{aligned}$$
For $j, q, e_{\mathrm{cl}} \in \mathbb{N}$ the *window constant* is
$$c'_l = (q + 2j)\frac{h - 1}{l - 1} + e_{\mathrm{cl}} + \Lambda_l(k, t, h, I; x_-, x_+, m).$$
For $N' \in \mathbb{N}$ the *short constant* $c^{\mathrm{sh}}_l$ is the same sum with $(h-1)/(l-1)$ replaced by $(h + N')/(l-1)$ and $H_l(h, I)$ replaced by $(I/l)(1 + \log^+(h/l))$, where $\log^+ x = \max(\log x, 0)$.

The numbers $U_N(\lambda, a)$ are the entries of $\lambda + \rho$; they are strictly decreasing when $\lambda$ is non-increasing. The term $(h-1)/(l-1)$ is the bound $v_l(n!) \leq (n-1)/(l-1)$ of Legendre for $n \leq h$, and it is paid $q + 2j$ times. The term $\Lambda_l$ bounds the cost of a block of equal rows; it depends on where the entries of the first $k$ rows sit in the window modulo $l$.

**Definition 4.3.2 (Window shape).** Let $N, k, j, t, q, h \in \mathbb{N}$, $\lambda : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ and $x_-, x_+ \in \mathbb{Z}$. Write $U(a) = U_N(\lambda, a)$. The data have *window shape* if:

1. $N = k + j + t + q$, and $\lambda$ is non-increasing on $[0, N)$;
2. $\lambda(a) = \lambda(k + j)$ for $k + j \leq a < k + j + t$;
3. $\lambda(0) + N \leq h + \lambda(N - 1) + 1$;
4. $x_- \leq U(a) \leq x_+$ for $a < k$, and $U(a) < x_-$ for $k \leq a < k + j$;
5. $U(k + j) < x_-$, $x_- \leq x_+$ and $x_+ - U(k + j) \leq h$.

So the first $k$ shifted entries lie in the window $[x_-, x_+]$, the next $j$ lie below it, a block of $t$ equal rows follows, and $q$ rows close the shape.

**Theorem 4.3.3 (Application window).** Assume Lemma D for the last block. Let $l$ be a prime number and $k, j, t, q, h \in \mathbb{N}$ with $t \geq 1$ and $h < l^2$. Put $N = k + j + t + q$. Let $\lambda : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ and $x_-, x_+ \in \mathbb{Z}$ have window shape. Let $e_{\mathrm{cl}} \in \mathbb{N}$ with $\mathrm{Exp}_l(\lambda|_k, \mu) \leq e_{\mathrm{cl}}$ for all $\mu \in \mathbb{N}^k$. Then for every $I \in \mathbb{N}$ and every $\mu \in \mathbb{N}^N$ with $d_{\varpi_{k+j}}(\lambda|_N, \mu) \leq I$,
$$\mathrm{Exp}_l(\lambda|_N, \mu) \leq \bigl\lfloor c'_l(k, j, t, q, h, e_{\mathrm{cl}}; x_-, x_+, U_N(\lambda, k + j); I) \bigr\rfloor.$$

Lean: `Formalism.CohomologicalDescent.DepthNonnegative.application_window`.

*Proof.* We build $\lambda|_N$ from $\lambda|_k$ in three steps, with Lemma D for the last block at each step. First, $j$ rows are added one at a time. Each row costs at most $\lfloor (h-1)/(l-1) \rfloor$; this is the one-row case of Lemma D for the last block, which applies because $\lambda(0) + N \leq h + \lambda(N-1) + 1$. This gives $\mathrm{Exp}_l(\lambda|_{k+j}, \mu) \leq e_{\mathrm{cl}} + j \lfloor (h-1)/(l-1) \rfloor$ for all $\mu$. Second, the block of $t$ equal rows is added in one step. Its cost is the largest $v_l(D_n(t, \lambda(0) - \lambda(k+j), i, \lambda^*))$ over $i \leq I$, where $\lambda^*(b) = \lambda(0) - \lambda(k+j-1-b)$; the bound holds for the weights $\mu$ with $d_{\varpi_{k+j}}(\lambda|_{k+j+t}, \mu) \leq I$. Third, the last $q$ rows are added one at a time with $\xi = \varpi_{k+j}$, which is non-increasing, for a cost of $q \lfloor (h-1)/(l-1) \rfloor$. It remains to bound the cost of the block. Shift the window by $q$: for $a < k + j$, $U_N(\lambda, a) - q$ is the shifted entry of $\lambda|_{k+j}$ in a shape of $k + j + t$ rows, and $U_N(\lambda, k+j) - q$ is the largest shifted entry of the block. The window conditions, with $h < l^2$, are then the hypotheses of a count of the $l$-adic valuations of the factors of $D_n$. This count gives a cost of at most $j(h-1)/(l-1) + \Lambda_l$ with the window shifted by $q$, and $\Lambda_l$ is unchanged when $x_-$, $x_+$ and $m$ are shifted together. Adding the three costs and using $\lfloor (h-1)/(l-1) \rfloor \leq (h-1)/(l-1)$ gives a total of at most $c'_l$. Since $\mathrm{Exp}_l \leq e$ implies $\mathrm{Exp}_l \leq e'$ for $e \leq e'$, and the total is an integer, the floor of $c'_l$ bounds the exponent. $\square$

**Definition 4.3.4 (Forms with values in a lattice).** Let $O$ be a commutative ring, $A$ an $O$-algebra, $G$ a group with subgroups $\Gamma$ and $K$, and $j_K : K \to A$ a homomorphism of monoids. For an $A$-module $V$, let $\mathcal{F}(V)$ be the $O$-module of maps $f : G \to V$ with $f(\gamma g \kappa) = j_K(\kappa^{-1}) f(g)$ for $\gamma \in \Gamma$, $\kappa \in K$. Let $T$ be a commutative $O$-algebra acting on $\mathcal{F}(V)$ (the Hecke operators) and $S \subseteq T$ a multiplicative set. For a sub-$O$-module $U \subseteq V$, let $\mathcal{F}(V; U)$ be the forms with values in $U$. For a finite set $\iota$ and sub-modules $B_\tau \subseteq N_\tau$, write $\bigotimes B$ for the image of $\bigotimes_O B_\tau$ in $\bigotimes_O N_\tau$. For $s \subseteq \iota$ let $B^{(s)}$ be the family with $B^{(s)}_\tau = N_\tau$ for $\tau \in s$ and $B_\tau$ otherwise.

**Definition 4.3.5 (Inclusion at the top).** Let $\iota$ be finite, $N \in \mathbb{N}$, $\lambda_\tau : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ for $\tau \in \iota$, and $V = \bigotimes_{\tau} \nabla_O(\lambda_\tau|_N)$, an $A$-module. Let $V^\circ = \bigotimes \Delta^\circ$ with $\Delta^\circ_\tau = \Delta_O(\lambda_\tau|_N) \cap \nabla_O(\lambda_\tau|_N)$. For $J \in \mathbb{N}$, we say that the *inclusion at the top holds with exponent $J$* if for every $y \in S^{-1}\mathcal{F}(V)$ there are $x \in \mathcal{F}(V; V^\circ)$ and $s \in S$ with $l^J y = x / s$.

If the inclusion holds with exponent $J$, it holds with every $J' \geq J$: multiply $x$ by $l^{J' - J}$.

**Theorem 4.3.6 (Weight theorem, core form).** Let $O$ be a commutative ring, $\iota$ a finite set, $N_\tau$ $O$-modules with sub-modules $B_\tau$, and $V = \bigotimes_O N_\tau$ an $A$-module. Let $l, \varpi \in O$, $n_0 \in \mathbb{N}$, and $\mathrm{pr}_\tau(w)$ endomorphisms of $N_\tau$ indexed by a set $\Omega$. Let $e_\tau(w) \in A$, let $(L_\mu)_{\mu \in \Lambda}$ be $A$-modules, $\mathrm{hw}(\mu, \tau) \in \Omega$, $\delta_\tau : \Omega \to \mathbb{Z}$, $i \in \mathbb{Z}^\iota$, $T_2 \subseteq \iota$, and $\mathrm{ex}, c' \in \mathbb{N}^\iota$. Assume: $V$ is Noetherian and $V / \bigotimes B$ is Artinian over $O$; $l \mid \varpi^{n_0}$; $A$ preserves each $\bigotimes B^{(s)}$; $e_\tau(w)$ acts as $\mathrm{pr}_\tau(w)$ on the factor $\tau$; the Hecke operators preserve forms with values in each $A$-submodule and commute with $A$-linear maps; every simple subquotient of $V / \varpi V$ is isomorphic to some $L_\mu$; each $e_\tau(\mathrm{hw}(\mu, \tau))$ is nonzero on $L_\mu$; if $L_\mu$ is a subquotient of $V / \varpi V$ and $S^{-1}\mathcal{F}(L_\mu) \neq 0$, then $\delta_\tau(\mathrm{hw}(\mu, \tau)) \leq i_\tau$ for $\tau \in T_2$; $l^{\mathrm{ex}(\tau)} N_\tau \subseteq B_\tau$ for all $\tau$; and $l^{c'(\tau)} \mathrm{pr}_\tau(w) N_\tau \subseteq B_\tau$ for $\tau \in T_2$ and $\delta_\tau(w) \leq i_\tau$. Put $c_\tau = \min(\mathrm{ex}(\tau), c'(\tau))$ for $\tau \in T_2$ and $c_\tau = \mathrm{ex}(\tau)$ otherwise. Then $l^{\sum_\tau c_\tau}\, S^{-1}\mathcal{F}(V)$ lies in the image of $\mathcal{F}(V; \bigotimes B)$.

Lean: `Formalism.CohomologicalDescent.DepthNonnegative.theorem_w_central_of_simple_quotients` (also `Formalism.CohomologicalDescent.DepthNonnegative.theorem_w_central_holds`).

*Proof.* Order $\iota$ and replace the factors $N_\tau$ by $B_\tau$ one at a time, along the chain of lattices $\bigotimes B^{(s)}$. At the factor $a$, if $c_a = \mathrm{ex}(a)$, then $l^{\mathrm{ex}(a)}$ maps $N_a$ into $B_a$ and so moves a form one step down the chain. If $c_a = c'(a) < \mathrm{ex}(a)$, then $a \in T_2$, and we run a dévissage over a composition series between the two lattices. This series exists because $V$ is Noetherian and the quotient is Artinian. Since $l \mid \varpi^{n_0}$, the module $\varpi^{n_0 \sum \mathrm{ex}} V$ lies in $\bigotimes B$, so each simple factor is a simple subquotient of a module between $\varpi^n V$ and $V$, hence a subquotient of $V/\varpi V$, hence some $L_\mu$. If $S^{-1}\mathcal{F}(L_\mu) = 0$, the forms pass this factor after multiplication by an element of $S$. Otherwise the depth of $\mathrm{hw}(\mu, a)$ is at most $i_a$, and $l^{c'(a)} \mathrm{pr}_a$ carries the factor into $B_a$. The total cost is $l^{\sum c_\tau}$. $\square$

**Definition 4.3.7 (Inputs at the top).** Fix $O$, $A$, $G$, $\Gamma$, $K$, $j_K$, $T$, $S$ as above, with $O$ a Noetherian domain, a finite set $\iota$, $l, N, k, t, h \in \mathbb{N}$, $\lambda_\tau$, $T_2 \subseteq \iota$, $j, q, e_{\mathrm{cl}}, \mathrm{ex}, i \in \mathbb{N}^\iota$, $x_\pm \in \mathbb{Z}^\iota$, $\varpi \in O$, $n_0 \in \mathbb{N}$, elements $e_\tau(w) \in A$ for $w \in \mathbb{N}^N$, $A$-modules $L_\mu$ and $\mathrm{hw}(\mu, \tau) \in \mathbb{N}^N$. The *inputs at the top* are:

1. $l$ is prime, every integer prime to $l$ is a unit of $O$, $O / lO$ is finite, $l \mid \varpi^{n_0}$, $h < l^2$ and $t \geq 1$;
2. for $\tau \in T_2$, the data $(N, k, j_\tau, t, q_\tau, h, \lambda_\tau, x_{-,\tau}, x_{+,\tau})$ have window shape, and $\mathrm{Exp}_l(\lambda_\tau|_k, \mu) \leq e_{\mathrm{cl}}(\tau)$ for all $\mu \in \mathbb{N}^k$;
3. for every $\tau$ there is an integer $m$ prime to $l$ with $m\, l^{\mathrm{ex}(\tau)} \nabla_{\mathbb{Z}}(\lambda_\tau|_N) \subseteq \Delta_{\mathbb{Z}}(\lambda_\tau|_N)$;
4. $A$ preserves each lattice $\bigotimes (\Delta^\circ)^{(s)}$;
5. $e_\tau(w)$ replaces the factor $\tau$ of a pure tensor by its component of row weight $w$;
6. the Hecke conditions and the conditions on the $L_\mu$ of the core form, with $\delta_\tau(w) = d_{\varpi_{k + j_\tau}}(\lambda_\tau|_N, w)$.

**Theorem 4.3.8 (Weight theorem at the top).** Assume Lemma D for the last block and the inputs at the top. For $\tau \in T_2$ let $c'_\tau = \lfloor c'_l(k, j_\tau, t, q_\tau, h, e_{\mathrm{cl}}(\tau); x_{-,\tau}, x_{+,\tau}, U_N(\lambda_\tau, k + j_\tau); i_\tau) \rfloor$, and let $c_\tau = \min(\mathrm{ex}(\tau), c'_\tau)$ for $\tau \in T_2$ and $c_\tau = \mathrm{ex}(\tau)$ otherwise. Then the inclusion at the top holds with exponent $\sum_\tau c_\tau$, and the least exponent for which it holds is at most $\sum_\tau c_\tau$.

Lean: `Formalism.CohomologicalDescent.DepthNonnegative.weight_theorem_top` (also `Formalism.CohomologicalDescent.DepthNonnegative.theorem_w_top_of_simple_quotients`, the form with the two facts on subquotients as hypotheses).

*Proof.* We apply the core form with $N_\tau = \nabla_O(\lambda_\tau|_N)$, $B_\tau = \Delta^\circ_\tau$, and $\mathrm{pr}_\tau(w)$ the projection to row weight $w$, which preserves $\nabla_O$ because $\nabla$ is graded by row weight. Input 3 gives $l^{\mathrm{ex}(\tau)} \nabla_O \subseteq \Delta^\circ_\tau$, since $m$ is a unit in $O$. The tensor product of these inclusions gives $l^{\sum \mathrm{ex}} V \subseteq V^\circ$. So $V / V^\circ$ is a finitely generated module over $O / l^{\sum \mathrm{ex}} O$, which is finite since $O$ is Noetherian and $O / lO$ is finite. Hence $V/V^\circ$ is finite and Artinian, and $V$ is Noetherian. Input 5 says that $e_\tau(w)$ acts by $\mathrm{pr}_\tau(w)$ on the factor $\tau$. For $\tau \in T_2$ and $w$ with $\delta_\tau(w) \leq i_\tau$, the [application window](/entry/application-window/) gives $\mathrm{Exp}_l(\lambda_\tau|_N, w) \leq c'_\tau$. Since integers prime to $l$ are units in $O$, this gives $l^{c'_\tau} \mathrm{pr}_\tau(w) x \in \Delta^\circ_\tau$ for all $x \in \nabla_O$. The core form now gives the inclusion with exponent $\sum c_\tau$, and the second claim follows from the first. The two facts on subquotients (a simple subquotient of a module between $\varpi^n V$ and $V$ is a subquotient of $V / \varpi V$, and being a subquotient is preserved by isomorphism) are proved separately. $\square$

**Lemma 4.3.9 (Short constant).** For $l \geq 2$ and $I \geq 0$, $c'_l \leq c^{\mathrm{sh}}_l$, for all values of the other arguments and all $N'$.

Lean: `Formalism.CohomologicalDescent.ShortWeightBounds.c_prime_le_short_bound_holds`.

*Proof.* For $I \geq 0$ we have $H_l(h, I) \leq (I/l)(1 + \log^+(h/l))$: if $h < l$ the left side is $0$, and otherwise $\log(h/l) = \log^+(h/l)$. Since $l - 1 > 0$ and $h - 1 \leq h + N'$, we get $(q + 2j)(h-1)/(l-1) \leq (q + 2j)(h + N')/(l-1)$. The other terms agree. $\square$

**Theorem 4.3.10 (Weight theorem at the top, short constant).** Under the hypotheses of the weight theorem at the top, the same two claims hold with $c'_\tau$ replaced by $\lfloor c^{\mathrm{sh}}_l(\dots) \rfloor$ with $N' = N$ and the same other arguments.

Lean: `Formalism.CohomologicalDescent.ShortWeightBounds.theorem_w_top_short_bound_holds`.

*Proof.* By the [weight theorem at the top](/entry/weight-theorem-top/), the inclusion holds with exponent $\sum c_\tau$. Since $l$ is prime, $l \geq 2$, and the lemma on the short constant gives $c'_\tau \leq \lfloor c^{\mathrm{sh}}_l \rfloor$; so $\min(\mathrm{ex}, c'_\tau)$ is at most the new term. The inclusion is monotone in the exponent, which gives the first claim. The second claim follows from the first. $\square$
