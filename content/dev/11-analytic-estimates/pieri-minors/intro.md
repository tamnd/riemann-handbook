---
title: Introduction
---
This chapter proves two groups of results. The first group is about integral forms of Weyl modules. Inside a representation of $\mathfrak{gl}_{p+q}$ there are two lattices, the Weyl lattice $\Delta$ and its dual $\Delta^\vee$, and $\Delta \subseteq \Delta^\vee$. A vector of $\Delta^\vee$ of a given weight lands in $\Delta$ after multiplication by an explicit power of $l$, with an exponent read off from the contents of the Young diagrams that the Pieri rule allows (Sections 1 and 2). The weight theorem at the top turns this into an inclusion of the forms with values in the dual Weyl modules into the forms with values in the Weyl lattices (Section 3). Sections 4 and 5 specialize it to the weights of the proof: for every prime $l$ above a threshold, $l^{J}$ moves the first space of forms into the second, with a short and then a coarse exponent $J$, and the weight inputs come from lattices with a Frobenius matrix. The proofs need [partitions and Young tableaux](/learn/combinatorics/#partitions-and-young-tableaux), [binomial coefficients and divided powers](/learn/combinatorics/#binomial-coefficients-factorials-and-divided-powers), [minors and determinant identities](/learn/combinatorics/#minors-and-determinant-identities), [the Pieri rule](/learn/combinatorics/#the-pieri-and-littlewood-richardson-rules), [highest weights](/learn/representations/#highest-weights-and-branching) and [lattices over rings](/learn/algebra/#rings-ideals-and-modules) from Volume 1, and Section 5 uses the [lattices of Wach and Breuil-Kisin](/learn/padic-hodge/#lattices-wach-breuil-kisin-and-prismatic-f-crystals) as background.

The second group is a row bound (Section 6). The terms of the row estimates carry a [hermitian lattice](/learn/hermitian-lattices/#lattices-in-quadratic-and-hermitian-spaces) and two flags, and two terms of a pair are glued by a linear map. When the gluing has corank one, [Theorem B](/entry/theorem-b-section/) makes one of the two terms small, and a weighted count over fibres bounds the sum over all such pairs. Both groups are used in [the step that makes the record of unitary hypotheses](/step/nonempty-hyps/), through its rows and frames: the first through [the inclusion of models](/entry/l-incl-of-models/), and the second through [the theorem on the rows of corank one](/entry/rows-one-of/).
