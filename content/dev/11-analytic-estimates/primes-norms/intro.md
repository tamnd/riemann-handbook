title: Introduction

This chapter proves two groups of results. The first group is analytic. For a prime $d \equiv 3 \pmod 4$ and a modulus $M$, an interval $(X, X(1+h)]$ contains at least $c'hX/((4dM^2)^A\log X)$ primes represented by a norm form of the [[quadratic field|7SV4WA92SM]] $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-d})$ with a congruence condition modulo $M$, as soon as $h \geq \exp(-c\sqrt{\log X})$ and $\log(4dM^2) \leq c\sqrt{\log X}$ (Sections 1 to 3). For $M \geq 3$, $(b, M) = 1$ and $u \geq M^{c_2}$, the sum $\varphi(M)\psi(u; M, b)$ is $u - \theta u^\beta/\beta$ up to a small relative error, where $\beta$ is a possible exceptional zero and $\theta$ is the value of its character at $b$; the same holds for the number of primes (Sections 4 to 6). The proofs need [Dirichlet characters](/learn/l-functions/#dirichlet-characters), [Dirichlet L-functions](/learn/l-functions/#dirichlet-l-functions) and [their zeros](/learn/analytic-number-theory/#zeros-of-dirichlet-l-functions), [the explicit formula](/learn/analytic-number-theory/#the-explicit-formula), [primes in progressions and in short intervals](/learn/analytic-number-theory/#primes-in-progressions-and-in-short-intervals), [primes represented by norm forms](/learn/analytic-number-theory/#primes-represented-by-norm-forms) and [Hecke characters](/learn/class-field-theory/#hecke-characters) from Volume 1.

The second group is about volumes. Let $W$ be a hermitian space of rank $M \geq 3$, with the same signature at every real place, over a CM field $E/F$ that is unramified at all finite places. Then $W$ has a vector $x$ of length $\pm 1$ for which the ratio $\mathrm{vol}(x^\perp)/\mathrm{vol}(W)$ is $((2\pi)^M/(M-1)!)^{[F:\mathbb{Q}]}|d_F|^{1/2 - M}/L_\eta(M)$. This holds under the hypothesis of a one-vector Siegel-Weil formula (Sections 7 to 9). The proof needs [Haar measure](/learn/analysis/#haar-measure), [norms and hermitian forms](/learn/class-field-theory/#norms-and-hermitian-forms), [local densities](/learn/hermitian-lattices/#local-densities) and [Siegel's mass formula and Tamagawa measures](/learn/hermitian-lattices/#siegels-mass-formula-and-tamagawa-measures) from Volume 1. Both groups of results are used in [the step on the characters of the literature](/step/every-character-of-literature/): the first through [the short interval norm theorem](/entry/short-norm/) and the count of primes in progressions, and the second as the volume clause of [the record of hypotheses](/step/hyps-alt-def/).
