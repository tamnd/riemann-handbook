## The built bounds at the infinite places

This section collects the results of the chapter into one statement on the infinite places. Under the compact form of [Theorem P](/entry/actual-compact-theorem/), a setting in leading form has positive weights, integrable densities, wave and lift bounds by places, and a sum form bound with a constant $C_4$ that depends only on the bound $C_b$ of the word operators. The proof replaces the vectors $f_v$ by compactly supported cutoffs, proves the bounds for the cutoffs with the [top degree identity](/entry/degree-top/) and local statements, and passes to the limit. For the background on $L^2$ estimates see [$L^2$ spaces and the Cauchy Schwarz inequality](/learn/analysis/#l2-spaces-and-the-cauchy-schwarz-inequality) and [Sobolev norms](/learn/analysis/#sobolev-norms).

**Definition 3.10.1 (Leading form setting).** Let $C_b, C_c \in \mathbb{R}$, let $E$ be a CM field and $S$ a setting over $E$ with places $p_v$ and vectors $f_v$, let $\chi$ be a cutoff and $A_0 \in \mathbb{R}$. The data are a *leading form setting* for $(C_b, C_c)$ if: $g_1, g_2 > 0$; $E$ is unramified in the required sense; $\chi$ is a cutoff of the standard shape; each place of the setting is admissible; the weight words $d, d'$ are words with $d_l \neq d'_l$ at the level $l$, where $1 \leq l$ and $l + 2 \leq k$; the grids are admissible and rich; the cut is long for $C_c$ and $C_c \leq C_A$; the budget
$$C_c (r + 1)^4 \big(N (2r + k + 1)^2 + (2r + k + 1)^3\big) \leq A_0$$
holds; and for each $v$ the place $p_v$ starts at the level $l + 1$, its word operators satisfy the standing bounds with constant $C_b$, the family of the setting at $v$ is the component family built from $p_v$, $f_v$ and $\chi$, and $f_v$ is smooth with all its derivatives bounded in terms of $A_0$.

For a finite set $Y$ of points, a kernel $\kappa$ on $Y$ is positive semidefinite in the sense of [the first section](/entry/local-cores/).

**Definition 3.10.2 (Sum form).** Let $D$ be the data of the setting, $\varepsilon > 0$, $2 \leq s \leq i \leq r + 1$, $w$ a word and $M \in \mathbb{R}$. The *sum form* $\mathrm{SF}(\varepsilon, i, s, w, M)$ holds if there is a probability weight $\theta$ on the set of words $W_b(|w| + sK)$ such that for every finite set $Y$ of points at the level $i - s$, every positive semidefinite kernel $\kappa$ on $Y$, every $t_0 \in E$ and every finite set $T$ of nonzero $t \in E$ with $t - t_0$ in the lattice $\Lambda(i - s + 1, s)$,
$$\sum_{t \in T} \mathrm{Re}\, N^*_\varepsilon(i, s, w, Y, \kappa)(t, t) \leq M \sum_{w' \in W_b(|w| + sK)} \theta(w')\, N^0_\varepsilon(i - s, w', Y, \kappa).$$
The *concrete sum form* with constant $C_4$ holds if for all such $\varepsilon, i, s$ and all words $w$ with $|w| + (s + 1)K \leq N$ and $\mathrm{vol}(i - s + 1, s) \leq 1$, the sum form holds with some
$$M \leq C_4^{f s} \prod_{s'=1}^{s-1} \mathrm{vol}(i - s + 1, s').$$

The sum form compares the diagonal of the shifted kernel $N^*$ at the points of a coset of a lattice with an average of unshifted kernels $N^0$. The factor $\prod \mathrm{vol}$ is the covolume of the lattices of the intermediate levels. The concrete sum form is the input of the recursion of [Theorem A](/entry/theorem-a-after/) at the infinite places.

**Lemma 3.10.3 (Sizes product).** Let $f \in \mathbb{N}$, $s \geq 2$, $C_4 \geq 1$, $0 \leq A, B \leq C_4$, and let $M_0, M_1, \dots, M_{s-1}$ and $\mathrm{vol}_1, \dots, \mathrm{vol}_{s-1}$ be nonnegative with $M_0 \leq A^f$ and $M_n \leq B^f\, \mathrm{vol}_n$ for $1 \leq n \leq s - 1$. Then $M_0 \prod_{n=1}^{s-1} M_n \leq C_4^{fs} \prod_{n=1}^{s-1} \mathrm{vol}_n$.

*Proof.* All the numbers are nonnegative, so the bounds multiply:
$$M_0 \prod_n M_n \leq A^f\, B^{f(s-1)} \prod_n \mathrm{vol}_n \leq C_4^f\, C_4^{f(s-1)} \prod_n \mathrm{vol}_n = C_4^{fs} \prod_n \mathrm{vol}_n. \qquad \square$$

**Theorem 3.10.4 (Sum form from local statements).** There is $C_4 \geq 1$ with the following property. Let a setting over a CM field have $g_1, g_2 > 0$, be unramified, have positive weights, admissible grids and integrable densities. Let $X_i(v)$ and $\Lambda(b, s, v)$ be numbers such that the wave bound holds at each place with $X$, the curvature barriers hold for $X$, the lift bound holds at each place with $\Lambda$, $0 \leq \Lambda(b, s, v) \leq \mathrm{sz}(b, s, v)$ for $1 \leq b, s$ and $b + s \leq r + 1$, the thresholds are positive, and the germ bound holds at each place. Then the concrete sum form holds with constant $C_4$.

Lean: `Formalism.FourierDecayAlt.P.sum_form_of_local_statements` (also `Formalism.FourierDecayAlt.P.PBC.sumFormOfLoc`).

*Proof.* Three constants come from three global statements: $C_s \geq 1$ from a bound on standard lattice sums, $B \geq 1$ from a bound on the supremum of the sizes, and $A \geq 1$ from a bound on the block lattice volumes. Put $C_4 = \max(A, B)$. Fix the data and $\varepsilon, i, s, w$. The wave bound, the barriers and the lift bound give, for each place $v$, the comparison of $N^*$ with $N^0$ one level at a time. The positivity of $\kappa$ and of the weights lets one multiply these comparisons, and the germ bound controls the remainder. This gives the sum form with $M = M_0 \prod_{n=1}^{s-1} M_n$, where $M_0 \leq A^f$ is the block lattice factor and $M_n \leq B^f \mathrm{vol}(i - s + 1, n)$ comes from the sizes at the level $n$ and the bound $\Lambda \leq \mathrm{sz}$. The Lemma on sizes products gives $M \leq C_4^{fs} \prod \mathrm{vol}$. $\square$

**Lemma 3.10.5 (Limits of positive kernels).** Let $Y$ be a finite set, and let $k_n$ be positive semidefinite kernels on $Y$ with $k_n(x, y) \to k(x, y)$ for all $x, y \in Y$. Then $k$ is positive semidefinite.

*Proof.* Hermitian symmetry passes to the limit: $k(y, x) = \lim k_n(y, x) = \lim \overline{k_n(x, y)} = \overline{k(x, y)}$, by continuity of conjugation. For $c \in \mathbb{C}^Y$ the quadratic form $\sum_{x, y} \bar c_x k_n(x, y) c_y$ is a finite sum, so its real part tends to that of $k$. Each real part is nonnegative, so the limit is nonnegative. $\square$

**Theorem 3.10.6 (Built bounds from parts).** Assume the following local statements: the wave bound, the lift bound, the top local bound, the [top degree identity](/entry/degree-top/), the types of the local vectors, the top ratio bound, the germ bound, a positivity bound, an integral bound, uniform decay and positive thresholds. Then for each $C_b$ there are $C_c$ and $C_4 \geq 1$ such that every leading form setting for $(C_b, C_c)$ has positive weights, integrable densities, a wave function $X$ with the wave bound by places and the curvature barriers, lift functions $\Lambda, \Lambda'$ with the lift bound below the top, the top diagonal bound and the lift thresholds, and the concrete sum form with constant $C_4$.

Lean: `Formalism.FourierDecayAlt.P.PBASM.core` (also `Formalism.FourierDecayAlt.P.PBASM.builtBounds2CptOfParts`, the form with the compact form of Theorem P as an extra hypothesis of each part).

*Proof.* Let $C_4$ be the constant of the Theorem on the sum form, and let $c_1, \dots, c_8$ be the constants of the limit of module forms and of the parts for $C_b$. Put $C_c = \max(c_1, \dots, c_8, 28e)$, and fix a leading form setting for $(C_b, C_c)$. For $n \in \mathbb{N}$ let
$$\mathrm{cut}_n(g) = \lambda\Big(\frac{4 - \|g\|^2/(n + 1)^2}{3}\Big),$$
where $\lambda$ is a smooth step function, $0$ on $(-\infty, 0]$ and $1$ on $[1, \infty)$, and $\|g\|$ is the Hilbert Schmidt norm of the matrix $g$. So $\mathrm{cut}_n = 1$ on $\|g\| \leq n + 1$ and $\mathrm{cut}_n = 0$ on $\|g\| \geq 2(n + 1)$. Let $f_{n, v, \varepsilon}$ be the cut of $f_v$ at $\varepsilon$ multiplied by $\mathrm{cut}_n$.

Each $f_{n, v, \varepsilon}$ has compactly supported coefficients. The limit of module forms, which uses the budget on $A_0$ and the bounds on the derivatives of $f_v$, shows that for every cut word $a$ of length at most $N$ and all points $P, Q$ the module form $\mathrm{RR}(f_{n, v, \varepsilon}, i, a, P, Q)$ tends to $\mathrm{RR}$ of the cut of $f_v$ as $n \to \infty$. Up to the positive factor $(b(P) b(Q) \Gamma_i)^{-1}$, this limit is the module form of the family of the setting at $v$. So the forms of the setting are limits of forms of compactly supported functions.

For compactly supported functions the parts give the bounds. The top local bound and the top degree identity bound the top letters, the top ratio bound and the types of the local vectors bound the ratios of the top kernels, and the wave, lift and germ bounds hold by hypothesis. The positivity bound gives positive weights, and the integral bound with uniform decay gives integrable densities. Each conclusion is a family of inequalities and of positive semidefinite kernels, with constants that do not depend on $n$. Inequalities pass to the limit, and positive semidefinite kernels pass to the limit by the Lemma on limits of positive kernels. The Theorem on the sum form, applied with the wave function, the lift functions and the germ bound of the limit, gives the concrete sum form. The term $28e$ in $C_c$ is a further lower bound that the Lean proof uses; we did not trace where. $\square$

**Theorem 3.10.7 (Built bounds from leaves).** Assume the radial head statement, the [head top formula](/entry/pb-head-top-of-mk-top/), the degree of the lift pairing, the top local bound, the top degree identity, the top ratio bound, the germ parts with compact support, and that the compact form of Theorem P implies uniform decay. Then the compact form of Theorem P implies the conclusion of the previous Theorem.

Lean: `Formalism.FourierDecayAlt.P.PBASM.builtBounds2CptOfLeaves` (also `Formalism.FourierDecayAlt.P.built_bounds_compact_of_leaves`).

*Proof.* Assume the compact form of Theorem P. With the radial head and the head top formula it gives a decay of standard pairings. That decay with uniform decay gives the integral bound. The wave bound comes from the decay of standard pairings, the lift bound from the degree of the lift pairing, and the germ bound from the integral bound and the germ parts. The types of the local vectors, the positivity bound and the positive thresholds are proved results. The previous Theorem ends the proof. $\square$

**Theorem 3.10.8 (Built bounds).** For each $C_b$ there are $C_c$ and $C_4 \geq 1$ with the following property. Assume the compact form of Theorem P. Then every leading form setting for $(C_b, C_c)$ has positive weights, integrable densities, wave and lift functions with the bounds by places, and the concrete sum form with constant $C_4$.

Lean: `Formalism.FourierDecayAlt.P.built_bounds_compact` (also `Formalism.FourierDecayAlt.P.pb_builtBounds2Cpt_of_mkTop`, `Formalism.FourierDecayAlt.P.built_bounds_compact_of_head_top` and `Formalism.FourierDecayAlt.Final.pb_builtBounds2Cpt`).

*Proof.* The top truncation of averages holds: for each place there are smooth compactly supported functions $b, b'$, a function $\beta$ smooth on the invertible matrices and $\kappa \neq 0$ that truncate the top averages on compact sets. It gives the head top formula. The radial head statement, the degree of the lift pairing, the top local bound, the top ratio bound and the germ parts hold, the top degree identity is the [Theorem 3.9.6](/entry/degree-top/) of the previous section, and uniform decay follows from the compact form of Theorem P through the decay of the heads. The previous Theorem gives the claim. $\square$

The compact form of Theorem P is itself a [theorem](/entry/actual-compact-theorem/), so [the built bounds](/entry/pb-built-bounds2-cpt/) hold without hypothesis when they are used.

## Notes and references

The chapter proves the analytic estimates at the infinite places of the CM field and the kernel inputs at the finite places. Its results reach the main argument by three paths. [The local cores](/entry/local-cores/), [the global cores](/entry/global-cores/) and [Theorem A](/entry/theorem-a-after/) are the kernel inputs of [the applied Theorem PB](/entry/pb-applied/). [The model places](/entry/model-place-built-holds/) and [Lemma E in the model](/entry/lemma-model/) give the limit of the energy at each infinite place. [The built bounds](/entry/pb-built-bounds2-cpt/) give the bounds by places and the sum form. The last two, with [the compact form of Theorem P](/entry/actual-compact-theorem/), make up [the carrier inputs from D](/entry/carrier-needs-from-d/), which lead to the trunk step [HypsAlt is nonempty](/step/nonempty-hyps/).

The closure rules for positive semidefinite kernels and the Schur product theorem are in R. A. Horn and C. R. Johnson, *Matrix Analysis*, 2nd ed., Chapter 7, Theorem 7.5.3. The local fields, their lattices and their Haar measures in the block kernel follow J. Neukirch, *Algebraic Number Theory*, Chapter II, and G. B. Folland, *A Course in Abstract Harmonic Analysis*, Chapter 2. The Markov inequality of Lemma E is in P. Borwein and T. Erdélyi, *Polynomials and Polynomial Inequalities*, Chapter 5, Section 5.1, and the Cauchy estimate in L. V. Ahlfors, *Complex Analysis*, 3rd ed., Chapter 4, Section 2.3. The interpolation formulas of the top degree identity are in P. J. Davis, *Interpolation and Approximation*, Chapter II. The decomposition of a real group along a section, which the radial decomposition makes infinitesimal, is the $KAK$ decomposition of A. W. Knapp, *Lie Groups Beyond an Introduction*, 2nd ed., Chapter VII, Section 3. Elliptic regularity for systems with smooth coefficients is in L. Hörmander, *The Analysis of Linear Partial Differential Operators I*, 2nd ed., Chapter VIII, Section 8.3.
