title: Introduction
---
This chapter proves the bounds on the energy of curvature operators that the proof needs at the places of a CM field. Sections 1 to 4 treat kernels: Section 1 gives the closure rules for [[positive definite|0KX66CZMBB]] kernels, Section 2 computes the block kernel of a [[local field|J85FZEQG38]] and its Gram property, Section 3 proves Theorem A for towers of kernels, and Section 4 bounds the local density by $2$ and collects the global cores. Sections 5 and 6 prove Lemma E, the limit of an energy along a cutoff, first for abstract energy systems and then in the model on an open set of $\mathbb{C}^{r+1}$. Sections 7 to 10 work at the infinite places: Section 7 proves the polynomial radial relation for the second shift, Section 8 shows that each built place is a model place, Section 9 proves the top degree identity by Lagrange interpolation, and Section 10 assembles the built bounds and the sum form. From Volume 1 the chapter uses [Haar measure](/learn/analysis/#haar-measure), [$L^2$ spaces and the Cauchy Schwarz inequality](/learn/analysis/#l2-spaces-and-the-cauchy-schwarz-inequality), [Sobolev norms](/learn/analysis/#sobolev-norms), [Lie groups and Lie algebras](/learn/analysis/#lie-groups-and-lie-algebras), [matrices](/learn/algebra/#matrices), [polynomials](/learn/algebra/#polynomials-and-power-series), [local fields](/learn/galois-cohomology/#local-fields), [discrete valuation rings and lattices](/learn/commutative-algebra/#discrete-valuation-rings-and-lattices), [unitary groups](/learn/groups/#unitary-groups), [real reductive groups](/learn/real-groups/#real-reductive-groups) and [representations of real groups](/learn/representations/#representations-of-real-groups).

Sections 1 to 4 feed [the applied Theorem PB](/entry/pb-applied/) through [the local cores](/entry/local-cores/) and [the global cores](/entry/global-cores/). Sections 5 to 8 give [the model places](/entry/model-place-built-holds/), and Sections 9 and 10 give [the built bounds](/entry/pb-built-bounds2-cpt/). Both reach [the carrier inputs from D](/entry/carrier-needs-from-d/), and through them the step [HypsAlt is nonempty](/step/nonempty-hyps/) of the proof.
