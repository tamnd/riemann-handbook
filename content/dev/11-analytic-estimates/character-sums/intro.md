title: Introduction
---
This chapter proves the estimates on [[Dirichlet characters|JR4QSEFXWF]] and their [[L-functions|24GAZHVTGB]] that the proof takes from the literature, together with four estimates of a different kind that are used at single places. Section 1 gives a zero density bound and a Deuring-Heilbronn bound, and Section 2 gives the prime number theorem for progressions with a relative error. Section 3 proves the explicit formula for $\psi(x, \chi)$. Sections 4 to 6 bound thin sums of characters, mean squares over moduli and the number of good pairs of primes. Section 7 bounds the mean number of bad $p$-adic invariant factors on a progression grid. Section 8 writes operators on homogeneous polynomials as short words in $x_a\partial_b$, bounds jets at a ramified prime, proves weighted square integrability on $\mathrm{U}(p, q)$, and bounds the share of bad tuples of random polynomials over a finite field. From Volume 1 the chapter uses [Dirichlet characters](/learn/l-functions/#dirichlet-characters), [Dirichlet L-functions](/learn/l-functions/#dirichlet-l-functions), [the zeros of Dirichlet L-functions](/learn/analytic-number-theory/#zeros-of-dirichlet-l-functions), [the explicit formula](/learn/analytic-number-theory/#the-explicit-formula), [mean values and the large sieve](/learn/analytic-number-theory/#mean-values-and-the-large-sieve), [Gauss and Jacobi sums](/learn/analytic-number-theory/#gauss-and-jacobi-sums), [discrete valuation rings](/learn/commutative-algebra/#discrete-valuation-rings-and-lattices), [highest weights](/learn/representations/#highest-weights-and-branching) and [finite fields](/learn/algebra/#finite-fields).

Sections 1, 2, 5 and 6 lead to the good pairs of primes, which feed [the supply of admissible data](/entry/supply-of-adm-of/). Sections 3 and 4 lead to the thin bound for a host, which feeds [the host theorem](/entry/host-of-field-of/). Section 7 feeds [the top theorem](/entry/top-theorem-mv7/), which leads through [the alternative mean value theorem](/entry/alternative-mean-value-theorem/) to [the rest theorem](/entry/rest-tm-exists/). The first three results of Section 8 feed [the operator weight bound](/entry/operator-weight-bound-holds/), [the entry divisor bound](/entry/entry-divisor-bound-holds/) and [the discrete series input](/entry/r0b-g/). Property W of Section 8 is used directly in the step [EveryCharacter from the literature](/step/every-character-of-literature/), which gives the [[generalized Riemann hypothesis|DNKA4WBKDS]] for Dirichlet L-functions in the form the proof needs.
