title: Introduction
---
This chapter proves the lattice, valuation and formal group bounds that the proof needs for sharp restriction and for the mean number of bad invariant factors on a progression. Section 1 compares the Weyl lattice and the dual Weyl lattice of a representation of $\mathrm{GL}_r$, and Section 2 proves a saturation theorem and a restriction theorem for level lattices. Section 3 bounds the weight of a branching path of highest weights by binomial coefficients. Section 4 classifies the bilinear cocycles of a Lubin-Tate formal group of height $2$ and bounds the jets of theta kernels. Section 5 turns these into local jet bounds and puts them together over Frobenius orbits. Section 6 gives the column valuations, the slope of the ramified line, the size of the auxiliary prime and Theorem B for families of Hermitian lattices. Section 7 proves an independence bound for self-conjugate charts and builds the CM host field. Section 8 proves a $p$-adic theorem of the cube for the sigma function. From Volume 1 the chapter uses [partitions and Young tableaux](/learn/combinatorics/#partitions-and-young-tableaux), [minors and determinant identities](/learn/combinatorics/#minors-and-determinant-identities), [highest weights and branching](/learn/representations/#highest-weights-and-branching), [discrete valuation rings](/learn/commutative-algebra/#discrete-valuation-rings-and-lattices), [formal group laws](/learn/formal-groups/#formal-group-laws), [the logarithm of a formal group](/learn/formal-groups/#the-invariant-derivation-and-the-logarithm), [lattices in Hermitian spaces](/learn/hermitian-lattices/#lattices-in-quadratic-and-hermitian-spaces), [the splitting of primes](/learn/number-fields/#the-splitting-of-primes), [global class field theory](/learn/class-field-theory/#global-class-field-theory) and [the Weierstrass function](/learn/elliptic-curves/#lattices-and-the-weierstrass-function).

Sections 1, 4, 5 and 6 feed the step [theta and local matching](/step/theta-local-matching/). Section 2 feeds [the mean bad count](/entry/mc-theorem-mv7/), which leads to [the alternative mean value theorem](/entry/alternative-mean-value-theorem/). Section 5 also gives the sum over primes of [the closed service bound](/entry/closed-serv-f-of-serv-t-of-d-gam-size/). Section 3 feeds [the small weight of atypical paths](/entry/atypical-path-small-weight/), Section 7 feeds [the label set theorem](/entry/label-set-geo-of-even-of-rank-ok-of-not-dvd-discr/) and [the host field example](/entry/host-ax0/), and Section 8 feeds [the biadditivity of the pairing $\beta$](/entry/cube-add-int-be-biadd/).
