This theorem only connects two lemmas. It turns the base change fundamental lemma for the unit element into the local matching statement, and then calls `gm_of_unit` ([global matching from the unit](/entry/gm-of-unit/)), which has the real content. The result, the global matching statement `GM`, is used by [automorphic induction from the fundamental lemma](/entry/ind-cs-of-bcfl/) and by other proofs below [EveryCharacter from the Galois row](/step/every-character/).

## Statement

Let $\mathrm{BCFLUnit}$ be the following statement. Let $E/k$ be an unramified extension of nonarchimedean local fields of characteristic 0, Galois with cyclic group generated by $\sigma$. Normalize the Haar measures so that $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathcal{O}_E)$ and $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathcal{O}_k)$ have volume 1. Then the twisted orbital integral of the unit $\mathbf{1}_{\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathcal{O}_E)}$ at a twisted regular semisimple $\delta$ equals the orbital integral of $\mathbf{1}_{\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathcal{O}_k)}$ at the norm $\gamma$ of $\delta$, with matched measures on the centralizers. The orbital integral at a regular semisimple $\gamma$ that is not a norm is 0. This is the base change fundamental lemma for the unit element (Kottwitz, "Base change for unit elements of Hecke algebras", Compositio Math. 60, 1986).

**Theorem.** If $\mathrm{BCFLUnit}$ holds, then $\mathrm{GM}$ holds.

$\mathrm{GM}$ is stated in the [entry on global matching](/entry/gm-of-unit/).

## Proof

1. `locUnit_of` proves the local matching `LocUnit` from $\mathrm{BCFLUnit}$ and the split case `UnitSplit`. Let $E/F$ be a cyclic extension of number fields of prime degree and $v$ a finite place of $F$ unramified in $E$. If $v$ splits in $E$, then `UnitSplit` gives the matching. If not, then there is one place $w$ above $v$ (`placeUniq_holds`), and `placeUnit_holds` gets the matching from $\mathrm{BCFLUnit}$ for $E_w/F_v$.
2. `L1.unitSplit` proves `UnitSplit`.
3. `gm_of_unit` proves $\mathrm{GM}$ from `LocUnit`.

## In the Lean code

The theorem is in [GmOfBcfl.lean](lean:Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/Convolution/GeneralizedMeasures/GmOfBcfl.lean#L5). The lemmas: `locUnit_of` and `UnitSplit` in [LocUnit.lean](lean:Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/ModularityArgument/TraceFormula/OrbitalIntegrals/LocUnit.lean#L13), and `BCFLUnit` in [InvTransposeGLInvTransposeGL.lean](lean:Development/WeilBound/RepresentationTheoryGL/TwistedFactors/IntegralUnitBaseChange/InvTransposeGLInvTransposeGL.lean#L263). The code also proves $\mathrm{BCFLUnit}$ as `bcflUnit`, in [BcflUnit.lean](lean:Development/WeilBound/RepresentationTheoryGL/TwistedFactors/IntegralUnitBaseChange/BcflUnit.lean#L30).

The proof passes `L1.unitSplit` through a lambda that drops two arguments. The reason is that `L1.unitSplit` proves a different `UnitSplit`, in [ThickFrames.lean](lean:Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/Convolution/IntegrableConvolution/ThickFrames.lean#L54), without the hypotheses that the degree is prime and that $n > 0$. Two names of the code, `GM.gm` and `Sph.gm`, prove $\mathrm{GM}$ by the same term.
