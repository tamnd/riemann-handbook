This theorem computes an archimedean twisted trace (see [real groups](/learn/representations/#representations-of-real-groups)). A module with two commuting actions of $\mathfrak{gl}_n$ carries an involution $A$ that swaps them. On the multiplicity space of a fixed $K$-type, the twisted trace of $A$ is $\pm 2^n$. Two global trace identities use the result through `onePlace`, and from there it goes to [EveryCharacter from the Galois row](/step/every-character/).

## Statement

Let $\lambda \in \mathbb{Z}^n$ be non-increasing. Let $E$ be a finite-dimensional continuous unitary representation $\rho$ of $U(n)$, with an operator $T$ such that $T^2 = 1$, $T$ commutes with $\rho$, and the traces of $\rho(k)$ and $\rho(k)T$ at $k = \mathrm{diag}(z)$ with the $z_a^2$ distinct are $\prod_{a,b}(1 + z_a/z_b)\,\overline{\chi_\lambda(z)}^{\,2}$ and $\prod_{a,b}(1 + z_a/z_b)\,\overline{\chi_\lambda(z^2)}$. Here $\chi_\lambda$ is the Weyl character (the condition `IsLefK`).

Let $V$ be a complex inner product space with operators $E^1_{ab}$, $E^2_{ab}$, $A$ and a homomorphism $\sigma \colon U(n) \to \mathrm{End}(V)$. Suppose that (the structure `IsCoreMod`):

- each family satisfies the relations of $\mathfrak{gl}_n$, the two families commute, $E^1 + E^2$ is Hermitian and $E^1 - E^2$ is skew;
- the Gelfand elements of $E^1$ and of $\bar E^2$ act by the infinitesimal character $\pm(\lambda_a + \tfrac{n-1}{2} - a)$;
- $V \neq 0$, and the algebra generated by the $E^i_{ab}$ moves each nonzero vector to every vector;
- $\sigma$ is unitary and locally finite, with derivative $E^1 + E^2$;
- $A^2 = 1$, $A$ is an isometry, $A$ commutes with $\sigma$, and $A E^1_{ab} = E^2_{ab} A$.

Let $\mathrm{Hom}_{U(n)}(E^\vee, V)$ be the space of maps $t$ with $t(\xi \circ \rho(k)^{-1}) = \sigma(k)\,t(\xi)$. Let $A$ act on it by $t \mapsto A \circ t \circ T^\vee$.

**Theorem.** If $\mathrm{Hom}_{U(n)}(E^\vee, V)$ is finite-dimensional and nonzero, then the trace of this action is $s \cdot 2^n$ with $s = \pm 1$.

## Proof

The proof is `coreG_of` with three inputs.

1. **Counting** (`countG_holds`). Cut $\{1, \dots, n\}$ into consecutive blocks on which $\lambda$ is constant. For such a cut, let $r_a$ be half the number of indices in later blocks minus half the number in earlier blocks. Let $H$ be the finite set of the weights $2\lambda + 2r$. For each $\mu \in H$, the space $V_\mu$ of highest weight vectors of weight $\mu$ is finite-dimensional and $A$-stable. Some $V_\mu$ is nonzero, and the twisted trace is $2^n \sum_{\mu \in H} \operatorname{tr}(A \mid V_\mu)$.
2. **Multiplicity one** (`multG_of_multFor multiplicity_formula_holds`). There is one $\mu_0 \in H$ with $V_\mu = 0$ for all other $\mu$, and $\dim V_{\mu_0} \le 1$.
3. **A sign** (`signOne`). An involution of a line has trace $\pm 1$.

By 1 and 2, $V_{\mu_0}$ is a line and the sum has one term. Then 3 gives the sign.
