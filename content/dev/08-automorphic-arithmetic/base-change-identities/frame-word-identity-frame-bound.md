This theorem proves the growth bound for traces of powers on the finite-dimensional part of a run frame. It is one of the three inputs of the isobaric base change identity in the step [Isobaric base change holds](/step/base-change-n/), together with the existence of frames ([frames from finiteness](/entry/frame-ex-of-fin/)) and the reading of eigenvalues. From there the result goes to [EveryCharacter from the Galois row](/step/every-character/). The theorem has no hypotheses.

## Statement

Let $E$ be a CM field unramified over $E^+$ at every finite place, $n \ge 1$, and $\pi$ an automorphic representation of $U_n$. Assume the reading limit `ReadLimit` and the algebra laws of block diagonal operators `DiagAlg`. Let $F$ be a run frame over a frame $F_0$ for $\pi$, with the structure condition `Struct`. The frame gives:

- Hilbert spaces $H_s$, one for each block $s$, and a space $\mathbb{C}^d$ with an operator $u$;
- generators $g_x = ((g_{x,s})_s, \mathrm{diag}(c_x))$ whose block parts commute with each other and with their adjoints, and a marked generator $g_{a_0}$;
- operators $\beta_s$ on $H_s$ and an exponent $m$.

**Theorem.** Let $a = (a_1, a_2)$ be in the $*$-algebra generated by the $g_x$, with $a = b\, g_{a_0}$ for some $b$ in that algebra. Then there are $C$, $N$ and $j$ such that, for all large $k$,
$$\bigl|\operatorname{tr}\bigl((u\, a_2)^k\bigr)\bigr| \le C\,(1 + k)^N \max_s \bigl\|(\beta_s^m\, a_{1,s})^{k-j}\bigr\|.$$

So the trace of powers on the finite-dimensional side grows at most like the norms of powers on the block side.

## Proof

1. **The word identity** (`wordId`). For each frame with `Struct`, there are constants $c_i$ such that, for each nonempty list of words in the generators and their adjoints, the trace on $\mathbb{C}^d$ of $u^{|ws|}$ times the product of the operators of the unitary side along the words is $\sum_i c_i$ times a scalar times an elliptic sum on the block $\mathrm{GL}_a \times \mathrm{GL}_{n-a}$ of index $i$. Each block test function also satisfies a test bound. `wordId` gets this from about fifteen lemmas on words: lengths, products, letters, adelic and archimedean pieces, test functions, traces and the unitary side. Its two inputs are `locWord`, an identity for local products of letters, and `locMatch`, local matching for those products, built from three matching lemmas of `MatchAll`. We have not unfolded these lemmas.
2. **From words to the bound** (`frameBound_of`). `PTop.gBound_words` turns the word identity into the bound above, with the commutation of $u$ with the diagonal operators.
