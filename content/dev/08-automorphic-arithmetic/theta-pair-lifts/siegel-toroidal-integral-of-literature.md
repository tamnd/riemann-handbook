## Statement

Let $g \ge 1$ and let $n \ge 3$ be odd. Let $\Lambda_n \subset \mathbb{C}$ be the ring generated by a primitive $n$-th root of unity $\zeta_n$ and by $1/n$. A *Siegel family* (the structure `SFam`) is a scheme $\mathcal{A}$, smooth of relative dimension $g(g+1)/2$ and quasi-projective over $\Lambda_n$, with a principally polarized abelian scheme of dimension $g$ with symplectic level-$n$ structure. It is universal for reduced bases with a dense characteristic-zero locus, and unique over local rings.

A *polarization function* $\varphi$ (the predicate `IsPolFan`) is a function on positive semidefinite forms of rank at most $g$. It is homogeneous, superadditive, integral and positive on nonzero integral forms, and it comes with a cone decomposition with finitely many cones up to $\mathrm{GL}$.

**Theorem.** For each $g \ge 1$ there is such a $\varphi$ with this property. For each odd $n \ge 3$ there are a Siegel family $\mathcal{A}$ and:

1. a scheme $\overline{S}$, projective and smooth of relative dimension $g(g+1)/2$ over $\Lambda_n$, and an open immersion $j \colon \mathcal{A} \to \overline{S}$ over $\Lambda_n$ whose image is dense in each fibre;
2. a semi-abelian scheme $G \to \overline{S}$, smooth of relative dimension $g$, that restricts to the universal abelian scheme on $\mathcal{A}$ and is abelian exactly over $j(\mathcal{A})$;
3. an effective Cartier divisor $B$ and $d \ge 1$ such that each point of $\overline{S}$ has a chart (the predicate `HasChart`). The chart is a complete regular local ring with parameters $t_1, \dots, t_m$. In it the boundary is $t_1 \cdots t_m = 0$, $G$ has a uniformization with monomial periods given by a cone $\gamma_1, \dots, \gamma_m$ of the decomposition, and $B$ is $\prod_i t_i^{a_i}$ with $a_i = d\,\varphi(n\gamma_i)$;
4. an $N \ge 1$ such that $\omega^N$ is generated by global sections, where $\omega$ is the Hodge line bundle of $G$, and $\omega^{Nk} \otimes \mathcal{O}(-B)$ is ample for all large $k$.

The project's list of literature cites Faltings and Chai, *Degeneration of Abelian Varieties* (Springer, 1990), Chapters IV and V, without theorem numbers.
