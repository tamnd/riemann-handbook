This theorem says that product test functions over $E$ and $F$ that match at every place also match globally, with one extra constant $\kappa$. [Matched test functions for one Hecke monomial](/entry/term-ex/) uses it. The proof only passes nine proved facts to `stable_twisted_trace_formula_comparison`, where the work is.

## Statement

Let $E/F$ be cyclic of prime degree, with generator $\sigma$, and $n > 0$. Fix Haar measures, with the local ones normalized on $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathcal{O}_E \otimes \mathcal{O}_v)$ and $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathcal{O}_v)$.

**Theorem.** There is $\kappa > 0$ with this property. Let $S$ be a finite set of finite places of $F$. Let $\varphi_\infty$, $f_\infty$ and $\varphi_v$, $f_v$ be continuous with compact support, equal to the unit functions with $c_v = 1$ for $v \notin S$. Suppose $\varphi_\infty$ matches $f_\infty$ with constant $c_\infty$, and $\varphi_v$ matches $f_v$ with constant $c_v$ for each $v$. Then the averages over the positive reals of $\varphi_\infty \otimes \bigotimes_v \varphi_v$ and $f_\infty \otimes \bigotimes_v f_v$ match globally (`GMatch`) with constant $\kappa\, c_\infty \prod_{v \in S} c_v$.

Local matching (`Match`) compares twisted orbital integrals with orbital integrals at elements with separable characteristic polynomial. `GMatch` does the same globally at elements with irreducible characteristic polynomial.

## Proof

The proof applies `stable_twisted_trace_formula_comparison` to proofs of nine statements $Q_1, Q_2, Q_3, N_3, N_5, P_1, P_2, K_1, H_1$. Among them are the twisted centralizers in the adelic group ($N_3$); the embedding of $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{A}_F)$ in $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{A}_E)$ and the modulus ($N_5$); local orbits that are compact modulo the centralizer ($P_2$); the twisted orbits of the units at almost all places ($K_1$); and a Hasse principle ($H_1$): an elliptic element that is a twisted norm at every place is a global twisted norm. $H_1$ comes from the statement `HasseNorm`, which we have not unfolded.
