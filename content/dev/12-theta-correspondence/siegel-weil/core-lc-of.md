This theorem proves `CoreLC` from `FFT`. It is the main input of [`lc_of`](/entry/lc-of/), which moves it back through the Fock map to the statement `LC`. It says that a linear form killed by certain operators $D_{ab}$ is a contraction after an element of the algebra of the operators $K_{ij}$. The proof uses the double commutant theorem on finite-dimensional pieces of the polynomial ring.

## Statement

Use the notation of [`lc_of`](/entry/lc-of/): polynomials $P_{m,n}$ in $x_{ai},\bar x_{ai}$, signs $\varepsilon_a$, and $c>0$. Define the operators

$$D_{ab}=\begin{cases}\sum_i\bigl(\bar x_{bi}\,\partial_{\bar x_{ai}}-x_{ai}\,\partial_{x_{bi}}\bigr)&\varepsilon_a=\varepsilon_b,\\ \sum_i\bigl(2c\,\partial_{x_{bi}}\partial_{\bar x_{ai}}-\tfrac{1}{2c}\,x_{ai}\bar x_{bi}\bigr)&\varepsilon_a\neq\varepsilon_b,\end{cases}\qquad K_{ij}=\sum_{\varepsilon_a=+}x_{ai}\,\partial_{x_{aj}}-\sum_{\varepsilon_a=-}\bar x_{aj}\,\partial_{\bar x_{ai}}.$$

Let $\mathcal{K}_\varepsilon$ be the algebra generated by the $K_{ij}$. Let $\operatorname{contr}_c$ be the linear form that sends a monomial $\prod x^{s}\bar x^{t}$ to $\prod s!\,(2c)^{s}$ if $s=t$, and to $0$ otherwise.

**Theorem.** Assume `FFT`. Let $\Lambda$ be a linear form on $P_{m,n}$ with $\Lambda(D_{ab}p)=0$ for all $a,b,p$. Then for each $p$ there is $w\in\mathcal{K}_\varepsilon$ with $\Lambda(p)=\operatorname{contr}_c(wp)$.

## Proof

The proof is `coreLC_of'` with seven proved facts. Split the variables in two families: $F_1$ holds $x_{ai}$ with $\varepsilon_a=+$ and $\bar x_{ai}$ with $\varepsilon_a=-$, and $F_2$ holds the rest. A *box* is the span of the $F_1$ monomials with given row degrees; it has finite dimension.

1. `repB`: there is an operator $B$ that keeps each box, commutes with the first-family part of the $D_{ab}$, and satisfies $\Lambda(fg)=\operatorname{contr}_c(Bf\cdot g)$ for $f\in F_1$, $g\in F_2$.
2. `formFacts`: a weighted form on $P_{m,n}$ is Hermitian and positive, $K_{ij}$ and $K_{ji}$ are adjoint for it, and each $K_{ij}$ keeps each box.
3. `equivLift`: an operator that keeps a box and commutes with all $K_{ij}$ agrees on the box with an element of the algebra of first-family operators. This step uses `FFT`, through `invOp`.
4. Fix $p$ and the box $V$ of its row degrees. By 3, every operator that commutes with the $K_{ij}|_V$ comes from the first-family algebra, so it commutes with $B|_V$.
5. `bicomm` is the double commutant theorem for a family closed under adjoints on a finite-dimensional space with a positive Hermitian form. With 2 and 4, it puts $B|_V$ in the algebra generated by the $K_{ij}|_V$. `kAlg_of_adjoin_restrict` lifts this to some $w\in\mathcal{K}_\varepsilon$.
6. Split each monomial of $p$ into its $F_1$ and $F_2$ parts and sum.
