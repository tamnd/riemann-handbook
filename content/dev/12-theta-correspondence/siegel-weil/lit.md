This theorem proves `SiegelWeilUnitarySchrodingerLit`: the Siegel Eisenstein series on $\mathrm{U}(n,n)$ continues to the point $s_0 = \tfrac{m-n}{2}$, and its value there is the theta integral of $\mathrm{U}(V)$. It is the main part of [the Siegel-Weil formula in the Schrödinger model](/entry/field/). The proof here only packs the hypotheses and calls three large results.

## Statement

Let $E/F$ be the CM extension of the data $D$, with quadratic character $\eta$. Let $V$ be a Hermitian space over $E$ of dimension $m$ and Witt index $r$, with $n < m \le 2n$ and $n + r < m$. Let $G = \mathrm{U}(n,n)(\mathbb{A})$, with Siegel parabolic $P = MN$ and standard compact subgroup $K$. Take:

- an additive character $\psi$ of $\mathbb{A}_F$ (`IsCharAF`, not unfolded), and a Hecke character $\chi$ with $\chi|_{\mathbb{A}_F^\times} = \eta^m$;
- a measure $\mu$ on $M_{m \times n}(\mathbb{A}_E)$ for which $M_{m \times n}(E)$ has covolume $1$;
- a representation $\omega$ of $G$ on $S(V(\mathbb{A})^n)$ that satisfies the [Schrödinger model](/learn/theta/#the-schrdinger-model) formulas for $m(a)$, $n(b)$ and $w$, with $\omega(w)$ the Fourier transform for $\mu$;
- a height $H$ with $H(pk) = |\det a|$ for $p \in P$ with Levi block $a$ and $k \in K$;
- a Haar measure $\nu$ on $\mathrm{U}(V)(\mathbb{A})$, and $\Phi$ in the span of the functions $p(x, \bar x) \cdot (\text{Gaussian}) \cdot \varphi_f(x_f)$, with $p$ a polynomial and $\varphi_f$ a Schwartz-Bruhat function.

Put $\Phi_z(g) = H(g)^{z - s_0}(\omega(g)\Phi)(0)$ and $E(g, z) = \sum_{\gamma \in P(F) \backslash G(F)} \Phi_z(\gamma g)$. Let $I(g)$ be the average of $\theta(g, h; \Phi) = \sum_{\xi \in V(E)^n} (\omega(g)\Phi)(h^{-1}\xi)$ over $\mathrm{U}(V)(F) \backslash \mathrm{U}(V)(\mathbb{A})$.

**Theorem.** For each $g \in G$ there are a set $U \ni s_0$ that contains a right half-plane and is stable under $z \mapsto z + t$ for $t \ge 0$, and a function $\tilde E$ meromorphic on $U$, equal to $E(g, \cdot)$ on a right half-plane, holomorphic at $s_0$, with $\tilde E(s_0) = I(g)$.

This is the identity $E(g, s_0, \Phi) = I(g, \Phi)$ of the Siegel-Weil formula with $\kappa = 1$, as on the [Learn page](/learn/theta/#the-siegel-weil-formula) (Weil, Acta Math. 113, 1965; Ichino, Math. Z. 247, 2004). Since $m \le 2n$, the point $s_0$ is not in the half-plane $\operatorname{Re} z > \tfrac n2$ where the series converges.

## Proof

The proof packs the hypotheses into `Std` and `InRange` and applies `top7` to three results:

1. `genA_hf`: $G$ is generated by the upper unipotent, block diagonal and lower unipotent matrices.
2. `gIdil_gen wtc genA_hf`: the statement `GIdil`, about theta functions along dilations at an archimedean place. We have not unfolded it.
3. `coefND`: for each Hermitian $\beta$ with $\det \beta \ne 0$, the $\beta$-th Fourier coefficient of $E(g, z)$ along $N$ continues holomorphically to $s_0$, with value the $\beta$-th coefficient of $I$. It combines `unfolding_holds`, `theta_coefficient_of_convergence wtc` and `whittaker_nondegenerate_of_iwasawa`.

`top7` adds `fTfact`, `schDeriv` and `derivOne wtc` and calls `top6`, then `top5` and further steps. We have not unfolded this chain, so we do not say how it treats the degenerate coefficients.

The value at $s_0$ is `HolValueAt`. Its domain need not be open, but it contains the ray from $s_0$ to the right. The series is `Eis.cosetSum`, a `tsum` with a chosen representative of each coset. It is the true series only where it converges. The average $I$ is `⨍`, so the volume is normalized to $1$.
