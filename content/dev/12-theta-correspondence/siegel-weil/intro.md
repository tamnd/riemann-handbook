This chapter proves the Siegel-Weil formula for unitary dual pairs over a CM extension $E/F$: an Eisenstein series of a Siegel parabolic subgroup, evaluated on the section of a Schwartz function, equals the average of its theta function over the unitary group. It first proves the formula in measure form, as an identity between the theta measure of a Gram fibre and an Euler product of local fibre measures, for singular targets and then for anisotropic forms by an induction on the rank from the rank one and binary cases. It compares Fourier coefficients to get Weil's formula in the convergent range $2n < m$. For the point $s_0$ it reduces the formula to three inputs and proves the archimedean ones: Gaussian laws at a real place, holomorphy of leading terms, the description of null polynomials by the first fundamental theorem of invariant theory, the absolute convergence theorem, and the entire continuation and order of vanishing of real Whittaker integrals. The chapter uses from Volume 1 the [Weil representation](/learn/theta/#the-weil-representation) in the [Schrödinger model](/learn/theta/#the-schrdinger-model), [theta kernels](/learn/theta/#theta-kernels), [Eisenstein series](/learn/automorphic-forms/#eisenstein-series) and their [Whittaker functions](/learn/automorphic-forms/#fourier-coefficients-and-whittaker-functions), [Haar measure](/learn/analysis/#haar-measure) and the [Fourier transform](/learn/analysis/#the-fourier-transform), [Hecke characters](/learn/class-field-theory/#hecke-characters), and [unitary groups](/learn/groups/#unitary-groups) with their [parabolic subgroups](/learn/groups/#parabolic-subgroups). The statement it proves is the one recalled in [the Siegel-Weil formula](/learn/theta/#the-siegel-weil-formula).

[The formula in the convergent range](/entry/weil-siegel-formula/) is the input to [the Weil representation of the unitary pair](/entry/weil-unitary/) in the chapter on Howe duality. The archimedean results are used in the step [EveryCharacter](/step/every-character/): [the holomorphy of leading terms](/entry/hol-lead-b/), [the theorem on null polynomials](/entry/lc-of/), [the averaging theorem](/entry/cyc-of/), [the absolute convergence theorem](/entry/abs-thm-of/), [the gain of decay](/entry/cls-gain-b/), [the real Whittaker integral](/entry/wr/) and [the binary forms](/entry/one-vec-binary/).
