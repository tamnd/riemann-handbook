This theorem is a second name for [`exists_locAt_big`](/entry/exists-loc-at-big/). Its one user is [the flat dictionary for large choices](/entry/flat-at-big/), which takes from it the fact `LocAt`. That entry, in turn, serves [the unfolding of the iterated theta lift](/entry/twisted-theta-integral-unfolding/).

## Statement

**Theorem.** For all large admissible choices $\mathrm{ch}$ (in the sense of `Big`) there is a sign $s \in \{\pm 1\}$ with $\mathrm{LocAt}(\mathrm{ch}, s)$.

$\mathrm{LocAt}(\mathrm{ch}, s)$ says that the unfolded integrand at the representative $b$ of a coset family $\beta$ is a scalar $\zeta$ times a combination of the family at a base point $b_0$. On staircase cosets the scalar is $\kappa z_t^{-1}$, with $\kappa$ fixed and $z_t$ in the subgroup generated by the local values of $\xi_t$. The full statement is in [the entry of `exists_locAt_big`](/entry/exists-loc-at-big/).

## Proof

The proof applies `GlobalTheory.RowVal.exists_locAt_big`. The statement is the same word for word.

## In the Lean code

The theorem is in [FlatDictionaryBaseVacuaLargeDepthStatement.lean](lean:Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/TwistedOperators/FlatDictionaryBaseVacuaLargeDepthStatement.lean#L12). The name speaks of "large depth", but the statement has no depth parameter. Largeness enters only through `Big`, that is, through the size $x$ of the size data and the scale of the choice.
