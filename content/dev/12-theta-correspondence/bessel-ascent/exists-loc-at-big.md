This theorem proves the fact `LocAt` for all large admissible choices. Through its second name [`flat_dictionary_base_vacua_large_depth`](/entry/flat-dictionary-base-vacua-large-depth/) it goes to [the flat dictionary for large choices](/entry/flat-at-big/), and from there to [the unfolding of the iterated theta lift](/entry/twisted-theta-integral-unfolding/). It is a global statement assembled from a statement at one place.

## Statement

**Theorem.** For all large admissible choices $\mathrm{ch}$ (in the sense of `Big`) there is a sign $s \in \{\pm 1\}$ with $\mathrm{LocAt}(\mathrm{ch}, s)$.

$\mathrm{LocAt}(\mathrm{ch}, s)$ says this. There are $\kappa \in \mathbb{C}$ and units $z_t$ in the subgroup generated by the local values of $\xi_t$ with the following property. Let $\tau$ be a finite flip expansion at $t$, $M$ a volume at $t$, $\beta$ a coset family with representative $b$ for the sign $s$, $b_0$ a base point (`IsBase`), and $\tau'$ an expansion of the term at $\beta$ with combination data $c$. Then there is $\zeta$, equal to $\kappa z_t^{-1}$ if $\beta$ is a staircase coset, such that for each polynomial datum $P$ and each real point $p$:

$$\sum_d \tau_d\, \mathrm{ev}\bigl(\mathrm{con}_M(\mathrm{fock}(d_2, P), p\,b)\bigr)(d_1) = \zeta \sum_d \tau'_d\, \mathrm{ev}\Bigl(\mathrm{comb}_{c(d)}\bigl(\mathrm{con}_M(\mathrm{fock}(v, P), p\,b_0)\bigr)\Bigr)\bigl(\mathrm{comb}_{c(d)}\bigr).$$

Here $v$ is the fixed vector family `rm.vT` of the real match. So the integrand at $b$ is a multiple of a combination of the integrand at the base point $b_0$.

## Proof

The proof joins three facts with `Big.and` and applies them pointwise.

1. `PlaceAt` ([`exists_sign_flatDictionaryAtOnePlace_largeDepth`](lean:Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/TwistedOperators/ExistsSignFlatDictionaryAtOnePlaceLargeDepthStatement.lean#L12)): the same identity at one place $w$, for a local coset, with a scalar $\zeta_w = \kappa_w z_{t,w}^{-1}$ on staircase cosets and $\kappa_w = 1$ outside $W_0$. Its proof, `FlatPlace.big_placeAt`, joins ten local facts and takes the sign $s = -1$.
2. `ConLin` (`unfoldedProduct_depends_on_dataProduct`): if a finite combination of products of theta functions is zero, then the same combination of the evaluated families is zero. So the family depends only on the product function.
3. `flat_dictionary_relative_base_vacua_large_depth`, that is, `LocAsm.locAt_of_placeAt`: `PlaceAt` and `ConLin` imply `LocAt`. It takes $\kappa = \prod_{w \in W_0} \kappa_w$, $z_t = \prod_w z_{t,w}$ and $\zeta = \prod_w \zeta_w$. It writes the difference of the two sides as one combination of products and shows that it vanishes by `rowTest_combination_depends_on_product_function`. `ConLin` enters through `flippedFiniteTensor_sum_prod_local_pure_tensors`.

The rest is bookkeeping.
