## The constant term along a Witt tower

Let $V = V' \oplus (X + X^*)$ be a Hermitian space with an isotropic subspace $X$ of rank $r$. This section proves the constant term formula for [theta towers](/learn/theta/#theta-towers): the constant term along the unipotent radical of the parabolic subgroup that fixes $X$ of the theta lift of a cusp form $F$ on $G_W$ from $V$ is the theta lift of $F$ from the smaller space $V'$. The Weil representations of the two levels must have the same splitting character on the $V$ side. This is the Rallis tower property, and the step [the nonempty hypotheses](/step/nonempty-hyps/) uses it to show that the first occurrence in a tower is cuspidal. We keep the notation of the [previous section](/entry/isotropic-integrals-determine-theta-mean-products/), but we write it out where it is used.

**Definition 8.2.1 (Isotropic splitting).** Let $V$ and $V'$ be Hermitian spaces and $r \ge 1$. An *isotropic splitting* is a pair of matrices $C \in M_{n_V \times r}(E)$ and $B \in M_{n_V \times n_{V'}}(E)$ such that:

1. the columns of $C$ are linearly independent and $C^* J_V C = 0$;
2. $B^* J_V B = J_{V'}$;
3. $C^* J_V B = 0$;
4. $n_V = n_{V'} + 2r$.

Here $C^*$ is the conjugate transpose. So $X = C E^r$ is isotropic, $B$ embeds $V'$ isometrically into $X^\perp$, and the ranks are those of $V = V' \oplus (X + X^*)$.

**Definition 8.2.2 (Restriction to the smaller space).** Let $(C, B)$ be an isotropic splitting and $W$ a Hermitian space. For $y \in M_{n_W \times r}(\mathbb{A}_E)$ and $x \in \mathbb{W}_{V',W}$ put
$$\iota_C(y) = y\, C^* J_V, \qquad \beta_B(x) = x\, J_{V'}^{-1} B^* J_V,$$
both in $\mathbb{W}_{V,W}$. Let $\mu$ be a Haar measure on $M_{n_W \times r}(\mathbb{A}_E)$ and $\mathcal{Y}$ a fundamental domain for $M_{n_W \times r}(E)$ with $\mu(\mathcal{Y}) = 1$. For a function $f$ on $\mathbb{W}_{V,W}$ put
$$\operatorname{res} f(x) = \int_{\mathcal{Y}} f\bigl(\iota_C(y) + \beta_B(x)\bigr)\, d\mu(y).$$
Two homomorphisms $\omega$ on $\mathbb{W}_{V,W}$ and $\omega'$ on $\mathbb{W}_{V',W}$ are *compatible* if $\operatorname{res}(\omega(1, h) f) = \omega'(1, h)(\operatorname{res} f)$ for every such $\mu, \mathcal{Y}$, every $h \in G_W$ and every theta function $f$.

The map $\operatorname{res}$ is the isotropic mean of the previous section in the coordinates of the splitting. It is the map from the mixed model of the Weil representation of $V$ to the Weil representation of $V'$ that the constant term of a theta series produces.

**Definition 8.2.3 (Theta lift).** For a homomorphism $\omega$ on $\mathbb{W}_{V,W}$, $g \in G_V$, $h \in G_W$ and a function $f$, the *theta kernel* is $\theta(g, h; f) = \omega(g, h) f(0)$. For a Haar measure $\nu$ on $G_W$, a fundamental domain $\mathcal{F}$ of $G_W(F)$ and a function $F$ on $G_W$, the *theta lift* is
$$\theta(f, F)(g) = \int_{\mathcal{F}} \theta(g, h; f)\, F(h)\, d\nu(h).$$

This is the [global theta lift](/learn/theta/#the-global-theta-lift). Let $N_C \subset G_V$ be the subgroup generated by the elements $n$ with $(n - 1) C = 0$, $C^* J_V (n - 1) = 0$, and $(n - 1) x \in C \mathbb{A}_E^r$ for every rational $x$ with $C^* J_V x = 0$. This is the unipotent radical of the parabolic subgroup that fixes $X$. A function $F$ on $G_W$ is *cuspidal* if $\int_{\mathcal{N}} F(n h)\, d\kappa(n) = 0$ for every isotropic frame of $W$, every Haar measure $\kappa$ on the unipotent radical of the frame, every fundamental domain $\mathcal{N}$ of its rational points, and every $h$. It is *rapidly decreasing* if it is left invariant under $G_W(F)$, continuous, and $|F(h)|\, \|h\|^N$ is bounded for each $N$, where $\|h\| \ge 1$ is the height of $h$ on the quotient. These are the [cusp forms](/learn/automorphic-forms/#constant-terms-and-cusp-forms) in the form used here.

**Definition 8.2.4 (Constant term formula).** Let $(C, B)$ be an isotropic splitting, and $\omega$, $\omega'$ homomorphisms as above. The *constant term formula* holds for $(\omega, \omega')$ if for all $\mu, \mathcal{Y}$ as above, all Haar $\nu$ and fundamental domains $\mathcal{F}$ of $G_W(F)$, all Haar $\kappa$ on $N_C$ and fundamental domains $\mathcal{N}$ of $N_C(F)$, every rapidly decreasing cuspidal $F$ on $G_W$, every theta function $f$ and every $g \in G_V$: $\kappa(\mathcal{N}) < \infty$, the integrals below converge, $\operatorname{res}(\omega(g, 1) f)$ is a theta function, and
$$\int_{\mathcal{N}} \theta(f, F)(n g)\, d\kappa(n) = \kappa(\mathcal{N}) \cdot \theta'\bigl(\operatorname{res}(\omega(g,1) f), F\bigr)(1).$$
Here $\theta'$ is the theta lift for $\omega'$.

We need two estimates first. Fix an isotropic splitting $(C, B)$ and a matrix $C' \in M_{n_V \times r}(E)$ with
$$C^* J_V C' = 1, \qquad C'^* J_V C' = 0, \qquad C'^* J_V B = 0.$$
So $C'$ spans a complement $X^*$ of $X^\perp$ dual to $X$. For a function $f$ on $\mathbb{W}_{V,W}$ and $y \in M_{n_W \times r}(E)$ put
$$t_y(f) = \operatorname{res}\bigl(\rho(\iota_{C'}(y)) f\bigr)(0),$$
where $\iota_{C'}(y) = y\, C'^* J_V$. These are the terms of the theta series in the mixed model: $\sum_y t_y(f) = f(0)$ for a theta function $f$.

**Proposition 8.2.5 (The mixed terms are summable with a height bound).** Let $\psi$ be standard and fix a family of archimedean directions. There are $A, d \ge 0$ with this property. For each open subgroup $U$ of $\mathbb{W}_{V,W}$ there is $K$ such that for all $g \in G_V$, $h \in G_W$ and every theta function $F$ with $\rho((g,h) \cdot u) F = F$ for all $u \in U$ with zero archimedean part,
$$\sum_{y} |t_y(F)| \le K\, (\|g\|\, \|h\|)^A \cdot S_d(F),$$
where $y$ runs over $M_{n_W \times r}(E)$, $\|g\|$ is the height of $g$, and $S_d(F)$ is the sum of $\sup |D F|$ over the words $D$ of length at most $d$ in the derivatives of the Heisenberg action along the archimedean directions.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.T.mixedModelThetaTerms_summable_heightBound` (also `GlobalTheory.MixedTerm.mixed_terms_bound`, the proof).

*Proof.* First, the denominators are bounded. For each $U$ there is $M$, and for each $(g, h)$ an integer $N \ge 1$ with $N \le M (\|g\| \|h\|)^e$, such that a nonzero term $t_y(F)$ for $F$ as in the statement has a vanishing derivative along each rational pairing direction unless $y$ satisfies an integrality condition with denominator $N$. Second, there are integral matrices $X_1, \dots, X_n$ whose rational pairings with $y$ separate the points $y$. Third, each term decays: derivatives along the directions $X_j$ give the bound $|t_y(F)| \le K_0\, S_{2n}(F) \cdot \prod_j (1 + |\langle y, X_j \rangle|^2)^{-1}$ in the pairing coordinates. A lattice sum with quadratic decay in $n$ coordinates on a lattice with denominator $N$ is at most $(5 N^2)^n$ times the constant. This gives $A = 2en$, $d = 2n$ and $K = K_0 (5 M^2)^n$. $\square$

**Theorem 8.2.6 (Moderate growth of the mixed theta series).** Let $(\psi, \chi_V, \chi_W, \omega)$ be a Weil datum for $(V, W)$. There is $A \ge 0$ such that for every theta function $f$ there is $c$ with, for all $g \in G_V$ and $h \in G_W$:

1. each map $(g, h) \mapsto t_y(\omega(g, h) f)$ is continuous;
2. $\sum_y |t_y(\omega(g, h) f)| \le c\, (\|g\|\, \|h\|)^A$.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.T.mixedModel_thetaSeries_moderate_growth` (also `GlobalTheory.MixedSeriesGrowth.mixed_series_growth`, the proof).

*Proof.* Choose a family of archimedean directions. The function $F = \omega(g, h) f$ is a theta function, and by covariance it is fixed by $\rho((g, h) \cdot u)$ for $u$ in the level $U$ of $f$. The [Proposition 8.2.5](/entry/mixed-model-theta-terms-summable-height-bound/) bounds the sum by $K (\|g\| \|h\|)^{A_1} S_d(\omega(g,h) f)$. The words in the Heisenberg derivatives of $\omega(g,h) f$ have sup norms of polynomial growth: $S_d(\omega(g, h) f) \le c_f (\|g\| \|h\|)^{A_2}$. Take $A = A_1 + A_2$ and $c = \max(K, 0) \max(c_f, 0)$. Continuity of each term follows from the continuity of $\omega$ and of the restricted Heisenberg translates at $0$. $\square$

**Theorem 8.2.7 (Constant term of a theta lift from compatible Weil representations).** Let $(C, B)$ be an isotropic splitting, $(\psi, \chi_V, \chi_W, \omega)$ a Weil datum for $(V, W)$ and $(\psi, \chi_{V'}, \chi_W', \omega')$ a Weil datum for $(V', W)$. If $\omega$ and $\omega'$ are compatible, then the constant term formula holds for $(\omega, \omega')$.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.T.thetaLift_constantTerm_compatibleWeil` (also `GlobalTheory.TowerCT.towerConstTermOfCompatible_holds`, the proof).

*Proof.* Choose $C'$ as above; it exists for every isotropic splitting. For a theta function $u$ the terms $t_y(u)$ are summable with sum $u(0)$, by the theta series on a fundamental domain of covolume $1$. Apply this to $u = \omega(n g, h) f$, so that $\theta(n g, h; f) = \sum_y t_y(\omega(n g, h) f)$.

The terms are permuted by rational elements: a rational unipotent element of $N_C$ fixes the set of terms, and a rational $\gamma \in G_W(F)$ sends $t_y$ to $t_{\gamma^{-1} y}$. Split the sum over $y \in M_{n_W \times r}(E)$ into three parts.

1. If $y^* J_W y \neq 0$, the term has mean zero over $\mathcal{N}$: $\int_{\mathcal{N}} t_y(\omega(n g, h) f)\, d\kappa(n) = 0$. The torus part of the action multiplies it by a nontrivial character.
2. If $y \neq 0$ and $y^* J_W y = 0$, write $y = L m$ with $L$ an isotropic frame of $W$ and $m$ of full rank. The mean of the term over $\mathcal{N}$ is invariant under the unipotent radical $N_L$ of $L$ in $G_W$. The rational stabiliser of the frame normalises $N_L$, so the sum of these terms over one $G_W(F)$-orbit, integrated against $F$ on $\mathcal{F}$, is an integral of $F$ over $N_L(F) \backslash N_L$. It vanishes since $F$ is cuspidal.
3. The term $y = 0$ is $\operatorname{res}(\omega(n g, h) f)(0)$. The restriction is invariant under $N_C$, so its integral over $\mathcal{N}$ is $\kappa(\mathcal{N})\, \operatorname{res}(\omega(g, h) f)(0)$. By compatibility this is $\kappa(\mathcal{N})\, \omega'(1, h)(\operatorname{res}(\omega(g, 1) f))(0)$, and its integral against $F$ is the right side of the formula.

The [Theorem 8.2.6](/entry/mixed-model-theta-series-moderate-growth/) bounds $\sum_y |t_y|$ by a power of the heights, and $F$ is rapidly decreasing, so all sums and integrals converge absolutely and Fubini moves the sum and the two integrals past each other. $\square$

The compatibility follows from the splitting characters.

**Theorem 8.2.8 (Compatibility from the same splitting character).** Let $(C, B)$ be an isotropic splitting, $(\psi, \chi_V, \chi_W, \omega)$ a Weil datum for $(V, W)$ and $(\psi, \chi_V, \chi_W', \omega')$ a Weil datum for $(V', W)$, with the same $\psi$ and the same first character $\chi_V$. Then $\omega$ and $\omega'$ are compatible.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.T.weilCompatible_of_sameSplittingCharacter` (also `GlobalTheory.SameCharCompat.compatibleOfSameChar`, the proof).

*Proof.* The restriction intertwines the two representations up to a character: there is a homomorphism $c : G_W \to \mathbb{C}^1$ with
$$\operatorname{res}(\omega(1, h) f) = c(h)\, \omega'(1, h)(\operatorname{res} f)$$
for every theta function $f$. This is the Stone-von Neumann theorem applied to $\operatorname{res}$, which intertwines the Heisenberg actions. The character $c$ has three properties. It is continuous. It is trivial on $G_W(F)$, because both representations fix $f \mapsto f(0)$ at rational points and the restriction of a theta function is a theta function. It is trivial on elements supported at one split place, because condition 6 of a Weil datum computes both actions of $(1, h)$ there as a positive number times $\chi_V(\det h)$, with the same $\chi_V$. A continuous character of $G_W$ that is trivial on $G_W(F)$ and on the elements supported at split places is trivial: the split places together with the rational points generate a dense subgroup modulo the determinant, by [strong approximation](/learn/groups/#strong-approximation). So $c = 1$. $\square$

**Theorem 8.2.9 (Constant term along a Witt tower).** Let $(C, B)$ be an isotropic splitting, $(\psi, \chi_V, \chi_W, \omega)$ a Weil datum for $(V, W)$ and $(\psi, \chi_V, \chi_W', \omega')$ a Weil datum for $(V', W)$. Then the constant term formula holds for $(\omega, \omega')$.

Lean: `GlobalTheory.T.constantTermLift_isotropicSubspace` (also `GlobalTheory.TowerConstTermObj.towerConstTerm`, the proof).

*Proof.* This is the [Theorem 8.2.7](/entry/theta-lift-constant-term-compatible-weil/) applied to the compatibility given by the [Theorem 8.2.8](/entry/weil-compatible-of-same-splitting-character/). $\square$
