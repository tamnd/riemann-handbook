This chapter proves the parts of the theta correspondence for unitary groups that the proof uses directly. Sections 1 to 3 prove an integral Witt theorem for Hermitian matrices over an unramified quadratic extension, write sums over a Gram class in a lattice model as combinations of self-dual vacua, and deduce unramified Howe duality: the spherical vectors of one member of the dual pair are generated by the vacuum under the other. Section 4 computes the unramified doubling integrals at inert and split places and the rapid decay of cuspidal differences. Section 5 constructs the global Weil representation of a unitary pair normalized by Hecke characters, the real metaplectic representation, and the Lie action on Fock vectors. Section 6 derives the agreement of theta integrals and local matching from Eisenstein continuation and the Siegel-Weil formula, Section 7 treats the Rallis inner product in the stable range and the injectivity of theta lifts, and Section 8 proves results quoted from the literature: real Bessel uniqueness, an archimedean input on discrete series, the unitary Siegel-Weil formula, prismatic Dieudonné theory, local Bloch-Kato and the type II correspondence. The chapter needs from Volume 1 the [Weil representation](/learn/theta/#the-weil-representation) with its [Fock model](/learn/theta/#the-fock-model), the [local theta correspondence](/learn/theta/#the-local-theta-correspondence), the [doubling method](/learn/theta/#the-doubling-method), the [Siegel-Weil formula](/learn/theta/#the-siegel-weil-formula), the [Rallis inner product formula](/learn/theta/#the-rallis-inner-product-formula), the [local classification of Hermitian lattices](/learn/hermitian-lattices/#local-classification), [discrete valuation rings and lattices](/learn/commutative-algebra/#discrete-valuation-rings-and-lattices) and [unramified representations](/learn/representations/#unramified-representations).

Unramified Howe duality and the real input are used in the [separation of functionals](/entry/functionals-separate-of-howe-unram-of-siegel-weil/) of the [Bessel seesaw chapter](/proof/bessel-seesaw/). The theorem on theta integrals and local matching gives the step [theta integrals agree and local matching](/step/theta-integrals-agree-and-local-matching-of-literature/) of the [theta matching chapter](/proof/theta-matching/). The lift properties feed the [real lift splitting](/entry/at-real-lift-splits-locally/) of the [unitary hypotheses chapter](/proof/unitary-hypotheses/), and the remaining results go to the steps [EveryCharacter from the literature](/step/every-character-of-literature/) and [EveryCharacter of rows](/step/every-character-of-row/).
