This chapter proves the unfolding laws behind the seesaw identities for Bessel periods of theta lifts on unitary groups. A chain of theta lifts climbs a tower of Hermitian spaces. Its Bessel and Fourier-Jacobi periods unfold into each other, rung by rung, and the chain closes with a seesaw identity at the bottom. We start with the mixed model of the Weil representation at the origin. Then we prove the evaluation form of the Bessel unfolding and the eight unfolding laws of a chain. After that we give the local inputs: the flat maps at split places, the archimedean facts, and the standard form of the Weil datum at an inert place. The last result is a quantitative Mahler criterion, which bounds the growth of automorphic functions near the cusps. For the background see [the Weil representation](/learn/theta/#the-weil-representation), [reductive dual pairs](/learn/theta/#reductive-dual-pairs), [the global theta lift](/learn/theta/#the-global-theta-lift) and [Bessel and Fourier-Jacobi periods](/learn/periods/#bessel-and-fourier-jacobi-periods).

The results are inputs to three steps of [the Bessel seesaw proof](/proof/bessel-seesaw/): [the nonempty hypotheses](/step/nonempty-hyps/), [every character, from the literature](/step/every-character-of-literature/), and [every character of the row](/step/every-character-of-row/).
