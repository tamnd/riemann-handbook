This chapter studies theta series of Hermitian lattices over a CM field and their transformation law. Sections 1 and 2 compute the multiplier of the theta function under a unitary change of columns and show that a change of representative changes the theta function by a root of unity. Sections 3 and 4 bound the difference between the theta side and the Eisenstein side of the Siegel-Weil formula, and prove the Siegel-Weil formula for definite Hermitian lattices in the range $4N < n$ by Hecke operators and a contraction. Section 5 deduces the kernel identity for doubled test functions, and Section 6 proves that theta series with harmonic polynomial coefficients span the bounded holomorphic forms, also at a level. Sections 7 and 8 prove an index formula in a Siegel parabolic, the spherical linear forms at inert places that it gives, and the assembly of the agreement of theta integrals and local matching from fourteen assumptions. The chapter needs from Volume 1 the [transformation formula](/learn/theta-functions-cm/#the-transformation-formula) and the [theta series of lattices](/learn/theta-functions-cm/#theta-series-of-lattices), the [lattices in Hermitian spaces](/learn/hermitian-lattices/#lattices-in-quadratic-and-hermitian-spaces), the [Weil representation](/learn/theta/#the-weil-representation) with its [Fock model](/learn/theta/#the-fock-model), and the [Siegel-Weil formula](/learn/theta/#the-siegel-weil-formula) with the [doubling method](/learn/theta/#the-doubling-method).

The span of theta series at a level is used in the step [EveryCharacter from the literature](/step/every-character-of-literature/). The spherical closure proves the [theorem on theta integrals and local matching](/entry/theorem-k-head2/), which gives the step [theta integrals agree and local matching](/step/theta-integrals-agree-and-local-matching-of-literature/) of [the theta matching chapter](/proof/theta-matching/). The change of representative feeds the [change of frame](/entry/short-rule-change-mat/) of Volume 15.
