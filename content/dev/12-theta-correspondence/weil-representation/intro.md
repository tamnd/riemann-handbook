This chapter builds the Weil representation of a unitary dual pair over a CM field and proves the properties of it that the theta correspondence of the main argument uses. Locally it proves that the norm residue sign is multiplicative, computes the Gauss integral of a Hermitian form, proves the big cell relation for the split unitary group, and from it builds the Schrödinger model of the split unitary group and the splitting of Kudla. At infinity it proves Howe duality in the Fock model and the density of the polynomial Fock vectors in the Schwartz space. Globally it proves a pointwise decay bound for the adelic Weil representation, Weil's theorem that rational points fix the theta distribution, the reading of the vacuum moved by finite points in a classical model with the finite data of a bottom vacuum, the transport of the bottom stage of a theta tower by isometries, and the rationality of theta lifts under Galois conjugation. The chapter needs from Volume 1 the [local class field theory](/learn/class-field-theory/#local-class-field-theory) and the [norms and Hermitian forms](/learn/class-field-theory/#norms-and-hermitian-forms) of a quadratic extension, the [unitary groups](/learn/groups/#unitary-groups) with their [parabolic subgroups](/learn/groups/#parabolic-subgroups), the [Heisenberg and metaplectic groups](/learn/groups/#the-heisenberg-and-metaplectic-groups), the [Stone-von Neumann theorem](/learn/theta/#the-stone-von-neumann-theorem), the [Schrödinger model](/learn/theta/#the-schrdinger-model) and the [Fock model](/learn/theta/#the-fock-model), the [Fourier transform](/learn/analysis/#the-fourier-transform) and the [Sobolev norms](/learn/analysis/#sobolev-norms).

The results feed [the moderate growth of theta functions](/entry/theta-moderate-growth/), [the invariance of the theta sum under rational points](/entry/theta-sum-omega-rational/), [the existence of an algebraic vector with nonzero definite theta lift](/entry/exists-algebraic-vector-definite-theta-ne/), [the constant ratio at the bottom of the theta tower](/entry/theta-tower-bottom-constant-ratio/) and [the identity of the Petersson norm of a theta lift with a Rallis integral](/entry/theta-lift-petersson-norm-eq-rallis-integral/). The Galois conjugates of rational theta lifts are used in the step [the hypotheses are nonempty](/step/nonempty-hyps/), on the definite side.
