This chapter proves the first term identity of the regularised Siegel-Weil formula for unitary groups in the form that the proof uses, and it uses it with the doubling method to show that a theta lift down to a smaller unitary group is not zero. Let $B$ be the sum of a Hermitian space $W$ of rank $r$ and $t$ hyperbolic planes, with $1 \le r < t$. The Siegel Eisenstein series of a Siegel-Weil section from $B$ has at most a simple pole at $s = t/2$, and its residue lies in the closed span of the theta functions of $W$. The first three sections bound the Eisenstein series near the edge of convergence by heights, compute its Fourier-Jacobi coefficients, and show that the theta functions of $W$ separate functions. The fourth section finds generators of the level subgroup, and the fifth and sixth write the residue functional as an orbit integral on the theta side. The seventh section proves the residue theorem with a Hecke operator at one good place. The last section computes the unramified doubling integral at an inert place and proves the non-vanishing of the down lift.

The chapter uses from Volume 1 the following results. [Unitary groups](/learn/groups/#unitary-groups), [parabolic subgroups](/learn/groups/#parabolic-subgroups) and [Haar measure](/learn/analysis/#haar-measure) give the groups, the level subgroups and the measures of all sections. [Eisenstein series](/learn/automorphic-forms/#eisenstein-series) and [Fourier coefficients](/learn/automorphic-forms/#fourier-coefficients-and-whittaker-functions) give the series and coefficients of the first three sections, and [Fourier-Jacobi periods](/learn/periods/#bessel-and-fourier-jacobi-periods) give the unfolding of the second. The [Weil representation](/learn/theta/#the-weil-representation), [theta kernels](/learn/theta/#theta-kernels) and the [Fourier transform](/learn/analysis/#the-fourier-transform) give the theta functions and orbit measures of the third, fifth and sixth sections, and [Hecke characters](/learn/class-field-theory/#hecke-characters) give the characters of the sections. The seventh section is a case of the [Siegel-Weil formula](/learn/theta/#the-siegel-weil-formula). [The doubling method](/learn/theta/#the-doubling-method), [the global theta lift](/learn/theta/#the-global-theta-lift) and [unramified representations](/learn/representations/#unramified-representations) give the last section. The results enter the step that proves the [non-vanishing of the down lift](/entry/down-ne-k-of-hypotheses-prime/) in the [unitary hypotheses](/proof/unitary-hypotheses/), and the [inert local factor](/entry/triv/) of the chapter on Howe duality.
