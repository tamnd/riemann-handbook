This chapter proves that the global theta lift from a definite unitary group of rank $n$ over a CM field to a definite unitary group of rank $d$ is not zero in the stable range $2n \le d$, for test vectors that it constructs, and it uses this to lift an automorphic representation of even rank to two automorphic representations of odd rank whose extra Hecke characters are disjoint. The first four sections build the Weil representation of a good unitary pair, split the constant term of the theta kernel, describe the vectors that are finite under the maximal compact subgroup by polynomials in the Fock space, and prove the Siegel-Weil formula for a definite space. The next three sections construct test vectors and bumps, prove the Rallis inner product identity and the non-vanishing of the lift, and read off the weights and Satake parameters of the two lifts of odd size. The last section proves, for finite general linear groups, that the Shintani lift of a product of cuspidal characters is in the span of the base change product.

The chapter uses from Volume 1 the following results. The [Weil representation](/learn/theta/#the-weil-representation), [reductive dual pairs](/learn/theta/#reductive-dual-pairs) and [splitting characters](/learn/theta/#splitting-characters) give the representation of the first section and the dictionary of the seventh. The [Fock model](/learn/theta/#the-fock-model) gives the polynomial description of the third section and the test vectors of the fifth. The [unitary groups](/learn/groups/#unitary-groups) and [Siegel Eisenstein series](/learn/automorphic-forms/#siegel-eisenstein-series), with [Poisson summation](/learn/complex-analysis/#poisson-summation-theta-and-mellin), give the constant term split of the second section and the [Siegel-Weil formula](/learn/theta/#the-siegel-weil-formula) of the fourth. [Theta kernels](/learn/theta/#theta-kernels) and [the global theta lift](/learn/theta/#the-global-theta-lift) define the lift of the sixth section, and [the doubling method](/learn/theta/#the-doubling-method) and [the Rallis inner product formula](/learn/theta/#the-rallis-inner-product-formula) give its inner product identity. Representations of [compact groups](/learn/representations/#compact-groups) and [highest weights and branching](/learn/representations/#highest-weights-and-branching) give the Schur orthogonality step and the weights of the lift in the fifth and sixth sections, and [Haar measure](/learn/analysis/#haar-measure) gives the normalization of the coefficients. [Hecke characters](/learn/class-field-theory/#hecke-characters) give the characters of the seventh section. [Parabolic induction](/learn/representations/#parabolic-induction-and-the-jacquet-module) and [cuspidal representations](/learn/representations/#cuspidal-representations-and-segments) give the Harish-Chandra products and cuspidal characters of the last section. The results enter the step that [attaches Galois representations in even rank](/entry/to-core-k/), through [the theta lift in the stable range](/entry/t-lift-holds-k/) and [the two lifts of odd size](/entry/two-up-ok-of-k/), and the [inertial type step](/entry/kills-all-holds/), through [the Shintani lift](/entry/kills-lift-all/).
