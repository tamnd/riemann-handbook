This theorem proves `B.EqP χ`: three transformation laws for a family of theta functions attached to pairs of self-dual hermitian lattices. It is a pass-through. It fills two inputs of `eqP_all` with proved theorems, `stdOK` and `split_pair_generation`, and passes its own hypotheses on. The laws themselves come from `SquareSplitPairs.eqP_of_pair`. It lies below the step [HypsAlt is nonempty from the closed bound](/step/nonempty-hyps/).

## Statement

Let $D$ be a datum with number fields $K \subset E$, a complex conjugation on $E$ and a finite set of embeddings $\tau : E \to \mathbb{C}$ (`Car`). Let $\mathfrak{c} \ne 0$ be an ideal of $\mathcal{O}_K$ with norm prime to $2d$ (`IsCurveIdeal`). Let $\delta$ satisfy $\delta^{12} = g_2^3 - 27 g_3^2$ for the lattice generated by the images of $\mathfrak{c}$. Let $B$ (`Base`) be the choices that fix a base theta function $\theta_0$ for this lattice. Let $\chi$ be a function on fractional ideals of $E$, multiplicative on ideals of norm one, with $\chi((z)) = \prod_\tau \tau(z)^{-1}$ for $z$ of norm one (`IsChi`).

For self-dual lattices $L \subset E^N$, $L' \subset E^{N'}$, the function $e(L, L')$ (`B.eNat χ`) is $\theta_0^{\otimes}$ moved from the standard lattices to $(L, L')$ by two averaging operators $I$, times $\chi(\det(L_0, L))^{N'/2} \chi(\det(L'_0, L'))^{-N/2}$.

**Theorem.** For even $N, N'$, `B.EqP χ` holds:

1. $e(\gamma L, \gamma' L') = e(L, L')$ pulled back by $(\gamma, \gamma')$, for unitary $\gamma, \gamma'$;
2. $e(L, L'') = \chi(\det(L', L''))^{-N/2}\, I\, e(L, L')$;
3. $e(L_1, L') = \chi(\det(L, L_1))^{N'/2}\, I\, e(L, L')$.

## Proof

The proof is one line: `eqP_all B hcc hδ (stdOK B hcc hδ) χ hχ split_pair_generation`. Then `eqP_all` proves $\delta \ne 0$ (`delta_ne`), and `eqP_gen` converts `SplitPairGen` to `SplitPair D` (`splitPair_of_gen`). `SplitPairGen` gives, for two self-dual lattices $L', L''$ of even rank and $n \ne 0$, a matrix $e$ that preserves both, with $e^2 - e$, $e^* e$ and $(1 - e)^*(1 - e)$ mapping both into $n (L' \cap L'')$.
