This theorem is the eighth link of the chain of reductions that proves the averaged bad-share bound `IntegralityFromMetricForLargeConstants D` (see [`theorem_A_closed_form_holds`](/entry/theorem-a-closed-form-holds/)). [`closed_of_symmetric_dims`](/entry/closed-of-symmetric-dims/) calls it. It adds a proved finiteness fact about the block lattices to the window hypothesis and passes the rest on to [`closed_of_coefficient_function`](/entry/closed-of-coefficient-function/). It has no mathematical content of its own.

## Statement

`RestFunY` is stated in [`closed_of_orthogonal_lattice`](/entry/closed-of-orthogonal-lattice/), `OrthLat` in [`closed_of_orthogonal_basis`](/entry/closed-of-orthogonal-basis/), and `SymmetryWindowLevelDimOnAdmissible D` in [`closed_of_symmetric_dims`](/entry/closed-of-symmetric-dims/). The block lattices $\Lambda$ (`Ljj`) and the nest of quantifiers $\mathcal{T}$ are as in [`integralityFromMetric_of_orthBasisLat`](/entry/integrality-from-metric-of-orth-basis-lat/).

- `RestLjjFin`: for each index $(d, a_{\mathrm{top}}, x)$, the $\mathbb{Z}_p$-module $\Lambda$ is finitely generated, and there is a finite-dimensional $\mathbb{Q}_p$-subspace $W$ of the polynomial ring with $\Lambda \subseteq W$.
- `SymmetryFinitenessWindowLevelDimOnAdmissible D`: as `SymmetryWindowLevelDimOnAdmissible D`, but `HI` implies `RestSymm`, `RestLjjFin`, `RestWin` $C_M$ and `RestLvlDim`.

**Theorem.** For each datum $D$, `RestFunY`, `OrthLat` and `SymmetryWindowLevelDimOnAdmissible D` imply `IntegralityFromMetricForLargeConstants D`.

## Proof

The lemma `symmetric_finite_of_windows` proves `SymmetryFinitenessWindowLevelDimOnAdmissible D` from `SymmetryWindowLevelDimOnAdmissible D`. It uses `thr_mono` to open the nest $\mathcal{T}$, and it inserts `RestLjjFin` from `restLjjFin_proved`, which holds for all $X$ and all constants with no hypothesis. That lemma is `ljjFin_gen` at each index: $\Lambda$ is finitely generated by `Ljj_fg`, and $W$ is the $\mathbb{Q}_p$-span of $\Lambda$, finite-dimensional by `Ljj_span_fd`. We have not opened the proofs of `Ljj_fg` and `Ljj_span_fd`. Then `closed_of_coefficient_function` is applied to `RestFunY`, `OrthLat` and the new statement. The rest is bookkeeping.
