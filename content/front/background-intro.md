---
title: Introduction
---
This volume has the mathematics that the proof uses. It goes from congruences and groups to periods of automorphic forms, in 17 chapters. Each chapter assumes the chapters before it.

The volume states definitions and theorems. It does not prove the standard theorems: each chapter ends with the sources where the proofs are. Some sections end with a short note on where the proof in Volume 2 uses the material, as in "the window bound Vol. 2, 2.12".

A reader who knows a chapter well can go past it. The guide at the start of each chapter of Volume 2 tells which sections of this volume that chapter uses.
