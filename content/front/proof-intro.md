title: Introduction
---
This volume is the top of the proof. Chapter 0 shows on one page how the Lean code gets the Riemann Hypothesis from one statement, `everyCharacter`, about Dirichlet L-functions. Chapter 1 shows how `everyCharacter` comes from one hypothesis, the row statement `Row0b`, through a chain of large inputs. Chapters 2 to 11 take those inputs in turn, from the Galois row down to the theta lifts.

Each chapter starts with a guide: what to learn first, the new objects of the chapter, and the path through it. Then comes the argument in words, in numbered sections. Last come the steps: each step is one Lean declaration with its statement and its proof in words.

The large inputs of Chapters 2 to 11 have proofs of tens or hundreds of thousands of declarations. This volume shows how each one is used. Volumes 3 and later show how each one is proved.
