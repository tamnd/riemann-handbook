## Numbers and fields

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| $\mathbb{N}$, $\mathbb{Z}$, $\mathbb{Q}$, $\mathbb{R}$, $\mathbb{C}$ | the natural numbers (with 0), the integers, the rational, real and complex numbers |
| $\mathbb{F}_q$ | the finite field with $q$ elements |
| $\mathbb{Z}_p$, $\mathbb{Q}_p$ | the $p$-adic integers and the $p$-adic numbers |
| $F$, $E$ | number fields; $E$ is often a CM field and $E^+$ its totally real subfield |
| $\mathcal{O}_F$ | the ring of integers of $F$ |
| $F_v$ | the completion of $F$ at a place $v$ |
| $\mathbb{A}_F$, $\mathbb{A}_F^\infty$ | the adeles of $F$ and the finite adeles |
| $\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell$ | an algebraic closure of $\mathbb{Q}_\ell$ |
| $\mathrm{Gal}(L/K)$, $G_F$ | the Galois group of $L/K$, and the absolute Galois group of $F$ |

## Functions

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| $\zeta(s)$ | the Riemann zeta function |
| $\chi$ | a Dirichlet character, or a Hecke character of a number field |
| $L(s, \chi)$, $L(s, \pi)$ | the L-function of a character, or of an automorphic representation |
| $\Gamma(s)$ | the Gamma function |
| $\Re s$, $\Im s$ | the real and imaginary parts of $s$ |

## Groups and representations

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| $\mathrm{GL}_n$, $\mathrm{SL}_n$ | the general and special linear groups |
| $\mathrm{U}(V)$, $\mathrm{Sp}(W)$, $\mathrm{O}(V)$ | the unitary, symplectic and orthogonal groups of a space with a form |
| $\pi$ | an automorphic representation, or a representation of a local group |
| $\rho$, $r_\ell(\pi)$ | a Galois representation, and the one attached to $\pi$ at $\ell$ |
| $\theta(\pi)$ | the theta lift of $\pi$ |

## Lean names

A Lean name such as `UpperTheory.FinalTheorems.everyCharacter` has namespaces before the last dot. The handbook often drops the namespaces when the name is clear: `everyCharacter`. The index of Lean names at the end of this volume uses the full names.
